Summary:
The Public Sector Iron and Steel Companies (Restructuring) and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1978, was enacted on April 30, 1978. The Act's purpose is to restructure iron and steel companies in the public sector to improve management and efficiency. The Act includes several chapters, each with its sections and provisions.

Chapter I, Preliminary, defines key terms used throughout the Act. The term "appointed day" refers to the date the Act comes into force, while "Companies Act" refers to the Companies Act of 1956. "Corresponding unit" refers to the unit of the Integral Company that corresponds to the unit of the dissolved company or transferred company in which the concerned officer or other employee was holding office before the transfer of the said unit. "Dissolved company" means a company specified in the First Schedule, while "Integral Company" means the Steel Authority of India Limited. "Transferee company" means the company to which the undertakings of a dissolved company or of a transferred unit stand transferred, while "transferred unit" means a unit of a dissolved company or transferred company.

Chapter II, Dissolution, Transfer and Vesting of Undertakings of Certain Companies, specifies that on the appointed day, the companies listed in the First Schedule shall stand dissolved, and all their undertakings shall stand transferred to and vest in the Integral Company. Additionally, the undertakings of certain transferred units, such as Kiriburu Iron Ore Mines and Meghahatuburu Iron Ore Project, shall stand transferred to and vest in the Integral Company.

Chapter III, Transfer of Shares, specifies the transfer of shares held by certain companies to the Central Government, Integral Company, or Bharat Refractories Limited. Specifically, the Bokaro Steel Limited's shares in the Bharat Refractories Limited shall stand transferred to the Central Government, while the Integral Company's shares in certain companies shall stand transferred to the Central Government or Bharat Refractories Limited.

Chapter IV, Provisions Relating to Officers and Employees, specifies provisions related to officers and other employees of dissolved companies or transferred units. Specifically, officers and other employees holding office immediately before the appointed day in a dissolved company or transferred unit shall continue to hold office as such in the corresponding unit of the Integral Company or transferee company, respectively, with the same tenure, terms, and conditions of service.

Chapter V, Financial Provisions, specifies provisions related to income tax and surtax. The Integral Company shall be liable to pay any sum under the provisions of the Income-tax Act or the Companies (Profits) Surtax Act which any dissolved company would have been liable to pay if the dissolution had not taken place.

Chapter VI, Miscellaneous, includes provisions related to the saving of contracts, legal proceedings, removing difficulties, overriding the provisions of other laws, and amending the memorandum and articles of association of the Integral Company or other transferee company.

The First Schedule lists the companies that shall stand dissolved on the appointed day, while the Second Schedule lists the companies in which the Integral Company holds shares.
