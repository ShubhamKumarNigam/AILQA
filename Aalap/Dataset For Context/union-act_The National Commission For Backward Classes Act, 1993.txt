

The National Commission For Backward Classes Act, 1993, establishes a National Commission for Backward Classes (NCBC) to examine requests for inclusion or exclusion of any class of citizens in the lists of backward classes and to hear complaints of over-inclusion or under-inclusion of any backward class in such lists. The Commission shall consist of a Chairperson, a social scientist, two persons with special knowledge in matters relating to backward classes, and a Member-Secretary. The Chairperson and Members shall hold office for a term of three years and may be removed by the Central Government on certain grounds. The salaries and allowances payable to the Chairperson and Members and the terms and conditions of service of the officers and other employees of the Commission shall be such as may be prescribed. The Commission shall have all the powers of a Civil Court trying a suit and may summon and enforce the attendance of any person, require the discovery and production of any document, receive evidence on affidavits, and issue commissions for the examination of witnesses and documents. The Central Government may undertake revision of the lists every ten years and shall consult the Commission in this regard. The Central Government shall pay grants to the Commission and the Commission shall maintain proper accounts and other relevant records, which shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India. The annual report of the Commission shall be laid before each House of Parliament. The Chairperson, Members, and employees of the Commission are deemed to be public servants within the meaning of section 21 of the Indian Penal Code (45 of 1860).
