Summary:

The Merchant Shipping (Medicines, Medical Stores and Appliances) Rules, 1966, apply to foreign-going Indian ships and home-trade Indian ships of 200 tons gross or more, with the exception of unberthed passenger ships and pilgrim ships. These rules set out the required medicines, medical stores, and appliances for ships, as well as their packaging, labelling, and storage. The medicines, medical stores, and appliances must be inspected by the Port Health Officer at least once every 12 months. The rules also allow for the use of substitutes for any particular item of medicine or medical store, if approved by the Port Health Officer. The Central Government may grant exemptions from these rules for specific ships and under certain conditions.

These rules are made under the authority of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, and use various legal terms such as "Act," "Medical Officer," "Official pharmacopoeia," "Port health officer," and "Schedule." The rules define these terms, with "Act" referring to the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, "Medical Officer" as a person with a medical degree, "Official pharmacopoeia" as the Indian Pharmacopoeia, National Formulary of India, or British Pharmaceutic Code, and "Port health officer" as a person appointed by the Central Government to oversee the health of a port. "Schedule" refers to a schedule attached to these rules.
