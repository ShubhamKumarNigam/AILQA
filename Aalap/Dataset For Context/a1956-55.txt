Summary:

The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956, is an Act with the title ACT NO. 55 OF 1956, which was enacted on the 16th of September, 1956. The Act's purpose is to increase the number of Judges in the Supreme Court, excluding the Chief Justice.

The Act comprises two sections. Section 1 is the Short title, which states that the Act may be referred to as the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956. Section 2 is the Maximum number of Supreme Court Judges other than Chief Justice. This section states that the maximum number of Judges of the Supreme Court, excluding the Chief Justice of India, shall be thirty-three.

A significant amendment was made to this Act by the Act 37 of 2019, which came into effect on 09-08-2019. The amendment substituted the previous number of "thirty" with "thirty-three" for the maximum number of Judges of the Supreme Court, excluding the Chief Justice.
