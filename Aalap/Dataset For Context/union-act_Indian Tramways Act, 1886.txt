

Summary:

The Indian Tramways Act, 1886, is a law that regulates the construction, maintenance, and use of tramways in India. The Act defines key terms such as "local authority," "road," "road-authority," "tramway," "order," "promoter," "undertaking," "carriage," "toll," "lessee," "District Magistrate," "District Court," "Collector," and "prescribed." The Act outlines the procedure for making orders authorizing the construction of tramways, including the requirement for publication of a draft order and the opportunity for objections or suggestions to be made. The Act specifies the contents of an order, including provisions for the acquisition of land, the conditions for opening and breaking up roads, the gauge of the tramway, the maintenance of the tramway, the application of material excavated during construction, the provision of crossings, passing-places, sidings, junctions, and other works, the motive power to be used, the nature, dimensions, fittings, appliances, and apparatus of the carriages, and the traffic that may be carried and the tolls that may be levied. The Act also provides for the grant of licenses to third parties to use tramways, the inspection of tramways before opening, agreements between road-authorities and promoters as to repair of roadways, the rights of promoters and the public over tramways, the carriage of dangerous or offensive goods, and the cessation of powers of promoters and lessees on discontinuance of tramways. The Act also includes provisions for the purchase of tramways by local authorities, the lease of, or working of, tramways by local authorities, and the making of rules. The Act imposes penalties for failure to comply with the Act or an order, obstruction of promoters in the exercise of their powers, interference with tramways, the use of tramways with carriages having flange-wheels, the evasion of payment of proper toll, the taking or sending of dangerous or offensive goods without giving notice, and the failure of a licensee to give an account of traffic or giving a false account. The Act also includes provisions for the settlement of differences between promoters or lessees and authorities, the recovery of moneys due from promoters and lessees, the recovery of tolls from licensees, the recovery of tolls from passengers, and savings, including the right of user only, the saving of power over roads traversed by tramways, and the saving of power of local authority and police to regulate traffic on roads. The Act also includes supplemental provisions, including the responsibility of promoters, lessees, and licensees for all injuries, the lack of sufficient funds as a reason for default, the power to exempt from municipal taxation, the application of local funds to tramways, the extension of the Act to existing tramways, the prohibition of construction of tramways except under this Act, and the transfer of control on exclusion of a local area from the circle of a local authority.
