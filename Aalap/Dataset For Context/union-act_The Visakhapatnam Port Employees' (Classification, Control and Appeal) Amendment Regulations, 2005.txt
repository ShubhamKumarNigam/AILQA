Summary:

The Visakhapatnam Port Employees' (Classification, Control and Appeal) Amendment Regulations, 2005, is an amendment to the Visakhapatnam Port Employees' Regulations of 1968. These amendments were made to modify the rules regarding the suspension of employees at the Visakhapatnam Port.

The amendment adds two new sub-regulations to Regulation 7. Sub-Regulation 8 states that an order of suspension made or deemed to have been made under this rule shall be reviewed by the authority competent to modify or revoke the suspension before the expiry of ninety days from the date of the order of suspension. This review must be made on the recommendation of the Review Committee constituted for the purpose and pass orders either extending or revoking the suspension. Subsequent reviews shall be made before the expiry of the extended period of suspension, and the extension of suspension shall not be for a period exceeding one hundred and eighty days at a time.

Sub-Regulation 9 states that notwithstanding anything contained in sub-Regulation 6 & 7, an order of suspension made or deemed to have been made under sub-Regulations 1 to 3 of this regulation shall not be valid after a period of ninety days, unless it is extended after review, for a further period before the expiry of ninety days.

These amendments were published in the Central Government Official Gazette and came into force from the date of publication. The Visakhapatnam Port Employees' Regulations of 1968 had been previously amended several times, including in 1969, 1970, 1971, 1975, 1977, 1979, 1986, 1991, 1994, 1997, 1999, and 2003. The amendment of 2005 is one of the many amendments made to the Visakhapatnam Port Employees' Regulations of 1968.
