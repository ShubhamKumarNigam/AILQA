Summary:

The Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights (Use of Denomination of Registered Variety) Rules, 2012 is a set of rules that govern the use of the denomination of a registered variety under the Protection of Plant Varieties and Farmers' Rights Act, 2001.

The rules define the use of a denomination in relation to a registered variety as the use of the denomination upon, or in any physical or other relation whatsoever, to such variety. The package or any other container containing the seeds of a variety registered under the Act must indicate the denomination and details of registration obtained under the Act.

The denomination must appear on each container of seed in a conspicuous place and on every other covering in which that container is packed. The use of a denomination by a registered agent or registered licensee is also considered use of the denomination, provided that the agent or licensee is connected in the course of the trade, the variety remains registered, they are registered as a licensee or registered agent under the Act, and they comply with the conditions and limitations to which the registration of a registered licensee or registered agent is subject.

Use of a denomination in India is considered use in India, and applying a denomination in India to a variety to be exported from India is also considered use of the denomination. The rules came into force on the date of their publication in the official Gazette.
