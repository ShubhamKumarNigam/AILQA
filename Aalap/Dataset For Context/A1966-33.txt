Summary:
THE POLICE-FORCES (RESTRICTION OF RIGHTS) ACT, 1966, is an Act to restrict certain rights conferred by Part III of the Constitution to the members of the Forces charged with the maintenance of public order. The Act extends to the whole of India and came into force on different dates in Union territories and States. The Act defines "member of a police-force" as any person appointed or enrolled under any enactment specified in the Schedule, and "police-force" includes any force charged with the maintenance of public order. The Act restricts the rights of members of a police-force in forming associations, freedom of speech, and communicating with the press or publishing any document. Any person who contravenes these provisions shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine which may extend to two thousand rupees, or with both. The Central Government may amend the Schedule by including or omitting any enactment relating to a force charged with the maintenance of public order. Every rule made under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament while it is in session for a total period of thirty days. The Schedule specifies various enactments relating to a force charged with the maintenance of public order.
