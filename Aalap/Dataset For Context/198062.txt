Summary:
THE JUTE COMPANIES (NATIONALISATION) ACT, 1980 (ACT NO. 62 OF 1980)

Introduction:
This Act aims to acquire and transfer the undertakings of specific jute companies, as listed in the First Schedule, to ensure the continued manufacture, production, and distribution of jute articles essential to the Indian economy. The Act was enacted on the 21st of December, 1980.

Key Definitions:
- Appointed day: The date of commencement of the Act.
- Commissioner: The Commissioner of Payments appointed under section 14.
- Jute companies: The companies specified in the First Schedule.
- Jute Manufactures Corporation: The National Jute Manufactures Corporation Limited.
- Notification: A notification published in the Official Gazette.
- Prescribed: Prescribed by rules made under this Act.
- Specified date: The date specified for the purposes of a provision of this Act.

Chapters:

Chapter I: Preliminary
This chapter defines various terms used in the Act.

Chapter II: Acquisition and Transfer of the Undertakings of the Jute Companies
This chapter deals with the transfer and vesting of the undertakings of the jute companies in the Central Government. The undertakings include all assets, rights, lease-holds, powers, authorities, privileges, and all property, movable and immovable, including lands, buildings, workshops, stores, instruments, machinery, and equipment. The liabilities of the jute companies are also specified in this chapter.

Chapter III: Payment of Amounts
This chapter specifies the payment of amounts to the jute companies for the transfer and vesting of their undertakings. The amounts are specified in the First Schedule. The jute companies are also entitled to further amounts for the deprivation of the management of their undertakings.

Chapter IV: Management, etc., of the Undertakings of the Jute Companies
This chapter deals with the management, direction, control, and general superintendence of the undertakings of the jute companies by the Central Government or the Jute Manufactures Corporation.

Chapter V: Provisions Relating to Employees of the Jute Companies
This chapter deals with the continuance of employees of the jute companies and their rights and privileges.

Chapter VI: Commissioner of Payments
This chapter deals with the appointment of the Commissioner of Payments and the payment of amounts to the jute companies.

Chapter VII: Miscellaneous
This chapter contains provisions related to the overriding effect of the Act, assumption of liability, management to continue to vest in certain persons until alternative arrangements have been made, contracts to cease to have effect unless ratified by the Central Government or Jute Manufactures Corporation, penalties, offenses by companies, protection of action taken in good faith, delegation of powers, power to make rules, and power to remove difficulties.

First Schedule:
The First Schedule lists the jute companies and the amount payable to them for the transfer and vesting of their undertakings.

Second Schedule:
The Second Schedule specifies the order of priorities for the payment of claims against the jute companies.

Forms:
No forms are provided in the Act.

Footnotes:
No footnotes are provided in the Act.
