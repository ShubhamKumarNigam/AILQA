Summary:

The Kalakshetra Foundation Act, 1993 (Act No. 6 of 1994) establishes the Kalakshetra Foundation to administer the institution known as Kalakshetra, a center for the performing arts, located in Madras, Tamil Nadu. The Act declares Kalakshetra to be an institution of national importance and provides for its incorporation, administration, and further development in accordance with its aims and objects.

The Act is divided into five chapters:

Chapter I: Preliminary
This chapter contains provisions related to the short title and commencement, declaration of Kalakshetra as an institution of national importance, and definitions of terms used in the Act.

Chapter II: Acquisition and Transfer of the Assets and Properties of Kalakshetra
This chapter deals with the transfer and vesting of the assets and properties of Kalakshetra in the Central Government, the general effect of such vesting, and the continuation of certain licenses and permits.

Chapter III: Kalakshetra Foundation
This chapter establishes the Kalakshetra Foundation, sets out its objects, and defines its authorities, including the Governing Board, the Academic Committee, and the Finance Committee.

Chapter IV: Finance, Accounts, and Audit
This chapter covers the financial provisions of the Act, including grants by the Central Government, the Fund of the Foundation, budget preparation, accounts and audit, and the duty to furnish returns, annual reports, and statements.

Chapter V: Miscellaneous
This chapter contains provisions related to prior approval of the Central Government for alienation of property, power of the Central Government to give directions to the Foundation, dissolution of the Foundation, protection of action taken in good faith, indemnity, power to make rules, power to make regulations, rules and regulations to be laid before Parliament, power to remove difficulties, repeal and saving.

The First Schedule to the Act lists the constituent units of Kalakshetra. The Second Schedule lists the assets and properties of Kalakshetra that are transferred and vested in the Central Government.

The Act includes provisions related to the transfer of service of existing employees, the appointment and duties of the Director, budget preparation, accounts and audit, and the duty to furnish returns, annual reports, and statements. The Act also includes provisions related to indemnity, power to make rules, power to make regulations, rules and regulations to be laid before Parliament, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and saving.

In summary, the Kalakshetra Foundation Act, 1993 establishes the Kalakshetra Foundation to administer the institution known as Kalakshetra, a center for the performing arts, and provides for its incorporation, administration, and further development in accordance with its aims and objects. The Act includes provisions related to the transfer and vesting of assets and properties, the authorities of the Foundation, financial provisions, and miscellaneous provisions. The First Schedule lists the constituent units of Kalakshetra, and the Second Schedule lists the assets and properties of Kalakshetra that are transferred and vested in the Central Government.
