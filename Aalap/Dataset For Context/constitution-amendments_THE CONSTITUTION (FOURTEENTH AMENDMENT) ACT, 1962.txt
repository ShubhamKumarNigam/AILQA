The Constitution (Fourteenth Amendment) Act, 1962, made several significant changes to the Constitution of India. Firstly, the amendment increased the number of members in the Lok Sabha from twenty to twenty-five for the Union territories. Secondly, it added a new entry to the First Schedule of the Constitution, which lists the Union territories. The new entry refers to the Union territory of Pondicherry, which includes the territories that were previously known as the French Establishments in India, specifically Pondicherry, Karikal, Mahe, and Yanam. 

Thirdly, the amendment inserted a new article, 239A, in the Constitution. This article grants Parliament the power to create a legislature or a council of ministers, or both, for specific Union territories, namely Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, Tripura, Goa, Daman and Diu, and Pondicherry. The article also specifies the constitution, powers, and functions of such legislatures or councils of ministers.

Fourthly, the amendment made changes to article 240 of the Constitution, which deals with the President's power to make regulations for the peace, progress, and good government of Union territories. The amendment added a new entry, "Pondicherry," to clause (1) of the article. It also added a proviso to the clause, which states that when a legislature is created for the Union territories of Goa, Daman and Diu or Pondicherry, the President shall not make any regulations for the peace, progress, and good government of that Union territory with effect from the date appointed for the first meeting of the Legislature.

Finally, the amendment made changes to the Fourth Schedule of the Constitution, which deals with the allocation of seats in the Council of States (Rajya Sabha) for the States and Union territories. The amendment added a new entry, "Pondicherry," to the Table in the Fourth Schedule and increased the total number of seats from 225 to 226.

The amendment also states that certain provisions of the Act, including the changes to the First and Fourth Schedules and the addition of article 239A, came into force on August 16, 1962. This date marks the official integration of the French colonies into the Indian Union.
