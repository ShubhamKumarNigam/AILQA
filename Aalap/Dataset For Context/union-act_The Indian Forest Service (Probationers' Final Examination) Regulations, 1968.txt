The Indian Forest Service (Probationers' Final Examination) Regulations, 1968 

 [ Repeal and savings. [Inserted by Notification No. 11041/2/85-AIS (III), dated 19th September, 1986.]

(1)The Indian Forest Service (Probationers' Final Examination) Regulations, 1951, are hereby repealed.(2)Notwithstanding such repeal, any action taken or thing done or any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under the said regulations shall, so far as it is not inconsistent with the provisions of these regulations, be deemed to have been taken, done or acquired under these regulations.

Summary:

The Indian Forest Service (Probationers' Final Examination) Regulations, 1968 outline the rules and regulations for the final examination that probationers of the Indian Forest Service must take in order to complete their training. The regulations cover topics such as the subjects and syllabus for the written and practical examinations and exercises, the qualifying tests, and the minimum pass marks for examinations and standards of qualifying tests. The regulations also specify the regional language in which probationers must be examined, based on the cadre to which they are allocated. The First Schedule to the regulations includes the syllabus for the written and practical examinations and exercises, as well as the syllabus for the qualifying tests. The Second Schedule lists the regional languages for each cadre. The regulations also include a provision for the award of the Associate of Indian Forest College diploma to probationers who are successful in the final examination. Finally, the regulations include a provision for the repeal and savings of the Indian Forest Service (Probationers' Final Examination) Regulations, 1951.
