Summary:

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (Regulatory Sandbox) Regulations, 2019, are a set of rules created to facilitate innovation in the insurance sector while also protecting the interests of policyholders. These regulations establish a regulatory sandbox environment, which is a testing ground for new business models, processes, and applications that may not be fully compliant with existing regulations.

The regulations define key terms such as "applicant," "authority," "regulations," "regulatory sandbox," and "sandbox environment." An applicant can be an insurer, intermediary, or any other person seeking permission to promote innovation in insurance in India. The authority is the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India, and regulations refer to the rules framed by the authority. A regulatory sandbox is an environment used for experimentation for a specific period, while a sandbox environment is a testing environment designed for such experimentation.

The regulations establish a procedure for seeking permission for promoting innovation in insurance in India. An applicant must submit an electronic application in a specified form, accompanied by a non-refundable processing fee of ten thousand rupees. The authority may grant permission if it is satisfied that the application promotes innovation, is in the interest of policyholders, is conducive for the orderly growth of the industry, promotes insurance penetration, and meets any other requirements specified by the authority.

The authority may revoke permission at any time if the activities carried out are not meeting the conditions given for granting permission or are in violation of the provisions of the Insurance Act, 1938, the Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority Act, 1999, and other applicable laws. The authority may also grant extensions of time, but under no circumstances will the applicant be granted extension of time beyond 12 months to complete the task.

After granting permission, the applicant must maintain the integrity of systems, maintain confidentiality of policyholder data, and put in place adequate internal mechanisms for reviewing, monitoring, and evaluating its controls, systems, procedures, and safeguards. The authority will review the progress of the proposal at regular intervals and may guide the applicant in the form of suggestions.

On completion of the allocated time period or size of the proposal, the applicant must submit a report to the authority on how the proposal met the objectives, along with feedback from policyholders and a plan of action as to how the proposal be brought under the extant regulatory framework. The authority may then accord permission to the applicant to adopt the proposal under the extant regulatory framework.

The regulations also give the authority the power to grant relaxation from the provisions of any regulations to an applicant in the regulatory sandbox, subject to certain conditions. The authority may call for, inspect, or look into any document, record, or communication from the applicant. If the authority is of the opinion that the operations of the applicant are not in the interest of the Indian market or the insurance policyholders, it reserves the right to take appropriate actions, including suspension or cancellation of permission granted.
