Summary:
The Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi Act, 1991 is an Act that supplements the provisions of the Constitution relating to the Legislative Assembly and a Council of Ministers for the National Capital Territory of Delhi. The Act is divided into five parts. Part I, Preliminary, defines terms used throughout the Act. Part II, Legislative Assembly, outlines the composition, qualifications, duration, sessions, Speaker and Deputy Speaker, oath or affirmation, voting, powers, privileges, and salaries and allowances of members of the Legislative Assembly. Part III, Delimitation of Constituencies, establishes the Election Commission's role in distributing seats and delimiting territorial constituencies. Part IV, Certain Provisions Relating to Lieutenant Governor and Ministers, outlines the Lieutenant Governor's discretionary powers, the role of Ministers, and the conduct of business. Part V, Miscellaneous and Transitional Provisions, includes provisions related to the Consolidated Fund of the Capital, the Public Account of the Capital, the Contingency Fund of the Capital, borrowing upon the security of the Consolidated Fund of the Capital, form of accounts of the Capital, audit reports, relation of Lieutenant Governor and his Ministers to President, period of order made under article 239AB and approval thereof by Parliament, authorisation of expenditure by President, contracts and suits, power of President to remove difficulties, laying of rules before Legislative Assembly, and repeal of Act 19 of 1966. The Schedule includes forms of oaths or affirmations for candidates for election to the Legislative Assembly, members of the Legislative Assembly, members of the Council of Ministers, and oaths of secrecy for members of the Council of Ministers.
