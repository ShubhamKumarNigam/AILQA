Summary:
THE DALMIA DADRI CEMENT LIMITED (ACQUISITION AND TRANSFER OF UNDERTAKINGS) ACT, 1981
This Act aims to acquire and transfer the undertakings of the Dalmia Dadri Cement Limited to ensure the continued manufacture, production, and distribution of cement, which is essential to the needs of the economy of the country. The Act was enacted on 15th September, 1981.
The document contains various chapters that define the short title and commencement, definitions, acquisition and transfer of the undertakings of the Company, payment of amounts, management, etc., of the undertakings of the Company, provisions relating to the employees of the Company, and miscellaneous provisions.
Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," defines the short title and commencement of the Act, and the definitions of various terms used in the Act.
Chapter II, titled "Acquisition and Transfer of the Undertakings of the Company," deals with the transfer of the undertakings of the Company to the Central Government and its vesting in the Central Government. The General Effect of Vesting is also discussed in this chapter.
Chapter III, titled "Payment of Amounts," specifies the payment of an amount of rupees eighty-four lakhs and eighty-seven thousand to the Company for the transfer of its undertakings.
Chapter IV, titled "Management, etc., of the Undertakings of the Company," discusses the management, etc., of the undertakings of the Company by the Central Government or the Cement Corporation.
Chapter V, titled "Provisions Relating to the Employees of the Company," provides for the employment of certain employees to continue and their rights and privileges.
Chapter VI, titled "Commissioner of Payments," deals with the appointment of a Commissioner of Payments for the disbursement of amounts payable to the Company.
Chapter VII, titled "Miscellaneous," contains provisions relating to the overriding effect of the Act, contracts to cease to have effect unless ratified by the Central Government or the Cement Corporation, protection of action taken in good faith, delegation of powers, penalties, offenses by companies, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and saving.
The Schedule to the Act lists the order of priorities for the discharge of liabilities of the Company.
The Appendix to the Act contains various forms required for the implementation of the Act.
