Summary:
The Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014, is an Act that aims to protect the rights of urban street vendors and regulate street vending activities. The Act is divided into ten chapters, with various sections and schedules that outline the specific provisions of the law.

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary matters, including the short title, extent, commencement, and provisions of the Act. The Act applies to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir, and came into force on May 1, 2014. The provisions of the Act do not apply to any land, premises, and trains owned and controlled by the Railways under the Railway Act, 1989.

Chapter II of the Act deals with the regulation of street vending. Section 3 of the Act requires the Town Vending Committee to conduct a survey of all existing street vendors within its jurisdiction and ensure that all existing street vendors are accommodated in the vending zones. The Town Vending Committee is also required to issue a certificate of vending to every street vendor who has completed the age of fourteen years or such age as may be prescribed by the appropriate Government.

Chapter III of the Act deals with the rights and obligations of street vendors. Section 12 of the Act recognizes the right of street vendors to carry on the business of street vending activities in accordance with the terms and conditions mentioned in the certificate of vending. However, street vendors are not allowed to carry out any vending activities in no-vending zones.

Chapter IV of the Act deals with the relocation and eviction of street vendors. Section 18 of the Act allows the local authority to declare a zone or part of it as a no-vending zone for any public purpose and relocate the street vendors vending in that area. The local authority is required to give a thirty-day notice to the street vendor before evicting him or her from the place specified in the certificate of vending.

Chapter V of the Act deals with the dispute redressal mechanism. Section 20 of the Act provides for the constitution of one or more committees to decide the applications received from street vendors with respect to issue of certificate of vending, cancellation or suspension of certificate of vending, or any other matter relating to the regulation of street vending.

Chapter VI of the Act deals with the plan for street vending. Section 21 of the Act requires every local authority to prepare a plan for street vending once in every five years, in consultation with the planning authority and on the recommendations of the Town Vending Committee.

Chapter VII of the Act deals with the Town Vending Committee. Section 22 of the Act provides for the term and the manner of constituting a Town Vending Committee in each local authority. The Town Vending Committee shall consist of a Chairperson who has been a civil judge or a judicial magistrate and two other professional having such experience as may be prescribed.

Chapter VIII of the Act deals with the prevention of harassment of street vendors. Section 27 of the Act prohibits any person or police or any other authority from preventing a street vendor from exercising his or her rights under the Act.

Chapter IX of the Act deals with penal provisions. Section 28 of the Act provides for a penalty for contraventions of the provisions of the Act.

Chapter X of the Act deals with miscellaneous matters. Section 36 of the Act empowers the appropriate Government to make rules for carrying out the provisions of the Act.

The First Schedule of the Act outlines the plan for street vending, while the Second Schedule specifies the matters to be provided in the scheme for street vendors. The Act also includes various forms and appendices that provide guidance on the implementation of the law.

Overall, the Street Vendors (Protection of Livelihood and Regulation of Street Vending) Act, 2014, is a significant piece of legislation that aims to protect the rights of street vendors and regulate street vending activities in India. The Act provides a comprehensive framework for the regulation of street vending, including provisions for the survey of street vendors, the issuance of certificates of vending, the regulation of vending zones, and the dispute redressal mechanism. The Act also recognizes the rights of street vendors and prohibits the harassment of street vendors by any person or authority.
