Summary:
The Government of Union Territories (Amendment) Act, 1984, abbreviated as ACT NO. 19 OF 1984, was enacted on the 8th of May, 1984. This Act aims to amend the Government of Union Territories Act, 1963.

The Act comprises four sections. Section 1 is the short title, stating that the Act may be called the Government of Union Territories (Amendment) Act, 1984.

Section 2 has been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988, and the First Schedule, which took effect on the 31st of March, 1988. This section initially amended section 3, but it has since been repealed.

Section 3 has also been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988, and the First Schedule, which took effect on the 31st of March, 1988. This section initially inserted a new section 43E, but it has since been repealed.

Section 4 deals with validation, stating that any action taken or anything done for the purpose of elections to the Legislative Assembly of the Union territory of Mizoram on or after the 1st day of March, 1984, and before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to be validly and effectively done or taken as if the provisions of the principal Act as amended by section 3 had been in force at all material times.

There are no annexures, appendices, forms, or footnotes in this Act.
