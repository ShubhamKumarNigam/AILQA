Summary:

THE STATE BANK OF INDIA ACT, 1955 was enacted on May 8, 1955, to establish a State Bank for India and transfer the undertaking of the Imperial Bank of India. The Act provides for the incorporation, share capital, transfer of undertaking, and service of existing officers and employees of the Imperial Bank to the State Bank.

The Act establishes the State Bank of India, which shall have perpetual succession and a common seal. The bank shall have the power to acquire and hold property, whether movable or immovable, for the purposes for which it is constituted and to dispose of the same. The authorized capital of the State Bank shall be five thousand crores of rupees divided into five hundred crores of fully paid-up shares of ten rupees each.

The Act provides for the transfer of the undertaking of the Imperial Bank of India to the State Bank of India on the appointed day. The undertaking of the Imperial Bank shall include all rights, powers, authorities, and privileges, and all property, movable and immovable, including cash balances, reserve funds, investments, and all other interests and rights in, or arising out of, such property as may be in the possession of that bank immediately before the appointed day. The Act also provides for the transfer of service of existing officers and employees of the Imperial Bank to the State Bank.

The Act also provides for the transfer of debts, liabilities, and obligations of whatever kind then existing of the Imperial Bank to the State Bank. Any suit, appeal, or other legal proceeding of whatever nature pending by or against the Imperial Bank on the appointed day shall not abate, be discontinued, or be in any way prejudicially affected by reason of the transfer to the State Bank of the undertaking of the Imperial Bank or of anything contained in this Act. The suit, appeal, or other proceeding may be continued, prosecuted, and enforced by or against the State Bank.

Furthermore, the Act provides that any person who, on the appointed day, is entitled to or is in receipt of a pension or other superannuation or compassionate allowance or benefit from the Imperial Bank or any provident, pension, or other fund or any authority administering such fund shall be entitled to be paid by, and to receive from, the State Bank or any provident, pension, or other fund or any authority administering such fund the same pension, allowance, or benefit so long as he observes the conditions on which the pension, allowance, or benefit was granted.

Overall, THE STATE BANK OF INDIA ACT, 1955 aims to constitute a State Bank for India, transfer the undertaking of the Imperial Bank of India, and provide for other matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.
