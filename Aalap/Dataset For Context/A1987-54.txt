Summary:
The Railway Claims Tribunal Act, 1987, establishes the Railway Claims Tribunal to inquire into and determine claims against railway administrations for loss, destruction, damage, deterioration, or non-delivery of animals or goods entrusted to them for carriage by railway or for the refund of fares or freight or for compensation for death or injury to passengers occurring as a result of railway accidents or untoward incidents. The Act defines key terms, establishes the composition and jurisdiction of the tribunal, and sets out the qualifications, appointment, term of office, resignation, removal, and other terms and conditions of service of the Chairman, Vice-Chairman, and other members. The Act also provides for the holding of offices by Chairman, Vice-Chairman, etc., on ceasing to be such Chairman or Vice-Chairman, etc., and the financial and administrative powers of the Chairman. The Act specifies the staff of the Claims Tribunal, the jurisdiction, powers, and authority of the Claims Tribunal, distribution of business amongst Benches, bar of jurisdiction, procedure, right to legal representation and presenting officers, power of Chairman to transfer cases from one Bench to another, decision to be by majority, execution of orders of Claims Tribunal, appeals, transfer of pending cases, proceedings before Claims Tribunal to be judicial proceedings, Members and staff of Claims Tribunal to be public servants, protection of action taken in good faith, act to have overriding effect, power to remove difficulties, and power to make rules.
