Amendment of article 16:

In article 16 of the Constitution, after clause (4B), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:

“(4C) Notwithstanding anything contained in this article, the unfilled vacancies of a year, which are reserved for being filled up in that year in accordance with any provision for reservation made under clause (4) or clause (4A), shall be considered as a separate class of vacancies to be filled up in any succeeding year or years and such class of vacancies shall not be considered together with the vacancies of the year in which they are being filled up for determining the ceiling of fifty per cent. reservation on total number of vacancies of that year.”.


This amendment to the Constitution aims to address the adverse impact of a previous order that limited the ability to fill unfilled reserved vacancies in a given year for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The Constitution (Eighty-first Amendment) Act, 2000 allows for these unfilled vacancies to be considered as a separate class of vacancies, which can be filled in any succeeding year or years. This amendment does not change the 50% limit on the total number of reservations in a year but instead allows for a separate consideration of unfilled reserved vacancies from the previous year. This amendment aims to restore the position that was prevalent before the previous order that limited the filling of unfilled reserved vacancies.
