Summary:
The Merchant Shipping (Amendment) Act, 2003, amends the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, by adding a new part to address marine pollution prevention. This new part applies to oil tankers, ships of a certain size, off-shore installations, and incidents causing grave and imminent danger to India's coastline or related interests from pollution or the threat of pollution in the sea. The Act defines key terms such as ballast, cargo, coasts, coastal waters, Convention, international pollution prevention certificate, mile, noxious liquid substance, off-shore installation, oil, oily mixture, oil tanker, reception facilities, and ship.

Under this new part, the Central Government can issue international pollution prevention certificates for Indian oil tankers and other Indian ships, requiring them to comply with specific construction and equipment standards to prevent pollution. Ships must maintain record books for substances subject to control by the Convention. Surveyors or authorized persons can inspect ships for compliance with the Act and certify copies of records.

If a ship contravenes any provision of the Convention within India's coastal waters, the Director-General or an authorized officer can detain the ship until the causes of the contravention are removed and recover the cost of pollution damage and prevention. The Central Government can request information from other countries regarding contraventions by Indian ships and take appropriate action against the owner or master of the ship.

The Act also amends other sections of the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, by substituting the words "oil reception facilities" with "reception facilities," and adding provisions for noxious liquid substances in sections 356J and 356K. The Central Government can prescribe limits of ballast, designate noxious liquid substances, prescribe forms for international pollution prevention certificates, and set requirements for equipment to prevent pollution. The Act also empowers the Central Government to prescribe any other matter necessary for implementing the Convention.
