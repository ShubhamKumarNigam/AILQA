Summary:
The Legislative Assembly of Nagaland (Change in Representation) Act, 1968, was enacted on December 31, 1968, to increase the total number of seats in the Legislative Assembly of Nagaland from forty-six to fifty-two. This change in representation is specific to the Tuensang district, which will see an increase in seats from six to twelve. These additional seats in the Tuensang district will be filled by persons chosen by the members of the regional council from amongst themselves. The remaining forty seats in the Legislative Assembly of Nagaland will be filled by persons chosen by direct elections from assembly constituencies in the rest of the State of Nagaland.

The Act also includes provisions for the repeal of certain sections of the State of Nagaland Act, 1962, and the Representation of the People Act, 1950. Specifically, sections 3 and 4 of this Act, which relate to the amendment of the aforementioned Acts, have been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1974.

Finally, the Act includes a saving clause, which states that nothing in this Act shall affect any representation in the present Legislative Assembly of Nagaland until its dissolution. This means that the changes brought about by this Act will only take effect after the present Legislative Assembly of Nagaland has been dissolved.
