Summary:

THE NATIONAL DAIRY DEVELOPMENT BOARD ACT, 1987

Act Title: THE NATIONAL DAIRY DEVELOPMENT BOARD ACT, 1987
Act ID: ACT NO. 37 OF 1987
Enactment Date: [15th September, 1987.]

The National Dairy Development Board Act, 1987, declares the National Dairy Development Board in the State of Gujarat to be an institution of national importance and provides for its incorporation. The Act vests the undertakings of the Indian Dairy Corporation in the National Dairy Development Board and dissolves the Indian Dairy Corporation. The Act also defines various terms used in the Act and establishes the management and powers of the National Dairy Development Board.
