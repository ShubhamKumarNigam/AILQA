Summary:

The Union Duties Of Excise (Electricity) Distribution Act, 1980 is an act that governs the payment of Union duties of excise on electricity to the states. The act came into force on April 1, 1979. The act defines "distributable Union duties of excise on electricity" as the net proceeds of Union duties of excise on electricity collected, excluding collections made in Union territories. The net proceeds are calculated as per the meaning of clause (1) of article 279 of the Constitution.

The act states that during the financial year commencing on April 1, 1984, sums equivalent to the distributable Union duties of excise on electricity collected in that year shall be paid out of the Consolidated Fund of India to the states. The expenditure on the payments in pursuance of section 3 of the act shall be charged on the Consolidated Fund of India. The Central Government may make rules providing for the time at which and the manner in which, any payments under this Act are to be made, for the making of adjustments between one financial year and another, and for any other incidental or ancillary matters. Every rule made under this section shall be laid before each House of Parliament, while it is in session, for a total period of thirty days. If both Houses agree in making any modification in the rule or both Houses agree that the rule should not be made, the rule shall have effect only in such modified form or be of no effect, as the case may be.
