Summary:

THE COAL MINES PROVIDENT FUND AND MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS ACT, 1948 is an Act that makes provisions for the framing of a Provident Fund Scheme, a Pension Scheme, a Deposit-linked Insurance Scheme, and a Bonus Scheme for persons employed in Coal Mines. The Act establishes the Coal Mines Provident Fund Scheme and a Board of Trustees to administer the Fund. The Act also defines various terms related to coal mines, such as 'coal mine,' 'employee,' 'employer,' 'Fund,' 'Insurance Fund,' 'Insurance Scheme,' and 'member.' The Act also provides for the transfer of accounts, the Coal Mines Pension Scheme, a special grant by the Central Government, the Coal Mines Deposit-linked Insurance Scheme, and the protection of the Fund against attachment. The Act also specifies penalties for contravention of its provisions and provides for the appointment of Inspectors to enforce the provisions of the Act. The Act also includes Schedules that provide for matters to be provided for in the Coal Mines Provident Fund Scheme, the Coal Mines Family Pension Scheme, the Coal Mines Pension Scheme, and the Coal Mines Deposit-linked Insurance Scheme. The Act applies to the whole of India.
