Summary:
The Easements (Extending Act V of 1882) was enacted on the 6th of March, 1891, with the purpose of extending the Indian Easements Act of 1882 to certain areas where it was not yet in force. Specifically, this Act extends the Indian Easements Act of 1882 to the territories administered by the Governor of Bombay in Council and the lieutenant-Governor of the North-Western Provinces and Chief Commissioner of Oudh. This extension was made to ensure that the same laws governing easements apply consistently across different regions of India. The Act does not include any schedules, annexures, appendices, or forms, but it does have two footnotes. The first footnote indicates that the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh were extended by Act 34 of 2019, section 95 and the fifth schedule, with effect from the 31st of October, 2019. The second footnote indicates that the North-Western Provinces are now known as Uttar Pradesh.
