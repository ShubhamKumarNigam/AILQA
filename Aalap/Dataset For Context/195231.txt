Summary:
The Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Act, 1952, is an Act to regulate certain matters relating to or connected with elections to the offices of President and Vice-President of India. The Act is divided into four parts. Part I is titled "Preliminary" and defines various terms used in the Act. Part II, titled "Conduct of Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections," outlines the process for conducting elections, including the appointment of a Returning Officer and Assistant Returning Officers, the appointment of dates for nominations, scrutiny, and withdrawal of candidatures, and the counting of votes. Part III, titled "Disputes Regarding Elections," outlines the process for challenging the results of an election through an election petition. Part IV, titled "Miscellaneous," contains provisions regarding the maintenance of secrecy of voting, the jurisdiction of civil courts, and the power to make rules. The Act also includes the Presidential and Vice-Presidential Elections Rules, 1974, which provide further details on the conduct of elections.
