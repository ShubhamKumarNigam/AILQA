Summary:

THE NATIONAL CO-OPERATIVE DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION ACT, 1962 is an Act to provide for the incorporation and regulation of a corporation for the purpose of planning and promoting programmes for the production, processing, marketing, storage, export and import of agricultural produce, foodstuffs, livestock, certain other commodities and services on co-operative principles.

The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including "agricultural produce," "bank," "Board," "Central financing institution," "Central Warehousing Corporation," "co-operative society," "Corporation," "foodstuffs," "General Council," "industrial goods," "livestock," "managing director," "nationalised bank," "notified commodity," "notified services," "prescribed," "Reserve Bank," "scheduled bank," "State Bank," "State Co-operative Bank," and "year."

The Act establishes the National Co-operative Development Corporation, which shall be a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal with power to acquire, hold and dispose of property and to contract, and may, in the said name, sue and be sued. The head-office of the Corporation shall be in New Delhi.

The Act defines the functions of the Corporation, which include planning, promoting, and financing programmes for the production, processing, marketing, storage, export, and import of agricultural produce, foodstuffs, livestock, certain other commodities and services on co-operative principles.

The Act also provides for the appointment of a managing director and other officers and employees of the Corporation, and sets out their powers and duties.

The Act establishes a General Council and a Board of Management for the Corporation, and sets out their composition and functions.

The Act also provides for the maintenance of accounts and audit of the Corporation, and requires the Corporation to furnish returns and reports to the Central Government.

The Act empowers the Central Government to give directions to the Corporation in all matters, and to dissolve the Corporation if it is of the opinion that the Corporation has failed to carry out its functions under the Act or that for any other reason it is not necessary to continue the Corporation.

The Act also empowers the Corporation to make regulations, and repeals and saves the Agricultural Produce (Development and Warehousing) Corporations Act, 1956 in so far as it relates to the National Co-operative Development and Warehousing Board.

The Schedule to the Act contains a declaration of fidelity and secrecy to be made by every member, auditor, officer, or employee of the Corporation.
