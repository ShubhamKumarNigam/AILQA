The Dam Safety Act, 2021, is a law that applies to the owners of specified dams in India. The Act defines various terms related to dam safety, such as "dam," "dam failure," "dam incident," "distress condition," and "remedial measures." The Act establishes the National Dam Safety Authority and the State Dam Safety Organisation to regulate dam safety procedures. The Act also requires the owner of every specified dam to maintain documentation related to the investigation, design, construction, operation, performance, maintenance, major repair, alteration, enlargement, and safety of the dam. The Act applies to both public and private sector dams that meet certain height, length, capacity, or flood discharge criteria, as well as dams with specially difficult foundation problems or unusual design. The Act also provides for the classification of dams based on their condition, location, damage, or hazard potential.
