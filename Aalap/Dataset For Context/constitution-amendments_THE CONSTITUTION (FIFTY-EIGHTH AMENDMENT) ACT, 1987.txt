
Amendment of the Constitution.-

In the Constitution, in Part XVII, after Article 348, the following article shall be inserted, namely:--

"349A. Publication of the Constitution in Hindi.--

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in Article 348, the President may cause to be published in the Official Gazette a translation of the Constitution or any part thereof in the Hindi language, signed by the members of the Constituent Assembly, with such modifications as may be necessary to bring it in conformity with the language, style and terminology adopted in the authoritative texts of Central Acts in the Hindi language.

(2) Every amendment of the Constitution made in English shall be published in the Official Gazette together with a translation of the same in the Hindi language signed by the members of the Constituent Assembly with such modifications as may be necessary to bring it in conformity with the language, style and terminology adopted in the authoritative texts of Central Acts in the Hindi language."
