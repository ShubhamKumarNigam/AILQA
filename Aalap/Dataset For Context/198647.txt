The National Security Guard Act, 1986 (Act No. 47 of 1986) is an Act to provide for the constitution and regulation of an armed force of the Union for combating terrorist activities with a view to protecting States against internal disturbances and for matters connected therewith. The Act is divided into several chapters, with Chapter I containing preliminary provisions such as the short title, commencement, and definitions of key terms. The Act applies to certain persons appointed in the National Security Guard, including officers, Assistant Commanders, Rangers, and combatised tradesmen. These individuals are subject to the provisions of the Act and the rules governing the Security Guard until they are repatriated, retired, released, discharged, removed, or dismissed from the Security Guard. Chapter II of the Act deals with the constitution of the Security Guard and the conditions of service of its members. The Security Guard is an armed force of the Union responsible for combating terrorist activities and protecting States against internal disturbances. The Security Guard is constituted and governed by rules prescribed by the Central Government, and the conditions of service of its members are also prescribed by rules. The general superintendence, direction, and control of the Security Guard vest in the Central Government, and the command and supervision of the Security Guard vest in an officer appointed by the Central Government as the Director-General of the Security Guard. The Director-General is assisted by Additional Directors-General, Inspectors-General, Deputy Inspectors-General, Group Commanders, and other officers appointed by the Central Government. Members of the Security Guard are liable to serve in any part of India as well as outside India. They are not permitted to resign their appointment or withdraw from their duties without the previous permission in writing of the prescribed authority. Every person subject to the Act holds office during the pleasure of the President, and the Central Government may dismiss or remove any person from service. Any such officer may reduce to a lower grade or rank any person under his command except an officer or an Assistant Commander. The exercise of any power under this section is subject to the provisions of the Act and the rules.
