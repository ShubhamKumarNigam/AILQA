Summary:

The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of completion of 20 Years of Vibrant Gujarat Summit) Rules, 2024 have been made under the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules specify the details of the commemorative coin to be issued on the completion of 20 years of the Vibrant Gujarat Summit. The coin will be of Twenty Rupees denomination, circular in shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters and will be composed of Quaternary Alloy Silver (50%), Copper (40%), Nickel (5%), and Zinc (5%). The obverse of the coin will bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the center, with the legend 'Satyameva Jayate' inscribed below, flanked on the left periphery with the word 'Bharat' in Devnagri script and on the right periphery with the word 'INDIA' in English. It shall also bear the Rupee symbol 'L' and denominational value '20' in the international numerals below the Lion Capitol. The reverse of the coin will bear the logo of Vibrant Gujarat Global Summit 2024, with the inscription 'Celebrating 20 Years of Vibrant Gujarat' in English at the bottom of the logo. The year '2003' and '2023' shall be inscribed on left and right side of the text 'Celebrating 20 Years of Vibrant Gujarat'. The text 'विकास और समृद्धि के 20 साल का परिषद 2047' shall be inscribed in Devnagari script on the upper periphery of the coin. The standard weight of the coin is 35 grams, with a remedy allowed in composition for Silver (1/500 th plus or minus) and weight (1/100 th plus or minus). These rules came into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
