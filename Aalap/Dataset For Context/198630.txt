Summary:
The Swadeshi Cotton Mills Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1986, is an Act to provide for the acquisition and transfer of certain textile undertakings of the Swadeshi Cotton Mills Company Limited, with a view to securing the proper management of such undertakings so as to subserve the interests of the general public by ensuring the continued manufacture, production and distribution of different varieties of cloth and yarn and thereby to give effect to the policy of the State towards securing the principles specified in clauses (b) and (c) of article 39 of the Constitution.
This Act consists of 33 sections and one schedule, divided into seven chapters.
Chapter I: Preliminary
This chapter defines the short title and commencement of the Act, and the definitions of various terms used in the Act.
Chapter II: Acquisition and Transfer of the Textile Undertakings
This chapter deals with the transfer and vesting of the textile undertakings, the general effect of vesting, the Company to be liable for certain prior liabilities, transfer of any textile undertaking or part thereof to a Subsidiary Textile Corporation, and shares to be issued by National Textile Corporation for the value of assets transferred to it by the Central Government.
Chapter III: Payment of Amounts
This chapter specifies the payment of amount, payment of further amounts, and the rate of interest on such amounts.
Chapter IV: Management, etc., of the Textile Undertakings
This chapter deals with the management, etc., of the textile undertakings, special provision for disposal of assets of the textile undertakings in certain circumstances, duty of persons in charge of management of the textile undertakings to deliver all assets, etc., and provisions relating to employees of the textile undertakings.
Chapter V: Commissioner of Payments
This chapter deals with the appointment of Commissioner of Payments, payment by the Central Government to the Commissioner, certain powers of the National Textile Corporation, claims to be made to the Commissioner, priorities of claims, examination of claims, admission or rejection of claims, disbursement of money by the Commissioner to claimants, undisbursed or unclaimed amount to be deposited with the general revenue account, and miscellaneous provisions.
Chapter VI: Miscellaneous
This chapter deals with the Act to have overriding effect, assumption of liability, contracts to cease to have effect unless ratified by National Textile Corporation, penalties, offences by companies, protection of action taken in good faith, delegation of powers, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, repeal and saving, and form of oath or affirmation.
The schedule to the Act specifies the order of priorities for the discharge of liabilities of the Company.
The Act aims to secure the proper management of the textile undertakings of the Swadeshi Cotton Mills Company Limited, to subserve the interests of the general public by ensuring the continued manufacture, production and distribution of different varieties of cloth and yarn, and to give effect to the policy of the State towards securing the principles specified in clauses (b) and (c) of article 39 of the Constitution.
