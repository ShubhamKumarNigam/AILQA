Summary:

The Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Act, 2019, with the ID ACT NO. 20 OF 2019, was enacted on 31st July, 2019, to protect the rights of married Muslim women and to prohibit divorce by pronouncing talaq by their husbands in India, excluding the state of Jammu and Kashmir. The Act defines talaq as talaq-e-biddat or any other similar form of talaq having the effect of instantaneous and irrevocable divorce pronounced by a Muslim husband.

Chapter I, titled 'Preliminary,' includes two sections. Section 1 specifies the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. Section 2 provides definitions for terms used in the Act.

Chapter II, titled 'Declaration of Talaq to be Void and Illegal,' contains two sections. Section 3 declares that any pronouncement of talaq by a Muslim husband upon his wife, regardless of the method, is void and illegal. Section 4 imposes punishment on any Muslim husband who pronounces talaq, with imprisonment for a term that may extend to three years and a fine.

Chapter III, titled 'Protection of Rights of Married Muslim Women,' includes three sections. Section 5 ensures that a married Muslim woman upon whom talaq is pronounced is entitled to receive subsistence allowance from her husband for herself and any dependent children. The amount is to be determined by the Magistrate. Section 6 grants a married Muslim woman custody of her minor children in the event of pronouncement of talaq by her husband, as determined by the Magistrate. Section 7 specifies that offences under this Act are cognizable, compoundable, and not eligible for bail unless specific conditions are met.

The Act includes a repeal and savings clause (Section 8) that repeals the Muslim Women (Protection of Rights on Marriage) Second Ordinance, 2019, and deems anything done or any action taken under the Ordinance to have been done or taken under the provisions of this Act.

A notification (Footnote 0) made on 30th October, 2019, extends the application of this Act to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh.
