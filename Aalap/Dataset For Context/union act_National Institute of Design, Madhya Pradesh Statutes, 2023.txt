The National Institute of Design, Madhya Pradesh Statutes, 2023 is a set of rules and regulations governing the functioning of the National Institute of Design, Madhya Pradesh, a public institution of higher education established under the National Institute of Design Act, 2014. The Statutes define various terms and authorities related to the Institute, such as the Governing Council, the Senate, the Director, the Standing Committee, Activity Chairpersons, and Discipline Leads. The Statutes also outline the powers and functions of these authorities, as well as the procedures for their meetings and decision-making.

The Statutes cover various aspects of the Institute's operations, including the appointment and terms of service of faculty and staff, the creation and abolition of posts, the classification of staff, the method of appointment of staff, the performance appraisal system, and the grievance redressal mechanism. The Statutes also provide for the establishment of halls of residence and the institution of fellowships, scholarships, medals, and prizes.

The Statutes are divided into several sections, each dealing with a specific topic. The first section deals with the short title and commencement of the Statutes, while the second section defines various terms used in the Statutes. The third section lists the authorities of the Institute, including the Governing Council, the Senate, the Director, the Standing Committee, and all Activity Chairpersons, Dean of Institute campuses, Registrar, Heads of Departments and Discipline Leads. The fourth section lays down the rules and procedures for the meetings of the Governing Council, such as the quorum, the notice of meetings, the agenda, and the minutes. The fifth section outlines the powers of the Governing Council, such as the power to assist the Central Government in the appointment of the Director, and the power to abolish, re-designate or change the nomenclature of any post except that of the Director. The sixth section deals with the powers of the Chairperson of the Governing Council in emergent cases.

The seventh section deals with the authentication of orders and decisions of the Governing Council, while the eighth section provides for the travelling allowances of members of the authorities of the Institute. The ninth section deals with the representation of donors in the Governing Council, and lays down the threshold of financial assistance or contribution and other requirements to qualify for nomination as a member of the Governing Council. The tenth section deals with the Standing Committee of the Governing Council, its composition, functions, and powers.

The eleventh section deals with the Director of the Institute, his powers and functions, and the delegation of administrative and financial powers to him. The twelfth section deals with the Senate of the Institute, its composition, functions, and powers, and the thirteenth section deals with the conferment of honorary degrees. The fourteenth section deals with the Faculty Streams of the Institute, their composition, and the courses offered by them. The fifteenth section deals with the nomenclature of education and training programmes, while the sixteenth section deals with the student fee.

The seventeenth section deals with the institution of fellowships, scholarships, medals, prizes, etc., while the eighteenth section deals with halls of residence for students. The nineteenth section deals with the Academic Advisory Committee, its composition, functions, and powers, while the twentieth section deals with the Faculty Forum. The twenty-first section deals with the Deans of Institute Campuses, their appointment, powers, and functions. The twenty-second section deals with the Activity Chairpersons, their appointment, functions, and powers. The twenty-third section deals with the Vice-Chair of the Activity Chairpersons, while the twenty-fourth section deals with the Registrar of the Institute, his powers, and functions.

The twenty-fifth section deals with the Chief Administrative Officer, while the twenty-sixth section deals with the Controller of Finance and Accounts. The twenty-seventh section deals with the Discipline Leads, their appointment, functions, and powers, while the twenty-eighth section deals with the classification of the members of the staff of the Institute. The twenty-ninth section deals with the method of appointment of staff, while the thirtieth section deals with the creation of posts. The thirty-first section deals with the Selection Committee for appointments, while the thirty-second section deals with the Performance Appraisal System.

The thirty-third section deals with the Grievances Committee, while the thirty-fourth section deals with the Provident Fund and Pension Schemes. The thirty-fifth section deals with the general service rules, regulations and delegation of administrative and financial powers, while the thirty-sixth section deals with the First Schedule, which lists the name, pay scales, and sanctioned strength of posts in the National Institute of Design, Madhya Pradesh. The Statutes are made under the authority of the National Institute of Design Act, 2014, and are subject to the provisions of the Act.
