Summary:

The Oil and Natural Gas Commission (Transfer of Undertaking and Repeal) Act, 1993, is an act that transfers and vests the undertaking of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission to the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956. The act consists of 12 sections, and the key sections are as follows:

Section 1: This section gives the act a short title and defines the commencement date.

Section 2: This section defines key terms used in the act.

Section 3: This section states that the undertaking of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission shall stand transferred to, and vest in, the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited on the appointed day.

Section 4: This section states that the entire capital of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission shall stand vested in the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited on the appointed day. The section also states that all assets, rights, powers, authorities, and privileges and all properties, movable and immovable, real and personal, corporeal or incorporeal, in possession or reservation, present or contingent, of whatever nature and wheresoever situate, including all liabilities and obligations of whatever kind, within or outside India, of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission in relation to its undertaking, shall stand transferred to and vest in the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited.

Section 5: This section states that all licenses, leases, concessions, permits, quotas, benefits, privileges, and exemptions granted to the Oil and Natural Gas Commission in connection with its affairs under any law for the time being in force shall be deemed to have been granted to the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited.

Section 6: This section states that any exemption from, or any assessment with respect to, any tax granted or made or any benefit by way of set off or carry forward of any unabsorbed depreciation or investment allowance or other allowance or loss extended or available to the Oil and Natural Gas Commission under the Income-tax Act, 1961, shall continue to have effect in relation to the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited.

Section 7: This section states that any guarantee given for or in favor of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission with respect to any loan or lease finance or other assistance shall continue to be operative in relation to the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited.

Section 8: This section states that every officer or other employee of the Oil and Natural Gas Commission (except a member or the chairman) serving in its employment immediately before the appointed day shall become an officer or other employee of the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited and shall hold his office or service therein by the same tenure, at the same remuneration, upon the same terms and conditions with the same obligations and with the same rights and privileges as to leave, passage, insurance, superannuation scheme, provident fund, other funds, retirement, pension, gratuity, and other benefits as he would have held under the Oil and Natural Gas Commission if its undertaking had not vested in the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited.

Section 9: This section states that the Central Government may authorize any officer or employee of the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited to enter and inspect any land, survey and take measurements of such land, dig holes or perform any other functions for the purposes of measuring or prospecting for the availability of oil and natural gas, and examine any document, book, register, or record in the possession or power of any person having the control of or connected with any such land and place marks of identification thereon and take extracts from and make copies of any such document, book, register, or record.

Section 10: This section states that the Central Government may make rules for carrying out the provisions of this Act.

Section 11: This section states that the Oil and Natural Gas Commission Act, 1959, shall stand repealed on the appointed day.

Section 12: This section states that the Oil and Natural Gas Commission (Transfer of Undertaking and Repeal) Ordinance, 1993, is hereby repealed, and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provision of this Act.

The act also includes a schedule that lists the provisions of the repealed Oil and Natural Gas Commission Act, 1959, that shall apply to the Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited for any of the purposes related to the annual accounts of the Commission.
