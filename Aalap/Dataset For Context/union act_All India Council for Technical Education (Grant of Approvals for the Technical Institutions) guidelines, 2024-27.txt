Summary:

The All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) has released the All India Council for Technical Education (Grant of Approvals for the Technical Institutions) guidelines, 2024-27. These guidelines are applicable for the applications submitted by institutions/institutions deemed to be universities offering/proposing to offer technical programs at diploma, post-diploma certificate, undergraduate degree, postgraduate diploma, and postgraduate degree levels. The guidelines cover various aspects related to approvals, such as setting up new technical institutions, extension of approval, introduction/continuation of seats for non-resident Indians, change of site/location, conversion of diploma level into degree level and vice-versa, starting new programs/levels in existing institutions, merger of institutions, and closure of institutions, among others. The guidelines also provide definitions for various terms used in the document. These guidelines will come into force with effect from the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
