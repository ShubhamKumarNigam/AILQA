The Constitution (Eighty-eighth Amendment) Act, 2003, introduces a new article, 268A, which allows the Government of India to levy taxes on services. The tax collected will be shared and appropriated by both the Union and State governments according to principles formulated by Parliament. This amendment also changes Article 270 by replacing "articles 268 and 269" with "articles 268, 268A, and 269." Additionally, the Seventh Schedule of the Constitution is amended to add a new entry, 92C, in List I-Union List, which deals with taxes on services. This amendment empowers the Union government to levy taxes on services and share the revenue with State governments, aiming to increase state revenues and pave the way for the integration of services under the state-level VAT.
