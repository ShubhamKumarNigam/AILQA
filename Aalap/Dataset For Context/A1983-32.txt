Summary:
The Punjab Disturbed Areas Act, 1983 (Act No. 32 of 1983) is a law enacted in Punjab, India. It came into force on December 8, 1983. The Act defines a "disturbed area" as an area declared by the State Government to be a disturbed area through a notification in the Official Gazette. The Act grants the State Government the power to declare any part of the state as a disturbed area.

The Act authorizes any Magistrate or Police Officer not below the rank of Sub-Inspector or Havildar in case of the Armed Branch of the Police to fire upon or use force against any person who is contravening any law or order for the time being in force in the disturbed area. This can be done if the officer believes it is necessary to maintain public order.

The Act also grants the power to destroy any arms dump, prepared or fortified position or shelter from which armed attacks are made or are likely to be made. This can be done if the officer believes it is necessary.

The Act provides protection to persons acting under sections 4 and 5 from any suit, prosecution, or other legal proceedings, except with the previous sanction of the Central Government.

Finally, the Act repeals the Punjab Disturbed Areas Ordinance, 1983 (5 of 1983) and states that anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
