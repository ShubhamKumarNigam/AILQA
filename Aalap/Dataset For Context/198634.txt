Summary:

The State of Mizoram Act, 1986, establishes the State of Mizoram and provides for its representation in the legislatures. The Act defines various terms used in the Act and establishes the common High Court for the States of Assam, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura, and Mizoram. The Act also provides for the authorization of expenditure pending its sanction by the Legislature and the distribution of revenues. Additionally, the Act deals with the transfer of proceedings from the High Court of Assam, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Manipur, and Tripura to the common High Court, as well as the interpretation, right to appear or to act in proceedings transferred to the common High Court, and saving.

The Act further includes provisions related to assets and liabilities, and the distribution of revenues, as well as provisions related to services. The Act establishes Advisory Committees to assist the Central Government in giving effect to the provisions of this Part and ensuring fair and equitable treatment to all persons affected by the provisions of this Part. The Act also prohibits representation after a certain period and provides for the power of the Central Government to give directions.

The Act includes several repealed sections and provisions related to legal and miscellaneous matters. The Act also provides for the continuance of existing laws and their adaptations, as well as the power to construe laws. The Act further provides for the continuance of courts, tribunals, and authorities and the effect of provisions of the Act inconsistent with other laws. The Act also includes provisions for removing difficulties and making rules.

In summary, the State of Mizoram Act, 1986, establishes the State of Mizoram and provides for its representation in the legislatures, as well as various provisions related to the High Court, assets and liabilities, services, and legal and miscellaneous matters.
