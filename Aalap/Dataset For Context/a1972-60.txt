Summary:

The Khadi and Other Handloom Industries Development (Additional Excise Duty on Cloth) Amendment Act, 1972, is an amendment to the Khadi and Other Handloom Industries Development (Additional Excise Duty on Cloth) Act, 1953. This Act validates the excise duty levied and collected on certain cotton fabrics during the period between March 1, 1969, and July 6, 1970. The duty was levied on cotton fabrics manufactured by a manufacturer who has a proprietary interest in at least two of the following businesses: spinning of cotton twist, yarn or thread, weaving of cotton fabrics, or processing of cotton fabrics with the aid of power. The cotton fabrics referred to in this Act include medium A fabrics, unprocessed, and medium B and coarse fabrics in various stages of processing, as well as specific types of fabrics such as "Dhoti," "Sari," "Long Cloth," "Shirting," or "Drill." This Act also states that no suit or other proceedings shall be maintained or continued in any court for the refund of any such duty so levied and collected, and any such duty levied or assessed under the principal Act in respect of the aforesaid period, but not collected, may be recovered in the manner provided under the principal Act.
