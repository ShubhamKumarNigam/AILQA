Summary:

The Births, Deaths And Marriages Registration Act of 1886 is a law that provides for the voluntary registration of births and deaths among certain classes of persons, as well as the registration of marriages under the Special Marriage Act of 1872 or the Indian Christian Marriage Act of 1872. The Act also establishes general registry offices for keeping records of these events. The law aims to provide unimpeachable evidence of births, deaths, and marriages, as the Indian Statute-Book did not previously contain a general law for the registration of these events. The Act was enacted to address the unsatisfactory nature of the system of registration of marriages under the Christian Marriage Act of 1872 and to provide for the authentication and custody of certain existing registers kept for these purposes. The Act extends to the whole of India except for the territories that were previously part of Part B States.
