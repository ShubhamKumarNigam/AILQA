Summary:
The Emergency Risks (Goods) Insurance Act, 1971 is a law in India that provides for the insurance of goods against damage arising from emergency risks. The Act is in force during the period of operation of a Proclamation of Emergency issued by the President under the Constitution of India. The Act defines emergency risks as risks arising from actions taken by an enemy, explosions or fires involving explosives or munitions, measures taken to avoid or mitigate the consequences of damage caused by enemy action, and precautionary or preparatory measures taken to prevent or hinder enemy action. The Act also defines enemy as any person or country committing external aggression against India, any person belonging to a country committing such aggression, and any other country or person declared by the Central Government to be assisting the country committing such aggression. The Act provides for the insurance of goods owned by a person carrying on business in India as a seller or supplier of goods, and extends to the undertaking by the Central Government of the liability of insuring a person against emergency risks in respect of goods in which he has an interest arising in the course of that business. The Act also provides for the establishment of an Emergency Risks (Goods) Insurance Scheme, which specifies the terms and conditions of insurance, including the amount of premium payable and the extent of liability of the Central Government as insurer. The Act empowers the Central Government to employ or authorize the employment of any person to act as its agent for the issue of policies and making recommendations for the settlement of claims under the Scheme. The Act makes it compulsory for a person carrying on business in India as a seller or supplier of goods to insure against emergency risks in respect of goods insurable under this Act which are from time to time owned by him in the course of that business. The Act provides for the recovery of a penalty from a person who has failed to insure as, or to the full amount, required by this Act, and for the restriction of carrying on of certain insurance business. The Act also establishes an Emergency Risks (Goods) Insurance Fund for the discharge of liabilities under the Scheme and for the payment of remuneration and expenses of agents employed for the purposes of the Scheme. The Act empowers the Central Government to obtain information and authorizes the punishment for giving false information. The Act also provides for the refund of premiums in certain cases and for the composition of offences. The Act bars legal proceedings against the Central Government or any person for anything done in good faith under this Act. The Act also empowers the Central Government to remove doubts as to whether a person is insurable in respect of any goods under this Act.
