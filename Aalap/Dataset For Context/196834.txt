Summary:

THE ENEMY PROPERTY ACT, 1968 (ACT NO. 34 OF 1968)

This Act, enacted on 20th August, 1968, aims to provide for the continued vesting of enemy property vested in the Custodian of Enemy Property for India under the Defence of India Rules, 1962 and the Defence of India Rules, 1971, and for matters connected therewith.

Key sections of the Act are:

- Section 1 defines the short title, extent, application, and commencement of the Act.
- Section 2 provides definitions for terms used in the Act.
- Section 3 deals with the appointment of the Custodian of Enemy Property for India and Deputy Custodians, etc.
- Section 4 discusses the appointment of Inspectors of Enemy Property.
- Section 5 states that property vested in the Custodian of Enemy Property for India under the Defence of India Rules, 1962 and the Defence of India Rules, 1971 shall continue to vest in the Custodian.
- Section 6 prohibits the transfer of property vested in the Custodian by an enemy, enemy subject, or enemy firm.
- Section 7 requires payment to the Custodian of money otherwise payable to an enemy, enemy subject, or enemy firm.
- Section 8 provides for the power of the Custodian in respect of enemy property vested in him.
- Section 8A enables the sale of property by the Custodian.
- Section 9 exempts all enemy property vested in the Custodian under this Act from attachment, seizure, or sale in execution of decree of a civil court or orders of any other authority.
- Section 10 deals with the transfer of securities belonging to an enemy.
- Section 10A enables the Custodian to issue a certificate of sale in favor of the person to whom enemy property has been sold.
- Section 11 empowers the Custodian to summon persons and call for documents.
- Section 12 provides protection for complying with orders of the Custodian.
- Section 13 validates actions taken in pursuance of orders of the Custodian.
- Section 14 prohibits proceedings against companies whose assets vest in the Custodian.
- Section 15 requires returns as to enemy property.
- Section 16 provides for registers of returns.
- Section 17 levies fees for the Custodian's services.
- Section 18 enables the transfer of property vested as enemy property in certain cases.
- Section 18A states that income received in respect of the enemy property by the Custodian shall not be returned or liable to be returned to any person.
- Section 18B excludes the jurisdiction of civil courts.
- Section 18C enables an appeal to the High Court.
- Section 19 provides protection for action taken under the Act.
- Section 20 imposes penalties for offenses under the Act.
- Section 21 deals with offenses by companies.
- Section 22 states that the provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force.
- Section 22A validates certain orders made under the Defence of India Rules, 1962 and the Defence of India Rules, 1971.
- Section 23 empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act.
- Section 24 states that certain orders made under the Defence of India Rules, 1962 and the Defence of India Rules, 1971 shall continue in force.
- Section 25 deals with the repeal and saving of the Act.
