Summary:
THE ANTI-HIJACKING ACT, 2016 is an Act to give effect to the Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Seizure of Aircraft and for matters connected therewith. This Act repeals the Anti-Hijacking Act, 1982 and extends to the whole of India. It applies to any offence committed outside India by any person. The Act defines various terms such as Agency, aircraft, aircraft registered in India, Convention country, Hague Convention, hostage, military aircraft, notification, and security personnel.

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary provisions and defines the short title, extent, application, and commencement of the Act.

Chapter II of the Act deals with hijacking and connected offences. It defines hijacking and specifies the punishment for hijacking, which includes death or imprisonment for life with fine, and confiscation of movable and immovable property. The Act also specifies the punishment for acts of violence connected with hijacking.

Chapter III of the Act deals with miscellaneous provisions. It provides for the application of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 to the proceedings before a Designated Court. It also specifies provisions as to extradition, bail, and protection of action taken in good faith. The Act empowers investigating officers to seize or attach property and provides for confiscation and forfeiture of property. It also specifies the general power to make rules and provides for repeal and savings.
