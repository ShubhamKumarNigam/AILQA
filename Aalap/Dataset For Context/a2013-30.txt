Summary:
The Right to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013 (Act No. 30 OF 2013) came into force on a date appointed by the Central Government within three months of the President's assent to the Act. The Act aims to ensure a fair, participative, informed, and transparent process for land acquisition for industrialization, infrastructure development, and urbanization. It applies to the acquisition of land by the appropriate government for its own use, hold, and control, including for public sector undertakings, public purpose, and strategic purposes related to national security or defense. The Act also applies to public-private partnership projects and private companies for public purpose, subject to certain conditions. The Act defines "affected family" to include families whose land or other immovable property has been acquired or whose primary source of livelihood is affected by the acquisition. The Act provides for just and fair compensation to the affected families and makes provisions for their rehabilitation and resettlement. The Act also includes provisions for the least disturbance to the owners of the land and other affected families. The Act includes provisions for the appointment of an Administrator for the purpose of rehabilitation and resettlement of affected families and defines "agricultural land" to include land used for agriculture, horticulture, dairy farming, poultry farming, pisciculture, sericulture, seed farming, breeding of livestock, nursery growing medicinal herbs, and other related activities.

State amendments have been made to the Act in Andhra Pradesh and Maharashtra, exempting the acquisition of land for certain projects listed in section 10A from the provisions of the first proviso to sub-section (2) of section 2.

If the Expert Group is of the opinion that the project does not serve any public purpose or the social costs and adverse social impacts outweigh the potential benefits, it shall recommend that the project be abandoned forthwith and no further steps to acquire the land will be initiated in respect of the same.

The appropriate Government must examine the report of the Collector, if any, and the report of the Expert Group on the Social Impact Assessment study and after considering all the reports, recommend such area for acquisition which would ensure minimum displacement of people, minimum disturbance to the infrastructure, ecology, and minimum adverse impact on the individuals affected.
