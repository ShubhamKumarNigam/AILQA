Summary:
The Warehousing Corporations (Amendment) Act, 2001, amends the Warehousing Corporations Act of 1962. The amendment act includes changes to sections 11, 20, 21, 22, and 24 of the principal act.

In section 11, the amendment act expands the scope of clauses (a) and (e) and adds two new clauses (ea) and (eb). Clause (a) now includes warehousing activities outside India, and clause (e) no longer contains a conjunction at the end. The new clauses (ea) and (eb) allow the Warehousing Corporations to enter into joint ventures with other corporations or companies, including foreign companies, and to establish subsidiary companies.

In section 20, the amendment act replaces the requirement of previous approval with intimation to the Central Government for certain activities. This applies to both sub-section (1), clause (c), and sub-section (2).

In section 21, the amendment act omits the words "the Central Warehousing Corporation or" from clause (v).

In section 22, the amendment act replaces the requirement of previous approval with intimation to the Central Government for certain activities.

In section 24, the amendment act replaces the requirement of previous approval with consultation for clause (a) and omits the conjunction at the end of clause (d). The amendment act also adds a new clause (da), allowing the Warehousing Corporations to enter into joint ventures with the Central Warehousing Corporation with the previous approval of the State Government.
