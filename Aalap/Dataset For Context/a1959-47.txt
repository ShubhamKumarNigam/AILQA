Summary:
The Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh (Transfer of Territories) Act, 1959, is an Act to provide for the transfer of certain territories from the State of Rajasthan to the State of Madhya Pradesh and for matters connected therewith.

The Act defines key terms such as "appointed day," "assembly constituency," "council constituency," "parliamentary constituency," and "sitting member." The appointed day is defined as the 1st day of October, 1959.

The Act specifies the territories to be transferred from Rajasthan to Madhya Pradesh in the First Schedule. These territories will be included in and form part of Bhanpura pargana of Mandsaur district in the State of Madhya Pradesh.

The Act also amends the First Schedule to the Constitution to reflect the transfer of territories. The amendment adds the words, brackets, and figures "and the First Schedule to the Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh (Transfer of Territories) Act, 1959" after the words and figures "States Reorganisation Act, 1956" in the entry against "6. Madhya Pradesh." Similarly, it adds the words, brackets, and figures "but excluding the territories specified in the First Schedule to the Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh (Transfer of Territories) Act, 1959" after the words and figures "States Reorganisation Act, 1956" in the entry against "11. Rajasthan."

The Act also modifies the Delimitation Orders and provides for the continuation of sitting members in the House of the People and the Legislative Assemblies of Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan.

The Act extends the jurisdiction of the High Court of Madhya Pradesh to the transferred territories and provides for the transfer of pending proceedings. The Act also provides for the transfer of assets and liabilities, the extension of laws, and the power to construe laws.

The Act includes provisions for legal proceedings, the transfer of pending proceedings, the effect of provisions inconsistent with other laws, the power to remove difficulties, and the power to make rules.

The First Schedule to the Act specifies the territories transferred from Rajasthan to Madhya Pradesh. The Second Schedule to the Act specifies the modifications of the First Schedule to the Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies Order, 1956, and the Delimitation of Council Constituencies (Madhya Pradesh) Order, 1957.

The Act includes footnotes that provide for amendments made to the Act by subsequent legislation.
