Summary:

The Rubber Act, 1947, is an Act to provide for the development of the rubber industry under the control of the Union. The Act extends to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir. The Act declares that it is expedient in the public interest that the Union should take under its control the rubber industry. The Act consists of various sections, including the short title and extent, declaration as to expediency of Union control, definitions, constitution of the Board, vacancies in the Board, salary and allowances of Chairman, Executive Officers of the Board, committees of the Board, functions of the Board, power of the Board to import rubber for sale or to purchase rubber in the internal market, consultation with the Board, funds of the Board, registration, power to prohibit or control imports and exports of rubber, imposition of new rubber cess, power to fix maximum and minimum prices for sale of rubber, licensing of transactions in rubber, provisions regarding licenses under section 14, restriction on possession of rubber, licences for planting or replanting, reports to be submitted by licensees, fees for special licenses, submission of returns and maintenance of accounts, inspection of land and premises, control by the Central Government, power of Central Government to issue directions to Board, appeal, accounts of the Board, power to delegate, power of Central Government to make rules, power to make regulations, penalties, compounding of offences, procedure for prosecutions, jurisdiction of Courts, bar of legal proceedings, and temporary powers of the Central Government. The Act also includes a schedule and annexure. The various sections of the Act provide for the development of the rubber industry, registration of owners, control of imports and exports, licensing of transactions, and penalties for non-compliance. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules and regulations for the implementation of the Act. The Act aims to promote the development of the rubber industry and regulate its production and marketing.
