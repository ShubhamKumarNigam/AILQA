In summary, the Constitution (One Hundred and Fourth Amendment) Act, 2019, amends Article 334 of the Constitution by extending the reservation of seats and special representation for socially and educationally backward classes, including Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Other Backward Classes, in educational institutions and public employment until 2030. The Act also amends the marginal heading of Article 334 to reflect the extension of the reservation period. The Act also removes the reservation for the Anglo-Indian community in Lok Sabha and state Legislative Assemblies. This extension of reservation aims to promote social and economic equality in India by ensuring equal opportunities for education and public employment for marginalized communities. However, the policy of reservation is a debated topic, with different opinions on the appropriate representation of various groups in educational institutions and public employment.
