Summary:
The Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951, is a law that governs the appointment, qualifications, disqualifications, and terms of office for members of the Finance Commission of India. The Finance Commission is a constitutional body established by the President to recommend the distribution of revenues between the central and state governments.

Under this Act, the Chairman of the Commission must have experience in public affairs, while the four other members must have experience or qualifications in judicial matters, financial knowledge, administration, or economics. The Act also specifies that a member must not have any financial or other interest that could affect their functions on the Commission.

The Act lays down the procedure for appointing members, including the requirement for the President to ensure that the member does not have any disqualifying interests. The Act also specifies that a member may be disqualified if they are of unsound mind, an undischarged insolvent, have been convicted of an offense involving moral turpitude, or have a financial or other interest that is likely to affect prejudicially their functions as a member of the Commission.

The Act also specifies the term of office for members, which may be specified in the order of appointment but is eligible for reappointment. Members are entitled to receive fees or salaries and allowances as determined by the Central Government.

The Act grants the Commission the power to determine their procedure and have the same powers as a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, in the performance of their functions. The Commission can summon and enforce the attendance of witnesses, require the production of any document, and requisition any public record from any court or office. The Commission can also require any person to furnish information and has the power to enforce the attendance of witnesses and to punish contempt.

Overall, the Finance Commission (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act, 1951, provides the legal framework for the appointment, qualifications, disqualifications, and terms of office for members of the Finance Commission, as well as the powers and procedures for the Commission's functioning.
