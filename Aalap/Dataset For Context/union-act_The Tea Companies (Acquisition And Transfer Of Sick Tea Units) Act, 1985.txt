Summary:

The Tea Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Sick Tea Units) Act, 1985, provides for the acquisition and transfer of the sick tea units specified in the First Schedule and the right, title, and interest of the tea companies in respect of the said tea units. The purpose of this Act is to secure proper reorganization and management of such tea units to augment the production and manufacture of different varieties of tea essential to the needs of the economy of the country. The Act specifies that the management of the sick tea units was taken over by the Central Government under the Tea Act, 1953, and that further investment of large sums of money is necessary for reorganizing and rehabilitating the said tea units. The Act also specifies that the acquisition of the sick tea units by the Central Government is necessary to protect the investments already made and to protect the interests of the workmen employed in the tea units. The Act came into force on April 8, 1985, and includes definitions of various terms used in the Act, such as "appointed day," "Commissioner," "notification," "prescribed," "sick tea unit," "specified date," "tea companies," "Tea Trading Corporation," and words and expressions used in the Tea Act, 1953, and the Companies Act, 1956. The Act also includes provisions related to the acquisition of rights of tea companies in respect of sick tea units, the general effect of vesting, tea companies to be liable for certain prior liabilities, payment of amounts, payment of further amounts, management, etc., of the sick tea units of the tea companies, duty of persons in charge of management of the sick tea units to deliver all assets, etc., duty of persons to account for assets, etc., in their possession, continuance of employees, provident fund and other funds, appointment of Commissioner of Payments, payment by the Central Government to the Commissioner, certain powers of the Tea Trading Corporation, claims to be made to the Commissioner, priority of claims, examination of claims, admission or rejection of claims, disbursement of money by the Commissioner, disbursement of amounts to the tea companies, undisbursed or unclaimed amounts to be deposited with the general revenue account, act to have overriding effect, assumption of liability, management to continue to vest in certain persons until alternative arrangements have been made, contracts to cease to have effect unless ratified by the Tea Trading Corporation, penalties, offenses by companies, protection of action taken in good faith, tea companies not to be wound up by the court, delegation of powers, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and saving.
