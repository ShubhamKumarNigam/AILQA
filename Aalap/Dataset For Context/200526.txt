Summary:

THE UNIVERSITY OF ALLAHABAD ACT, 2005, declares the University of Allahabad to be an institution of national importance and provides for its incorporation and matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act incorporates the University of Allahabad, established under the Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, as a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, and confers upon it the power to sue and be sued by the said name. The Act also specifies the objects of the University, which include disseminating and advancing knowledge by providing instructional and research facilities in various branches of learning, making provisions for integrated courses, promoting innovations in teaching-learning process, interdisciplinary and professional studies and research, removal of gender disparities and the digital divide, and the application of knowledge to social advancement, national progress and human welfare. The Act also defines various terms used in the Act, such as "Academic Council", "academic staff", "appointed day", "Centre", "Chancellor", "Constituent College", "Constituent Institute", "Court", "Department", "Director", "employee", "Executive Council", "Faculty", "Finance Officer", "Ordinances", "Principal", "Pro-Vice-Chancellor", "Registrar", "Regulations", "Statutes", "teacher", "University", "University appointed teacher", "University College", "University Institute", "University recognised teacher", and "Vice-Chancellor". The Act also specifies the powers and functions of the University, including providing instruction and conducting research, granting degrees and other academic distinctions, instituting Chairs, Principalships, Professorships, Readerships and Lecturerships and appointing persons to such Chairs, Principalships, Professorships, Readerships and Lecturerships and other teaching and academic positions, recognising persons as University recognised teachers, declaring persons working in any other University or organisation as teachers of the University, appointing, on contract or otherwise, visiting Professors, Emeritus Professors, Consultants, Scholars and such other persons who may contribute to the advancement of the objects of the University, creating administrative, ministerial and other posts and making appointments thereto, laying down conditions of service of all categories of employees, including their code of conduct, establishing and maintaining University Colleges and University Institutes for imparting instruction and conducting research, admitting to its privileges colleges and institutions situated within the territorial jurisdiction of the University, as University Colleges, Constituent Institutes and Constituent Colleges, and withdrawing all or any of those privileges in accordance with such conditions as may be prescribed by the Statutes, co-operating or collaborating or associating with any other University or authority or institution of higher learning in such manner as may be prescribed and for such purposes as the University may determine, determining standards of admission, including examination, evaluation or any other method of testing, to the University, and the institutions maintained by or admitted to the privileges of the University, demanding and receiving payment of fees and other charges, establishing and recognising hostels and supervising the residence of the students of the University, making arrangements for promoting their health and general welfare and guiding the Constituent Colleges and the Constituent Institutes to like ends in respect of the students enrolled thereat, regulating and enforcing discipline among the students and the employees, and taking such disciplinary measures in this regard as may be deemed by the University to be necessary, instituting and awarding fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals and prizes, receiving benefactions, donations and gifts and to acquire, hold, manage and dispose of any property, movable or immovable, including trust and endowment properties, for the purposes of the University, borrowing, with the approval of the Central Government, on the security of the property of the University, money for the purposes of the University, and doing all such other acts and things as may be necessary, incidental or conducive to the attainment of all or any of the objects of the University. The Act also specifies the jurisdiction of the University, which is within a radius of sixteen kilometres from the Convocation Hall of the University, without prejudice to the territorial jurisdiction over the said area that may be assigned to any other University. The Act also provides that the University shall be open to all persons of either sex and of whatever caste, creed, race or class, and it shall not be lawful for the University to adopt or impose on any person, any test whatsoever of religious belief or profession in order to entitle him to be appointed as a teacher of the University or to hold any office therein or be admitted as a student in the University or to graduate thereat or to enjoy or exercise any privilege thereof, provided that nothing in this section shall be deemed to prevent the University from making special provisions for the employment or admission of women, physically handicapped or of persons belonging to the weaker sections of the society and, in particular, of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes. The Act also provides for the appointment of the Visitor, the Chief Rector, the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Deans of Faculties, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, and other officers of the University, and the constitution, powers and functions of the authorities and other bodies of the University. The Act also provides for the submission of annual reports and accounts to the Court and the Visitor, and the laying of the annual accounts before both Houses of Parliament. The Act also provides for the furnishing of returns, etc., to the Central Government, and the conditions of service of employees. The Act also provides for the removal of difficulties, the amendment of the Uttar Pradesh State Universities Act, 1973, and the transitional provisions. The Act also contains the Schedule, which sets out the Statutes of the University. The Statutes provide for the appointment of the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Deans of Faculties, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Boards of Faculties, the Finance Committee, and other authorities. The Statutes also provide for the powers and duties of the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Deans of Faculties, the Registrar, and the Finance Officer, and the meetings of the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, and the Boards of Faculties. The Statutes also provide for the appointment of teachers and other academic staff, the recognition of persons as University recognised teachers, the creation of teaching and other academic posts, the appointment of examiners and moderators, and the grant of leave of absence to salaried officers of the University. The Statutes also provide for the maintenance of discipline among employees and students, the registration of graduates, the powers of the Vice-Chancellor to make regulations, the annual convocations of the University, and the quorum for meetings of the authorities and other bodies of the University.
