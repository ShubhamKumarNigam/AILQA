Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

In the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, after entry 57, the following entries shall be inserted, namely:-

"57A. The Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1969 (Kerala Act 3 of 1970);

57B. The Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1971 (Kerala Act 22 of 1971)."


The Constitution (Twenty-ninth Amendment) Act, 1972, is an amendment to the Constitution of India that adds the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1969 and the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1971 to the Ninth Schedule of the Constitution. By adding these Acts to the Ninth Schedule, they receive protection under article 31B of the Constitution. This protection ensures that the validity of these Acts cannot be challenged on the grounds that they violate the fundamental rights guaranteed under article 14, 19, or 31 of the Constitution. The purpose of this amendment is to remove any uncertainty or doubt regarding the validity of the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1969 and the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1971.
