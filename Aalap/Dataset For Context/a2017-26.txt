Summary:
The Central Goods and Services Tax (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Act, 2017, extends the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, along with all rules, notifications, and orders made thereunder by the Central Government, to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. This extension took effect on July 8, 2017. The Act amends the principal Act in several ways, including removing the words "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir" from section 1, sub-section (2), adding the words "except for the State of Jammu and Kashmir" to section 109, sub-section (6), and inserting the words "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir" in section 22, Explanation, clause (ii). The Act also establishes that the State Bench of the Goods and Services Tax Appellate Tribunal constituted under this Act shall be the State Appellate Tribunal constituted under the Jammu and Kashmir Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, for the State of Jammu and Kashmir. The Central Goods and Services Tax (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Ordinance, 2017, is repealed, but anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance is deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act. As of October 30, 2019, this Act is made applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh.
