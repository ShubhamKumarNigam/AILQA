

Summary:

The Constitution (Eighty-fourth Amendment) Act, 2002, extends the freeze on fresh delimitation of constituencies until the year 2026, with the goal of promoting population stabilization. This means that the distribution of parliamentary seats based on the 1971 Census will remain unchanged until the first Census after 2026. Additionally, the Act calls for the readjustment and rationalization of territorial constituencies in the States based on the population ascertained at the census for the year 1991, without altering the number of seats allotted to each State in the House of the People and Legislative Assemblies of the States, including the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes constituencies. The Act also proposes to refix the number of seats reserved for the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes in the House of the People and the Legislative Assemblies of the States on the basis of the population ascertained at the census for the year 1991.

The Act amends various articles of the Constitution, including articles 55, 81, 82, 170, 330, and 332, to reflect these changes. For example, the Act substitutes the figures "2000" with "2026" in article 55, which relates to the manner of election of the President, to extend the current freeze on undertaking fresh delimitation. Similarly, the Act substitutes the figures "2000" with "2026" in article 81, which deals with the composition of the House of the People, to extend the current allocation of seats in the House of the People to the States. The Act also substitutes the figures "2000" with "2026" in article 170, which deals with the composition of the Legislative Assemblies of States, to extend the current allocation of seats in the Legislative Assemblies of States. The Act also substitutes the figures "2000" with "2026" in article 330 and article 332, which deal with the reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the House of the People and the Legislative Assemblies of States, respectively, to extend the current reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes.
