Summary:
The Rampur Raza Library Act, 1975 (Act No. 22 of 1975) declares the Rampur Raza Library to be an institution of national importance and provides for its administration and matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act is divided into five chapters.

Chapter I, Preliminary, consists of three sections. Section 1 outlines the short title and commencement of the Act, while Section 2 declares the Rampur Raza Library as an institution of national importance. Section 3 provides definitions for various terms used throughout the Act.

Chapter II, Rampur Raza Library Board, establishes and incorporates the Rampur Raza Library Board, which is responsible for the administration and management of the library. The chapter consists of six sections, with Section 4 outlining the establishment and incorporation of the Board, and Section 5 specifying the composition of the Board. Section 6 details the term of office and fresh nomination in certain cases, while Section 7 states that vacancies or defects in the constitution of the Board do not invalidate acts. Section 8 requires the Government to ensure that nominated members have no financial or other interest likely to affect prejudicially the exercise or performance of their functions, and Section 9 outlines the meetings of the Board.

Chapter III, Property, Liabilities and Functions of Board, consists of three sections. Section 15 outlines the properties and liabilities of the Board, while Section 16 specifies the duties of the Board. Section 17 provides the Board with the power to exercise all such powers as may be necessary or expedient for the purpose of carrying out its functions and duties under this Act.

Chapter IV, Finance, Accounts, Audit and Reports, consists of five sections. Section 18 outlines the grants by the Central Government to the Board, while Section 19 provides for the Fund of the Board. Section 20 outlines the budget, while Section 21 specifies the accounts and audit of the Board. Section 22 outlines the returns and reports of the Board.

Chapter V, Miscellaneous, consists of six sections. Section 23 states that the Board shall be bound by such directions on questions of policy as the Central Government may give to it from time to time. Section 24 allows the Board to delegate its powers and duties. Section 25 states that officers and employees of the Board are public servants. Section 26 provides protection of action taken under the Act. Section 27 allows the Central Government to make rules, while Section 28 allows the Board to make regulations. Section 29 states that the provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any law other than this Act for the time being in force or any instrument having effect any virtue of any enactment other than this Act. The Act was enacted on the 9th May, 1975.
