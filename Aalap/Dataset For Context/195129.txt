Summary:
The Visva-Bharati Act, 1951, declares the institution known as Visva-Bharati to be an institution of national importance and constitutes it as a university. The Act defines the powers of the University, including the power to provide facilities for studies, undertake educational experiments, make arrangements for promoting the health and general welfare of employees, borrow money, establish campuses, and admit students from other universities. The Act also specifies the territorial jurisdiction of the University and provides for the maintenance of examination centers outside the territorial limits. Additionally, the Act establishes the University as an open institution, prohibiting discrimination based on race, creed, caste, or class. The Act also outlines the powers and duties of the officers of the University, including the Acharya (Chancellor), Upacharya (Vice-Chancellor), Karma-Sachiva (Registrar), and Vitta-Adhikari (Finance Officer). The Act also establishes various authorities of the University, including the Samsad (Court), Karma-Samiti (Executive Council), and Siksha-Samiti (Academic Council). The Act also provides for the appointment of adhyapakas and other academic staff, as well as the creation of a pension or provident fund for the benefit of employees. The Act also establishes procedures for the admission and examination of students and provides for the annual report and accounts of the University.
