Summary:
The Footwear Design and Development Institute Act, 2017 establishes and declares the Footwear Design and Development Institute as an institution of national importance for the promotion and development of quality and excellence in education, research, and training in all disciplines relating to footwear and leather products design and development. The Act defines key terms such as "design," "development," and "leather products."

The Act consists of three chapters. Chapter I, entitled "Preliminary," includes sections related to the short title and commencement of the Act, the declaration of the Footwear Design and Development Institute as an institution of national importance, and definitions of key terms used throughout the Act.

Chapter II, entitled "The Institute," includes sections related to the establishment of the Institute, the vesting of properties, the effect of incorporation of the Institute, the functions of the Institute, the powers of the Governing Council, the Institute's being open to all races, creeds, and classes, teaching at the Institute, the Visitor, authorities of the Institute, the Senate, the functions of the Senate, the functions, powers, and duties of the Chairperson, the Managing Director, the Secretary, the Executive Director, and other authorities and officers, grants by the Central Government, funds of the Institute, setting up of endowment funds, accounts and audit, pension and provident fund, appointment, Statutes, how Statutes are made, Ordinances, how Ordinances are made, a Tribunal of Arbitration, act and proceedings not to be invalidated by vacancies, sponsored schemes, power of the Institute to grant degrees, etc., the Institute to be a public authority under the Right to Information Act, 2005, power of the Central Government to make rules, returns and information to be provided to the Central Government, transitional provisions, Statutes and Ordinances to be published in the Official Gazette and to be laid before Parliament, and power to remove difficulties.

Chapter III, entitled "Miscellaneous," includes sections related to returns and information to be provided to the Central Government, transitional provisions, Statutes and Ordinances to be published in the Official Gazette and to be laid before Parliament, and power to remove difficulties.

The Schedule to the Act lists the Institute campuses and their locations.
