Summary:

THE SUPREME COURT JUDGES (SALARIES AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE) ACT, 1958, is an Act that regulates the salaries and certain conditions of service of the Judges of the Supreme Court in India. The Act includes chapters on Preliminary, Leave, Salaries and Pensions, and Miscellaneous provisions.

Under Chapter I, Preliminary, the Act defines various terms used in the Act, such as "acting Chief Justice," "High Court," "Judge," "prescribed," "service for pension," and "vacation."

Chapter II, Leave, outlines the kinds of leave admissible to a Judge, including leave on full allowances, leave on half allowances, and leave partly on full allowances and partly on half allowances. The chapter also specifies the leave account showing the amount of leave due to a Judge, leave encashment, the aggregate amount of leave which may be granted, and the authority competent to grant leave.

Chapter III, Salaries and Pensions, sets out the salaries of the Chief Justice and Judges of the Supreme Court, pension payable to Judges, special provisions for pension in respect of Judges who are members of service, power of President to add to the service for pension, extraordinary pension, and family pension and gratuity.

Chapter IV, Miscellaneous, includes provisions related to traveling allowance to a Judge, facilities for rent-free houses and other conditions of service, conveyance facilities, sumptuary allowance, medical facilities for retired Judges, and exemption from liability to pay Income-tax on certain perquisites received by a Judge.

The Act also includes a Schedule that outlines the pension payable to a Judge who has not held any other pensionable post under the Union or a State and also applies to a person who was in service as a Judge on the 20th May, 1954. The Schedule sets out the pension payable to a Chief Justice and other Judges, and the additional pension payable to a Judge who has held any pensionable post under the Union or a State.

In conclusion, THE SUPREME COURT JUDGES (SALARIES AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE) ACT, 1958, is a comprehensive Act that regulates the salaries and conditions of service of the Judges of the Supreme Court in India. The Act includes provisions related to leave, salaries and pensions, and miscellaneous provisions, and provides a Schedule that outlines the pension payable to a Judge who has not held any other pensionable post under the Union or a State.
