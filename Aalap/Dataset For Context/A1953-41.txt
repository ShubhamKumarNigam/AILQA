Summary:
The Calcutta High Court (Extension of Jurisdiction) Act, 1953, extends the jurisdiction of the High Court at Calcutta to Chandernagore and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. This Act, which received the President's assent on December 18, 1953, deems the High Court at Calcutta to be the High Court for Chandernagore and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands as of May 2, 1950.

The Act includes provisions for the effect of certain orders. Any order made by the highest court of appeal in relation to Chandernagore before May 2, 1950, or any order made by the Chief Commissioner of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands before the commencement of this Act shall have effect as if it were an order made by the High Court at Calcutta.

Additionally, the High Court at Calcutta may make rules to carry out the purposes of this Act and for the purpose of effectively exercising its jurisdiction in or in relation to Chandernagore and the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. These rules are subject to the provisions of any law for the time being in force.
