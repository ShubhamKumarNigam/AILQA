Summary:

THE GOODS AND SERVICES TAX (COMPENSATION TO STATES) ACT, 2017 is an Act to provide for compensation to the States for the loss of revenue arising on account of implementation of the goods and services tax in pursuance of the provisions of the Constitution (One Hundred and First Amendment) Act, 2016. The Act consists of 14 sections and a schedule. The Act defines various terms used in the Act, such as 'central tax', 'Central Goods and Services Tax Act', 'cess', 'compensation', 'Council', 'Fund', 'input tax', 'integrated tax', 'prescribed', 'projected growth rate', 'Schedule', 'State', 'State tax', 'State Goods and Services Tax Act', 'taxable supply', 'transition date', 'transition period', and 'Union Territories Goods and Services Tax Act'. The Act specifies the projected nominal growth rate of revenue subsumed for a State during the transition period as 14% per annum and the base year for calculating the compensation amount payable in any financial year during the transition period as the financial year ending 31st March, 2016. The Act also specifies the base year revenue for a State, which includes the revenue collected by the State and the local bodies during the base year, on account of the taxes levied by the respective State or Union and net of refunds, with respect to various taxes imposed by the respective State or Union, which are subsumed into goods and services tax. The Act also specifies the manner of calculating the compensation amount payable to any State during the transition period and the manner of levy and collection of cess. The Act also provides for the crediting of proceeds of cess to the Goods and Services Tax Compensation Fund and the utilization of such funds for purposes specified in the Act. The Act also specifies the manner of furnishing returns, making payments, and claiming refunds of cess. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out the provisions of the Act. The Act also provides for the laying of rules before Parliament and the power to remove difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act. The Schedule to the Act specifies the rate of cess on various supplies of goods and services.
