Summary:

THE REPEALING AND AMENDING ACT, 2016

Act ID: ACT NO. 23 OF 2016

Enactment Date: [6th May, 2016.]

This Act is called The Repealing and Amending Act, 2016. The Act repeals certain enactments and amends certain other enactments to the extent mentioned in the Fourth Column of the First and Second Schedules.

The First Schedule lists the enactments that are repealed to the extent mentioned in the Fourth Column. These enactments include various acts related to excise, customs, income tax, and other financial matters.

The Second Schedule lists the enactments that are amended to the extent mentioned in the Fourth Column. These enactments include various acts related to governors' emoluments, prevention of sexual harassment of women at work, and the representation of the people.

Section 4 of the Act contains savings provisions that ensure that the repeal of any enactment shall not affect any other enactment in which the repealed enactment has been applied, incorporated, or referred to. The section also provides that the Act shall not affect the validity, effect, or consequences of anything already done or suffered, or any right, title, obligation, or liability already acquired, accrued, or incurred.

The Appendix to the Act contains forms for the purposes of the Act.

In summary, this Act repeals certain enactments and amends certain other enactments to the extent mentioned in the Schedules. The savings provisions in Section 4 ensure that the repeal of any enactment does not affect any other enactment in which the repealed enactment has been applied, incorporated, or referred to. The Appendix to the Act contains forms for the purposes of the Act.
