Summary:

The Nuclear Liability Fund Rules, 2015, establish the Nuclear Liability Fund, a fund managed by the Central Government of India. The Fund is created for the purpose of providing financial assistance in the event of a nuclear accident. The Fund is composed of levies collected from operators of nuclear installations, who are required to pay a levy for every unit of electricity sold to their customers. The levy rate ranges from 0.05 to 0.10 rupees per unit of electricity. The Fund's balance must be maintained at two thousand crore rupees at all times. Payments from the Fund are subject to approval by the Parliament of India. In the case of a delay in payment by the operator, interest at the rate of 18% per annum is calculated and charged on a daily basis.
