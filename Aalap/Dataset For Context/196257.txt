The Delhi Motor Vehicles Taxation Act, 1962, is a law that imposes a tax on motor vehicles in the Union territory of Delhi. The Act consists of 25 sections, which cover various aspects of motor vehicle taxation, including the levy and collection of tax, declaration and payment of tax, issue of tokens, additional declaration, liability for additional tax, production of certificate of insurance, liability to pay arrears of tax of person succeeding to the ownership, possession or control of motor vehicles, refund of tax, penalty payable when tax not paid, arrears of tax and penalty recoverable as arrears of land revenue, exemptions, reduction of tax in cases of certain motor vehicles, effect of payment of tax in other States with respect to motor vehicles brought into Delhi, appeal, powers of police officers and other officers, penalty for incomplete and untrue declaration, etc., and other penalties. The Act also includes a schedule that specifies the description of motor vehicles and the annual rate of tax for each motor vehicle. The Act was enacted on December 15, 1962, and has been amended several times since then. The most recent amendment was made by the Delhi Motor Vehicles Taxation (Amendment) Act, 1989.
