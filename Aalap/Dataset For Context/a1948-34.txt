THE EMPLOYEES’ STATE INSURANCE ACT, 1948 (ACT NO. 34 OF 1948)

Title: The Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948
Act ID: ACT NO. 34 OF 1948
Enactment Date: [19th April, 1948.]

The Employees’ State Insurance Act, 1948 (hereafter referred to as the "Act") is an Act that provides benefits to employees in case of sickness, maternity, and employment injury. The Act also covers other related matters.

The Act applies to factories, including government factories, except for seasonal factories. However, certain provisions of the Act may not apply to factories or establishments belonging to or under the control of the government, whose employees are already receiving benefits substantially similar or superior to the benefits provided under this Act. The appropriate government may extend the provisions of this Act to any other establishment or class of establishments, industrial, commercial, agricultural, or otherwise.

The Act defines various terms used in the Act, such as "appropriate government," "confinement," "contribution," "Corporation," "dependant," "duly appointed," "employment injury," "employee," "exempted employee," "family," "factory," "immediate employer," "insurable employment," "insured person," "managing agent," "manufacturing process," "principal employer," "state government," and "wages."

The Act provides for the establishment of the Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (hereafter referred to as the "Corporation") to administer the provisions of the Act. The Corporation shall consist of a Chairman, a Vice-Chairman, and not more than fifteen other members, appointed by the Central Government.

The Act provides for the payment of contributions by the principal employer and the employee towards the Employees’ State Insurance Fund. The contributions shall be paid in respect of every employee who is employed for wages in or in connection with the work of a factory or establishment to which this Act applies.

The Act provides for various benefits to insured persons, such as sickness benefit, maternity benefit, disablement benefit, and dependant's benefit. The Act also provides for medical benefits to insured persons and their families.

The Act provides for the appointment of Inspectors to inspect factories and establishments to ensure compliance with the provisions of the Act. The Inspectors may also investigate any accident or disease that occurs in a factory or establishment.

The Act provides for penalties for non-compliance with the provisions of the Act. The penalties include fines and imprisonment.

The Act contains provisions for the administration and enforcement of the Act, including the power to make rules and regulations.
