THE REGIONAL CENTRE FOR BIOTECHNOLOGY ACT, 2016

The Regional Centre for Biotechnology Act, 2016 establishes an institution of national importance to be known as the Regional Centre for Biotechnology. The Act aims to provide for the establishment of the Regional Centre for Biotechnology and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act was enacted on 29th July, 2016.

The Act defines several key terms, including "academic staff," "Board," "Board of Studies," "Chairperson," "employee," "Executive Committee," "Executive Director," "existing Regional Centre," "hall," "institution," "Ordinances," "Programme Advisory Committee," "region," "Regional Centre," "Regulations," "Statutes," and "UNESCO."

The Act establishes the Regional Centre for Biotechnology as a body corporate, with perpetual succession and a common seal. The Regional Centre shall consist of a Board of Governors and other authorities specified in section 13. The headquarters of the Regional Centre shall be at its campus in the National Capital Region, Faridabad.

The Act declares the Regional Centre for Biotechnology as an institution of national importance, and on the date of commencement of this Act, all properties and assets, rights and liabilities, debts, obligations, and liabilities of the existing Regional Centre shall vest in the Regional Centre.

The Act defines the objectives and functions of the Regional Centre, which include disseminating and advancing knowledge in biotechnology and related fields, providing capacity-building through education, training, and research, facilitating transfer of knowledge and technology relating to biotechnology, creating a hub of biotechnology expertise, promoting international cooperation, and promoting collaborative research and development networking programmes.

The Act grants the Regional Centre the power to establish infrastructure and technology platforms, provide education and training, produce human resources, undertake research and development, hold scientific symposia and conferences, collect and disseminate bio-information, promote local knowledge, develop and implement a policy for intellectual property rights, disseminate research outcomes, and promote collaborative research and development networking programmes.

The Act provides for the composition and powers of the Board of Governors, the Programme Advisory Committee, the Executive Committee, the Finance Committee, and the Board of Studies. The Act also provides for the appointment and powers and duties of the officers of the Regional Centre, including the Executive Director, Deans, Sub-Deans, Associate Director (Administration), Registrar, and Finance Officer.

The Act provides for the appointment and conditions of service of employees of the Regional Centre and grants the Central Government the power to make grants and loans to the Regional Centre. The Act also provides for the preparation and submission of annual reports and accounts, the submission of returns and information, and the review of the functioning of the Regional Centre.

The Act provides for the protection of action taken in good faith, arbitration in case of disputes, and the making of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations. The Act also provides for the publication of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations in the Official Gazette and their laying before Parliament.
