Summary:
Title: THE ACQUISITION OF CERTAIN AREA AT AYODHYA ACT, 1993
Act ID: ACT NO. 33 OF 1993
Enactment Date: [3rd April, 1993.]

This Act aims to acquire certain areas at Ayodhya and address matters connected to it. The document highlights the long-standing dispute over the structure (including the premises of the inner and outer courtyards) known as the Ram Janma Bhumi-Babri Masjid in village Kot Ramchandra. Due to this dispute, public order and harmony between different communities in the country have been affected. This Act seeks to maintain public order and promote communal harmony and the spirit of common brotherhood amongst the people of India.

Chapter I: Preliminary
- Short title and commencement
- Definitions

Chapter II: Acquisition of the Area in Ayodhya
- Acquisition of rights in respect of certain area
- General effect of vesting
- Duty of person or State Government in charge of the management of the area to deliver all assets, etc.
- Power of Central Government to direct vesting of the area in another authority or body or trust

Chapter III: Management and Administration of Property
- Management of property by Government

Chapter IV: Miscellaneous
- Payment of amount
- Act to override all other enactments
- Penalties
- Protection of action taken in good faith
- Power to make rules
- Repeal and saving

The Schedule:
- Description of the Area, including village, pargana, tehsil, district, state, and plot numbers, along with the corresponding bigha, biswa, and biswansi measurements.
