Summary:

THE ADVOCATES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1976, ACT NO. 107 OF 1976, was enacted on the 13th of September, 1976, to further amend the Advocates Act of 1961. This Amendment Act repeals several sections and amends others, primarily related to the composition and powers of the Bar Council of India and various State Bar Councils.

The Act begins by stating its short title and the date of its commencement. The subsequent sections repeal several existing sections of the Advocates Act, 1961, including sections 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, and 9, and amend several others, such as sections 15, 24, 46, and 55.

The repealed sections relate to various aspects of the Bar Council of India and State Bar Councils, including their composition, powers, and functions. The amended sections primarily focus on the appointment, qualifications, and powers of the Chairman and Vice-Chairman of the Bar Council of India and various State Bar Councils.

The Act also includes a transitional provision that states that every person holding office as Chairman or Vice-Chairman of the Bar Council of India or any State Bar Council immediately before the coming into force of the relevant sections of this Act shall cease to hold such office on such commencement.

In summary, THE ADVOCATES (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1976, significantly alters the composition and powers of the Bar Council of India and various State Bar Councils, with the aim of improving the administration and regulation of the legal profession in India.
