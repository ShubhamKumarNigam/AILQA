The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024 is an Act to prevent unfair means in public examinations and provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. This Act defines various terms such as candidate, communication device, competent authority, computer network, computer resource, computer system, conduct of public examination, institution, notification, organized crime, person associated with a service provider, prescribed, public examination, public examination authority, public examination centre, and service provider.

The Act prohibits unfair means during the conduct of a public examination. Unfair means include acts such as leakage of question paper or answer key, participating in collusion to effect leakage, accessing or taking possession of question paper or answer key without authority, providing solutions to questions by unauthorized persons, assisting candidates unauthorizedly, tampering with answer sheets, altering assessments, violating norms or standards set up by the Central Government, tampering with documents necessary for short-listing or finalizing merit or rank, disrupting the conduct of a public examination, tampering with computer networks, manipulating seating arrangements, threatening or obstructing persons associated with the public examination authority or service provider, creating fake websites, and conducting fake examinations.

The Act also prohibits conspiracy for unfair means and disruption to the conduct of a public examination. It mandates that service providers report offenses to the concerned police authorities and the public examination authority. It is an offense for any person or service provider to use any premises other than the examination center for conducting a public examination without the written approval of the public examination authority.

The Act prescribes penalties for offenses under this Act. All offenses under this Act are cognizable, non-bailable, and non-compoundable. Any person resorting to unfair means and offenses under this Act shall be punished with imprisonment for a term not less than three years but which may extend to five years and with a fine of up to ten lakh rupees. The service provider shall also be liable to be punished with a fine of up to one crore rupees and proportionate cost of examination. Where an offense committed by a service provider is established during investigation to have been committed with the consent or connivance of any director, manager, secretary, or other officer of such service provider, such person shall also be liable to be proceeded against.

The Act also provides for the investigation of offenses under this Act by an officer not below the rank of Deputy Superintendent of Police or Assistant Commissioner of Police. The Central Government has the power to refer the investigation to any Central Investigating Agency.

The Act also provides that the Chairperson, Members, officers, and other employees of the public examination authority shall be deemed to be public servants within the meaning of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. No suit, prosecution, or other legal proceedings under this Act shall lie against any public servant in respect of anything which is done in good faith or intended to be done in the discharge of his official functions or in exercise of his powers. The provisions of this Act shall be in addition to, and not in derogation of, any other law for the time being in force.

The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act. Every rule made under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament while it is in session for a total period of thirty days. The Act also provides for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of this Act by the Central Government.

The Act amends the Criminal Law (Amendment) Ordinance, 1944, by adding a new entry in the Schedule to include an offense punishable under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024.

In summary, the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024 is an Act that aims to prevent unfair means in public examinations and provides for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act defines various terms and prohibits unfair means during the conduct of a public examination. It also prescribes penalties for offenses under this Act and provides for the investigation of offenses under this Act. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act and provides for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of this Act. The Act amends the Criminal Law (Amendment) Ordinance, 1944, by adding a new entry in the Schedule to include an offense punishable under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024.
