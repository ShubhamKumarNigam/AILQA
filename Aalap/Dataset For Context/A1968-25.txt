Summary:

THE CENTRAL LAWS (EXTENSION TO JAMMU AND KASHMIR) ACT, 1968, extends certain Central Laws to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. The Act is divided into six sections. Section 1 outlines the short title and commencement of the Act. Section 2 allows for the extension and amendment of certain laws, with any references to laws not in force in Jammu and Kashmir being construed as references to the corresponding law in force in that State. Section 3 establishes the construction of references to authorities where new authorities have been constituted. Section 4 provides for repeals and savings, with any law corresponding to an Act now extended to Jammu and Kashmir standing repealed on such commencement, subject to certain provisions. Section 5 allows for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of any Act now extended to Jammu and Kashmir. The Schedule to the Act lists the Acts that are extended to Jammu and Kashmir, along with any necessary amendments. The Act applies to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh, as per a notification dated 30th October, 2019.
