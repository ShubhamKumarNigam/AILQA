Summary:
THE UNIVERSITY OF HYDERABAD ACT, 1974 is an Act to establish and incorporate a teaching University in the State of Andhra Pradesh and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act defines the University, its short title, and commencement date. The Act includes various sections related to the University's authorities, officers, powers, and functions.

Section 2 provides definitions for various terms used in the Act.

Section 3 establishes the University, its headquarters, and the body corporate.

Section 4 outlines the objects of the University, which include disseminating and advancing knowledge, providing instructional and research facilities, and making special provisions for integrated courses in humanities and science.

Section 5 lists the powers of the University, including providing instructions, granting degrees, organizing extra-mural studies, establishing campuses, and supervising the residences of students.

Section 6 defines the University's jurisdiction and the conditions for recognizing institutions affiliated to or associated with other universities.

Section 7 states that the University shall be open to all classes, castes, and creed, and prohibits the University from adopting any test of religious belief or profession for admission, teaching, or holding any office.

Section 8 establishes the Visitor of the University, who has the power to cause inspections and inquiries, address the Vice-Chancellor with recommendations, and issue directions to the University.

Section 9 appoints the Governor of the State of Andhra Pradesh as the Chief Rector of the University.

Section 10 lists the officers of the University, including the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Deans of Schools, Registrar, and Finance Officer.

Section 11 defines the powers and functions of the Chancellor, who is the head of the University and presides at convocations.

Section 12 defines the powers and functions of the Vice-Chancellor, who is the principal executive and academic officer of the University and exercises general supervision and control over the affairs of the University.

Section 13 defines the powers and functions of the Pro-Vice-Chancellor.

Section 14 defines the powers and functions of the Deans of Schools.

Section 15 defines the powers and functions of the Registrar.

Section 16 defines the powers and functions of the Finance Officer.

Section 17 lists the other officers of the University and their powers and functions.

Section 18 lists the authorities of the University, including the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Boards of Schools, and other authorities.

Section 19 defines the powers and functions of the Court, which include reviewing the broad policies and programs of the University, considering the annual report and accounts, and advising the Visitor.

Section 20 defines the powers and functions of the Executive Council, which is the principal executive body of the University and exercises general supervision and control over the affairs of the University.

Section 21 defines the powers and functions of the Academic Council, which is the principal academic body of the University and coordinates and exercises general supervision over the academic policies of the University.

Section 22 defines the powers and functions of other authorities of the University.

Section 23 establishes a Planning Board to advise generally on the planning and development of the University and keep under review the standard of education and research.

Section 24 provides for the making of Statutes by the Executive Council with the approval of the Visitor.

Section 25 provides for the first Statutes to be those set out in the Schedule.

Section 26 provides for the making of Ordinances by the authorities of the University.

Section 27 provides for the making of Regulations by the authorities of the University.

Section 28 requires the annual report of the University to be prepared under the direction of the Executive Council and submitted to the Court.

Section 29 requires the annual accounts and balance-sheet of the University to be prepared under the directions of the Executive Council and audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India.

Section 30 provides for the conditions of service of employees and the procedure for arbitration in cases of dispute between employees or students and the University.

Section 31 provides for the procedure of appeal and arbitration in disciplinary cases against students.

Section 32 provides for the right to appeal.

Section 33 provides for the constitution of provident and pension funds for the benefit of the University's employees.

Section 34 provides for disputes as to the constitution of University authorities and bodies to be referred to the Visitor.

Section 35 provides for the constitution of Committees.

Section 36 provides for filling casual vacancies.

Section 37 provides that the proceedings of University authorities or bodies shall not be invalidated by vacancies.

Section 38 provides for the protection of action taken in good faith.

Section 39 provides for the mode of proof of University records.

Section 40 provides for the removal of difficulties.

Section 41 provides for transitional provisions.

The Schedule contains the first Statutes of the University.
