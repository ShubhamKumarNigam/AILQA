Summary:

The Indirect Tax Dispute Resolution Scheme Rules, 2016, provide guidelines for implementing the Indirect Tax Dispute Resolution Scheme, 2016, which is a provision under Chapter XI of the Finance Act, 2016. The rules specify the format for filing a declaration under section 214 of the Finance Act, 2016, and the manner of verification of such declaration. The rules also provide for the reporting of deposits made by the declarant and the issuance of an order of discharge of dues by the designated authority. The rules aim to provide a mechanism for the resolution of disputes related to indirect taxes, including duty, tax, interest, and penalty. The declarant is required to furnish the declaration to the designated authority, who shall issue an acknowledgment within seven days of receipt. The declarant must also intimate the concerned Commissioner (Appeals) about the deposit made within seven days of making the deposit. The designated authority shall issue an order of discharge of dues within fifteen days of receiving information about the deposit made. The declarant must intimate the concerned Commissioner (Appeals) about the order of discharge of dues issued by the designated authority before the expiry of the specified period of sixty days. The Commissioner (Appeals) shall remove the appeal from the list of pending appeals and intimate the declarant within seven days of receiving the information. The rules provide for the use of specific forms for filing a declaration, issuing an acknowledgment, reporting deposits, and issuing an order of discharge of dues. The forms require the declarant to provide details about their name, registration number, address, telephone number, order number, date of order, amount demanded, duty/tax, interest, penalty, Commissioner (Appeals) before whom appeal is pending, appeal number, amounts deposited, and any other information. The declaration must be verified and signed by the declarant or any person competent to act on their behalf. The rules specify the instructions for filling the forms, including the requirement to sign the forms, provide complete and correct information, and calculate the amount payable as per the provisions of the scheme. The rules came into force on the 1st day of June, 2016.
