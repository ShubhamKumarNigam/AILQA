Summary:

The CESS AND OTHER TAXES ON MINERALS (VALIDATION) ACT, 1992, is an Act that validates the imposition and collection of cesses and certain other taxes on minerals under certain State laws. The Act extends to the whole of India and came into force on the 15th day of February, 1992.

The Act validates certain State laws and actions taken and things done under those laws. The laws specified in the Schedule to this Act are deemed to have been as valid as if the provisions relating to cesses or other taxes on minerals had been enacted by Parliament. All actions taken, things done, rules made, notifications issued, or cesses or other taxes on minerals realized under any such laws are deemed to have been validly taken, done, made, issued, or realized. No suit or other proceeding shall be maintained or continued in any court for the refund of the cesses or other taxes realized under any such laws.

However, nothing in the Act prevents any person from claiming a refund of any cess or tax paid by him in excess of the amount due from him under any such laws. The Cess and Other Taxes on Minerals (Validation) Ordinance, 1992, is repealed, and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.

The Schedule to this Act contains the Andhra Pradesh (Mineral Rights) Tax Act, 1975, the Andhra Pradesh (Andhra Area) District Boards Act, 1920, the Andhra Pradesh (Telengana Area) District Boards Act, 1955, the Cess Act, 1880, the Karnataka Zilla Parishads, Taluk Panchayat Samitis, Mandal Panchayats and Nyaya Panchayats Act, 1983, the Karnataka (Mineral Rights) Tax Act, 1984, the Madhya Pradesh Karadhan Adhiniyam, 1982, the Madhya Pradesh Upkar Adhiniyam, 1981, the Maharashtra Zilla Parishads and Panchayat Samitis (Amendment and Validation) Act, 1981, the Orissa Cess Act, 1962, and the Tamil Nadu Panchayat Act, 1958.
