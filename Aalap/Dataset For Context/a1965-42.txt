Summary:
The Cardamom Act, 1965, is an Act to provide for the development under the control of the Union of the cardamom industry in India. This Act extends to the whole of India, except for the State of Jammu and Kashmir, where it only applies to the control of export of cardamom from India and import thereof into India. The Act declares that it is expedient in the public interest that the Union should take under its control the cardamom industry. The document contains several sections that have been repealed, including sections related to the establishment and constitution of the Board, acts or proceedings of the Board or its Committees, salary and allowances of the Chairman, executive officers of the Board and other staff, committees of the Board, functions of the Board, dissolution of the Board, registration of owners of cardamom estates, power of State Government to make rules, returns to be made by registered owners, imposition of a cess on cardamom exported, payment of proceeds of cess to the Board, grants and loans by the Central Government, constitution of the Fund, borrowing powers of the Board, accounts and audit, power to control price and distribution of cardamom, power to prohibit or control imports and exports of cardamom, directions by Government, penalty for making false returns, penalty for obstructing an officer or member of the Board in the discharge of his duties and for failure to produce books and records, penalty for contravention of order relating to control of price, etc., other penalties, offences by companies, jurisdiction of court, previous sanction of Central Government, protection of action taken in good faith, power to delegate, suspension of operation of Act, and power of Central Government to make rules. The Act came into force on the 5th of April, 1966, except for Chapter III, which came into force on the 11th of April, 1966, in respect of sections 14 and 15, and on the 12th of July, 1966, in respect of Chapter III. The Act is now applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and Union territory of Ladakh.
