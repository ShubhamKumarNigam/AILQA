Amendment of article 71.-

In article 71 of the Constitution, in clause (1), after the words "by the High Court of Judicature for a State", the words "or by such other authority as may be provided for by Parliament by law" shall be inserted and shall be deemed always to have been so inserted.


Insertion of new articles 329A and 329B.-

After article 329 of the Constitution, the following articles shall be inserted, namely:-

"329A. Disputes respecting President and Vice-President.-

(1) Subject to the provisions of clause (2), no election to either House of Parliament or to the House or either House of the Legislature of a State shall be called in question except by an election petition presented to such authority and in such manner as may be provided for by or under any law made by Parliament.

(2) For the removal of doubts, it is hereby declared that

(a) there shall be no appeal to any court from any order or decision of such authority, and

(b) no court shall entertain any second election petition on the same cause of action on which an election petition has already been presented to such authority and rejected.

329B. Bar of jurisdiction of courts in election matters.-

No election law in force in the territory of India or any part thereof or any rule or order made under any such law shall be called in question in any court.
