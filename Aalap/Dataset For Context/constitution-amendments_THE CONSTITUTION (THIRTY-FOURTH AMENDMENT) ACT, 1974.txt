Amendment of article 31B.

In article 31B of the Constitution, in the proviso, for the words "the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971", the words "this Constitution as amended by the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971 and the Constitution (Thirty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1974" shall be substituted.


Explanation.-

For the removal of doubts, it is hereby declared that nothing in the Constitution (Thirty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1974 shall affect the provisions of article 31A contained in the Constitution (First Amendment) Act, 1951 and the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971.


The Constitution (Thirty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1974, adds several state laws related to land reforms and tenancy to the Ninth Schedule of the Constitution of India. The Ninth Schedule provides protection to laws included in it from being challenged on the grounds of violation of fundamental rights under Articles 14, 19, and 31. The Act also amends Article 31B to include the Constitution (Thirty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1974, along with the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971, in the proviso of the Article. The Act also clarifies that nothing in the Act affects the provisions of Article 31A as contained in the Constitution (First Amendment) Act, 1951 and the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971.
