Summary:
The Britannia Engineering Company Limited (Mokameh Unit) and the Arthur Butler and Company (Muzaffarpore) Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1978, was enacted on December 8, 1978, to provide for the acquisition and transfer of the right, title, and interest of the undertakings of Britannia Engineering Company in relation to the Mokameh unit owned by it and the right, title, and interest of Arthur Butler and Company in relation to the undertakings owned by it. This Act aims to ensure the continued manufacture of railway wagons and other goods essential to the needs of the country in general and the Railways in particular.
The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including "appointed day," "Arthur Butler and Company," "Britannia Engineering Company," "Commissioner," "Government company," "Mokameh unit," "notification," "prescribed," "specified date," "wagon and other undertaking," and "words and expressions used herein."
The Act is divided into seven chapters. Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," defines the short title and commencement of the Act. Chapter II, titled "Acquisition and Transfer of Mokameh Unit and Undertakings of Arthur Butler and Company," discusses the vesting of the Mokameh unit and the undertakings of Arthur Butler and Company in the Central Government and the general effect of such vesting. Chapter III, titled "Payment of Amount," discusses the payment of amounts to Britannia Engineering Company and Arthur Butler and Company. Chapter IV, titled "Management, etc., of Mokameh Unit and the Undertakings of Arthur Butler and Company," discusses the management, etc., of the Mokameh unit and the undertakings of Arthur Butler and Company. Chapter V, titled "Provisions Relating to Employees of Mokameh Unit and of Arthur Butler and Company," discusses the employment of employees to continue and the payment of further amounts. Chapter VI, titled "Commissioner of Payments," discusses the appointment of a Commissioner of Payments and payment by the Central Government to the Commissioner. Chapter VII, titled "Miscellaneous," discusses various miscellaneous provisions, including the overriding effect of the Act, contracts to cease to have effect unless ratified by the Central Government or the Government company, penalties, offenses by companies, protection of action taken in good faith, delegation of powers, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, and declaration as to the policy of the State.
The Act also includes two schedules. The First Schedule lists the amount due to Britannia Engineering Company and Arthur Butler and Company. The Second Schedule lists the order of priorities for the payment of claims.
The Annexure includes various forms required for the implementation of the Act. The Appendix includes a list of abbreviations used in the Act. The Footnotes provide additional information and references related to the Act.
