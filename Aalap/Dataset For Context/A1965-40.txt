Summary:
The Railways (Employment of Members of the Armed Forces) Act, 1965, is a law that extends to the entire country of India. It outlines provisions for the employment of members of the Armed Forces of the Union in the working and management of railways.

Under this Act, when a member of the Armed Forces is employed to assist a railway administration in connection with the service of a railway, any provision of the Indian Railways Act, 1890, or of the rules made thereunder, which confers a power, status, or immunity, or imposes a duty or liability, upon a railway servant, is construed as conferring the same power, status, or immunity or imposing the same duty or liability, upon such member of the Armed Forces.

Furthermore, the employment of a member of the Armed Forces, in addition to or in the place of any railway servant, does not affect any liability that would have attached to the railway administration had such member been a railway servant.

If the whole of the working, management, and maintenance of a railway, or of a specific portion or section of a railway, is assumed by the Armed Forces of the Union, the Central Government may notify the fact of such assumption in the Official Gazette, and the Indian Railways Act, 1890, shall cease to be applicable to the railway or the portion or section of the railway concerned.

This Act repeals the Railways (Employment of Members of the Armed Forces) Ordinance, 1965, and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance is deemed to have been done or taken under this Act.
