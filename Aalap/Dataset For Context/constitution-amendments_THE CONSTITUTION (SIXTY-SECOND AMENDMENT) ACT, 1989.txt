
 Amendment of article 368.-

 In article 368 of the Constitution, after clause (1), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:-

"(1A) Notwithstanding anything in clauses (1), (2) and (3), a Bill for the amendment of this Constitution, which seeks to make any provision with respect to,-

(a) the abolition or creation of any State,-

(b) the admission or establishment of new States,

(c) the alteration of the boundaries of any State,

(d) the alteration of the name of any State,

may be introduced in, and passed by, either House of Parliament, and if such Bill is passed by both Houses of Parliament by a majority of the total membership of that House and by a majority of not less than two-thirds of the members of that House present and voting, it shall be presented to the President for his assent and upon such assent being given to the Bill, the same shall become law and the provisions of article 368 shall not apply to such amendment.".
