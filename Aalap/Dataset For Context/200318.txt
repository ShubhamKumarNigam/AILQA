Summary:

THE BIOLOGICAL DIVERSITY ACT, 2002, also known as Act No. 18 of 2003, was enacted on 5th February, 2003. The Act aims to conserve biological diversity, promote sustainable use of its components, and ensure fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising out of the use of biological resources, knowledge, and associated traditional practices. The Act is divided into 12 chapters and includes provisions for the regulation of access to biological diversity, the establishment of the National Biodiversity Authority, the functions and powers of the National Biodiversity Authority, the approval process for certain activities, the determination of equitable benefit sharing, the establishment of State Biodiversity Boards, finance, accounts, and audit, duties of the Central and State Governments, and miscellaneous provisions. The Act applies to the whole of India and includes definitions for various terms used throughout the document.
