Summary:

The Constitution (Twelfth Amendment) Act, 1962, came into force on December 20, 1961, and is related to the integration of Goa, Daman, and Diu into India as a Union territory. This amendment added a new entry to the First Schedule of the Constitution under the heading "THE UNION TERRITORIES", listing the territories comprising Goa, Daman, and Diu. Additionally, the amendment inserted a new entry (d) in clause (1) of Article 240, which allows the President of India to establish rules for the peace, progress, and good governance of Goa, Daman, and Diu. The purpose of this amendment was to facilitate the integration of these territories into India's political and administrative structure.
