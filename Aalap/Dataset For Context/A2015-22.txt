Summary:
The Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act, 2015, is an Act to make provisions to deal with the problem of Black Money that is undisclosed foreign income and assets, the procedure for dealing with such income and assets and to provide for imposition of tax on any undisclosed foreign income and asset held outside India and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

The Act is divided into seven chapters. Chapter I is the preliminary chapter that deals with the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. It also defines various terms used in the Act. Chapter II deals with the basis of charge and imposes a tax of 30% on the total undisclosed foreign income and asset of any previous year of an assessee. Chapter III deals with tax management and provides for tax authorities, change of incumbent, powers regarding discovery and production of evidence, proceedings before tax authorities to be judicial proceedings, assessment, time limit for completion of assessment and reassessment, rectification of mistake, notice of demand, direct assessment or recovery not barred, appeals to the Commissioner (Appeals), procedure to be followed in appeal, powers of Commissioner (Appeals), appeals to the Appellate Tribunal, appeal to High Court, hearing before Supreme Court, revision of orders prejudicial to revenue, revision of other orders, tax to be paid pending appeal, execution of order for costs awarded by Supreme Court, amendment of assessment on appeal, exclusion of time taken for obtaining copy, filing of appeal by tax authority, and recovery of tax dues by Assessing Officer. Chapter IV deals with penalties and provides for penalty in relation to undisclosed foreign income and asset, penalty for failure to furnish return in relation to foreign income and asset, penalty for failure to furnish in return of income, an information or furnish inaccurate particulars about an asset (including financial interest in any entity) located outside India, penalty for default in payment of tax arrear, penalty for other defaults, procedure, bar of limitation for imposing penalty, and prosecution to be at instance of Principal Chief Commissioner or Chief Commissioner or Principal Commissioner or Commissioner. Chapter V deals with offences and prosecutions and provides for punishment for failure to furnish return in relation to foreign income and asset, punishment for failure to furnish in return of income, any information about an asset (including financial interest in any entity) located outside India, punishment for wilful attempt to evade tax, punishment for false statement in verification, punishment for abetment, presumption as to culpable mental state, and proof of entries in records of documents. Chapter VI deals with tax compliance for undisclosed foreign income and assets and provides for declaration of undisclosed foreign asset, charge of tax, penalty, manner of declaration, time for payment of tax, undisclosed foreign asset declared not to be included in total income, undisclosed foreign asset declared not to affect finality of completed assessments, tax in respect of voluntarily disclosed asset not refundable, declaration not admissible in evidence against declarant, declaration by misrepresentation of facts to be void, exemption from wealth-tax in respect of assets specified in declaration, and applicability of certain provisions of Income-tax Act and of Chapter V of Wealth-tax Act. Chapter VII deals with general provisions and provides for agreement with foreign countries or specified territories, service of notice generally, authentication of notices and other documents, notice deemed to be valid in certain circumstances, appearance by approved valuer in certain matters, appearance by authorized representative, rounding off of income, value of asset and tax, cognizance of offences, assessment not to be invalid on certain grounds, bar of suits in civil courts, income-tax papers to be available for purposes of this Act, application of provisions of Income-tax Act, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, amendment of section 2 of Act 54 of 1963, amendment of Act of 15 of 2003, and schedule.

The Act provides for the imposition of tax on undisclosed foreign income and assets held outside India and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. It also provides for penalties and prosecutions for non-compliance and offences related to undisclosed foreign income and assets. The Act also provides for the agreement with foreign countries or specified territories, service of notice generally, authentication of notices and other documents, notice deemed to be valid in certain circumstances, appearance by approved valuer in certain matters, appearance by authorized representative, rounding off of income, value of asset and tax, cognizance of offences, assessment not to be invalid on certain grounds, bar of suits in civil courts, income-tax papers to be available for purposes of this Act, application of provisions of Income-tax Act, power to make rules, power to remove difficulties, amendment of section 2 of Act 54 of 1963, amendment of Act of 15 of 2003, and schedule.
