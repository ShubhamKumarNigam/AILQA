The Railways (Punitive Charges for Overloading of Wagon) Rules, 2006, were made by the Central Government under the powers conferred by the Railways Act, 1989. These rules came into force on January 1, 2007. The rules define various terms used in the Act, such as "Act," "section," "class rate," "permissible carrying capacity," "Schedule," "normal freight," and other words and expressions.

The rules provide for punitive charges for overloading of wagons. If commodities are overloaded in a railway wagon, the railway administration shall recover punitive charges from the consignor, consignee, or endorsee, as the case may be, for the entire distance to be traveled by the train hauling the wagon from the originating station to the destination point, irrespective of the point of detection of overloading. However, no punitive charges will be levied if the customer carries out load adjustment at the originating station itself in case of detection of overloading at the originating point. The Schedule to these rules specifies the punitive charges for overloading in different situations.
