Summary:

The Wild Life (Transactions and Taxidermy) Rules, 2024, are made under the powers conferred by the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972. These rules regulate the acquisition, possession, custody, control, and transport of specified animals, animal articles, trophies, and uncured trophies derived from specified animals. The rules also govern the process of taxidermy and the making of animal articles.

The rules define various terms used in the Act, such as "Act," "Form," "Chief Wild Life Warden," "Licensee," and "specified animal." A specified animal is any animal specified in Schedule I to the Act and which is captured, kept, bred in captivity, or found wild in nature.

According to Rule 3, no licensee shall acquire, receive, keep, or put under a process of taxidermy or make animal articles containing part or whole of a specified animal without the previous permission of the authorized officer. Every application for such permission shall be made in Form I, and the Chief Wild Life Warden may grant or refuse the permission after making such inquiry as he may think fit.

Rule 4 requires every licensee to whom permission has been granted to submit a report regarding the stocks of specified animals or animal articles, trophies, uncured trophies, or meat derived therefrom, in Form III within a period of seven days of the acquisition, receipt, or keeping of the same in his control, custody, or possession.

Rule 5 governs the sale of specified animals, animal articles, trophies, and uncured trophies derived from specified animals. No licensed dealer shall sell or offer for sale any specified animal or any animal article, trophy, or uncured trophy derived therefrom, except to a person authorized to purchase by a permission granted by the Chief Wild Life Warden. Every application for permission to purchase shall be made in Form IV, and the Officer may grant or refuse the permission after making such inquiry as he may think fit. Every permission granted shall be in Form V and shall be valid up to a period of one month from the date of issue.

Rule 6 governs the taxidermy or making of animal articles. Every licensed taxidermist or licensed manufacturer shall, at the time of returning the trophy or animal article, issue a voucher to the owner of the said trophy or animal article. Each voucher shall contain the following particulars, namely: - (a) date of issue of voucher; (b) charges realized or to be realized; (c) name and address of the licensed taxidermist or manufacturer issuing the voucher; (d) name and address of the person to whom the voucher is issued; (e) description of the trophy or animal article and number; (f) whether uncured trophy or trophy animal article was required to be declared under section 40, or section 44, of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972) and if so, whether it has been declared; (g) signature of the licensed taxidermist or manufacturer issuing the voucher.

Rule 7 requires the maintenance of vouchers in triplicate and serially numbered. Every book containing blank vouchers shall be presented to the Chief Wild Life Warden for affixing his initials or stamps on such book before it is brought into use. Every licensed dealer, licensed taxidermist, or licensed manufacturer shall send, in monthly batches not later than the seventh day of every month, the duplicate copies or vouchers retained by him, to the Chief Wild Life Warden.

Rule 8 governs the transport of specified animals, animal articles, trophies, and uncured trophies derived from specified animals. No licensee shall transport from one place to another within the State any specified animal, animal article, trophy, or uncured trophy derived therefrom, except with the previous permission of the Chief Wild Life Warden. Every application for such permission shall be made in Form VI, and the Chief Wild Life Warden may grant or refuse to grant the permissions after making such inquiry as he may think fit.

Rule 9 provides for an appeal to the State Government against an order made by the Chief Wild Life Warden granting the permission under Rule 3, Rule 5, or Rule 8. Any person aggrieved by an order made by the Chief Wild Life Warden may prefer an appeal to the State Government within fifteen days from the date of the communication to the applicant of the order appealed against.

The rules also provide for various forms, such as Form I for application for permission to acquire, receive, keep, put under a process of taxidermy, or make animal articles, Form II for permission to acquire, receive, keep, put under a process of taxidermy, or make animal articles, Form III for report of stocks, Form IV for application for permission to purchase, Form V for permission to purchase, Form VI for application for permission to transport, and Form VII for permission to transport. These forms are appended to the rules and are to be used as prescribed.
