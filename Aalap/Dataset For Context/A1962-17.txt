Summary:

THE AIR CORPORATIONS (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1962, is an Act that further amends the Air Corporations Act, 1953. The Act comprises seven sections, with the first section stating the short title of the Act as the Air Corporations (Amendment) Act, 1962.

Section 2 to Section 6 of the Act repeal certain sections of the Air Corporations Act, 1953, specifically sections 18, 30, 36, 44, and 45. These sections relate to the appointment of directors, the Air Transport Council, and the dissolution of the Air Corporation.

Section 7 of the Act changes the name of the Corporation established under section 3 of the principal Act known as “Air India International” to “Air India” with effect from such date as the Central Government may specify. This change of name does not affect any rights and obligations of the Corporation or render defective any legal proceedings by or against it.

Section 8 of the Act dissolves the Air Transport Council constituted under section 30 of the principal Act with effect from the commencement of this Act.

In summary, the Air Corporations (Amendment) Act, 1962, amends the Air Corporations Act, 1953, by repealing certain sections and changing the name of the Corporation. The Act also dissolves the Air Transport Council.
