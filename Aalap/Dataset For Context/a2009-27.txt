Summary:

THE PREVENTION AND CONTROL OF INFECTIOUS AND CONTAGIOUS DISEASES IN ANIMALS ACT, 2009 (ACT NO. 27 OF 2009) is an Act to provide for the prevention, control, and eradication of infectious and contagious diseases affecting animals, for prevention of outbreak or spreading of such diseases from one State to another, and to meet the international obligations of India for facilitating import and export of animals and animal products and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

The Act defines "animal" as cattle, buffalo, sheep, goat, yak, mithun, dog, cat, pig, horse, camel, ass, mule, poultry, bees, and any other animal or bird as the Central Government may specify. The Act also defines other terms such as Check Post, Competent Officer, compulsory vaccination, controlled area, defective vaccine, Director, free area, infected animal, infected area, notification, prescribed, publication, Quarantine Camp, scheduled disease, Veterinarian, Veterinary Officer, and Village Officer.

The Act is divided into seven chapters. Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," contains the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act, as well as the definitions section. Chapter II, titled "Control of Scheduled Diseases," deals with the appointment of Veterinary Officers, reporting of scheduled diseases, segregation of infected animals, notification of controlled areas and free areas, prohibition of movement of animals from controlled areas, vaccination, marking, and issue of vaccination certificate, and entry and exit of animals into controlled and free areas. Chapter III, titled "Infected Areas," deals with the declaration of infected areas, effect of declaration of infected areas, and denotification of infected areas. Chapter IV, titled "Infected Animals," deals with the segregation, examination, and treatment of infected animals, drawing of samples from animals, and resort to euthanasia for infected animals. Chapter V, titled "Enforcement and Penalties," deals with enforcement of orders and recovery of expenses, village officers, etc., to assist, penalty for issuing vaccination certificate without authority or administering defective vaccine, penalties, penalty for placing infected animal or carcass in river, etc., offenses by companies, and powers of Central Government to make rules. Chapter VI, titled "Precautionary Measures on Causative Organism, etc.," deals with prevention of escape of causative organism, and power to delegate. Chapter VII, titled "Miscellaneous," deals with officers and authorities to function subject to Government control, power to amend the Schedule, power of Central Government to make rules, power of State Government to make rules, laying of rules, repeal and savings, and forms.

The Schedule to the Act lists the scheduled diseases, which are divided into multiple species diseases, cattle diseases, sheep and goat diseases, equine diseases, swine diseases, avian diseases, lagomorph diseases, bee diseases, fish diseases, mollusc diseases, crustacean diseases, amphibian disease, and other diseases. The Act also provides for penalties for offenses committed under the Act, including fines and imprisonment. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out the provisions of the Act, and the State Government to make rules for carrying out the purposes of the Act with the prior approval of the Central Government. The Act repeals the Glanders and Farcy Act, 1899, the Dourine Act, 1910, and any other corresponding law of any State, so far as it is inconsistent with the provisions of this Act.
