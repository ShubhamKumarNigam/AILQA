Summary:
The National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences, Bangalore Act, 2012 (Act No. 38 of 2012) declares the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences, Bangalore, as an institution of national importance and provides for its incorporation. The Act defines certain terms, including the Fund, the Governing Body, the Institute, member, prescribed, and specified. The Institute is established as a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, and is composed of various members including ex officio members, nominated members, and elected members. The Act outlines the term of office of members, the powers and functions of the President, the Vice-President, and other members, and the allowances to be paid to them. The Governing Body is constituted to exercise the powers and discharge the functions of the Institute, and the Director is appointed as the chief executive officer of the Institute. The objects of the Institute include developing patterns of teaching in undergraduate and postgraduate medical education in all its branches with a focus on mental health, neuro-sciences, and allied specialities, bringing together educational facilities of the highest order for the training of personnel in all important branches of health activity, evolving innovative strategies to offer diagnostic and comprehensive therapeutic service facilities in the field of mental health and neuro-sciences, and making an in-depth study and research in the field of mental health, neuro-sciences, and allied specialities. The functions of the Institute include providing for undergraduate and postgraduate teaching in the science of modern medicine and other allied sciences, providing facilities for research in the various branches of such sciences, providing for the teaching of humanities, conducting experiments in new methods of medical education, specifying courses and curricula for both undergraduate and postgraduate studies, establishing and maintaining medical institutions, nursing colleges, and other institutions for the training of different types of health workers, training teachers from different medical colleges in India, holding examinations and granting degrees, diplomas, and other academic distinctions and titles, receiving grants from the Government and gifts, donations, benefactions, bequests, and transfers of properties, dealing with any property belonging to, or vested in, the Institute, and borrowing money. The Act also provides for the vesting of property, payment to the Institute, the Fund of the Institute, the budget of the Institute, accounts and audit, the annual report, pension and provident funds, authentication of orders and instruments of the Institute, acts and proceedings not to be invalidated by vacancies, etc., grant of medical degrees, diplomas, etc., by the Institute, recognition of medical qualifications granted by the Institute, control by the Central Government, resolution of differences, returns and information, transfer of service of existing employees, power to make rules, power to make regulations, laying of rules and regulations before Parliament, and power to remove difficulties. The Schedule to the Act outlines the provisions for the transfer of service of existing employees. The Act was enacted on the 13th of September, 2012.
