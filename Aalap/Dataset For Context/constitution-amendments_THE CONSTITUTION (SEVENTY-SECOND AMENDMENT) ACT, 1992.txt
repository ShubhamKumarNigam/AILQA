Amendment of article 332.-In article 332 of the Constitution, after clause (4), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:

"(5) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (1) or clause (4), the seats reserved for the Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of the State of Tripura shall be determined in such manner as the President may, by order, provide and until the re-adjustment of seats on the basis of the first census after the year 2000 under article 170 of the Constitution for the State of Tripura, the number of seats so reserved shall not be less than twelve:

Provided that for the purpose of determining the number of seats reserved for the Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of the State of Tripura under this clause, the seats which are reserved for the Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of the State of Tripura immediately before the commencement of the Constitution (Seventy-second Amendment) Act, 1992, shall be taken into account.

Explanation.-In this clause, the expression "re-adjustment of seats" has the same meaning as in article 170."



In summary, the Constitution (Seventy-Second Amendment) Act, 1992 is an amendment to the Constitution of India that aims to make a temporary provision for determining the number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the State of Tripura. The amendment adds a new clause (5) to article 332 of the Constitution, which allows the President to determine the number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of Tripura through an order. The number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes shall not be less than twelve until the re-adjustment of seats on the basis of the first census after the year 2000 under article 170 of the Constitution for the State of Tripura. The seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of Tripura immediately before the commencement of the Constitution (Seventy-second Amendment) Act, 1992 shall be taken into account for determining the number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of Tripura under this clause. The amendment will come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government.
