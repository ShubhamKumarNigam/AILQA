Summary:
The Wild Life (Protection) Licencing (Additional Matters for Consideration) Rules, 2024, are a set of rules made under the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972, by the Central Government. These rules came into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. The rules provide additional matters for consideration when granting a license under sub-section (1) of section 44 of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972. The Chief Wild Life Warden or the authorized officer, as the case may be, must consider the following factors before granting a license: (i) the capacity of the applicant to handle the business concerned, including their facilities, equipment, and suitability of the premises for such business, (ii) the source and manner in which the supplies for the business concerned would be obtained, (iii) the number of licenses for the relevant business already in existence in the area concerned, and (iv) the implications that the grant of such license would have on the hunting or trade of wild animals concerned. However, no license shall be granted if it relates to any wild animal specified in Schedule I to the Act, except with the previous consultation of the Central Government.
