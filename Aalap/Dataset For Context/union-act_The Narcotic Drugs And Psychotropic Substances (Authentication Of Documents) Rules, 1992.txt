The Narcotic Drugs And Psychotropic Substances (Authentication Of Documents) Rules, 1992 

 Interpretation

.-In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, -
(a) "First Mentioned Act" means the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 (61 of 1985);
(b) "document" includes any paper, book, map, plan, chart, photograph or other device in which information is recorded or stored, by any means, whether tangible or otherwise.


Summary:

The Narcotic Drugs And Psychotropic Substances (Authentication Of Documents) Rules, 1992 were made under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 (61 of 1985) to govern the authentication of documents received from outside India in the course of investigations of offenses under the Act. These rules state that any document purporting to have the seal and signature of a person authorized by section 3 of the Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Oath and Fees) Act, 1948 (41 of 1948) to do any notarial act, shall be deemed to be duly authenticated for the purposes of clause (ii) of section 66 of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985. The rules also define "First Mentioned Act" and "document" for the purposes of these rules.
