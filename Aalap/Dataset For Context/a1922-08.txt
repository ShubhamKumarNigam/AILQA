THE DELHI UNIVERSITY ACT, 1922 (ACT NO. 8 OF 1922)

This Act establishes and incorporates a teaching and affiliating University at Delhi. It outlines the powers and duties of the University, including the ability to provide instruction in various branches of learning, hold examinations, grant degrees and other academic distinctions, and make arrangements for promoting the health and general welfare of students. The Act also includes provisions for the composition and powers of various authorities within the University, such as the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, and the Faculties. The Act also includes provisions for the admission and residence of students, the conduct of examinations, and the annual report and accounts of the University.

Key legal terms and concepts in this Act include:

* University: a teaching and affiliating University established and incorporated at Delhi
* Teaching and affiliating University: a type of university that provides instruction and also recognizes and supervises affiliated colleges
* Court: the supreme authority of the University
* Executive Council: the executive body of the University
* Academic Council: the academic body of the University responsible for the maintenance of standards of instruction, education, and examination
* Faculties: academic divisions within the University
* Statutes: regulations made by the University that provide for its constitution, powers, and duties
* Ordinances: regulations made by the University that provide for all other matters
* Regulations: rules made by the authorities of the University for the conduct of their business and the management of the University's affairs
* Officers of the University: the Chancellor, Pro-Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Treasurer, Registrar, Deans of the Faculties, and other persons in the service of the University as may be declared by the Statutes to be officers of the University
* Authorities of the University: the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Finance Committee, and the Faculties
* University Boards: the Residence, Health and Discipline Board and other boards as may be prescribed by the Statutes
* Honorary degrees: academic distinctions conferred on approved persons in the manner laid down in the Statutes
* Fellowships, Scholarships, Studentships, Exhibitions, Medals and Prizes: forms of financial assistance and recognition awarded to students in accordance with the Ordinances
* Examinations: tests of knowledge and competence administered by the University
* Fees: charges levied by the University for courses of study and admission to examinations, degrees, and diplomas
* Discipline: the maintenance of order and proper behavior among students
* Residence: the requirement that students reside in a College or Hall or under specified conditions
* Colleges: institutions maintained or admitted to the privileges of the University that provide instruction up to the Bachelor's degree but exclusive of Honours and post-graduate degrees
* Halls: units of residence for students of the University provided, maintained, or recognized by it
* Patron of the University: a person who has made a donation of not less than one lakh of rupees to the funds of the University and has been declared by the Chancellor to be a patron of the University
* Principal: the head of a College, including when there is no Principal, the person for the time being duly appointed to act as principal, and in the absence of the principal or the acting principal, a Vice-Principal duly appointed as such
* Teachers: professors, readers, lecturers, and other persons imparting instruction in the University or in any College or Hall
* Teachers of the University: persons appointed or recognized by the University for the purpose of imparting instruction in the University or in any College
* Warden: the head of a Hall.
