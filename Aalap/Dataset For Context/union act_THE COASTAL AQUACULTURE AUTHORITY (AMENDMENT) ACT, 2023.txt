Summary:

The Coastal Aquaculture Authority (Amendment) Act, 2023 amends the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act, 2005. The Amendment Act includes new definitions in section 2, such as aquaculture input, aqua mapping, aqua zonation, Authority, biosecured facility, biosecurity, Brood Stock Multiplication Centre, operator, owner, pharmacologically active substance or antimicrobial agent, specific pathogen free or specific pathogen resistant or specific pathogen tolerant, and State. The Amendment Act also modifies section 4(3) to include a member from the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying of the Central Government, and removes clause (h). Additionally, the Amendment Act inserts new sections 7A, 9A, 12A, 13A, and 28. Section 7A allows the Authority to constitute committees, section 9A establishes the Secretary of the Authority, section 12A prohibits the use of certain materials in coastal aquaculture activities, section 13A authorizes officers to exercise powers and discharge duties, and section 28 validates certain provisions and amendments retrospectively. The Amendment Act also modifies sections 4(3), 7(2), 11, 12, and 25, and adds new clauses to section 24(2). These amendments aim to regulate and promote sustainable coastal aquaculture practices in India.
