Summary:
THE LALIT KALA AKADAMI (TAKING OVER OF MANAGEMENT) ACT, 1997 is an Act passed by the Indian government to take over the management of the Lalit Kala Akadami, an apex cultural body in the field of visual arts, for a limited period in the public interest. The Act came into force on January 24, 1997, and vests the management of the society in the Central Government for a period of three years, which may be extended up to a maximum of five years.
The Act defines the Administrator as a person appointed by the Central Government to take over the administration of the society and carry on its management for and on behalf of the Central Government. The Administrator has the power to exercise the functions of the General Council or the Executive Board of the society, including the power to dispose of any property or assets of the society.
The Act also specifies the powers and duties of the Administrator, including the power to issue directions to the Administrator, the power to exercise the powers of the General Council or the Executive Board of the society, the power to receive remuneration from the funds of the society, and the power to demand the delivery of property, books, papers, works of art, and other documents relating to the management of the society.
The Act further provides that no person in respect of whom any contract of management or other arrangement is terminated by reason of the provisions contained in the Act shall be entitled to claim any compensation for the premature termination of the contract of administration or other arrangement or for the loss of his office.
The Act also includes provisions for the relinquishment of the administration of the society, the application of the Societies Registration Act, 1860, penalties for offenses committed under the Act, offenses by companies, exclusion of the period of operation of the Act in computing the period of limitation for any suit or application, the Act to have overriding effect, protection of action taken in good faith, power to terminate contract of employment, power to make rules, and repeal and saving.
In summary, the Lalit Kala Akadami (Taking Over of Management) Act, 1997 is a law passed by the Indian government to take over the management of the Lalit Kala Akadami for a limited period in the public interest due to serious difficulties in its management. The Act vests the management of the society in the Central Government, defines the powers and duties of the Administrator, and includes provisions for the relinquishment of the administration of the society, penalties for offenses committed under the Act, exclusion of the period of operation of the Act in computing the period of limitation for any suit or application, and the Act to have overriding effect.
