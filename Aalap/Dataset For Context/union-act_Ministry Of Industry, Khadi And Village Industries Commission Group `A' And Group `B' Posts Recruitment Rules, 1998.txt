Summary:

The Ministry of Industry, Khadi And Village Industries Commission Group `A' And Group `B' Posts Recruitment Rules, 1998, outlines the regulations for recruitment to various Group `A' and Group `B' posts in the Ministry of Industry, Khadi and Village Industries Commission. These rules came into effect on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

The recruitment rules apply to the posts specified in the Schedule annexed to the rules. The number of posts, their classification, and the scale of pay attached to them are also specified in the Schedule.

The method of recruitment, age limit, qualifications, and other related matters for the posts are specified in the Schedule.

At the initial constitution, Group `A' and Group `B' Officers working in the Commission on a regular basis will be deemed to have been appointed to the posts corresponding to those they were holding. Their inter se seniority will be determined based on the date of regular appointment to the respective grades.

Officers appointed under these rules will be liable to serve anywhere in India and undergo such training and courses of instruction as the Commission may decide. Any person who opts to discontinue their service within three years of completing training will be liable to refund the expenses or cost of training.

A person appointed under these rules shall not have entered into or contracted a marriage with a person having a spouse living, and vice versa, unless exempted by the Central Government.

The Central Government has the power to relax any of the provisions of these rules with respect to any class or category of persons.

Nothing in these rules affects reservations, relaxation of age limit, and other concessions required to be provided for the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, Ex-servicemen, and other special categories of persons in accordance with the orders issued by the Central Government from time to time.
