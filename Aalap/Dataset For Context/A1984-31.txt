Summary:

THE VISVA-BHARATI (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1984, with the Act ID ACT NO. 31 OF 1984 and the enactment date of [21st May, 1984.] aims to further amend the Visva-Bharati Act, 1951. The Act comprises several sections, including sections 1 to 29, which deal with various aspects of the amendment.

Section 1 specifies the short title and commencement of the Act. Section 2, which was repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988, dealt with the substitution of certain expressions for certain other expressions. Sections 3 to 13, which were also repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988, dealt with substitution of new sections, insertion of new sections, and amendment of sections.

Section 14, which was repealed by s. 2 and the First Schedule, ibid. (w.e.f. 31-3-1988), dealt with substitution of new sections for sections 22 and 23. Sections 15 to 28, which were also repealed by s. 2 and the First Schedule, ibid. (w.e.f. 31-3-1988), dealt with amendment of sections and substitution of new sections.

Section 29, which deals with saving, states that anything done, any action taken, or any degree or other academic distinction conferred by the Visva-Bharati before the commencement of this Act shall be valid as if such thing were done, action were taken, or degree or other academic distinction were conferred under the provisions of the principal Act as amended by this Act.

Section 30 deals with the powers of the Paridarsaka (Visitor) to make appointments in certain cases. The Paridarsaka (Visitor) may, in consultation with the Upacharya (Vice-Chancellor), by order make any appointment or do anything which appears to him necessary or expedient for the proper establishment of any authority of the University or for the first meeting of that authority.

The Act also includes a schedule that was repealed by s. 2 and the First Schedule, ibid. (w.e.f. 31-3-1988). The schedule dealt with the omission of the First Schedule, amendment of the Second Schedule, and amendment of statutes.

Overall, the THE VISVA-BHARATI (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1984, aims to further amend the Visva-Bharati Act, 1951, by substituting new sections, inserting new sections, and amending sections. The Act also includes provisions for saving and powers of the Paridarsaka (Visitor) to make appointments in certain cases.
