Summary:

The STATE OF ARUNACHAL PRADESH ACT, 1986 (ACT NO. 69 OF 1986) was enacted on 24th December, 1986. The Act establishes the State of Arunachal Pradesh, consisting of the territories which immediately before that day were comprised in the existing Union territory of Arunachal Pradesh. The Act provides for the representation in the Legislatures, consisting of the Council of States, the House of the People, and the Legislative Assembly. The Council of States will have one sitting member deemed to have been duly elected under clause (4) of article 80 to fill the seat allotted to the State of Arunachal Pradesh in that Council. The House of the People will have two seats allocated to the State of Arunachal Pradesh, and the existing parliamentary constituencies of the Union territory of Arunachal Pradesh will become the parliamentary constituencies of the State of Arunachal Pradesh. The Legislative Assembly will have a total number of sixty seats, out of which fifty-nine seats will be reserved for the Scheduled Tribes. The Act also provides for the establishment of a common High Court for the States of Assam, Nagaland, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura, Mizoram and Arunachal Pradesh, to be known as the Gauhati High Court. The expenditure in respect of the salaries and allowances of the Judges of the common High Court shall be allocated amongst the States of Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland and Tripura in such proportion as the President may, by order, determine. The existing laws in force in the existing Union territory of Arunachal Pradesh shall continue to be in force in the State of Arunachal Pradesh until altered, repealed or amended by a competent Legislature or other competent authority.
