THE PRESS AND REGISTRATION OF BOOKS ACT, 1867 (ACT NO. 25 OF 1867)

This Act provides for the regulation of printing-presses and of newspapers, for the preservation of copies of books and newspapers printed in India, and for the registration of such books and newspapers.

The Act defines key terms such as 'Book', 'Editor', 'Magistrate', 'Newspaper', 'Paper', and 'Press Registrar'. It outlines the rules for the printing of books and papers, specifying that every book or paper printed within India must have the name of the printer and the place of printing, and (if the book or paper be published) the name of the publisher and the place of publication printed legibly on it.

The Act also details the rules for the publication of newspapers, stating that no newspaper shall be published in India except in conformity with the rules laid down in the Act. These rules include the requirement for the printer and publisher of every newspaper to appear before a District, Presidency or Sub-divisional Magistrate and make and subscribe a declaration.

The Act specifies the particulars that must be included in the declaration, including the title of the newspaper, the language in which it is to be published, and the periodicity of its publication. It also outlines the penalties for non-compliance with these rules, including fines and imprisonment.

The Act includes provisions for the delivery of books printed in India to the Government, requiring the printer to deliver printed copies of the whole of every book which shall be printed in India after this Act comes into force. The Act also specifies the manner in which these copies are to be delivered and the penalties for non-compliance.

The Act establishes a Register of Books, requiring that a memorandum of every book which shall have been delivered pursuant to the Act be registered in the Catalogue of Books printed in India. The Act also specifies the particulars that the memorandum must contain, including the title of the book, the language in which it is written, the name of the author, translator or editor, and the place of printing and publication.

The Act includes provisions for the registration of newspapers, requiring the publisher of every newspaper to deliver free of expense to the Press Registrar one copy of each issue of such newspaper as soon as it is published. The Act also establishes a Press Registrar and other officers to perform the functions assigned to them by or under this Act.

The Act includes penalties for non-compliance with its provisions, including fines and imprisonment. It also includes provisions for the recovery of forfeitures and the disposal of fines.

The Act includes miscellaneous provisions, including the power of the State Government to make rules, the power of the Central Government to make rules, the power of the Press Registrar and other officers to delegate their powers, and the punishment for improper disclosure of information.

The Act applies to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir. It came into force on 22nd March, 1867.
