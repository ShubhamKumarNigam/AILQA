Summary:
The Parliamentary Proceedings (Protection of Publication) Act, 1977, was enacted on April 18, 1977, to protect the publication of reports of proceedings of Parliament. This Act applies to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir, and it came into force on March 25, 1977.

The Act defines a "newspaper" as any printed periodical work containing public news or comments on public news, including a news agency supplying material for publication in a newspaper.

According to the Act, the publication of a substantially true report of any proceedings of either House of Parliament in a newspaper is privileged, unless it is proven that the publication was made with malice. However, this protection does not apply to the publication of any matter that is not for the public good.

The Act also applies to reports or matters broadcast by means of wireless telegraphy as part of any programme or service provided by means of a broadcasting station situate within the territories to which this Act extends.

In 2019, through Notification No. S.O. 3912 (E), dated 30th October, 2019, this Act was made applicable to the Union territory of Jammuand Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh.
