The Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes Act, 2019, is an Act to provide for a comprehensive mechanism to ban unregulated deposit schemes, other than deposits taken in the ordinary course of business, and to protect the interest of depositors and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act defines various terms such as 'appropriate Government', 'company', 'Competent Authority', 'Designated Court', 'insurer', 'notification', 'person', 'prescribed', 'property', 'public financial institution', 'Regulated Deposit Scheme', 'Regulator', 'Schedule', and 'Unregulated Deposit Scheme'.

The Act has various chapters, each dealing with different aspects of the law. Chapter I, titled 'Preliminary', deals with the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. Chapter II, titled 'Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes', prohibits unregulated deposit schemes and fraudulent default in regulated deposit schemes. Chapter III, titled 'Authorities', establishes the Competent Authority and Designated Court for the purposes of the Act. Chapter IV, titled 'Information on Deposit Takers', requires deposit takers to intimate the authority about their business and share information with the Competent Authority. Chapter V, titled 'Restitution to Depositors', deals with the priority of depositors' claims, precedence of attachment, payment in lieu of attachment, and powers of the Designated Court. Chapter VI, titled 'Offences and Punishments', specifies the punishments for contravention of various provisions of the Act. Chapter VII, titled 'Investigation, Search and Seizure', deals with the powers of the police to enter, search, and seize without a warrant. Chapter VIII, titled 'Miscellaneous', contains provisions related to the publication of advertisements of Unregulated Deposit Schemes, the Act to have overriding effect, application of other laws not barred, protection of action taken in good faith, power of Central Government to make rules, power of State Government, etc., to make rules, laying of rules, power to amend the First Schedule, act not to apply certain deposits, amendment to certain enactments, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and saving.

The First Schedule to the Act lists various Regulated Deposit Schemes, including those regulated by the Securities and Exchange Board of India, Reserve Bank of India, Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India, State Government, Union territory Government, National Housing Bank, Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority, Employees' Provident Fund Organisation, and Central Registrar, Multi-State Co-operative Societies. The Second Schedule to the Act lists various amendments to certain enactments, including the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, Securities and Exchange Board of India Act, 1992, and Multi-State Co-operative Societies Act, 2002.
