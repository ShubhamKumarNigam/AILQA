 Amendment of article 164.-

In article 164 of the Constitution, in clause (1), after the words "Chief Minister", the words "who shall be elected by the members of the Legislative Assembly of the State from amongst themselves" shall be inserted.


 Amendment of the Fourth Schedule.-

In the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution, in the Table, after entry 15, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"16. Sikkim

Salary of the Chief Minister and other Ministers

Rs. 1,500 per mensem

Rs. 1,200 per mensem"





The Constitution (Twenty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1972, makes two significant changes to the Constitution of India. Firstly, it amends Article 164 to require that the Chief Minister of a State must be elected by the members of the Legislative Assembly of the State from amongst themselves. This ensures that the Chief Minister has the support of the majority of the Assembly members.

Secondly, the Act amends the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution by adding a new entry for Sikkim. This entry specifies the salary of the Chief Minister and other Ministers of Sikkim as Rs. 1,500 and Rs. 1,200 per mensem, respectively. This provision ensures that the elected representatives of Sikkim receive a fixed salary, which helps to maintain the integrity of the democratic process.

In summary, the Constitution (Twenty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1972, makes important changes to the Constitution of India to ensure that the democratic process is maintained and that elected representatives receive a fixed salary. The Act requires that the Chief Minister of a State must be elected by the members of the Legislative Assembly of the State from amongst themselves, and it adds a new entry to the Fourth Schedule of the Constitution for Sikkim, specifying the salary of the Chief Minister and other Ministers.
