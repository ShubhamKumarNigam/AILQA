The Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (Meetings) Regulations, 2012 establish guidelines for the conduct of meetings of the Warehousing Development and Regulatory Authority (Authority), which is a regulatory body established under the Warehousing (Development and Regulation) Act, 2007. These regulations define key terms such as "Act," "Authority," "Chairperson," "Committee," "member," and "Registrar." The Act refers to the Warehousing (Development and Regulation) Act, 2007, which provides the legal framework for the Authority's establishment and functions. The Authority is the regulatory body responsible for the development and regulation of warehousing in India. The Chairperson is the head of the Authority, and Committees are groups formed by the Authority for specific purposes. Members are individuals appointed to serve on the Authority, and the Registrar is an officer responsible for various administrative tasks related to the Authority's meetings. These regulations outline the procedures for calling and conducting meetings, quorum requirements, voting procedures, and the recording and circulation of minutes.
