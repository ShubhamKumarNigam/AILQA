(a) "Act" means the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 (29 of 1986);

(b) "authentic record" means a document containing information about the generation, storage, treatment, transportation, import and export, recycling, recovery or disposal of hazardous and other wastes, which is duly authenticated by the generator, owner or operator, as the case may be;

(c) "Board" means the Central Pollution Control Board constituted under section 3 of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (6 of 1974);

(d) "competent authority" means the State Pollution Control Board or the Pollution Control Committee of the Union territory, as the case may be, constituted under section 4 of the Water (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 (6 of 1974);

(e) "disposal" means the final disposal of hazardous and other wastes by any method, including dumping in land, sea or air, that results in the loss of the wastes or any of its components;

(f) "environment" includes water, air and land and the inter-relationship which exists among and between water, air and land and human beings, other living creatures, plants, micro-organisms and property;

(g) "generator" means any person who produces hazardous and other wastes as specified in the Schedules to these rules;

(h) "handler" means any person who stores, treats, recycles, reuses, transports, imports, exports or disposes of hazardous and other wastes as specified in the Schedules to these rules;

(i) "hazardous waste" means any waste as specified in Column 3 of Schedule I;

(j) "import" means bringing into India from a place outside India;

(k) "inspector" means any person authorized by the Central Government or the State Government or the Central Pollution Control Board or the State Pollution Control Board or the Pollution Control Committee of the Union territory to inspect any premises, vehicle, vessel, aircraft, installation or other place where hazardous and other wastes are generated, treated, stored, transported, imported, exported, recycled, recovered or disposed of or for taking samples of such wastes for the purpose of examination, analysis or testing;

(l) "occupier" means every person who has control over and is responsible for the conduct of any industry, operation, or process or for the condition or maintenance of any premises or for the disposal of any waste;

(m) "owner" means a person who owns, holds or enjoys any premises or has the control over the affairs of the premises;

(n) "prescribed" means prescribed by rules made under the Act;

(o) "recycling" means any operation the primary purpose of which is to recover a waste and to reuse that waste without any other pre-processing, except for the removal of impurities;

(p) "recovery" means any operation the primary purpose of which is to extract or remove valuable components from waste, which can be subsequently reused without any other pre-processing;

(q) "Schedule" means a Schedule appended to these rules;

(r) "Schedule I" means the Schedule appended to these rules and relating to hazardous wastes;

(s) "Schedule II" means the Schedule appended to these rules and relating to other wastes;

(t) "State Government" means the Government of any State of the Union of India;

(u) "storage" means the holding of hazardous and other wastes for a temporary period, pending their treatment, recycling, reuse, recovery, disposal or export;

(v) "treatment" means any method, technique or process, including neutralization, designed to change the physical, chemical or biological character or composition of any hazardous and other wastes to render it safer for transport, storage, recycling, reuse, recovery, disposal or export or for reducing the volume of such wastes;

(w) "transboundary movement" means any movement of hazardous and other wastes from an area under the national jurisdiction of one country to or through an area under the national jurisdiction of another country or to or through the high seas;

(x) "transport" means the movement of hazardous and other wastes by road, rail, sea, air or pipeline from one place to another;

(y) "vehicle" means any conveyance used for the transport of hazardous and other wastes by road or rail;

(z) "waste" means any substance, whether gaseous, liquid or solid, which is discharged, discarded or abandoned by the person generating it, and is not covered under the categories mentioned in clauses (a) to (e) of sub-rule (1) of rule 2.


**Summary:**

The Hazardous and Other Wastes (Management and Transboundary Movement) Rules, 2016, is a set of rules that govern the management of hazardous and other wastes in India. These rules apply to the management of hazardous and other wastes as specified in the Schedules to these rules, but do not apply to waste-water and exhaust gases, wastes arising out of the operation from ships beyond five kilometers of the relevant baseline, radio-active wastes, bio-medical wastes, and municipal solid wastes.

The rules define various terms related to the management of hazardous and other wastes, such as "authentic record," "competent authority," "disposal," "environment," "generator," "handler," "hazardous waste," "import," "inspector," "occupier," "owner," "prescribed," "recycling," "recovery," "Schedule," "Schedule I," "Schedule II," "State Government," "storage," "treatment," "transboundary movement," "transport," "vehicle," and "waste."

Under these rules, the generator of hazardous and other wastes is responsible for ensuring that such wastes are managed in an environmentally sound manner. The handler of hazardous and other wastes is responsible for storing, treating, recycling, reusing, transporting, importing, exporting, or disposing of such wastes in accordance with the rules. The competent authority is responsible for implementing and enforcing the rules. The inspector is authorized to inspect any premises, vehicle, vessel, aircraft, installation, or other place where hazardous and other wastes are generated, treated, stored, transported, imported, exported, recycled, recovered, or disposed of.
