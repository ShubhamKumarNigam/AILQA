Summary of THE LIFE INSURANCE CORPORATION (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1957:
The Life Insurance Corporation (Amendment) Act, 1957, is an amendment to the Life Insurance Corporation Act of 1956. The Act consists of seven sections, with the first section defining the short title as the Life Insurance Corporation (Amendment) Act, 1957.

Section 2, which has been repealed, originally amended section 11 of the principal Act. However, this amendment was repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act (58 of 1960) on December 26, 1960.

Similarly, sections 3, 4, and 5 have also been repealed. Section 3 originally amended section 43 of the principal Act, while section 4 substituted a new section for section 45. Section 5 amended section 49 of the principal Act. These amendments were all repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act (58 of 1960) on December 26, 1986.

Section 6 of the Act validates orders passed before the commencement of the Act that alter the remuneration and other terms and conditions of service of employees referred to in sub-section (2) of section 11 of the principal Act. These orders are deemed to have been made under the amended sub-section (2) of section 11 as if the amendment was in force on the date the order was intended to take effect.

Finally, section 7, which repealed the entire Act, was also repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act (58 of 1960) on December 26, 1960.
