Summary:

THE WAREHOUSING CORPORATIONS ACT, 1962 is an Act to provide for the incorporation and regulation of corporations for the purpose of warehousing of agricultural produce and certain other commodities. The Act establishes the Central Warehousing Corporation and State Warehousing Corporations, which are bodies corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal. The Act also provides for the management and administration of these corporations, including the appointment of directors, officers, and employees, and the maintenance of accounts and audit. The Act also grants borrowing powers to the corporations and provides for the investment of their funds. The Act also specifies the rights and duties of shareholders, and the resolution of disputes between the Central Warehousing Corporation and State Warehousing Corporations. The Act also includes provisions for the protection of the corporations and their officers and employees, and for the punishment of offences. The Act also includes provisions for the repeal and savings of the Agricultural Produce (Development and Warehousing) Corporations Act, 1956. The Act also empowers the appropriate Government to make rules and the Warehousing Corporations to make regulations for the purpose of giving effect to the provisions of this Act.
