Summary:

The Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission for the UT of Jammu & Kashmir and the UT of Ladakh (Electricity Trading) Regulations, 2023, have been established under the Electricity Act, 2003. These regulations outline the eligibility criteria for obtaining a trading license, the duties of an electricity trader, and other related matters. The regulations define key terms and provide capital adequacy and creditworthiness requirements for applicants. The eligibility conditions for being an electricity trader include a minimum net worth based on the volume of electricity proposed to be traded. The regulations also outline the procedure for granting a license for trading, including the application process, document requirements, and the Commission's decision-making process. The regulations also specify the trading margin for various transactions undertaken by the Trading Licensee. The trader is required to maintain separate accounts, submit accounting statements, and comply with the orders and directions of the State Load Dispatch Centre, State Transmission Utility, and Central Electricity Authority. The regulations also provide for the revocation of a license and the power to remove difficulties and amend the regulations.
