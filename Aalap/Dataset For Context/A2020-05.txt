Summary:

THE CENTRAL SANSKRIT UNIVERSITIES ACT, 2020

Act Title: THE CENTRAL SANSKRIT UNIVERSITIES ACT, 2020
Act ID: ACT NO. 5 OF 2020
Enactment Date: [25th March, 2020.]

This Act establishes and incorporates three Central Universities, namely, the Central Sanskrit University, Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri National Sanskrit University, and the National Sanskrit University, for teaching and research in Sanskrit and related subjects. The Act defines the powers and functions of the authorities of the Universities, including the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Deans of School of Studies, Registrar, Finance Officer, Controller of Examinations, and Librarian. It also sets out the procedures for the appointment, removal, and terms of office of these authorities.

The Act lays down the objects of the Universities, which include the dissemination and advancement of knowledge through instructional, research, and extension facilities, the promotion of Sanskrit education, and the education and training of manpower for the overall development, promotion, preservation, and research in the field of Sanskrit and Sanskrit traditional subjects.

The Act empowers the Universities to grant degrees, diplomas, and other academic distinctions, to provide facilities for education through distance education, to establish and maintain colleges, institutions, schools of studies, and departments, and to institute fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals, and prizes. It also provides for the appointment of teachers, academic staff, and other employees of the Universities, their emoluments and conditions of service, and the principles governing seniority of service and disciplinary actions.

The Act sets out the powers and functions of the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Board of Studies, the Finance Committee, and the Planning and Monitoring Board, which are the authorities of the Universities. It also provides for the appointment, removal, and terms of office of the members of these authorities.

The Act provides for the maintenance of discipline among the employees and students of the Universities, and sets out the procedure for appeal and arbitration in disciplinary cases against students. It also provides for the right to appeal of every employee or student of the Universities against the decision of any officer or authority of the Universities, and the conditions and time limits for such appeals.

The Act provides for the constitution of councils, boards, standing committees, and cells of the Universities, and sets out the procedure for the appointment of committees.

The First Schedule to the Act lists the names of the states and the campuses of the Central Sanskrit University, Shri Lal Bahadur Shastri National Sanskrit University, and the National Sanskrit University. The Second Schedule to the Act contains the Statutes of the University, which include the provisions regarding the appointment, removal, and terms of office of the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Deans of School of Studies, Registrar, Finance Officer, Controller of Examinations, and Librarian, and the powers and functions of these authorities. The Second Schedule also contains the provisions regarding the appointment, removal, and terms of office of the members of the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Board of Studies, the Finance Committee, and the Planning and Monitoring Board, and the powers and functions of these authorities.

The Act provides for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act, and for the publication and laying of the Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations before Parliament. The Act also provides for the transitional provisions regarding the continuation of the officers and employees of the deemed to be Universities, the migration of students admitted in any programme or course in a deemed to be University, and the continuation of the regulations, bye-laws, or orders made prior to the commencement of this Act.
