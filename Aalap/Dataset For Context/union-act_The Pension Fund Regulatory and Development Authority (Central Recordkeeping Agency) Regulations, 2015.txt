Summary:

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (Central Recordkeeping Agency) Regulations, 2015 sets standards for the eligibility, governance, organization and operational conduct of the Central Recordkeeping Agency. The regulations aim to provide centralized recordkeeping, administration and customer service functions to all subscribers of the National Pension System or any other pension scheme referred to in clause(b) of sub-section (1) of section 12 of the Act. The regulations define various terms used in the document, such as Act, auditor, certificate, company, compliance officer, Drawing and Disbursing Officer, I-Pin, Key Stakeholder or Stakeholder, National Pension System data, nodal office, Pay and Accounts Office, PRAN, principal officer, Service Level Agreement, Third Party Systems, and T-Pin. The regulations outline the eligibility criteria for an applicant seeking to obtain a certificate of registration as a central recordkeeping agency, including net profit and cash loss requirements, and information technology capabilities. The regulations also specify the disclosure of information, verification of information, consideration of application, and conditions of certificate of registration. The regulations also provide for the effect of refusal to grant certificate or expiry of certificate, registration fee, annual fee, commencement of operations, tenure and exit of central recordkeeping agency, extension of registration, process of transfer of assets in case of termination or surrender of certificate of registration, roles and responsibilities of central recordkeeping agency, service to stakeholders or intermediaries, service levels for central recordkeeping agency, service fees or charges-charges, exit management plan, redressal of subscriber grievances, separation of National Pension System accounts and activities from other businesses, inspection and audit, notice before inspection or audit, obligations on inspection and audit, submission of report, payment of inspection and audit fees, inspection and audit by National Pension System Trust, cancellation or suspension of certificate of registration, surrender of any certificate of registration, effect of suspension, cancellation or surrender of certificate, appointment of designated authority, issuance of notice, reply by noticee, action in case of default, procedure for action on recommendation, intimation of order, penalty and adjudication, appeals to Securities Appellate Tribunal, confidentiality, management by administrator, conflict of interest, compliance and corporate governance, and appointment of Compliance officer. The regulations also provide for the power of Authority to issue clarifications.
