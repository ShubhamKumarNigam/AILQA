Summary:
The Andhra Pradesh Legislative Council Act, 2005, establishes a Legislative Council for the State of Andhra Pradesh. This Act was enacted on January 11, 2006. The Council will have 90 seats, with 31 seats filled by persons elected by the electorates in sub-clauses (a), (b), and (c) of clause (3) of article 171, 31 seats filled by persons elected by the members of the Legislative Assembly of Andhra Pradesh, and 12 seats filled by persons nominated by the Governor of Andhra Pradesh. The President, after consultation with the Election Commission, will determine the constituencies, their extent, and the number of seats allotted to each constituency.

The Act also amends the Representation of the People Act, 1950, by inserting a new entry for Andhra Pradesh in the Third Schedule, before the entry for Bihar, with 90 seats, 31 seats for municipal corporations, 8 seats for municipalities, 8 seats for nagar panchayats, 31 seats for cantonment boards, 12 seats for zila parishads, and 12 seats for mandal parishads. Additionally, the Act inserts a new heading for Andhra Pradesh before the heading for Bihar in the Fourth Schedule of the Representation of the People Act, 1950.

Furthermore, the Act amends section 15A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, by substituting the words and figures “under the Andhra Pradesh Legislative Council Act, 2005” for the words and figures “under the Legislative Councils Act, 1957”. The Legislative Council for Andhra Pradesh came into existence on March 30, 2007.
