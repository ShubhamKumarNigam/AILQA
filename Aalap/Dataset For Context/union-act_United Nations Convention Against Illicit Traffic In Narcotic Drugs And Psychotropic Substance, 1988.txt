United Nations Convention Against Illicit Traffic In Narcotic Drugs And Psychotropic Substance, 1988 

 The offences established in accordance with this article shall include:(a)Participation in an illicit traffic;(b)Attempt to commit such an offence;(c)Conspiracy to commit such an offence;(d)Aiding, abetting, counselling or procuring the commission of such an offence;(e)The use of a postal service, a telecommunication service, or any means of communication, which is send or transmitted in any manner and by any means whatsoever, to commit an offence established in accordance with this article;(f)The manufacture, transportation, or distribution of equipment, instruments, or software, knowing that they are to be used for the unlawful production, processing, preparation, or distribution of narcotic drugs or psychotropic substances.
