Summary:

THE METRO RAILWAYS (CONSTRUCTION OF WORKS) AMENDMENT ACT, 1982 is an amendment act to the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978. This act came into force on the date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette. The act consists of 17 sections, all of which have been repealed and replaced by the Repealing and Amending Act (19 of 1988) with effect from 31st March 1988.

The amendments made by this act include the substitution of the term "arbitration" with "appellate authority" and the insertion of new sections such as sections 15A, 16A, 16B, and 16C. The act also amended various sections of the principal act, including sections 2, 9, 11, 13, 14, 16, 19, 21, 22, 25, 36, and the Schedule.

Furthermore, the act includes a provision for the transfer of applications pending before an arbitrator under the principal act to the appellate authority. This transfer applies to applications that were pending before an arbitrator at the time of the commencement of this act. The appellate authority may deal with such applications as if they were appeals pending with them, and either party to the case may demand that the previous proceedings be reopened or that they be re-heard.

In summary, THE METRO RAILWAYS (CONSTRUCTION OF WORKS) AMENDMENT ACT, 1982 made significant changes to the Metro Railways (Construction of Works) Act, 1978, by repealing and replacing various sections and inserting new sections. The act also included provisions for the transfer of applications pending before an arbitrator to the appellate authority. These changes came into effect on the date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
