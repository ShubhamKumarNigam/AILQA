Summary:
The High Court at Patna (Establishment of a Permanent Bench at Ranchi) Act, 1976 was enacted on April 8, 1976. This Act provides for the establishment of a permanent bench of the High Court at Patna at Ranchi. The Chief Justice of the High Court at Patna may nominate not less than three judges to sit at Ranchi to exercise the jurisdiction and power vested in that High Court for cases arising in the districts of Hazaribagh, Giridih, Dhanbad, Ranchi, Palamau, and Singhbhum. The Chief Justice may also order that any case or class of cases arising in any such district shall be heard at Patna. This Act is known as the High Court at Patna (Establishment of a Permanent Bench at Ranchi) Act, 1976.
