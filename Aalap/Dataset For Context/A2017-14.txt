Summary:

The Union Territory Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, is an Act to make provisions for levy and collection of tax on intra-State supply of goods or services or both by the Union territories. The Act consists of 26 sections divided into 9 chapters. The chapters cover various aspects of the Act, including preliminary provisions, administration, levy and collection of tax, payment of tax, inspection, search, seizure and arrest, demands and recovery, advance ruling, transition provisions, and miscellaneous provisions. The Act also includes a schedule and an annexure. The Act empowers the Administrator to appoint officers for carrying out the purposes of this Act, and authorizes officers to exercise various powers and discharge duties as specified. The Act also provides for the authorization of officers of central tax as proper officers in certain circumstances. The Act imposes a tax on all intra-State supplies of goods or services or both, except on the supply of alcoholic liquor for human consumption, on the value determined under section 15 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, and collected in such manner as may be prescribed. The Act also provides for the power to grant exemption from tax, the payment of tax, transfer of input tax credit, recovery of tax, advance ruling, and transitional provisions. The Act also includes provisions for the removal of difficulties and the laying of rules, regulations, and notifications. The Act also empowers the Commissioner to issue instructions or directions for the purpose of uniformity in the implementation of this Act.
