Summary:
The Aligarh Muslim University Act, 1920 (Act No. 40 of 1920) is an Act to incorporate a teaching and residential Muslim University at Aligarh. The Act was enacted on the 14th of September, 1920, and came into force on such date as the Central Government may appoint. The Act defines the University as the educational institution of their choice established by the Muslims of India, which originated as the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College, Aligarh, and which was subsequently incorporated as the Aligarh Muslim University.

The Act defines various terms used in the Act and the Statutes made thereunder, including the terms "Academic Council", "Board of Studies", "Chancellor", "Pro-Chancellor", "Vice-Chancellor", "Court", "Department", "Executive Council", "Faculty", "Hall", "non-teaching staff", "Statutes", "Ordinances", and "Teachers".

The Act establishes the University as a body corporate, with perpetual succession and a common seal, and vests all properties and rights of the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental College, Aligarh, and the Muslim University Association in the University. The Act also transfers all debts, liabilities, and obligations of the said Societies and Committee to the University and provides that all references in any enactment to either of the said Societies or to the said Committee shall be construed as references to the University.

The Act grants the University the power to provide instruction in such branches of learning as the University may think fit, and to make provision for research and for the advancement and dissemination of knowledge. The University is also empowered to promote Oriental and Islamic studies and give instruction in Muslim theology and religion, promote the study of the religions, civilisation, and culture of India, promote especially the educational and cultural advancement of the Muslims of India, hold examinations and grant diplomas or certificates to, and confer degrees and other academic distinctions on, persons subject to such conditions as the University may determine, and withdraw any such diplomas, certificates, degrees, or other academic distinctions for good and sufficient cause.

The Act also provides for the recognition of degrees, diplomas, and other academic distinctions granted or conferred to or on persons by the University, which shall be recognized by the Central and State Governments as are the corresponding degrees, diplomas, and other academic distinctions granted by any other University incorporated under any enactment.

The Act establishes a reserve fund, which shall consist of a sum of thirty lakhs of rupees as a permanent endowment to meet the recurring charges of the University other than charges in respect of fellowships, scholarships, prizes, and rewards.

The Act provides that the University shall be open to all persons (including the teachers and taught) of either sex and of whatever race, religion, creed, caste, or class, and that every student of the University shall reside either in a Hall or a hostel or under such conditions as may be prescribed by the Ordinances. The Act also provides that all recognized teaching for the purpose of University degrees, diplomas, and certificates shall be conducted under the supervision of the Academic Council and in accordance with the Statutes and Ordinances.

The Act grants the University the power to establish and maintain High Schools within a radius of fifteen miles from the University Mosque and to establish and maintain such Special Centres, Specialised Laboratories, or such other institutions for research or instruction as are necessary for the furtherance of its objects either on its own or in co-operation or collaboration with any other institution.

The Act establishes the Visitor, the Chief Rector, and various officers and authorities of the University, including the Chancellor, the Pro-Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Honorary Treasurer, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, the Deans of the Faculties, and such other officers as may be declared by the Statutes to be officers of the University. The Act also provides for the powers and duties of these officers and authorities.

The Act establishes the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Finance Committee, the Faculties, and such other authorities as may be declared by the Statutes to be authorities of the University. The Act provides for the composition, powers, and functions of these authorities and for the election and appointment of their members.

The Act grants the University the power to make Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations, subject to the provisions of the Act and the Statutes. The Act provides for the making and amending of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations, and for their submission to the Visitor for approval.

The Act provides for the admission and examination of students and for the annual report and accounts of the University. The Act also provides for the filling of casual vacancies, the proceedings of University authorities not to be invalidated by vacancies, the protection of action taken in good faith, the mode of proof of University records, and the annual report and accounts of the University.

The Act includes a Schedule, which sets out the Statutes of the University, and an Appendix, which sets out various forms. The Act also includes various footnotes that provide additional information and context for the provisions of the Act.
