Summary:

THE BRITISH INDIA CORPORATION LIMITED (ACQUISITION OF SHARES) ACT, 1981

The British India Corporation Limited (Acquisition of Shares) Act, 1981, was enacted on September 11, 1981, to acquire certain shares of the British India Corporation Limited. The Act aims to secure the proper management of the Company's affairs and ensure the continuity and development of the production of goods vital to the country's needs.

The Act has three chapters.

Chapter I: Preliminary

This chapter defines the short title and commencement of the Act, as well as the definitions of various terms used throughout the Act. The term "share" is defined to exclude shares held by the Central Government, State Government, State Bank of India, Life Insurance Corporation of India, Unit Trust of India, any corresponding new bank, and any general insurance company nationalized by the General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972.

Chapter II: Acquisition of the Shares of the Company

This chapter outlines the acquisition of the Company's shares by the Central Government, the management of the Company, the payment of amounts to shareholders, and the appointment of a Commissioner of Payments.

Under section 3, all the shares of the Company stand transferred to and vest in the Central Government on the appointed day. The Central Government is deemed to have been registered in the Register of members of the Company as the holder of each share. All trusts, liabilities, obligations, mortgages, charges, liens, and other encumbrances affecting the shares stand freed and discharged.

The Central Government may make provisions (including changes in the Board of Directors and amendments in the memorandum and articles of association) to enable the Company to function as a Government company.

The Central Government shall pay an amount of rupees twenty-two lakhs and sixty thousand to the shareholders of the Company in cash and in the manner specified in section 7. The amount shall carry simple interest at the rate of four percent per annum for the period commencing on the appointed day and ending on the date on which payment of such amount is made by the Central Government to the Commissioner.

The Central Government shall, for the purpose of disbursing the amounts payable to shareholders of the Company, appoint a Commissioner of Payments.

Chapter III: Miscellaneous

This chapter deals with various miscellaneous provisions, including the Act's overriding effect, penalties, offences by companies, limitation of cognizance of offences, power to make rules, and repeal and saving.

Any person who makes any claim for payment under this Act, knowing or having reason to believe that such claim is false or without any basis, shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine which may extend to two thousand rupees, or with both.

Where an offence under this Act has been committed by a company, every person who was in charge of, and responsible to, the company for the conduct of the business of the company, as well as the company, shall be deemed to be guilty of the offence and shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly.

No court shall take cognizance of an offence punishable under this Act, except with the previous sanction of the Central Government or of an officer authorized by that Government in this behalf.

The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act. Every rule made by the Central Government under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament for a total period of thirty days.

The British India Corporation (Acquisition of Shares) Ordinance, 1981, is hereby repealed, and anything done or any action taken under the Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
