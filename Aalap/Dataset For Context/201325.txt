Summary:
THE PROHIBITION OF EMPLOYMENT AS MANUAL SCAVENGERS AND THEIR REHABILITATION ACT, 2013 (ACT NO. 25 OF 2013) aims to prohibit the employment of manual scavengers and rehabilitate them to a life of dignity. The Act defines "manual scavenger" as a person engaged or employed, at the commencement of this Act or at any time thereafter, by an individual or a local authority or an agency or a contractor, for manually cleaning, carrying, disposing of, or otherwise handling in any manner, human excreta in an insanitary latrine or in an open drain or pit into which the excreta is discharged or flushed out, or on a railway track or in such other spaces or premises, as the Central Government or a State Government may notify, before the excreta fully decomposes in such manner as may be prescribed. The Act prohibits the construction of insanitary latrines and the employment of manual scavengers. It also provides for the rehabilitation of manual scavengers, including the provision of initial, one-time cash assistance, scholarships for their children, residential plots or houses, skill training, and subsidies and concessional loans for taking up alternative occupations. The Act also imposes penalties for contravention of its provisions, with higher penalties for subsequent contraventions. The Act applies to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir, and came into force on 1st October, 2013.
