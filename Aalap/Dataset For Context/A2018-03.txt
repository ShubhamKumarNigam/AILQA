Summary:

THE INDIAN INSTITUTE OF PETROLEUM AND ENERGY ACT, 2017 is an Act to declare the institution known as the Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy as an institution of national importance and to provide for its incorporation and related matters. The Act consists of 45 sections and a schedule. The main provisions of the Act are as follows:

- The Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy, Vishakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, an institution registered under the Andhra Pradesh Societies Registration Act, 2001, is declared as an institution of national importance (Section 2).
- The Institute is incorporated as a body corporate having perpetual succession and a common seal with power to acquire, hold and dispose of property and to contract, and shall, by that name, sue and be sued (Section 4).
- A Board of Governors is constituted for the purposes of this Act, consisting of the President, the Director, two persons from the Board of Directors of the promoting companies, one Professor of the Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, five eminent experts in the field of petroleum technology, renewable and non-renewable energy covering the entire hydrocarbon value chain having specialised knowledge or operational experience in respect of education, research, engineering and technology, two Professors of the Institute, and one representative of the graduates of the Institute (Section 5).
- The term of office of the members of the Board, other than ex officio members, is three years (Section 6).
- On and from the appointed day, all properties which had vested in the Society immediately before that day shall vest in the Institute (Section 7).
- The Institute shall perform various functions, including nurturing and promoting quality and excellence in education and research in the area of petroleum and hydrocarbons and energy, providing for programmes and courses of instruction and research leading to the award of degrees, diplomas, certificates or other academic distinctions, granting degrees, diplomas, certificates or other academic distinctions to candidates who have attained the prescribed standard of proficiency, fostering close educational and research interaction with national, regional and international players in the oil, gas and petrochemical industry and the energy sector, co-operating with educational and research institutions in any part of the world, organizing national and international symposia, seminars and conferences in the area of petroleum and hydrocarbons and energy, establishing, maintaining and managing halls, residences and hostels for students, supervising, controlling and regulating the discipline of all categories of employees of the Institute, and framing Statutes and to alter, modify or rescind the same (Section 9).
- The Board shall be responsible for the general superintendence, direction and control of the affairs of the Institute and shall exercise all the powers not otherwise provided for by this Act, the Statutes and the Ordinances, and shall have the power to review the acts of the Senate (Section 10).
- The General Council shall be constituted, for the purposes of this Act, consisting of the Secretary, Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas in the Central Government, ex officio, who shall be the Chairperson, the Chairman, Indian Oil Corporation Limited, ex officio, the Chairman and Managing Director, Hindustan Petroleum Corporation Limited, ex officio, the Chairman and Managing Director, Oil and Natural Gas Corporation, ex officio, the Chairman and Managing Director, Gas Authority of India Limited, ex officio, the Director General of Hydrocarbons, ex officio, the Principal Advisor (Energy), NITI Aayog, ex officio, the Executive Director, Oil Industry Safety Directorate, ex officio, the Director, Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, ex officio, the Director, Indian Institute of Chemical Technology, Hyderabad, ex officio, the Secretary, Oil Industry Development Board, ex officio, the President of the Board, ex officio, the Director of the Institute, ex officio, and persons, not less than two but not exceeding four, representing the private entities in the field of petroleum sector operating in the country, to be nominated by the Chairperson (Section 15).
- The Senate shall be the principal academic body of the Institute, and its composition shall be such as may be provided by the Statutes (Section 17).
- The Institute shall maintain a fund to which shall be credited all moneys provided by the Central Government, all fees and other charges, all moneys received by the Institute by way of grants, gifts, donations, benefactions, bequests or transfers, and all moneys received by the Institute in any other manner or from any other source (Section 24).
- The Institute may, for the purpose of enabling it to discharge its functions efficiently under this Act, receive grants from the Central Government (Section 23).
- The Institute may, after due appropriation made by Parliament by law in this behalf, pay to the Institute such sums of money and in such manner as it may think fit (Section 23).
- The Comptroller and Auditor-General of India shall audit the accounts of the Institute (Section 27).
- The annual accounts of the Institute, including the balance-sheet, shall be forwarded to the Central Government (Section 27).
- The annual report of the Institute shall be laid before both Houses of Parliament within one month of its receipt (Section 28).
- The Institute may, for the benefit of its employees, constitute for the benefit of its employees, such pension, insurance and provident fund scheme as it deems fit, in such manner and subject to such conditions as may be provided by the Statutes (Section 29).
- The Institute may, with the previous approval of the Central Government, borrow money for the purposes of the Institute with or without security of the property of the Institute (Section 10).
- The Institute may, notwithstanding anything contained in section 24, set-up an endowment fund and any other fund for a specified purpose (Section 25).
- The Institute shall prepare, in such form and at such time every year, a budget in respect of the financial year next ensuing, showing the estimated receipts and expenditure of the Institute (Section 26).
- The Institute shall maintain proper accounts and other relevant records and prepare an annual statement of accounts, including the balance-sheet, in such form as may be prescribed by the Central Government in consultation with the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India (Section 27).
- The Institute shall have power to affiliate itself with any other university or institution in or outside India (Section 44).
- The Act shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint, and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Act (Section 1).

The Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy Act, 2017 provides for the incorporation of the Indian Institute of Petroleum and Energy as an institution of national importance and lays down its functions, powers and duties. The Act also provides for the constitution of a Board of Governors and a General Council, and the composition and functions of the Senate. The Act also provides for the maintenance of accounts and audit by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India, and the laying of the annual report before Parliament. The Act also empowers the Institute to borrow money and set-up an endowment fund and any other fund for a specified purpose. The Act also provides for the affiliation of the Institute with any other university or institution in or outside India. The Act came into force on the date notified by the Central Government in the Official Gazette.
