Summary:
The Departmentalisation of Union Accounts (Transfer of Personnel) Act, 1976, is a law that enables the transfer of officers and employees from the Indian Audit and Accounts Department to any Ministry, Department, or office of the Central Government. The Act aims to facilitate the efficient discharge of the responsibility of compiling accounts by the Ministry, Department, or office to which the officers or employees are transferred.
The Act came into force on March 1, 1976, and applies to officers and employees who are transferred from the Indian Audit and Accounts Department to any Ministry, Department, or office of the Central Government. The transfer can only be made by order, and the Central Government may establish one or more Advisory Committees to assist it in regard to the transfer of officers and employees.
An officer or employee transferred to any Ministry, Department, or office of the Central Government shall cease to be an officer or employee in the Indian Audit and Accounts Department and shall hold office in such Ministry, Department, or office with such designation as the Central Government may specify. The transferred officer or employee shall be entitled to be appointed to a post carrying a scale of pay not less favourable than that of the post which he held immediately before the date of such transfer and in the same capacity in which he held the post last mentioned.
The Act also provides for the protection of pay for the unexpired period of any leave vacancy or any other vacancy of a specified duration in which an officer or employee was officiating immediately before the date of transfer.
The Departmentalisation of Union Accounts (Transfer of Personnel) Ordinance, 1976, is repealed by this Act, but anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
