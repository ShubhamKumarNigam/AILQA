In article 317 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "sixty years", the words "sixty-two years" shall be substituted. [The Constitution (Forty-First Amendment) Act, 1976, changed the retirement age of the Chairman and members of the Union Public Service Commission from 60 to 62 years, by amending Article 317(1) of the Constitution. However, it was later amended to 58 for employees of the All-India Services, the Central Government, and many State governments. Consequently, they are not interested in joining the Commission because they will only have two years to serve, which is seen as undesirable for the efficient functioning of the Commission. Insertion of new article 320A.-

After article 320 of the Constitution, the following article shall be inserted, namely:-

"320A. (1) The Union and every State shall establish a Public Service Commission for making recruitment to services and posts under the Union and the State respectively.

(2) Subject to the provisions of clause (3), a Public Service Commission shall be consulted in relation to -

(a) the appointment of persons to services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State;

(b) the posting, promotion and transfer of persons serving under the Government of India or of any State;

(c) the making of appointments by promotion and by transfer to services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State;

(d) the carrying out of periodic and other reviews of the recruitment, promotion and transfer service rules relating to services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State; and

(e) the disciplinary matters affecting persons serving under the Government of India or of any State in relation to the services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State, as the case may be.

(3) Nothing in clause (2) shall require a Public Service Commission to be consulted -

(a) in relation to the appointment of persons to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply; or

(b) in relation to the posting, promotion and transfer of persons to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply and which have been declared to be excluded from the operation of this article for a period not exceeding five years by the President or, as the case may be, the Governor of the State, by notification in the Official Gazette; or

(c) in relation to the making of appointments by promotion and by transfer to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply and which have been declared to be excluded from the operation of this article for a period not exceeding five years by the President or, as the case may be, the Governor of the State, by notification in the Official Gazette; or

(d) in relation to the carrying out of periodic and other reviews of the recruitment, promotion and transfer service rules relating to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply and which have been declared to be excluded from the operation of this article for a period not exceeding three years by the President or, as the case may be, the Governor of the State, by notification in the Official Gazette; or

(e) in relation to the disciplinary matters affecting persons appointed to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply; or

(f) in relation to any class or category of services or posts to which the provisions of article 312 do not apply where the President or the Governor of the State is of opinion that it is not necessary to consult the Public Service Commission in relation to the matters aforesaid.

(4) Nothing in this article shall apply to -

(a) the appointment of the Chairman or members of a Public Service Commission;

(b) the removal of the Chairman or any member of a Public Service Commission under clause (1) of article 317; or

(c) the remuneration and terms and conditions of service of the Chairman and members of a Public Service Commission.

(5) The President may by order define the matters in which, and the circumstances under which, other authorities may be consulted in relation to the appointments, postings, promotions and transfers of persons serving under the Government of India or of any State in respect of services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State, as the case may be, instead of the Union Public Service Commission or the State Public Service Commission, as the case may be." [The Constitution (Forty-First Amendment) Act, 1976, inserted a new article 320A in the Constitution, which provides for the establishment of a Public Service Commission for making recruitment to services and posts under the Union and the State respectively. The Commission shall be consulted in relation to the appointment, posting, promotion, transfer, and disciplinary matters affecting persons serving under the Government of India or of any State in relation to the services and posts in connection with the affairs of the Union or of any State. However, the President may define the matters in which, and the circumstances under which, other authorities may be consulted in relation to the appointments, postings, promotions, and transfers of persons serving under the Government of India or of any State, instead of the Union Public Service Commission or the State Public Service Commission. Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

The Ninth Schedule to the Constitution shall stand amended in accordance with the Schedule annexed to this Act. [The Constitution (Forty-First Amendment) Act, 1976, amended the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, which contains various laws that are exempted from the purview of judicial review. The Act added several laws to the Ninth Schedule, including the Nationalization Acts, the Banking Companies (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1970, and the Sick Textile Undertakings (Nationalization) Act, 1974. These laws were added to the Ninth Schedule to protect them from being challenged in courts on the ground of violation of fundamental rights. However, the Supreme Court has held that laws added to the Ninth Schedule after April 24, 1973, are not immune from judicial review. Also Refer]


Short title and commencement.-

(1) This Act may be called the Constitution (Forty-first Amendment) Act, 1976.

(2) It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 3rd day of May, 1976.
