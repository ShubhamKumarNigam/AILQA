Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

In the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, after entry 82, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"83. The Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1963 (Kerala Act 18 of 1963)."


Explanation.-For the purposes of this Act, the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1963 (Kerala Act 18 of 1963) shall be deemed to have been enacted on the 25th day of January, 1980.


This is a summary of the Constitution (Forty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1980. The Act, which came into force on January 25, 1980, amends the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution of India. Specifically, it adds the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1963 (Kerala Act 18 of 1963) to the Ninth Schedule. This means that the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1963 is now protected from judicial review, as are all other entries in the Ninth Schedule. The Act also specifies that the Kerala Land Reforms (Amendment) Act, 1963 shall be deemed to have been enacted on January 25, 1980 for the purposes of this Act.
