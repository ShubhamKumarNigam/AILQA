Summary:

THE INTEREST ACT, 1978

Title: The Interest Act, 1978
Act ID: ACT NO. 14 OF 1978
Enactment Date: 31st March, 1978

This Act aims to consolidate and amend the law relating to the allowance of interest in certain cases. The following sections outline the key provisions of the Act:

Section 1:
- The Act can be called the Interest Act, 1978.
- It extends to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir (now applicable as per Notification No. S.O. 3912 (E), dated 30th October, 2019).
- The Act will come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.

Section 2:
- Definitions of certain terms used in the Act are provided.
- "Court" includes a tribunal and an arbitrator.
- "Current rate of interest" refers to the highest of the maximum rates at which interest may be paid on different classes of deposits by different classes of scheduled banks in accordance with the directions given by the Reserve Bank of India under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

Section 3:
- The court has the power to allow interest in any debt or damages proceedings.
- The interest rate should not exceed the current rate of interest.
- The court may allow interest for the whole or part of the period from the date when the debt is payable or from the date mentioned in a written notice, to the date of institution of the proceedings.

Section 4:
- Interest is payable in all cases where it is payable by virtue of any enactment or other rule of law or usage having the force of law.
- The court shall allow interest from the date specified in cases like security deposit, fiduciary relationship, fraud, or claim for dower or maintenance.

Section 5:
- Nothing in this Act shall affect the provisions of section 34 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908.

Section 6:
- The Interest Act, 1839, and any other corresponding law in force in any State before the commencement of this Act stand repealed.
- The provisions of this Act shall not apply to any suit or other legal proceeding pending at the commencement of this Act, and the provisions of the corresponding law applicable immediately before such commencement shall continue to apply.

Note: The Act has been made applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh as per Notification No. S.O. 3912 (E), dated 30th October, 2019.
