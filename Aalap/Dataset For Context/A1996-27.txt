THE BUILDING AND OTHER CONSTRUCTION WORKERS (REGULATION OF EMPLOYMENT AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE) ACT, 1996

The Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 is an Act to regulate the employment and conditions of service of building and other construction workers and to provide for their safety, health and welfare measures and for other matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act applies to every establishment which employs, or had employed on any day of the preceding twelve months, ten or more building workers in any building or other construction work.

The Act contains 64 sections and 5 chapters, which cover various aspects of the employment and conditions of service of building and other construction workers. The chapters are as follows:

Chapter I - Preliminary
Chapter II - The Advisory Committees and Expert Committees
Chapter III - Registration of Establishments
Chapter IV - Registration of Building Workers as Beneficiaries
Chapter V - Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Boards
Chapter VI - Hours of Work, Welfare Measures and Other Conditions of Service of Building Workers
Chapter VII - Safety and Health Measures
Chapter VIII - Inspecting Staff
Chapter IX - Special Provisions
Chapter X - Penalties and Procedure
Chapter XI - Miscellaneous

Some of the key provisions of the Act include the registration of establishments, registration of building workers as beneficiaries, the constitution of Building and Other Construction Workers' Welfare Boards, the powers and duties of Inspectors, and the penalties for contravention of the provisions of the Act.

The Act also includes provisions for the safety and health of building workers, such as the requirement for employers to provide drinking water, latrines and urinals, accommodation, creches, first-aid facilities, and canteens. The Act also lays down the responsibilities of employers, including the responsibility for providing constant and adequate supervision of any building or other construction work in their establishment and the responsibility for payment of wages and compensation.

The Act also includes provisions for the recovery of penalty, offences by companies, cognizance of offences, limitation of prosecutions, delegation of powers, returns, application of the Workmen's Compensation Act, 1923 to building workers, protection of action taken in good faith, power of Central Government to give directions, power to remove difficulties, and power to make rules.

In summary, the Building and Other Construction Workers (Regulation of Employment and Conditions of Service) Act, 1996 is an important piece of legislation that aims to regulate the employment and conditions of service of building and other construction workers and to provide for their safety, health and welfare measures. The Act contains various provisions to ensure the welfare and protection of building and other construction workers and to promote fair and decent working conditions in the construction industry.
