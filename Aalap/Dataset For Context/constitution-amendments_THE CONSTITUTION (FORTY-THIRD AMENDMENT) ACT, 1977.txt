
Amendment of article 31D.-

Article 31D of the Constitution is hereby omitted.



Amendment of article 145.-

In article 145 of the Constitution, in clause (3), the words "or of article 32A" shall be omitted.



Amendment of article 228.-

In article 228 of the Constitution, for clause (2), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:-

"(2) The jurisdiction under clause (1) shall be exercised by a Judge in Chambers or by a Special Bench of the High Court."



Amendment of article 366.-

In article 366 of the Constitution, in clause (21), the words "or article 32A" shall be omitted.



Repeal of certain articles.-

Articles 32A, 131A, 144A, 226A and 228A of the Constitution are hereby repealed.



Saving.-

Nothing in this Act shall affect the jurisdiction exercised by any court or the punishment or penalty imposed on any person before the commencement of this Act, and every such jurisdiction, punishment or penalty shall continue to be exercised, imposed and executed as if this Act had not been passed.

Explanation.-

In this section, "commencement of this Act" means the date on which clause (a) of section 2 of the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976 comes into force.



Transitional provisions.-

(1) The provisions of article 144A of the Constitution, as in force immediately before the commencement of this Act, shall continue to apply to any case pending before any court or tribunal immediately before such commencement as if this Act had not been passed.

(2) The provisions of articles 32A, 131A, 226A and 228A of the Constitution, as in force immediately before the commencement of this Act, shall continue to apply to any case pending before any court or tribunal immediately before such commencement as if this Act had not been passed.

(3) The provisions of article 228 of the Constitution, as amended by this Act, shall not apply to any case pending before a court or tribunal immediately before the commencement of this Act, and the provisions of article 228 of the Constitution, as in force immediately before such commencement, shall continue to apply to such case as if this Act had not been passed.



Consequential amendments.-

(1) The provisions of section 6 of the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, shall have effect as if the repeal effected by section 5 of this Act had not taken effect.

(2) The provisions of section 10 of the Constitution (Forty-second Amendment) Act, 1976, shall have effect as if the repeal effected by section 5 of this Act had not taken effect.
