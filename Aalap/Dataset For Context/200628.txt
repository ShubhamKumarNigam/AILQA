Summary:
The National Institute of Fashion Technology Act, 2006, establishes and incorporates the National Institute of Fashion Technology for the promotion and development of education and research in fashion technology. The Act defines key terms such as "Institute," "Institute Campus," "Senate," and "Society." The Institute will consist of a Board of Governors, which includes representatives from the government, academia, and industry. The Board is responsible for the general superintendence, direction, and control of the affairs of the Institute, and has the power to review the acts of the Senate. The Institute will have the power to grant degrees, diplomas, certificates, and other academic distinctions, which will be equivalent to those granted by any University or Institute established or incorporated under any other law. The Act also includes provisions for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act, and transitional provisions for the continuation of the Board of Governors of the Society until a new Board is constituted for the Institute under this Act. The Act also includes provisions for the making of Statutes and Ordinances, which will be published in the Official Gazette and laid before Parliament.
