
The Cold Storage Order, 1980, is a legal document that regulates the construction, operation, and licensing of cold storage facilities in India. It establishes the powers and duties of the Licensing Officer, who is responsible for granting permission to construct or expand cold storage facilities, issuing licenses to operate them, and ensuring compliance with the Order's provisions.

The Order defines key terms such as "cold storage," "cold storage receipt," "foodstuffs," "form," "hirer," "license," "licensing officer," "notified order," and "schedule." It also lays down the factors to be considered in granting or refusing permission to construct or expand a cold storage or in granting or refusing a license to operate one.

The Order prohibits the construction of new cold storage facilities or the expansion of existing ones without prior permission from the Licensing Officer. It also prohibits the operation of a cold storage or expanded cold storage without a license, except in certain cases where the Licensing Officer may grant an exemption.

The Order specifies the fees to be paid for permission to construct or expand a cold storage and for a license to operate one. It also sets out the terms and conditions of the license, which include compliance with the refrigeration, electrical, insulation, sanitary, and safety conditions specified in the Schedule.

The Order provides for the relaxation of conditions of the license in certain cases, the cancellation of a license for specified reasons, and the non-entitlement of the licensee to compensation or refund of the license fee in case of cancellation. It also specifies the period of validity of the license and the procedure for its renewal.

The Order lays down the provisions regarding the storage of foodstuffs, including the maximum charges that a licensee may charge for storing any foodstuff in cold storage or for any other service connected therewith. It also specifies the records to be maintained by the licensee and the returns and statements to be submitted to the Licensing Officer.

The Order empowers the Licensing Officer or any other officer authorized by him to inspect the cold storage premises, machinery, equipment, foodstuffs, stored therein, books, records, and accounts at any time. It also authorizes the Licensing Officer to call for information, enter and search any premises, and prohibit the storage or disposal of any foodstuffs in respect of which he has reason to believe that any contravention of this Order has taken place.

Finally, the Order provides for an appeal to the Central Government against the order of the Licensing Officer and repeals the Cold Storage Order, 1964, except in respect of things done or omitted to be done thereunder before such repeal.
