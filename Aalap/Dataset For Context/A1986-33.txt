Summary:

THE MERCHANT SHIPPING (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986, with the Act ID ACT NO. 33 OF 1986, was enacted on August 14, 1986. This Act aims to further amend the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958.

The Act contains several sections, but some of them have been repealed. Section 80 and 86, which were amended, have been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 2001 (30 of 2001), with effect from September 3, 2001. Similarly, Section 87A, which was also amended, has also been repealed by the same Act.

The Act also contains a saving section, i.e., Section 5, which clarifies that the amendments made in the principal Act by this Act shall not apply to, or in relation to, any certificate of service granted under section 80 or recognized under section 86 of the principal Act before the commencement of this Act. The principal Act shall apply in relation to such certificates as if this Act had not been enacted.

Therefore, this Act's primary purpose is to amend the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, and clarify that the amendments made in the principal Act by this Act shall not apply to any certificate of service granted or recognized under the principal Act before the commencement of this Act.
