Summary:
THE HIMACHAL PRADESH AND BILASPUR (NEW STATE) ACT, 1954

The Himachal Pradesh and Bilaspur (New State) Act, 1954, was enacted on May 28, 1954, to form a new Part C State called Himachal Pradesh by uniting the existing States of Himachal Pradesh and Bilaspur. The Act has four parts: Preliminary, Formation of the New State of Himachal Pradesh, Representation in the Legislatures, and Miscellaneous.

Part I, Preliminary, includes two sections. Section 1 provides the short title and commencement of the Act, while Section 2 defines various terms used in the Act.

Part II, Formation of the New State of Himachal Pradesh, has two sections. Section 3 provides for the formation of the new State of Himachal Pradesh, and Section 4 amends the First Schedule to the Constitution, changing the names of the existing States and their territories.

Part III, Representation in the Legislatures, has three subheadings: Council of States, House of the People, and Legislative Assembly of the New State. Each subheading contains sections related to representation, amendment of schedules, and rules of procedure.

Part IV, Courts, has one section, 25, which amalgamates the existing Courts of the Judicial Commissioner for the existing States into the Court of the Judicial Commissioner for the new State. The new Court has all the original, appellate, and other jurisdictions as under any law exercisable by either of the existing Courts in respect of any area forming part of the new State.

Part V, Administrative and Miscellaneous Provisions, includes four sections. Section 27 states that all authorities and officers exercising lawful functions in any area forming part of the new State shall continue to exercise their respective functions as far as may be. Section 28 states that all the law in force immediately before the commencement of this Act in any area forming part of the new State shall continue to be in force. Section 29 enables any Court, tribunal, or authority to construe the law with such alterations not affecting the substance as may be necessary or proper to adapt it to the matter before the Court, tribunal, or authority. Section 30 states that all taxes, duties, cesses, and fees shall continue to be levied in the same manner and to the same extent until other provision is made by a competent legislature or authority.

The First Schedule of the Act contains modifications in the Delimitation of Parliamentary Constituencies (Part C States) Order, 1951. The Second Schedule amends the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) (Part C States) Order, 1951. The Act also includes an Annexure with forms and footnotes.
