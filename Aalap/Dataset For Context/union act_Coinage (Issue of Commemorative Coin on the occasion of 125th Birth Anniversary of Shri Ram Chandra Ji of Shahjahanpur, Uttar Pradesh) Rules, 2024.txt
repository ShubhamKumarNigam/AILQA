Summary:
The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of 125th Birth Anniversary of Shri Ram Chandra Ji of Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh) Rules, 2024 have been made under the authority of the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules specify the details of a commemorative coin to be issued on the occasion of the 125th birth anniversary of Shri Ram Chandra Ji of Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh. The coin will be of one hundred and twenty-five rupees denomination and will be circular in shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters. It will be composed of 50% silver, 40% copper, 5% nickel, and 5% zinc. The coin's obverse will feature the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar with the legend "सत्यमेव जयते" inscribed below, along with the word "भारत" in Devnagri script and "INDIA" in English. The reverse of the coin will bear the portrait of Shri Ram Chandra Ji of Shahjahanpur, Uttar Pradesh, with the inscription "125Th Birth Anniversary of Shri Ram Chandra Ji of Shahjahanpur, U.P." in English and "शाहजहाँपुर, उ.प्र. के श्री राम चन्द्र जी की 125वीं जयंती।" in Devnagari script. The year "2024" will be written below the portrait of the coin. The standard weight of the coin will be 35 grams, with a remedy allowed in making the coin of 1/500 th plus or minus for silver and 1/100 th plus or minus for weight, making the weight range from 34.65 grams to 35.35 grams.
