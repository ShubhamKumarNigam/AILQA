Summary:
The Indian Railways (Amendment) Act, 1983, is an Act that further amends the Indian Railways Act, 1890. This amendment Act includes four repealed sections, namely section 2, 3, 4, and 5. These sections were previously amendments to sections 82A, 82B, and 82C of the Indian Railways Act, 1890, but have now been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988.

The Act also includes a validation section, section 5, which states that any action or thing taken or done before the commencement of this Act under the principal Act shall be deemed to be validly and effectively taken or done. This means that any actions taken under the Indian Railways Act, 1890, before the enactment of this amendment Act, will still be considered valid.

In summary, the Indian Railways (Amendment) Act, 1983, repeals certain amendments to the Indian Railways Act, 1890, and validates any actions taken under the principal Act before its enactment.
