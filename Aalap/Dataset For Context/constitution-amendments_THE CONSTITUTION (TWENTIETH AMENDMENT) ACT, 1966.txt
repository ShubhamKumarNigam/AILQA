Amendment of the Seventh Schedule.-

In the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution, in List I-Union List, after entry 92, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"92A. Elections to Parliament and to the Legislature of every State and to the offices of President and Vice-President of India."


Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

In the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, after entry 26, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"27. The Representation of the People Act, 1950 (43 of 1950) and the Representation of the People Act, 1951 (43 of 1951) excepting clause (4) of section 14 and section 21 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950 (43 of 1950) and clause (4) of section 62 and section 66 of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 (43 of 1951)."
