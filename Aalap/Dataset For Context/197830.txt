Summary:
The Coast Guard Act, 1978 is an Act of the Parliament of India that provides for the constitution and regulation of an Armed Force of the Union for ensuring the security of the maritime zones of India with a view to the protection of maritime and other national interests in such zones. The Act defines the Coast Guard as the Coast Guard constituted under this Act, and defines various terms used in the Act. The Act applies to all persons subject to this Act, including officers, subordinate officers, sailors, and other enrolled persons. The Coast Guard is under the control and supervision of the Central Government, and is headed by the Director-General. The Coast Guard is responsible for protecting the maritime and other national interests in the maritime zones of India, including ensuring the safety and protection of artificial islands, offshore terminals, installations and other structures and devices in any maritime zone, providing protection to fishermen including assistance to them at sea while in distress, taking measures to preserve and protect the maritime environment and to prevent and control marine pollution, assisting the customs and other authorities in anti-smuggling operations, enforcing the provisions of enactments in force in the maritime zones, and such other matters as may be prescribed. The Coast Guard is also responsible for investigating and prosecuting offences committed under this Act, and for maintaining discipline among its members. The Act defines various offences and provides for the punishment of persons convicted of such offences. The Act also provides for the trial of offences by Coast Guard Courts, and for the appointment of Law Officers to assist Coast Guard Courts in the performance of their functions. The Act also provides for the suspension and remission of sentences, and for the pardon and release of persons convicted of offences under this Act. The Act also contains provisions for the protection of members of the Coast Guard in the performance of their duties, and for the making of rules to carry out the provisions of the Act. The Act came into force on 18th August, 1978.
