THE CONSTITUTION (FIFTY-FOURTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986 

 Amendment of the Second Schedule.-

In Part D of the Second Schedule to the Constitution, in entry 1, for the figures "5,000", the figures "10,000" shall be substituted.


THE CONSTITUTION (FIFTY-FOURTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986 

 Amendment of article 125.-

In article 125 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "five thousand rupees", the words "ten thousand rupees" shall be substituted.


THE CONSTITUTION (FIFTY-FOURTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986 

 Amendment of article 221.-

In article 221 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "five thousand rupees", the words "ten thousand rupees" shall be substituted.


THE CONSTITUTION (FIFTY-FOURTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1986 

 Insertion of new article 125A.-

After article 125 of the Constitution, the following article shall be inserted, namely:-

"125A. Parliament may by law provide for the determination of the salary and allowances of the Judges of the Supreme Court and the High Courts and the conditions of service of the Judges of the High Courts."

Summary:
The Constitution (Fifty-Fourth Amendment) Act, 1986, seeks to increase the salaries of the Judges of the Supreme Court and High Courts, which have remained static since 1950. The Act increases the salary of the Chief Justice of India to Rs. 10,000 per month, Judges of the Supreme Court to Rs. 9,000 per month, Chief Justice of a High Court to Rs. 9,000 per month, and Judges of a High Court to Rs. 8,000 per month. The Act also amends Part D of the Second Schedule to the Constitution to give effect to the above increases in the salaries of Judges. Additionally, the Act makes an enabling provision in articles 125 and 221 to provide for changes in the salaries of Judges in future by Parliament by law. The Act also inserts a new article 125A, which allows Parliament to provide for the determination of the salary and allowances of the Judges of the Supreme Court and the High Courts and the conditions of service of the Judges of the High Courts. The Act came into force on April 1, 1986.
