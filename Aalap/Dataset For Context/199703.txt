Summary:
The Mahatma Gandhi Antarrashtriya Hindi Vishwavidyalaya Act, 1996 establishes and incorporates a teaching university for the promotion and development of Hindi language and literature through teaching and research. The university aims to enable Hindi to achieve greater functional efficiency and recognition as a major international language, and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

The jurisdiction of the university extends to the whole of India, and it is open to all classes, castes, and creeds, with special provisions for the employment or admission of women, physically handicapped, and persons belonging to the weaker sections of society, including the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes.

The university has various authorities, including the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Board of Studies, the Finance Committee, and other authorities as may be declared by the Statutes. The powers and functions of these authorities are specified in the Act and the Statutes.

The Act also provides for the appointment of officers, including the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Deans of Schools, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, and the Librarian. The manner of appointment, powers, and duties of these officers are specified in the Act and the Statutes.

The Act empowers the university to make Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations, subject to the provisions of the Act and the Statutes. The Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations are required to be consistent with the Act and the Statutes, and they provide for all matters necessary for the proper functioning of the university.

The Act also provides for the appointment of employees, their emoluments and other conditions of service, the creation of provident fund or pension fund or the provision of insurance schemes, and the management of institutions established by the university.

The Act also provides for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act, and the transitional provisions for the first Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, and Finance Officer, and the first Court, Executive Council, Academic Council, and Board of Studies.

The Schedule to the Act contains the first Statutes of the university, which provide for the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, the Deans of Schools, the Heads of Departments, the Proctor, the Librarian, the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Board of Studies, the Finance Committee, and the Selection Committees. The Statutes also provide for the appointment of teachers and other academic staff, the creation of teaching and academic posts, the appointment of examiners and moderators, the conduct of examinations, and the maintenance of discipline among employees and students.

Overall, the Mahatma Gandhi Antarrashtriya Hindi Vishwavidyalaya Act, 1996 provides a legal framework for the establishment and functioning of a teaching university for the promotion and development of Hindi language and literature through teaching and research.
