Summary:

The Constitution (Sixtieth Amendment) Act, 1988, aimed to revise the ceiling of profession tax in India. At the time, the ceiling was set at 250 rupees per annum, but due to inflation and other factors, several State Governments requested an upward revision. The amendment proposed to increase the ceiling to 2,500 rupees per annum, making the tax less regressive and helping State Governments raise additional resources. The amendment also sought to omit the proviso to clause (2) of article 276, as it was no longer relevant. The Bill was enacted to achieve these objectives and was called the Constitution (Sixtieth Amendment) Act, 1988.
