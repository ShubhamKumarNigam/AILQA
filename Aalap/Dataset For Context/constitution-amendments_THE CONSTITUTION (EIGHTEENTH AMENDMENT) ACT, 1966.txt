Amendment of article 169.-

In article 169 of the Constitution, in clause (1), after the words "under this Constitution", the words "or by any law of a State made in this behalf" shall be inserted.

[The Constitution (Eighteenth Amendment) Act, 1966, added a new provision to article 169 of the Constitution. This amendment allowed the State legislature to create a Legislative Council through a law for the same. Before this amendment, the State legislatures were not authorized to form a Legislative Council. The Constitution provided for the establishment of a Legislative Council in the States but did not specify the method for its creation. Therefore, the Eighteenth Amendment was crucial to clarify the legislative procedure for the creation of a Legislative Council in the States. This amendment was in line with the democratic principle of federalism.]
