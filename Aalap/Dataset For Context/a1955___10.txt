Summary:
THE ESSENTIAL COMMODITIES ACT, 1955 is an Act that provides for the control of the production, supply, and distribution of, and trade and commerce in, certain commodities. The Act extends to the whole of India and includes definitions for terms used throughout the Act. The Act also includes provisions for the declaration of essential commodities and the amendment of the Schedule of essential commodities. Section 3 of the Act grants the Central Government the power to control the production, supply, and distribution of essential commodities in the interest of the general public. The Act also includes provisions for the imposition of duties on State Governments, the delegation of powers, and the effect of orders inconsistent with other enactments. Section 6A of the Act allows for the confiscation of essential commodities, and Section 6B outlines the procedure for issuing show cause notices before confiscation. Section 7 of the Act outlines penalties for contravening orders made under the Act, and Section 10 provides for offenses committed by companies. Section 10A states that offenses under the Act are cognizable, and Section 10B allows for the publication of the names and other details of companies convicted under the Act. Section 10C provides for the presumption of culpable mental state, and Section 11 states that no court shall take cognizance of any offence punishable under this Act except on a report in writing of the facts constituting such offence made by a person who is a public servant or any person aggrieved or any recognized consumer association. Section 12 allows for the imposition of fines exceeding five thousand rupees, and Section 12A allows for the power to try summarily. Section 12B states that no civil court shall grant an injunction or make any order for any other relief against the Central Government or any State Government or a Public officer in respect of any Act done or purporting to be done by such Government or such officer in his official capacity, under this Act or any order made thereunder. Section 13 provides for a presumption as to orders, and Section 14 places the burden of proof in certain cases on the person contravening an order made under the Act. Section 15 provides protection of action taken under the Act, and Section 15A provides for the prosecution of public servants. Section 16 repeals the Essential Commodities Ordinance, 1955 and any other law in force in any State immediately before the commencement of this Act in so far as such law controls or authorises the control of the production, supply and distribution of, and trade and commerce in, any essential commodity.
