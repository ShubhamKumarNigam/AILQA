Summary:

THE COAL INDIA (REGULATION OF TRANSFERS AND VALIDATION) ACT, 2000

Act No. 45 of 2000

This Act, enacted on December 8, 2000, empowers the Central Government to direct the transfer of land or rights in or over land, or the right, title, and interest in relation to a coal mine, coking coal mine, or coke oven plant from Coal India Limited or its subsidiary companies to any other subsidiary company of Coal India Limited.

Key Definitions:

* Coal India: The Coal India Limited, a Government company incorporated under the Companies Act, 1956, having its registered office at Calcutta, and includes its predecessor Government company, namely, the Coal Mines Authority Limited.
* Subsidiary company: Subsidiaries of Coal India Limited, including the Central Coal Fields Limited, Bharat Coking Coal Limited, Western Coal Fields Limited, Eastern Coal Fields Limited, Central Mine Planning and Design Institute Limited, South-Eastern Coal Fields Limited, Northern Coal Fields Limited, and Mahanadi Coal Fields Limited.

Key Provisions:

* Section 3: The Central Government may direct the transfer of land or rights in or over land, or the right, title, and interest in relation to a coal mine, coking coal mine, or coke oven plant from Coal India or its subsidiary companies to any other subsidiary company of Coal India if it is satisfied that the subsidiary company is willing to comply with the terms and conditions imposed by the Central Government.
* Section 4: A subsidiary company that was operating or in control of any coal mine, coking coal mine, or coke oven plant vested in Coal India or any other subsidiary company immediately before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been vested with the land or rights in or over such land or the right, title, and interest in relation to such coal mine, coking coal mine, or coke oven plant. Such vesting shall be deemed to have been valid and effective at all material times as if a direction had been made by the Central Government under sub-section (1) of section 3. No suit or other proceeding shall be instituted, maintained, or continued in any court on the ground that such subsidiary company was not competent to operate or control such coal mine, coking coal mine, or coke oven plant.

This Act aims to regulate the transfer of land, rights, title, or interest in relation to coal mines, coking coal mines, or coke oven plants from Coal India or its subsidiary companies to any other subsidiary company of Coal India. The Act also validates certain transfers made before the commencement of this Act and deems them to be valid and effective.
