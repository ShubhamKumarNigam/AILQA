
Amendment  of  article  31.-



In  article  31  of  the  Constitution,---

(a)in  clause  (1),  the  words  "or  in  any  other  manner"  shall  be  omitted;

(b)for clause (2), the following clause shall be substituted, namely:---

"(2) No  property  shall  be  compulsorily  acquired  or  requisitioned  save  in accordance  with  the  provisions  contained  in  a  law  made  by  the  appropriate Legislature or other competent authority authorized by such law, and no such law shall be called in question in any court on the ground that it does not provide for compensation, or on the ground that it provides for a smaller amount of compensation than the market value of the property, or on any other ground whatsoever."



Amendment  of  article  19.-



In  article  19  of  the  Constitution,  in  clause  (6),  the  words  "or  in  any other manner"  shall  be  omitted.



Amendment  of  article  305.-



In  article  305  of  the  Constitution,  for  the  words  "the  power  to  make  any law  including  a  law  providing  for  the  levy  of  taxes  for  the  purposes  of the Union  or  of  a  State",  the  words  "the  power  to  make  any  law  for  the purposes  of  the  Union  or  of  a  State"  shall  be  substituted.



Insertion  of  new  clause  2A  in  article  368.-



In  article  368  of  the  Constitution,  after  clause  (2),  the  following clause  shall  be  inserted,  namely:---

"(2A)  No  amendment  of  this  Constitution  (including  the  provisions  of Part III)  made  after  the  commencement  of  the  Constitution  (Eighth Amendment) Act, 1959, shall  be  called  in  question  in  any  court  on  any  ground."



Insertion  of  new  article  31A.-



After  article  31,  the  following  article  shall  be  inserted,  namely:---

"31A.  Saving  of  laws  providing  for  acquisition  of  estates,  etc.-

(1)  Notwithstanding  anything  contained  in  article  13,  no  law  providing  for  the  acquisition  by  the  State  of  any  estate  or  of  any  rights  therein or  of  any  rights  in  or  over  any  land,  or  in  any  benefited  land,  in  which  any  estate  or  any  rights  therein  have  been  acquired  shall  be deemed  to  be  void  on  the  ground  that  it  is  inconsistent  with,  or  takes  away or  abridges  any  of  the  rights  conferred  by,---

(a)article  14,  or



(b)article  19,  or

(c)the  provisions  contained  in  clause  (2)  of  article  31,  or

(d)any  of  the  provisions  contained  in  Part  III,  if  such  law  is  made  by  the  appropriate  Legislature  and  if  it  provides  for  the  payment  of  compensation  as  defined  in  clause  (2)  of  article  31  for  the  acquisition  of  any  estate  or  of  any  rights  therein or  of  any  rights  in  or  over  any  land,  or  in  any  benefited  land,  in  which  any  estate  or  any  rights  therein  have  been  acquired  and  either ---

(i)such  compensation  is  paid  in  accordance  with  the  principles  contained  in  that  clause,  or



(ii)the  market  value  of  the  property  to  be  acquired  is  determined  in  accordance  with  the  principles  contained  in  that  clause.

(2)  In  this  article,---

(a)the  expression  "estate"  shall,  in  relation  to  any  local  area,  have  the  same  meaning  as  that  expression  or  its  local  equivalent  had  in  the existing law  in  force  in  that  area  immediately  before  the  commencement  of  the  Constitution  (Seventh Amendment) Act, 1956;

(b)the  expression  "benefited  land"  means  any  land  on  which  any estate  is  situated  and  the  expression  "rights  in  or  over  such  land"  includes  rights  to  derive  benefit  from  the  estate  through  the  use  of  such  land  or  the  resources  thereof;

(c)the  expression  "compensation"  means  compensation  for  the  acquisition  of  any  estate  or  of  any  rights  therein  or  in  or  over  any  land  or  in  or  over  any  benefited  land,  and  includes ---

(i)the  market  value  thereof,  that  is,  the  price  that  such  property  would  bring  if  sold  in  the  open  market  on  the  date  of  the  acquisition  or  requisition  by  the  appropriate  Government  or  authority,  as  the  case  may  be,  or



(ii)if  the  property  is  not  readily  saleable  in  the  open  market  on  account  of  any  special  features,  the  value  of  the  property,  as  determined  in  accordance  with  the  principles  contained  in  clause  (2)  of  article  31,

and  shall  be  deemed  to  include  interest  at  the  rate  of  four  per  cent  per  annum  from  the  date  of  the  acquisition  or  requisition  until  the  date  on  which  the  compensation  is  paid  to  the  person  entitled  thereto  under  this  article;

(d)the  expression  "the  appropriate  Government  or  authority"  means  in  relation  to  any  estate  or  any  rights  therein  or  in  or  over  any  land  or  in  or  over  any  benefited  land  the  Government  or  authority  empowered  by  law  to  acquire  such  estate  or  rights  or  such  land  or  benefited  land,  as  the  case  may  be."



Insertion  of  new  article  31B.-



After  article  31A,  the  following  article  shall  be  inserted,  namely:---

"31B.  Validation  of  certain  Acts  and  Regulations.-

(1)  Notwithstanding  anything  contained  in  article  13,  no  Act  of  the  appropriate Legislature  or  of  the  Parliament  or  any  Regulation  made  by  the President  or  by  the  Governor  of  a  State  under  article  240  shall  be deemed  to  be  void  on  the  ground  that  it  is  inconsistent  with,  or  takes  away  or  abridges  any  of  the  rights  conferred  by,  the  provisions  contained  in  clause  (2)  of  article  31  if  such  Act  or  Regulation  is  made  before  the  commencement  of  the  Constitution  (Eighth Amendment) Act, 1959, and  provides  for  the  payment  of  compensation  as  defined  in  clause  (2)  of  article  31  for  the  acquisition  of  any  estate  or  of  any  rights  therein  or  in  or  over  any  land,  or  in  any  benefited  land,  in  which  any  estate  or  any  rights  therein  have  been  acquired  and  either ---

(i)such  compensation  is  paid  in  accordance  with  the  principles  contained  in  that  clause,  or



(ii)the  market  value  of  the  property  to  be  acquired  is  determined  in  accordance  with  the  principles  contained  in  that  clause.

(2)  In  this  article,  the  expression  "estate"  and  "benefited  land"  shall  have  the  same  meanings  as  in  article  31A."

Insertion  of  new  clause  2  in  article  368.-



In  article  368  of  the  Constitution,  after  clause  (1),  the  following  clause  shall  be  inserted,  namely:---

"(2)  No  amendment  of  this  Constitution  (including  the  provisions  of Part  III)  made  before  the  commencement  of  the  Constitution  (Eighth Amendment) Act, 1959, shall  be  called  in  question  in  any  court  on  any  ground."
