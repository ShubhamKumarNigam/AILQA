Title: The Airports Authority of India Act, 1994
Act ID: ACT NO. 55 OF 1994
Enactment Date: September 12, 1994

The Airports Authority of India Act, 1994, establishes the Airports Authority of India, a body corporate responsible for managing and operating airports and related services in India. The Act defines key terms such as "airport," "aeronautical communication station," "air traffic service," and "air transport service." It outlines the composition, disqualifications, and terms of office for members of the Authority. The Authority has the power to acquire, hold, and dispose of property, and to contract and sue in its own name. The Act also provides for the removal of members under certain circumstances and their eligibility for re-appointment.

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary matters, including the short title, commencement, and application of the Act. Chapter II establishes the Airports Authority of India and outlines its composition, powers, and duties. The Act includes provisions for the appointment and removal of members, as well as their terms of office and conditions of service. It also sets out the procedures for meetings and decision-making within the Authority. Overall, the Airports Authority of India Act, 1994, provides a legal framework for the management and operation of airports and related services in India.

Section 15 of the Act states that all licenses, permits, quotas, and exemptions granted to the International Airports Authority or the National Airports Authority in connection with their affairs and business will be deemed to have been granted to the Authority.

Section 16 provides that any tax exemptions or benefits granted to the International Airports Authority or the National Airports Authority under the Income-tax Act, 1961, will continue to have effect in relation to the Authority. Additionally, any payments made by the International Airports Authority or the National Airports Authority that are exempt from deduction of tax at source will continue to be available as if the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961, were operative in relation to the Authority.

Section 17 states that any guarantees given for or in favor of the International Airports Authority or the National Airports Authority with respect to any loan or lease finance will continue to be operative in relation to the Authority.

Section 18 outlines the provisions in respect of officers and other employees of the International Airports Authority and the National Airports Authority. Every officer or other employee of the International Airports Authority serving in its employment immediately before the appointed day shall, in connection with the undertaking that has vested in the Authority, become an officer or other employee of the International Airports Division of the Authority. Similarly, every officer or other employee of the National Airports Authority serving in its employment immediately before the appointed day shall become an officer or other employee of the National Airports Division of the Authority.

Section 19 states that any land required by the Authority for the discharge of its functions under this Act shall be deemed to be needed for a public purpose and such land may be acquired for the Authority under the provisions of the Land Acquisition Act, 1894, or of any other corresponding law for the time being in force.

Section 20 states that the Authority shall be competent to enter into and perform any contract necessary for the discharge of its functions under this Act.

Section 21 outlines the mode of executing contracts on behalf of the Authority. Every contract shall be made by the Chairperson or such other member or such officer of the Authority as may be generally or specially empowered in this behalf by the Authority.
