Summary:
The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of Inauguration of New Parliament Building) Rules, 2023, have been made by the Central Government under the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules will come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. According to these rules, a coin of Seventy Five Rupees denomination will be coined to commemorate the Inauguration of New Parliament Building. This coin will have a circular shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters and 200 serrations. The coin will be made of Quaternary AlloySilver, with 50% Silver, 40% Copper, 5% Nickel, and 5% Zinc.

The design of the coin is specified in the First Schedule. The obverse of the coin will bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the center, with the legend " सत्यमेव जयते " inscribed below in Devnagri script. The word " भारत " will be written in Devnagri script on the left periphery, and the word "INDIA" will be written in English on the right periphery. The Rupee symbol " ⟨₹⟩ " and the denominational value "75" will be inscribed below the Lion Capitol. The reverse of the coin will bear the image of the Parliament Complex, with the inscription " संसद संकुल " written in Devnagari script on the upper periphery and the inscription “PARLIAMENT COMPLEX” in English written on the lower periphery. The year “2023” will be written below the image of the Parliament Complex.

The Second Schedule specifies the standard weight and remedy allowed in making the coin. The standard weight of the Seventy Five Rupees coin will be 35 grams, with a remedy allowed of 1/500th plus or minus for Silver and 1/100th plus or minus for the weight. This means that the Silver content may vary from 498 to 502 per 1000, and the weight could vary from 34.65 grams to 35.35 grams.
