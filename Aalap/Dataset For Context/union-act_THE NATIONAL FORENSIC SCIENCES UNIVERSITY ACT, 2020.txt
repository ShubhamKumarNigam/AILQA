The National Forensic Sciences University Act, 2020 declares the National Forensic Sciences University as an institution of national importance. It establishes the University by merging the Gujarat Forensic Sciences University, Gandhinagar, Gujarat and the Lok Nayak Jayaprakash Narayan National Institute of Criminology and Forensic Sciences, New Delhi. The University shall have headquarters at Gandhinagar, Gujarat and campuses at Gandhinagar, Gujarat and Rohini, New Delhi. The objects of the University include facilitating and promoting academic learning and practices in the field of forensic science, fostering research and applied applications in forensic science and related disciplines, providing advanced institutional and research facilities, creating capacities and capabilities of global standards of education, training and research, coordinating with the Central and State Governments to improve investigation, crime detection and prevention, advising and assisting the Central and State Governments in formulation of relevant policies, coordinating and networking with institutions having specialization in the fields of forensic science, law, legal studies, criminology and other allied areas and technology, administering, maintaining and managing the University and establishing off-site campuses and off-shore centres for education, training and research. The University shall have the power to create academic and other teaching posts, appoint on contract or otherwise visiting professors, scholars and consultants, create non-teaching, administrative, ministerial and other posts, cooperate, collaborate or partner or associate with educational or other institutions and organizations, institute and award fellowships, scholarships, exhibitions, prizes and medals, provide for the preparation of instructional material, sponsor and make provision for research and development, enter into, carry out, vary or cancel contracts, demand and receive such fees and other charges as may be specified, receive benefactions, donations and gifts, acquire, hold, manage and dispose of any property, initiate measures to enlist the cooperation of the industry, establish off-shore campus at any place outside the country, provide, control and maintain discipline among the students and all categories of employees, and conduct innovative experiments and develop new methods and technologies in the field of science, technology and management in relation to the domains of investigation, prevention and detection of crimes and furthering the cause of criminal justice systems in order to achieve international standards of such education, training, research and consultancy. The University shall be open to all persons irrespective of gender, race, caste, creed, disability, domicile, ethnicity, social or economic background and shall not discriminate on any of these grounds. The University shall maintain an all-India character and high standards of teaching and research and shall admit students for courses in the University on an all-India basis. All teaching at the University and its campuses or affiliated colleges shall be conducted by and in the name of the University in accordance with the Statutes and Ordinances made in this behalf. The authorities of the University shall include the Chancellor, Court, Board of Governors, Academic Council, Board for Affiliation and Recognition, Finance Committee and such other authorities as may be laid down in the Statutes. The Chancellor shall be the Head of the University and shall preside at the convocations of the University held for conferring degrees. The Court shall review, from time to time, the broad policies and programmes of the University, consider and pass resolutions on the annual report and the annual accounts of the University and the audit report on such accounts, and perform such other functions as may be laid down in the Statutes. The Board of Governors shall be responsible for the general superintendence, direction and the control of affairs of the University and shall exercise all the powers of the University not otherwise provided by this Act, Statutes or Ordinances and shall have the power to review the acts of the Academic Council and the Finance Committee and other committees or authorities of the University. The Academic Council shall specify the academic policies of the University and be responsible for the maintenance and improvement of standards of instruction, education and evaluation in the University. The Vice-Chancellor shall be the principal academic and executive officer of the University and shall exercise general supervision and control over the affairs of the University and give effect to the decisions of all the authorities of the University. The University shall maintain a Fund to which shall be credited all moneys provided by the Central Government, all moneys received from State Governments, all fees and other charges received by the University, all moneys received by the University by way of grants, gifts, donations, benefactions, bequests or transfers, all interest from corpus or any other such earnings, any loans taken by the University, the moneys received by the University from the collaborating industries in terms of the provisions of the Memorandum of Understanding entered between the University and such industry for establishment of sponsored chairs, fellowships or infrastructure facilities of the University, and all moneys received by the University in any other manner or from any other source. The accounts of the University shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India and any expenditure incurred by it in connection with such audit shall be payable by it to the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India. The University may constitute for the benefit of its employees such provident or pension fund or provide such insurance scheme as it may deem fit in such manner and subject to such conditions as may be laid down in the Statutes. The University may receive funds from the Central Government or State Governments or other sources or use its funds to maintain and operate a corpus of the University.
