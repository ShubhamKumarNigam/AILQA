Summary:

The Central Industrial Security Force (Amendment and Validation) Act, 1999, is an Act that amends the Central Industrial Security Force Act, 1968. The long title of the principal Act has been amended to provide for the constitution and regulation of an armed force of the Union for the better protection and security of industrial undertakings owned by the Central Government, certain other industrial undertakings, employees of all such undertakings and to provide technical consultancy services to industrial establishments in the private sector and for matters connected therewith.

The Act amends section 2 of the principal Act to include the definition of "industrial establishment" which means an industrial undertaking or a company as defined under section 3 of the Companies Act, 1956 or a firm registered under section 59 of the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 which is engaged in any industry, or in any trade, business or service.

The Act amends section 3 of the principal Act to include the duty of the Force to perform such other duties as may be entrusted to it by the Central Government.

The Act amends section 8 of the principal Act to include the power of the Central Government to remove, order for compulsory retirement of, withholding of increment of pay with or without cumulative effect, withholding of promotion, and censure an enrolled member of the Force.

The Act amends section 9 of the principal Act to include the provision for an enrolled member of the Force aggrieved by an order passed in appeal under sub-section (1) may, within a period of six months from the date on which the order is communicated to him, prefer a revision petition against the order to such authority as may be prescribed and in disposing of the revision petition, the said authority shall follow such procedure as may be prescribed.

The Act amends section 10 of the principal Act to include the duty of the Force to provide technical consultancy services relating to security, to protect and safeguard the organisations owned or funded by the Government and the employees of such organisations as may be entrusted to him by the Central Government and any other duty which may be entrusted to him by the Central Government from time to time.

The Act inserts new section 14A to provide technical consultancy services relating to security, to private sector industrial establishments on a request received from the Managing Director of an industrial establishment or any other person authorised by him in this behalf, on payment of such fee as may be prescribed.

The Act amends section 22 of the principal Act to include the power to prescribe authority under sub-section (2A) of section 9 and the procedure to be followed by such authority in disposing of the revision petition, prescribing authority under sub-section (2B) of section 9, the period within which such authority may call for the records and the manner in which such authority may make inquiry and the manner in which and the fee on payment of which the technical consultancy services shall be provided under sub-section (1) of section 14A.

The Act validates the disposal of certain revision petitions under rule 49 of the Central Industrial Security Force Rules, 1969 before the commencement of this Act or any order made or purporting to have been made or any action or thing taken or done in or under such petition shall not be deemed to be invalid or ever to have become invalid merely on the ground that the Central Government had no power under the principal Act to make the said rule 49.
