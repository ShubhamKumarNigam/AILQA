Summary:
The International Airports Authority (Amendment) Act, 1985, is an Act that amends the International Airports Authority Act, 1971. This Amendment Act has three sections and one validation section.

Section 1 of the Act states that the short title of the Act is the International Airports Authority (Amendment) Act, 1985.

Section 2 of the Act amends section 36 of the principal Act by repealing sections 2 and 3 through the Repealing and Amending Act, 2001 (30 of 2001), section 2 and the First Schedule (w.e.f. 3-9-2001). Further, this section validates the International Airports Authority of India (Conditions of Service of the Chairman and other whole-time Members) Rules, 1973, which were published with the notification of the Government of India in the then Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation No. S.O. 717(E), dated the 29th November, 1973. These rules are deemed to be, and to have always been, made under section 36 of the principal Act as amended by clause (a) of section 2 of this Act.

Section 3 of the Act amends section 37 of the principal Act.

Section 4 of the Act validates the International Airports Authority of India (Conditions of Service of the Chairman and other whole-time Members) Rules, 1973, published with the notification of the Government of India in the then Ministry of Tourism and Civil Aviation No. S.O. 717(E), dated the 29th November, 1973, under section 36 of the principal Act as amended by clause (a) of section 2 of this Act. This section also validates every order made or purporting to have been made by the Central Government under rule 7 of the 1973 Rules before the date of commencement of this Act and any action or thing taken or done under such order.

Overall, this Amendment Act aims to clarify and validate the International Airports Authority of India (Conditions of Service of the Chairman and other whole-time Members) Rules, 1973, under the International Airports Authority Act, 1971.
