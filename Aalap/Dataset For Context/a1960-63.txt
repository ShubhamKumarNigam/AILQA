The Act regulates dividends on preference shares in certain cases, depending on whether the stipulated dividend is free of income-tax or subject to income-tax. If the stipulated dividend is free of income-tax, the share will carry a preferential right to be paid without any deduction aforesaid such amount as would exceed the stipulated dividend by thirty per cent. If the stipulated dividend is subject to income-tax, every such share shall carry a preferential right to be paid subject to the deduction aforesaid such amount as would exceed the stipulated dividend by eleven per cent. The Act also includes special provisions for companies where a portion of their income is not chargeable to income-tax. The Act has overriding effect and applies notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any law for the time being in force or in the memorandum or articles of a company or in any agreement between the company and its shareholders or in any resolution passed by the company in a general meeting or by its Board of directors. The Central Government may make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act, which shall be laid before each House of Parliament.
