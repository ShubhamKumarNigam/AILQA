Summary:
The Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act, 1957, is an Act that makes special provisions for requirement as to residence in regard to certain classes of public employment in certain areas. This Act is enacted in pursuance of clause (3) of article 16 of the Constitution.

The Act has four sections. Section 1 is about the short title and commencement of the Act. The Act can be called the Public Employment (Requirement as to Residence) Act, 1957, and it can come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government.

Section 2 of the Act repeals existing laws that prescribe any requirement as to residence prior to employment or appointment to an office under the Government of, or any local or other authority within, a State or Union territory.

Section 3 empowers the Central Government to make rules prescribing any requirement as to residence within the Telangana area or the Union territory of Himachal Pradesh, Manipur, or Tripura prior to appointment to certain classes of public employment.

Section 4 lays down the procedure for parliamentary scrutiny of rules made under this Act. Every rule made under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament for a total period of thirty days, and if both Houses agree in making any modification in the rule or both Houses agree that the rule should not be made, the rule shall have effect only in the modified form or be of no effect.

Section 5 states that section 3 and all rules made thereunder shall cease to have effect on the expiration of fifteen years from the commencement of this Act. However, this cesser shall not affect the validity of any appointment previously made in pursuance of the said rules.

The Act also includes a schedule, annexure, and appendix, but they are not relevant to the summary. There are also some footnotes that provide information about the amendments made to the Act.
