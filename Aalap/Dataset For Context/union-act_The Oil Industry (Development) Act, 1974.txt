
The Oil Industry (Development) Act, 1974 is a law enacted by the Parliament of India to provide for the establishment of a Board for the development of the oil industry. The Act establishes the Oil Industry Development Board, which is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal, and has the power to acquire, hold and dispose of property, both movable and immovable, and to contract. The Board is responsible for rendering financial and other assistance for the promotion of measures that are conducive to the development of the oil industry. The Act specifies the functions of the Board, which include making grants or advancing loans to oil industrial concerns or other persons engaged in activities related to the oil industry, guaranteeing loans raised by oil industrial concerns or other persons, guaranteeing deferred payments due from oil industrial concerns or other persons, guaranteeing loans raised from banks or financial institutions in foreign currency, underwriting the issue of stock, shares, bonds or debentures by oil industrial concerns, acting as an agent for the Central Government or any overseas financial organization or credit agency in the transaction of any business with any oil industrial concern, subscribing to the stock or shares of any oil industrial concern, and subscribing to the debentures of any oil industrial concern. The Act also specifies the conditions and restrictions for the rendering of assistance by the Board, and the principles and conditions for the enforcement of claims by the Board. The Act further provides for the appointment of Directors of oil industrial concerns by the Board when the management of such concerns is taken over by the Board. The Act also specifies that the duties of excise shall be levied and collected as a cess for the purposes of this Act, and the proceeds of the duties of excise shall be credited to the Consolidated Fund of India. The Central Government may also pay to the Board by way of grants or loans such sums of money as the Central Government may consider necessary.
