Summary:
The Acquired Territories (Merger) Act, 1960, was enacted on December 28, 1960, to merge certain territories acquired from Pakistan into the States of Assam, Punjab, and West Bengal. The Act provides for the administration of the acquired territories by the respective State Governments and amends the First Schedule to the Constitution to include the acquired territories in the relevant State. The Act also makes provisions for the construction of references to existing constituencies, the status of sitting members, property and assets, appropriation of moneys for expenditure in the acquired territories, extension of laws, and the removal of difficulties. The Second Schedule to the Act contains the relevant extracts of the agreements between the Governments of India and Pakistan regarding the acquisition of these territories.
