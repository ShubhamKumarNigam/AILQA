Summary:

The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of 75th Republic Day Celebrations) Rules, 2024, have been made under the authority of the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules specify the details of a commemorative coin to be issued on the occasion of the 75th Republic Day Celebrations in India.

The coin will be of seventy-five rupees denomination and will be circular in shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters. It will be made of pure silver, with a composition of 99.9 per cent silver.

The design of the coin will be as specified in the First Schedule to these rules. The obverse of the coin will bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the center, with the legend "Satyameva Jayate" inscribed below, flanked on the left periphery with the word "Bharat" in Devnagari script and on the right periphery with the word "INDIA" in English. It will also bear the Rupee symbol "L" and denominational value "75" in the international numerals below the Lion Capitol.

The reverse of the coin will bear the image of the new Parliament building in front of the image of the old Parliament building. The people of India will be shown behind the image of the Parliament building. The text "75oka svarajya" in Devnagari script and the text "75TH REPUBLIC DAY" in English will be inscribed on the upper periphery of the coin. The year "2024" in the international numerals will be inscribed below the image of the New Parliament Building.

The standard weight of the coin will be 40 grams, and the remedy allowed in making of such coin will be as specified in the Second Schedule to these rules. The coin will contain 999 parts of silver per 1000, with a tolerance for silver content of 998.8 and above. The weight of the coin could be 39.995 grams to 40.005 grams. These rules will come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
