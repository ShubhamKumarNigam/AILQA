Summary:
The Delhi High Court (Amendment) Act, 1980, is an Act that amends the Delhi High Court Act, 1966. This amendment act came into force on 1st October, 1980. The act has four sections, and no schedule, annexure, or appendix.

Section 1 of the act specifies the short title and commencement of the act. It states that this act may be called the Delhi High Court (Amendment) Act, 1980, and it shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may appoint by notification in the Official Gazette.

Section 2 of the act is repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act (19 of 1988), and the First Schedule, with effect from 31-3-1988.

Section 3 of the act is also repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act (19 of 1988), and the First Schedule, with effect from 31-3-1988.

Section 4 of the act grants power to the Chief Justice of the High Court of Delhi to transfer any suit or other proceedings which is or are pending in the High Court immediately before the commencement of this Act and in which no witnesses have been examined before such commencement to such subordinate court in the Union territory of Delhi as would have jurisdiction to entertain such suit or proceedings had such suit or proceedings been instituted or filed for the first time after such commencement.

In summary, this act grants the Chief Justice of the High Court of Delhi the power to transfer certain pending suits and proceedings to subordinate courts in the Union territory of Delhi, and it repeals certain sections of the Delhi High Court Act, 1966, and Punjab Act VI of 1918, as in force in the Union territory of Delhi, with effect from 31-3-1988.
