Summary:
The Central Government, in consultation with the Election Commission, has amended the Conduct of Elections Rules, 1961, resulting in the new title, Conduct of Elections(Amendment) Rules, 2020. In Rule 27A, several changes have been introduced. Firstly, in clause (aa), the phrase "or COVID 19 suspect or affected persons" has been added after "or persons with disability". Secondly, in clause (e), the age limit of 80 years has been revised to 65 years. Lastly, a new clause (f) has been inserted, defining "COVID 19 suspect or affected persons" as those who have tested positive for COVID-19, or are under home or institutional quarantine due to the disease, as certified by a competent authority.
