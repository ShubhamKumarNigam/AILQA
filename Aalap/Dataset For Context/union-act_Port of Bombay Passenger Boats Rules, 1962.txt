 Definitions

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires, -

 (a) "Act" means the Indian Ports Act, 1908 (15 of 1908);
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (b) "authorised person" means a person authorized by the Port Trust to inspect boats and to enforce these rules;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (c) "boat" means a mechanically propelled boat carrying passengers for hire;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (d) "crew" includes the master, engineer, helmsman and all other persons employed for the navigation, management or maintenance of the boat;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (e) "master" means the person in charge of the navigation of the boat;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (f) "owner" means the person who is the owner of the boat for the time being, whether he be the builder, the purchaser, the mortgagee in possession or any other person who is for the time being entitled to receive the rents and profits of the boat;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (g) "passenger" means a person carried in a boat for hire;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (h) "Port Trust" means the Bombay Port Trust;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (i) "Table of Fares" means the Table of Fares appended to these rules;
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (j) "Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage" means the Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage appended to these rules;



 Licensing of boats

(1) No person shall use or permit to be used for the carriage of passengers for hire any boat in the Port of Bombay unless such boat is licensed under these rules.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (2) Every application for the grant or renewal of a license shall be made to the Secretary, Bombay Port Trust, in such form and accompanied by such fees as may be prescribed.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (3) Every license shall be granted or renewed for a period of one year from the date of issue or renewal, as the case may be.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (4) Every license shall specify the maximum number of passengers that may be carried in the boat and the maximum fare that may be charged.



 Table of Fares

(1) The Table of Fares appended to these rules shall come into force on the date of the publication of these rules.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (2) No person shall charge or receive from any passenger in respect of any journey any fare in excess of the fare specified in the Table of Fares.



 Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage

(1) The Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage appended to these rules shall come into force on the date of the publication of these rules.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (2) No person shall carry in any boat more passengers or crew than are specified in the Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage for the tonnage of the boat.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (3) Every master of a boat shall keep a list of the names and addresses of all the passengers carried in the boat and shall produce the same for inspection on demand by an authorized person.



 Power to inspect boats

An authorized person may at any time inspect any boat for the purpose of ensuring that the provisions of these rules are being complied with.



 Penalties

(1) If any person contravenes any of the provisions of these rules, he shall be punishable with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (2) If any person, after having been convicted of an offence under these rules, is again found guilty of an offence under these rules, he shall be punishable with fine which may extend to one thousand rupees.



 Repeal and saving

(1) The Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, Table of Fares and Table of number of Crew and Passenger to be carried in each class of Boat according to Tonnage, published with the Notification of the Government of Bombay in the Marine Department, No. 186, dated the 25th January, 1921 are hereby repealed.
Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 

 (2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or any action taken under the said rules shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of these rules.



 Summary:

The Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, 1962 is a set of rules that apply to the port of Mumbai. The rules define various terms related to boats and their operation, such as "boat," "crew," "master," "owner," "passenger," "Port Trust," "Table of Fares," and "Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage."

The rules require that all boats used for the carriage of passengers for hire in the Port of Bombay must be licensed. The license specifies the maximum number of passengers that may be carried in the boat and the maximum fare that may be charged. The Table of Fares specifies the fare that may be charged for any journey, and the Table of number of Crew and Passengers to be carried in each Class of Boat According to Tonnage specifies the maximum number of passengers and crew that may be carried in a boat based on its tonnage.

An authorized person may inspect any boat to ensure compliance with the rules. Penalties for non-compliance include a fine that may extend to five hundred rupees for the first offence and a fine that may extend to one thousand rupees for subsequent offences. The rules repeal the Port of Bombay Passenger Boats Rules, Table of Fares and Table of number of Crew and Passenger to be carried in each class of Boat according to Tonnage, published with the Notification of the Government of Bombay in the Marine Department, No. 186, dated the 25th January, 1921, but anything done or any action taken under the said rules shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of these rules.
