Summary:

The Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) (Inapplicability to Major Ports) Act, 1997, is a law that exempts dock workers in major port trusts from the provisions of the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948. The Act received the President's assent on August 18, 1997, and came into force on January 5, 1998. The Act defines four key terms: "appointed day," "Board," "Dock Labour Board," and "major port."

The Act comprises four sections and no schedules, annexures, or appendices. Section 1 is about the short title and commencement of the Act, stating that it may be called the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) (Inapplicability to Major Ports) Act, 1997, and that it shall come into force on the date appointed by the Central Government.

Section 2 defines the terms used in the Act, including "appointed day," "Board," "Dock Labour Board," and "major port." The appointed day refers to the date specified under section 3 for a particular major port. The Board means the major port trust as defined in the Major Port Trusts Act, 1963. The Dock Labour Board refers to a Dock Labour Board established under section 5A of the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948. A major port has the same meaning as in the Indian Ports Act, 1908.

Section 3 deals with the inapplicability of the provisions of the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948, to major ports. The Central Government may direct, by notification in the Official Gazette, that the provisions of the Dock Workers (Regulation of Employment) Act, 1948, shall cease to have effect in relation to a major port with effect from the date specified in that notification. This can only be done after a settlement is arrived at between the Dock Labour Board, its workmen, and the management of the major port in accordance with the provisions of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

Section 4 deals with the transfer of assets and liabilities of the Dock Labour Board, etc., to the Board. On the appointed day, all property, assets, and funds vested in the Dock Labour Board shall vest in the Board. All debts, obligations, and liabilities incurred, all contracts entered into, and all matters and things engaged to be done by, with, or for the Dock Labour Board shall be deemed to have been incurred, entered into, and engaged to be done by, with, or for the Board. Every employee and worker serving under the Dock Labour Board shall hold office or service under the Board on the terms and conditions which are not in any way less favorable than those which would have been admissible to him if there had not been a transfer of his services to the Board. The transfer of the services of any employee under this section to the Board shall not entitle such an employee to any compensation under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, or any other law.
