Summary:

The Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University Act, 1994 (Act No. 58 of 1994) establishes the Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University in Uttar Pradesh, India. The University aims to promote advanced knowledge and research in various fields, with a focus on science, technology, and the principles of national integration, social justice, and democratic way of life. The Act provides for the establishment of various authorities, such as the Board of Management, the Academic Council, the Planning Board, and the Boards of Studies, which are responsible for managing and overseeing the academic and administrative affairs of the University. The Act also specifies the powers and duties of the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellors, the Deans of Schools, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, and other officers of the University. The Act includes provisions for the appointment, terms, and conditions of service of these officers, as well as for the appointment and terms of service of teachers and other employees of the University. The Act also includes provisions for the admission of students, the conduct of examinations, and the award of degrees and other academic distinctions. The Act includes a schedule specifying the objects of the University, and another schedule containing the first Statutes of the University.
