The Extradition Treaty between the Government of the Republic of India and the United Arab Emirates outlines the conditions for extradition between the two countries. The treaty applies to offences committed within the jurisdiction of the contracting parties and extradition shall be granted only if the offence is punishable under the laws of both contracting parties by imprisonment or other form of detention for a maximum period of at least one year or by a more severe penalty. Extradition shall be granted regardless of the nationality of the person whose extradition is requested, but it shall not be granted if the person has been tried and sentenced or is being proceeded against for the same offence in the requested State, or if the prosecution for the offence has become barred by lapse of time according to the laws of the requested State or when the person has been granted asylum in the requested State.
