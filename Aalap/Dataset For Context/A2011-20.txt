Summary:

THE NATIONAL CAPITAL TERRITORY OF DELHI LAWS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) SECOND ACT, 2011 (Act No. 20 of 2011) is an Act to make special provisions for the National Capital Territory of Delhi for a further period up to the 31st day of December, 2020. The Act aims to address the issue of phenomenal increase in the population of Delhi, resulting in tremendous pressure on land and infrastructure, leading to encroachment or unauthorized developments. The Act provides for special provisions related to the regularization of unauthorized colonies, village abadi area, and their extensions, and special areas. It also provides for the implementation of schemes for improvement of slums and jhuggi-jhompri clusters, and preparation of housing schemes for such persons. The Act also includes provisions for the relocation and rehabilitation of slum dwellers and jhuggi-jhompri clusters, regularization of schools, dispensaries, religious institutions, and cultural institutions established in non-conforming areas, and guidelines for redevelopment of existing godown clusters in non-conforming areas. The Act also provides for the maintenance of status quo in respect of encroachment or unauthorized development, and prohibits punitive action against such encroachments or unauthorized developments, subject to certain conditions. The Act also empowers the Central Government to give directions to local authorities for the implementation of the provisions of the Act. The Act also validates all acts done or omitted to be done during the period beginning on or after the 1st day of January, 2012 and ending immediately before the date of enactment of this Act, in conformity with the provisions of this Act.
