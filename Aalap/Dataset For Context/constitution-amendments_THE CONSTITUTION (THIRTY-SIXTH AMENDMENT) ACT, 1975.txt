THE CONSTITUTION (THIRTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1975 

 Insertion of new article 371F.-

 After article 371E of the Constitution, the following article shall be inserted, namely:-

"371F. Special provision with respect to the State of Sikkim.--

(1) Notwithstanding anything in this Constitution,

(a) the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim shall consist of not less than thirty members;

(b) as from the date of commencement of the Constitution (Thirty-sixth Amendment) Act, 1975 (hereafter in this article referred to as the appointed day), the executive power of the Union shall extend to the State of Sikkim and the President may make such orders as may seem to him necessary for the purpose of bringing the administration of the State of Sikkim under the control of the Union;

(c) the Governor of Sikkim shall be appointed by the President and shall hold office during the pleasure of the President;

(d) the High Court of Sikkim shall be the court of Judicial and shall have such jurisdiction, powers and authority in relation to the State of Sikkim as the President may by order specify;

(e) the protector for Sikkim shall be appointed by the President and shall have such duties and functions as may be prescribed by or under any law made by Parliament;

(f) the Chief Commissioner of Sikkim shall be appointed by the President and shall exercise such powers and discharge such functions as may be prescribed by or under any law made by Parliament;

(g) the rights and safeguards in respect of the autonomy of the Chogyal of Sikkim and of the institution, properties, rights and interests in respect of the Buddhist religion and the Tibetan Buddhist Sect, as mentioned in the Treaty of 1817 and the Treaty of 1819 shall be guaranteed and the Government of India shall take appropriate steps to secure the fulfilment of such guarantees;

(h) the Government of India shall secure the continuance of the existing arrangements in respect of the management of the monasteries, religious and charitable trusts and institutions in Sikkim and the protection of the interests of the Bonnets and the Bhutias in Sikkim;

(i) the Government of India shall secure the continuance of the existing arrangements for the protection of the Lepchas in Sikkim;

(j) the Government of India shall secure the continuance of the existing arrangements for the reservation of forest lands and other lands for the benefit of the Bhutias and Lepchas in Sikkim;

(k) the Government of India shall secure the continuance of the existing arrangements for the promotion of the interests of the Bhutias and Lepchas in Sikkim;

(l) the Government of India shall secure the continuance of the existing arrangements for the protection of the unique culture, language, script and tradition of Sikkim;

(m) the Government of India shall take appropriate steps for the development of the State of Sikkim and for the protection and promotion of the interests of the Sikkimese people.

(2) The Governor of Sikkim shall have special responsibility for peace and for an equitable arrangement for ensuring the social and economic advancement of different sections of the population of Sikkim and shall report to the President, from time to time, the measures taken by him for that purpose.

(3) The President may, by order, made after consultation with the Governor of Sikkim, provide for the allotment of seats in the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim to different sections of the population of Sikkim.

(4) The President may, by order, made after consultation with the Governor of Sikkim, provide for the constitution of an electoral college for the election of members of the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim.
