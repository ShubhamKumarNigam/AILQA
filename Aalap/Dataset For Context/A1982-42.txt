Summary:
The East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction (Chandigarh Amendment) Act, 1982 is an Act that further amends the East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction Act, 1949, as in force in the Union territory of Chandigarh. This Act has three sections. Section 1 states that this Act may be called the East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction (Chandigarh Amendment) Act, 1982. Section 2, which has been repealed, repeals the East Punjab Act III of 1949. Section 3, which has also been repealed, repeals the amendment of section 2. Section 4 is a special provision as to pending cases. It states that the provisions of the principal Act, as amended by this Act, shall apply to and in relation to every case, under the principal Act, for eviction of a tenant from a building let under a single tenancy for use for the purpose of business or trade and also for the purpose of residence which is pending immediately before the commencement of this Act. An explanation is provided for the purposes of this section, which defines a case relating to eviction of a tenant as pending if no order has been made in such case for the eviction or otherwise of the tenant or if an order has been made in such case for the eviction of the tenant and such order remains to be executed.
