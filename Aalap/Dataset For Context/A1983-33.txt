Summary:

The Chandigarh Disturbed Areas Act, 1983 (Act No. 33 of 1983) is a law that aims to suppress disorder and maintain public order in disturbed areas of the Union territory of Chandigarh. The Act extends to the whole of Chandigarh and came into force on October 7, 1983.

The Act defines "disturbed area" as an area declared by notification under section 3 to be a disturbed area. The Administrator may declare any part of Chandigarh as a disturbed area if he deems it necessary to maintain public order.

Under section 4, any Magistrate or Police Officer not below the rank of Sub-Inspector or Havildar in case of the Armed Branch of the Police may use force, including lethal force, against any person who is acting in contravention of any law or order for the time being in force in the disturbed area.

Section 5 empowers any Magistrate or Police Officer not below the rank of a Sub-Inspector to destroy any arms dump, fortified position, or shelter from which armed attacks are made or are likely to be made.

Under section 6, no suit, prosecution, or other legal proceedings shall be instituted against any person in respect of anything done or purporting to be done in exercise of the powers conferred by sections 4 and 5, except with the previous sanction of the Central Government.

The Chandigarh Disturbed Areas Ordinance, 1983 (6 of 1983) has been repealed by this Act, and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
