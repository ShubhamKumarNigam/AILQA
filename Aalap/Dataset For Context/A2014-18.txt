Summary:
The National Institutes of Design Act, 2014 (Act No. 18 of 2014) declares certain institutions of design to be institutions of national importance for the promotion of quality and excellence in education, research, and training in all disciplines relating to Design. The Act consists of three chapters. Chapter I, Preliminary, includes the short title and commencement, declaration of certain institutions as institutions of national importance, and definitions. Chapter II, The Institute, includes incorporation of the Institute, effect of incorporation of the Institute, powers of the Institute, Institute to be open to all races, creeds, and classes, teaching at the Institute, Visitor, authorities of the Institute, Governing Council, term of office of, vacancies among, and allowances payable to Chairperson and other members of Governing Council, meeting of Governing Council, powers and functions of Governing Council, Senate, functions of Senate, functions, powers, and duties of Chairperson, Director, Dean, Registrar, and powers and duties of other authorities and officers, Grants by Central Government, Fund of Institute, Setting up of Endowment Fund, accounts and audit, pension and provident fund, and appointment of staff. Chapter III, Miscellaneous, includes acts and proceedings not to be invalidated by vacancies, etc., sponsored schemes, power of Institute to grant degrees, etc., powers of Central Government to issue directions, Institute to be public authority under Right to Information Act, 2005, power of Central Government to make rules, transitional provisions, Statutes and Ordinances to be published in the Official Gazette and to be laid before Parliament, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and savings. The Schedule includes the name of the institutions, state, and society incorporated under this Act.
