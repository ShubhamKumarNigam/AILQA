


The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Formation of State of Nagaland.

This Act forms the State of Nagaland, comprising the territories of the Naga Hills-Tuensang Area, which was previously a part of the State of Assam. The new state will consist of three districts: Kohima, Mokokchung, and Tuensang.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Amendment of First Schedule to the Constitution.

This Act amends the First Schedule of the Constitution to include Nagaland as a separate state, and to exclude the Naga Hills-Tuensang Area from the State of Assam.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Amendment of Sixth Schedule to the Constitution.

This Act omits the Naga Hills-Tuensang Area from the Sixth Schedule to the Constitution, which deals with the administration of tribal areas.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Representation in Council of States.

This Act allots one seat to the State of Nagaland in the Council of States (the upper house of the Indian Parliament) and inserts a new entry for Nagaland in the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Representation in House of the People.

This Act allots one seat to the State of Nagaland in the House of the People (the lower house of the Indian Parliament) and creates a single parliamentary constituency for the entire state.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Provision as to sitting member.

This Act allows the sitting member of the House of the People representing the Naga Hills-Tuensang Area to continue representing the State of Nagaland in that house until a new member is elected.





The State Of Nagaland Act, 1962 

 Strength of Legislative Assembly.

This Act sets the total number of seats in the Legislative Assembly of Nagaland at 60, with 12 seats allocated to the Tuensang district and filled by members of the regional council, and the remaining 48 seats filled by direct election from assembly constituencies in the rest of the state. For the period referred to in clause (2) of article 371A, the total number of seats in the Legislative Assembly of Nagaland shall be 52, of which 12 seats shall be allocated to the Tuensang district and shall be filled by persons chosen by the members of the regional council from amongst themselves in such manner as the Governor, after consulting that council, may by notification in the Official Gazette specify. The Election Commission shall delimit the assembly constituencies in accordance with the provisions of the Constitution on the basis of the latest census figures.
