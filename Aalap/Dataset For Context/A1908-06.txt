Summary:
The Explosive Substances Act of 1908, also known as Act No. 6 of 1908, aims to regulate and prevent the misuse of explosive substances. The Act applies to the whole of India and its citizens living abroad. The Act defines an "explosive substance" as any material used or intended to be used for causing an explosion, including any apparatus, machine, implement, or material used for such purposes. The term "special category explosive substance" refers to specific explosives such as RDX, PETN, HMX, TNT, LTPE, CE, OCTOL, PEK-1, and RDX/TNT compounds, as well as any remote control devices causing explosions and other similar substances specified by the Central Government.

The Act imposes penalties for causing explosions that endanger lives or property. Under Section 3, causing an explosion using any explosive substance that is likely to endanger life or cause serious injury to property is punishable with life imprisonment or rigorous imprisonment for a term not less than ten years, along with a fine. If the explosion is caused using a special category explosive substance, the offender can be punished with death, life imprisonment, or rigorous imprisonment for a term not less than ten years, along with a fine.

Section 4 outlines the punishment for attempting to cause an explosion or for making or possessing explosives with the intent to endanger life or property. The offender can be punished with life imprisonment or rigorous imprisonment for a term not exceeding ten years, along with a fine. If the explosive substance used is a special category explosive substance, the offender can be punished with rigorous imprisonment for life or for a term not exceeding ten years, along with a fine.

Under Section 5, any person who makes or possesses explosives under suspicious circumstances can be punished with imprisonment for a term not exceeding ten years and a fine. If the explosive substance is a special category explosive substance, the offender can be punished with rigorous imprisonment for life or for a term not exceeding ten years, along with a fine.

Section 6 states that any person who abets or is an accessory to the commission of an offence under this Act shall be punished with the same punishment as provided for the offence.

No court shall proceed to the trial of any person for an offence against this Act except with the consent of the District Magistrate, as per Section 7. This Act has been extended to various territories, including Goa, Daman and Diu, Pondicherry, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, Lakshadweep, and the State of Sikkim.
