
The Service Tax Return Preparer Scheme, 2009 is a framework established by the Central Board of Excise and Customs under the Finance Act, 1994. The scheme aims to provide training, enrollment, and certification to individuals to prepare and furnish service tax returns for assessees. The scheme is applicable to all assessees and defines various terms used in the scheme, such as "Act," "Board," "Partner Organisation," "Resource Centre," "return," "rules," and "Service Tax Return Preparer."

To become a Service Tax Return Preparer, an individual must have successfully completed education up to the senior secondary level under the 10+2 education system. The Partner Organization, selected by the Board, invites applications from eligible individuals and conducts a screening, test, enrollment, training, and examination process. Those who qualify are issued a Service Tax Return Preparer Certificate and a unique identification number.

An assessee may choose to furnish their service tax return through a Service Tax Return Preparer. The preparer must prepare the return with due diligence, sign it, and furnish it to the Superintendent of Central Excise or any other authorized person. The preparer must also provide a copy of the return to the assessee and maintain records of the returns prepared and furnished.

The assessee has the duty to give consent to the preparer, ensure the facts in the return are true and correct, and verify and sign the return before submission. The Service Tax Return Preparer must prepare the return with due diligence, sign it, and furnish it to the authorized person. The preparer must also provide a copy of the return to the assessee, maintain records, and submit a monthly statement of particulars to the Resource Centre.

The Partner Organization is responsible for implementing the scheme, maintaining the profile of the Service Tax Return Preparers, monitoring their performance, and performing functions to the satisfaction of the Resource Centre. The Resource Centre is responsible for day-to-day administration of the scheme, specifying the number of persons to be enrolled, the number of centers for training, the curriculum, maintaining particulars of Service Tax Return Preparers, and any other function assigned by the Board.

The Resource Centre may warn or cancel the certificate of a Service Tax Return Preparer for various reasons, including failure to provide a copy of the return to the assessee, making repeated mistakes, financial irregularity, forgery, fraud, or involving in any other irregularity that is grave in nature. A Service Tax Return Preparer may continue to act as such unless their certificate is suspended or withdrawn by the Resource Centre or the scheme is withdrawn by the Board.
