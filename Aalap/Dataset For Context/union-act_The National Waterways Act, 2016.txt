Summary:
The National Waterways Act, 2016 is a document that specifies the existence and regulation of national waterways in India. The Act declares certain inland waterways as national waterways (section 2), and it also gives the Union control over these waterways for the purpose of shipping and navigation (section 3). The Act also amends the Inland Waterways Authority of India Act, 1985 to include the new national waterways (section 4). Furthermore, the Act repeals certain enactments related to national waterways and states that any actions taken under these repealed Acts will be considered valid under the provisions of this Act (section 5). The Act came into force on a date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
