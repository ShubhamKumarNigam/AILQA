Summary:
The University Grants Commission Act, 1956, establishes the University Grants Commission to coordinate and determine standards in universities and provide funding to universities established or incorporated by or under a Central Act, a Provincial Act, or a State Act. The Commission is composed of a Chairman, a Vice-Chairman, and ten other members appointed by the Central Government. The Chairman and Vice-Chairman are whole-time and salaried positions, while other members serve on a part-time basis. The Commission is responsible for promoting and coordinating university education, determining and maintaining standards of teaching, examination, and research in universities, and allocating and disbursing grants to universities. The Commission may also advise the Central Government or any State Government on the allocation of grants to universities and advise on the establishment of new universities or the expansion of existing universities. The Act also includes provisions for the regulation of fees and prohibition of donations in certain cases, and the establishment of a Fund to finance the activities of the Commission.
