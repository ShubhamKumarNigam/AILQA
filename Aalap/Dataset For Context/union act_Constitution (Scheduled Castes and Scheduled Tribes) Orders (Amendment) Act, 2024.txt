Summary:
The Constitution (Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes) Orders (Amendment) Act, 2024 is an Act that modifies the list of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in relation to the State of Odisha. The Act is divided into four sections, with the first section being the short title, and the second section providing definitions for terms used in the Act.

The third section of the Act amends the Scheduled Castes Order, 1950 by omitting entries 87 and 88 from Part XIII-Odisha. The fourth section amends the Scheduled Tribes Order, 1950 by making several changes to Part XII-Orissa. These changes include updating the name of the region from "Orissa" to "Odisha", adding and modifying entries for various groups, and adding two new entries for the Muka Dora and Konda Reddy communities in the undivided Koraput district.

Overall, the purpose of the Act is to update and modify the list of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the State of Odisha in order to accurately reflect the current communities and groups that are included in these categories.
