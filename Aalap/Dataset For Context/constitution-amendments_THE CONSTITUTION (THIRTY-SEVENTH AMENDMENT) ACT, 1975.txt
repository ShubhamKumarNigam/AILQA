Amendment of article 240.-

In article 240 of the Constitution, in clause (1), after the words "Union territory of Arunachal Pradesh", the words "or, as the case may be, when the Legislative Assembly for the Union territory is dissolved or its functioning remains suspended," shall be inserted.


 
This amendment act is related to the Constitution of India and specifically focuses on amending article 240. The article pertains to the President's power to make regulations for the Union territory of Arunachal Pradesh. The amendment aims to modify the President's regulatory powers by adding a clause that restricts the President's power to make regulations when the Legislative Assembly for the Union territory is not dissolved or its functioning is not suspended. This amendment aims to align the regulatory powers for Arunachal Pradesh with those of other Union territories with Legislatures.
