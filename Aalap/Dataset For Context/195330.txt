Summary:
The Andhra State Act, 1953, is an Act of the Indian Parliament that provides for the formation of the State of Andhra, the increasing of the area of the State of Mysore, and the diminishing of the area of the State of Madras. The Act is divided into five parts. Part I, titled "Preliminary," contains the short title and commencement of the Act, as well as definitions of terms used throughout the Act. Part II, titled "Formation of Andhra State and Transfer of Territory from Madras to Mysore," outlines the formation of the State of Andhra and the transfer of territory from the State of Madras to the State of Mysore. Part III, titled "Representation in the Legislatures," deals with the representation of the States of Andhra, Madras, and Mysore in the Council of States and the House of the People. Part IV, titled "High Courts," establishes a separate High Court for the State of Andhra and deals with the jurisdiction, powers, and procedures of the High Court of Andhra. Part V, titled "Financial Provisions," deals with the authorization of expenditure, the vote on account by the Madras Legislative Assembly, the authorization under the Madras Appropriation Act, reports relating to the accounts of Madras State, the apportionment of assets and liabilities, contracts, liability in respect of actionable wrongs, liability as guarantor, power of the President to order allocation or adjustment in certain cases, and certain expenditure to be charged on the Consolidated Fund of the State. Part VI, titled "Legal Provisions," deals with the territorial extent of laws, power to adapt laws, power to construe laws, power to name authorities, etc., for exercising statutory functions, legal proceedings concerning the State of Madras, and provisions as to certain pending proceedings. Part VII, titled "Administrative and Miscellaneous Provisions," deals with provisions for detention in, and power to commit to, certain jails and other institutions, continuance of facilities in certain State institutions, provisions relating to the Indian Administrative Service and the Indian Police Service, provisions relating to the Indian Civil Service, the Indian Police, the Indian Service of Engineers and the Indian Forest Service, provisions relating to other Services, power of the President to give directions, report of the Madras Public Service Commission, special provisions with regard to Tungabhadra Project, allowances and privileges of the Governor of Andhra, effect of provisions of the Act inconsistent with other laws, and power to remove difficulties. The Act also includes several schedules, including the First Schedule, which deals with the Andhra Members of the Council of States; the Second Schedule, which deals with the modification of the Delimitation of Parliamentary and Assembly Constituencies (Madras) Order, 1951; the Third Schedule, which deals with the modification of the Delimitation of Council Constituencies (Madras) Order, 1951; the Fourth Schedule, which deals with the list of members of the Madras Legislative Council; the Fifth Schedule, which deals with the modification of the Delimitation of Council Constituencies (Mysore) Order, 1951; the Sixth Schedule, which deals with modifications in the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, and the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950; and the Seventh Schedule, which deals with provisions as to apportionment of assets and liabilities between Madras, Andhra, and Mysore.
