
The Electricity (Removal Of Difficulty)third Order, 2005 is an order made by the Central Government in exercise of its powers conferred by section 183 of the Electricity Act, 2003. This order was made to address difficulties that have arisen in respect of the disposal of free electricity received by State Governments from hydro power generating companies.

The order allows State Governments to have discretion in disposing of free electricity received from hydro power generating stations in a manner they deem fit, as long as it is not inconsistent with the provisions of the Act. If the electricity is sold by the State Government to a distribution licensee, the concerned State Commission will have the power to regulate the price at which such electricity is procured by the distribution licensee.

This order helps to clarify the powers of State Governments and State Commissions in relation to the disposal of free electricity received from hydro power generating stations and the regulation of the price at which such electricity is procured by distribution licensees.
