Summary:

The Delhi High Court Act, 1966 establishes a High Court for the Union territory of Delhi, and extends the jurisdiction of that High Court to the Union territory of Himachal Pradesh. The Act includes definitions of terms used in the Act, and specifies the jurisdiction and powers of the High Court of Delhi. The Act also includes provisions for the transfer of proceedings from the High Court of Punjab to the High Court of Delhi, and for the practice and procedure in the High Court of Delhi. The Act also specifies the custody of the Seal of the High Court of Delhi, the form of writs and other processes, and the powers of Judges. The Act also includes provisions for the procedure as to appeals to the Supreme Court, and for the transfer of proceedings from the High Court of Punjab to the High Court of Delhi. The Act also includes provisions for the right to appear or to act in proceedings transferred to the High Court of Delhi, and for interpretation and savings. The Act also includes provisions for pending proceedings before subordinate courts in Delhi, and for the extension of the jurisdiction of the High Court of Delhi. The Act also includes a rule of construction, and provisions for the removal of difficulties and for the power to adapt laws. The Act also includes a schedule, which has been repealed. The Act was enacted on 5th September, 1966, and came into force on the date appointed by the Central Government.
