Summary:

The Standards of Weights and Measures (Approval of Models) Rules, 1987 outline the procedures for the approval of models of weights and measures in India. The rules define important terms such as "Act," "Director," "Model," and "Principal Officer." The rules also establish general provisions for laboratories, including the recognition of laboratories and the submission of models for approval. The rules specify the information required in an application for the approval of a model, including a description of the weight or measure, data regarding metrological and technical characteristics, and test procedures followed by the manufacturer. The rules also outline the tests needed for the assessment of the fitness or otherwise of a model submitted for approval, as well as the procedure for the issue of a certificate of approval. The rules also specify the mark of approval that must be affixed to the approved model and each weight or measure produced in accordance with the approved model.
