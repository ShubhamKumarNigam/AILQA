Summary:

THE BIRD AND COMPANY LIMITED (ACQUISITION AND TRANSFER OF UNDERTAKINGS AND OTHER PROPERTIES) ACT, 1980

Introduction:
This Act, enacted on December 27, 1980, aims to acquire and transfer the undertakings of the Bird and Company Limited to ensure the continuity of production of goods vital to the country's needs. The Act also seeks to acquire shares held by the Bird and Company Limited in specified companies to secure facilities and advantages for the undertakings and enable the Central Government to control the affairs of those companies.

Key Provisions:

Chapter I: Preliminary

* This chapter defines various terms used in the Act.
* The Act short title is "The Bird and Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings and Other Properties) Act, 1980," and it came into force on October 25, 1980.

Chapter II: Acquisition and Transfer of the Undertakings of the Company and of Shares Held by the Company in the Specified Companies

* This chapter deals with the transfer and vesting of the undertakings of the Bird and Company Limited and its shares in specified companies.
* The undertakings of the Company and the right, title, and interest of the Company in relation to its undertakings stand transferred to and vest in the Central Government.
* The shares held by the Company in the specified companies also stand transferred to and vest in the Central Government.
* The Central Government shall be deemed to have been registered in the Register of Members of the concerned specified company as the holder of each share transferred to it.

Chapter III: Payment of Amounts

* This chapter deals with the payment of amounts to the Company and specified companies.
* The Central Government shall pay an aggregate amount of rupees two hundred and eighty-three lakhs for the transfer and vesting of the undertakings of the Company and an aggregate amount of rupees twenty-seven lakhs for the transfer and vesting of the shares held by the Company in the specified companies.

Chapter IV: Management, etc., of the Undertakings of the Company

* This chapter deals with the management and control of the undertakings of the Company.
* The general superintendence, direction, control, and management of the affairs and business of the undertakings of the Company shall vest in the Central Government or a Government company.

Chapter V: Provisions Relating to Employees of the Company

* This chapter deals with the continuance of employees of the Company.
* Every employee of the Company shall become an employee of the Central Government or a Government company.

Chapter VI: Commissioner of Payments

* This chapter deals with the appointment of a Commissioner of Payments for disbursing the amounts payable to the Company.
* The Central Government shall, for the purpose of disbursing the amounts payable to the Company, by notification, appoint a Commissioner of Payments.

Chapter VII: Miscellaneous

* This chapter deals with various miscellaneous provisions.
* The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force.

Conclusion:

The Bird and Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings and Other Properties) Act, 1980, is a significant legislation aimed at acquiring and transferring the undertakings of the Bird and Company Limited to ensure the continuity of production of goods vital to the country's needs. The Act also seeks to acquire shares held by the Bird and Company Limited in specified companies to secure facilities and advantages for the undertakings and enable the Central Government to control the affairs of those companies. The Act contains various provisions related to the transfer and vesting of undertakings, payment of amounts, management, and control of undertakings, and provisions relating to employees of the Company. The Act also contains various miscellaneous provisions.
