Summary:
THE HAJ COMMITTEE ACT, 2002 establishes a Haj Committee of India and State Haj Committees for making arrangements for the pilgrimage of Muslims for Haj. The Act includes definitions of terms used in the Act, such as bye-laws, Chief Executive Officer or Executive Officer, member, notification, pilgrim, prescribed, State Committee, and State Government. The Act also includes provisions for the establishment, composition, and duties of the Haj Committee of India, as well as provisions for the appointment, term of office, and disqualification of members. The Act also includes provisions for the establishment, composition, and duties of State Haj Committees, as well as provisions for the appointment, term of office, and disqualification of members. The Act also includes provisions for the finance, accounts, and audit of the Committees. The Act includes miscellaneous provisions, such as the power to amend the Schedule, redressal of grievances, vesting of properties and other rights, etc., in Committees, power to make rules, power to make bye-laws, power to delegate, power to make rules by State Governments, provisions as to employees of the existing Committee before the commencement of this Act, protection of action taken in good faith, power to remove difficulties, power to give directions, and repeal. The Schedule to the Act lists the zones comprising contiguous States or Union territories.
