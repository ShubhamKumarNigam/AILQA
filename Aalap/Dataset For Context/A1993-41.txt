Summary:

The Beedi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Amendment Act, 1993, is an Act that amends the Beedi and Cigar Workers (Conditions of Employment) Act, 1966. The Act includes several amendments to the principal Act, such as:

1. Section 1: The Act will come into force on a date appointed by the State Government in a State, and different dates may be appointed for different areas and provisions of the Act.
2. Section 2: The definition of certain terms in the principal Act has been amended. These include the addition of the term "godown" and the expansion of the definition of "establishment" to include a "go down attached thereto."
3. Section 3: A new section has been inserted to prohibit inspectors from disclosing the source of any complaint made to them. Inspectors also cannot disclose to the employer or contractor that an inspection is being made in pursuance of a complaint.
4. Section 4: The minimum number of days required for qualifying as an adult worker has been reduced from fifty to thirty.
5. Section 5: An explanation has been added to clarify how overtime rates should be calculated in cases where an employee had not worked on any day of the week immediately preceding the week in which the overtime work has been done.
6. Section 6: A copy of every notice exhibited in the industrial premises must be sent to the Inspector having jurisdiction over the industrial premises within two weeks.
7. Section 7: The appellate authority has been given the same powers as a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, in respect of enforcing the attendance of any person, examining him on oath, and compelling the production of documents and material objects.
8. Section 8: The maximum period for which an employer can be imprisoned for non-compliance with the provisions of the Act has been increased from three months to six months, and the maximum fine has been increased from five hundred rupees to five thousand rupees.
9. Section 9: The time within which a dispute specified in sub-section (2) of section 39 shall be referred for settlement, the authority by which and the summary manner in which such dispute shall be settled have been amended.
10. Section 10: The words "authority by which and the manner in which a dispute as to the issue of raw materials shall be settled" have been replaced with "the time within which a dispute specified in sub-section (2) of section 39 shall be referred for settlement, the authority by which and the summary manner in which such dispute shall be settled."

These amendments aim to improve the working conditions of beedi and cigar workers in India and ensure that employers comply with the provisions of the Act.
