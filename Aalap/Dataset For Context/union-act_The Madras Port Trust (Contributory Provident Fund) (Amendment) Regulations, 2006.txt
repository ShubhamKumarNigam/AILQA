Summary:

The Madras Port Trust (Contributory Provident Fund) (Amendment) Regulations, 2006, are a set of rules that amend the Madras Port Trust (Contributory Provident Fund) Regulations, 1997. These amendments will come into force from the date of publication of the Regulation in the Official Gazette. The amendments include the deletion of Regulation 5, which was titled "Provident Fund Reserve Fund" in the principal regulations. The Madras Port Trust (Contributory Provident Fund) Regulations, 1997, were approved by the Central Government and notified in the Gazette of India vide Ministry of Surface Transports Notification No. G.S.R. 281(E), dated 27-5-1997.
