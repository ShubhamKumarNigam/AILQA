THE DELHI LANDS (RESTRICTIONS ON TRANSFER) ACT, 1972

The Delhi Lands (Restrictions on Transfer) Act, 1972, is an Act that imposes certain restrictions on the transfer of lands in the Union territory of Delhi. The Act aims to prevent large-scale transactions of purported transfers or transfers of lands to unwary public.

The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including the "Administrator," "competent authority," "Development Act," "prescribed," and "Scheme."

Under section 3, no person shall transfer any land or part thereof situated in Delhi, which has been acquired by the Central Government under the Land Acquisition Act, 1984, or under any other law providing for acquisition of land for a public purpose.

Section 4 prohibits the transfer of any land or part thereof situated in Delhi, which is proposed to be acquired in connection with the Scheme, without the previous permission in writing of the competent authority.

Any person desiring to transfer any land referred to in section 4 may make an application in writing to the competent authority containing such particulars as may be prescribed. The competent authority may grant or refuse to grant the permission applied for based on specific grounds mentioned in section 5(3).

An appeal against the order of the competent authority may be filed to the prescribed authority within thirty days of the date of receipt of the order. The prescribed authority shall dispose of the appeal as expeditiously as possible, and the order made by the prescribed authority shall be final.

Where the competent authority has made any order refusing to grant permission to transfer any land, the order shall be in operation only for a period of three years from the date of the order made by the competent authority.

If any person contravenes the provisions of section 3 or section 4, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to three years or with fine or with both.

The Act also includes provisions for offences by companies and the power to make rules.
