Summary:

The Calcutta Metro Railway (Operation and Maintenance) Temporary Provisions Act, 1985 (Act No. 10 of 1985) is an Act that makes temporary provisions for the operation and maintenance of the Calcutta metro railway. The Act includes definitions of key terms, such as "Calcutta metro railway administration" or "metro railway administration," "commissioner," "Construction Act," "metro railway," and "prescribed."

The Act is divided into three chapters. Chapter I, "Preliminary," includes two sections. Section 1 outlines the short title, commencement, and application of the Act. Section 2 provides definitions of terms used in the Act.

Chapter II, "The Calcutta Metro Railway Administration," includes two sections. Section 3 states that the Calcutta metro railway administration is responsible for the operation and maintenance of the metro railway. Section 4 states that no metro railway shall be opened for the public carriage of passengers except with the previous sanction of the Central Government.

Chapter III, "Special Provisions for the Running of the Metro Railway," includes six sections. Section 5 states that no person shall, while travelling in the metro railway, carry with him any goods other than a small baggage containing personal belongings not exceeding such volume and weight as may be prescribed. Section 6 states that it shall not be necessary for the metro railway administration to reserve any compartment in any train for the exclusive use of females. Section 7 states that no person shall take or cause to be taken any dangerous or offensive goods upon the metro railway. Section 8 states that if any person takes or causes to be taken any offensive goods upon the metro railway, he shall be punishable with fine which may extend to five hundred rupees. If any person takes or causes to be taken any dangerous goods upon the metro railway, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to four years and with fine which may extend to five thousand rupees. Section 9 states that no person shall smoke in any compartment or carriage of the metro railway or in any underground metro railway station. Section 10 states that if any person is in a state of intoxication or commits any nuisance or act of indecency, or uses obscene or abusive language or wilfully or without excuse interferes in any way with the comfort of any passenger in any carriage or upon any part of the metro railway, he shall be punishable with fine which may extend to two hundred and fifty rupees and shall also be liable to forfeiture of the fare which he may have paid or any pass or ticket which he may have obtained or purchased, or be removed from such carriage or part by any metro railway official authorised by the metro railway administration in this behalf. Section 11 states that no demonstration of any kind whatsoever shall be held on any part of the metro railway or other premises thereof and it shall be open to the metro railway administration to exclude from such premises any person attending such demonstrations whether or not he is in possession of a pass or ticket entitling him to be in the said premises. Section 12 states that if any passenger travels on the roof of a train or persists in travelling in any part of a train not intended for the use of passengers or projects any part of his body out of a train after being warned by any metro railway official to desist, he shall be punishable with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one month, or with fine which may extend to fifty rupees, or with both.

This document outlines various sections of an Indian law related to the metro railway. The laws cover a range of offenses and penalties, including:

* Section 13: Penalty for unlawfully entering or remaining upon the metro railway or walking on the metro railway line. The penalty for this offense is imprisonment for up to three months, a fine of up to 250 rupees, or both.
* Section 14: Endangering the safety of passengers. A metro railway official who endangers the safety of a passenger through rash or negligent acts or omissions, or by disobeying rules or orders, is punishable with imprisonment for up to five years, a fine of up to 6,000 rupees, or both.
* Section 15: Abandoning train, etc., without authority. A metro railway official who abandons his duty before reaching a designated station or place without authority or without properly handing over the train or rolling stock to another authorized official is punishable with imprisonment for up to four years, a fine of up to 5,000 rupees, or both.
* Section 16: Obstructing running of trains, etc. A person who obstructs or attempts to obstruct a train or other rolling stock on the metro railway, or interferes with the working mechanism, is liable to be removed by an authorized official and punishable with imprisonment for up to four years, a fine of up to 5,000 rupees, or both.
* Section 17: Offenses by companies. If a company commits an offense under this Act, every person who was in charge of and responsible for the conduct of the company at the time the offense was committed, as well as the company, is deemed to be guilty of the offense and liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly.
* Section 18: Application of Act 9 of 1890 and the rules, etc., made thereunder to the metro railway. The provisions of the Indian Railways Act, 1890, and the rules, orders, or notifications made or issued thereunder, apply to the operation and maintenance of the metro railway, subject to modifications as may be necessary.
* Section 19: Effect of Act and rules, etc., inconsistent with other enactments. The provisions of this Act or any rule made or any notification issued thereunder shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any enactment other than this Act or in any instrument having effect by virtue of any enactment other than this Act.
* Section 20: Protection of action taken in good faith. No suit, prosecution, or other legal proceeding shall lie against the Central Government, the metro railway administration, or any officer or other employee of that Government or the metro railway administration for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done under this Act.
* Section 21: Power to remove difficulties. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to the provisions of this Act, the Central Government may, by order, do anything not inconsistent with such provisions which appears to it to be necessary or expedient for the purpose of removing the difficulty.
* Section 22: Power to make rules. The Central Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act, including rules regarding the terms and conditions of service of the officers and other employees of the metro railway administration, the volume and weight of baggage containing personal belongings that may be carried by a person while traveling in the metro railway, and any other matter which is required to be, or may be, prescribed.
* Section 23: Repeal and saving. The Calcutta Metro Railway (Operation and Maintenance) Temporary Provisions Ordinance, 1984, is repealed, but anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
