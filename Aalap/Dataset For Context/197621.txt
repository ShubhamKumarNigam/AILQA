Summary:

The Regional Rural Banks Act, 1976, establishes Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) with the objective of developing the rural economy by providing credit and other facilities to small and marginal farmers, agricultural laborers, artisans, and small entrepreneurs in rural areas. The Act defines key terms, including "Board," "Chairman," "director," "National Bank," "notified area," "prescribed," "Regional Rural Bank," "Sponsor Bank," "State Government," and references to other Acts.

Chapter I, Preliminary, includes the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. It also provides definitions for various terms used throughout the Act.

Chapter II, Incorporation and Capital of Regional Rural Banks, discusses the establishment and incorporation of RRBs, their offices and agencies, authorized capital, issued capital, and the shares to be approved securities.

Chapter III, Management, outlines the management of RRBs, including the Board of directors, term of office of directors, Chairman, disqualifications, vacation of the seat of directors, meetings of the Board, committees of the Board, fees and allowances of directors and members of committees, and staff of Regional Rural Banks.

Chapter IV, Business of a Regional Rural Bank, specifies the business that an RRB may transact, including granting loans and advances to small and marginal farmers, agricultural laborers, artisans, small entrepreneurs, and co-operative societies.

Chapter V, Accounts and Audit, requires RRBs to close and balance their accounts annually and appoint auditors for the audit of their accounts. It also outlines the duties and powers of auditors, the preparation of annual reports, and the disposal of profits.

Chapter VA, Amalgamation of Regional Rural Banks, allows the Central Government to provide for the amalgamation of two or more RRBs into a single RRB with specified constitution, property, powers, rights, interests, authorities, and privileges, and with specified liabilities, duties, and obligations.

Chapter VI, Miscellaneous, includes provisions related to the power of the Central Government to give directions, inspection, audit, and scrutiny by the Sponsor Bank, obligations as to fidelity and secrecy, bar to liquidation of Regional Rural Banks, defects in appointment or constitution not to invalidate acts or proceedings, indemnity of directors, etc., power to make rules, power to make regulations, removal of difficulties, and act to override the provisions of other laws.

The Schedule to the Act includes a declaration of fidelity and secrecy to be made by every director, member of a committee, auditor, officer, employee, or any other person appointed or designated to perform duties for an RRB.
