 Offences by companies .-If the person committing an offence under this Act is a company, every person who, at the time the offence was committed, was in charge of, and was responsible to, the company for the conduct of the business of the company, as well as the company, shall be deemed to be guilty of the offence and shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished accordingly:

Provided that nothing contained in this section shall render any such person liable to any punishment under this Act, if he proves that the offence was committed without his knowledge or that he exercised all due diligence to prevent the commission of such offence.

Summary:

The Elephants Preservation Act, 1879 is an Act to prevent the indiscriminate destruction of wild elephants in British Burma. The Act prohibits the killing, injuring, or capture of wild elephants, except in certain cases such as self-defense, when the elephant is found damaging property or in the immediate vicinity of main public roads, railways, canals, or as permitted by a license under the Act. The Act also provides for the grant of licenses for shooting wild male elephants upon waste or forest lands that are the property of the Government, and for the making of rules as to the grant and renewal of such licenses. The Act applies to the territories now administered by the Lieutenant-Governor of the North-Western Provinces and the Chief Commissioners of Oudh, the Central Provinces, and Coorg, and can be extended to any other local area by notification in the Official Gazette. The Act imposes a penalty for contravening the provisions of the Act, and also provides for the punishment of any person who breaks any condition contained in a license granted under the Act. The Act also provides for the production and showing of licenses on the requisition of certain officers, and imposes a penalty for refusal or inability to produce such license. The Act also provides for the punishment of offences committed by companies, and for the protection of public servants from being compelled to disclose any communication made to them in official confidence.
