Summary:

THE PRE-CONCEPTION AND PRE-NATAL DIAGNOSTIC TECHNIQUES (PROHIBITION OF SEX SELECTION) ACT, 1994

This Act, enacted on September 20, 1994, aims to prohibit sex selection before or after conception and regulate pre-natal diagnostic techniques for detecting genetic abnormalities, metabolic disorders, chromosomal abnormalities, congenital malformations, or sex-linked disorders. The Act prohibits the determination of the sex of the foetus and the misuse of pre-natal diagnostic techniques for sex selection.

Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," includes sections related to the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act.

Chapter II, titled "Regulation of Genetic Counselling Centres, Genetic Laboratories and Genetic Clinics," includes sections related to the regulation of these centers and clinics, as well as the prohibition of sex selection.

Chapter III, titled "Regulation of Pre-Natal Diagnostic Techniques," includes sections related to the regulation of pre-natal diagnostic techniques and the conditions under which they can be conducted.

Chapter IV, titled "Central Supervisory Board," includes sections related to the constitution, functions, and powers of the Central Supervisory Board.

Chapter V, titled "Appropriate Authority and Advisory Committee," includes sections related to the appointment and functions of the Appropriate Authority and the Advisory Committee.

Chapter VI, titled "Registration of Genetic Counselling Centres, Genetic Laboratories and Genetic Clinics," includes sections related to the registration and certification of these centers and clinics.

Chapter VII, titled "Offences and Penalties," includes sections related to the prohibition of advertisement relating to pre-conception and pre-natal determination of sex and punishment for contravention, as well as other offences and penalties.

Chapter VIII, titled "Miscellaneous," includes sections related to the maintenance of records, power to search and seize records, etc., protection of action taken in good faith, removal of difficulties, power to make rules, power to make regulations, and rules and regulations to be laid before Parliament.

The Schedule to the Act includes provisions related to the constitution of the Central Supervisory Board.

The Act aims to prevent the misuse of pre-natal diagnostic techniques for sex selection and to ensure that these techniques are used only for the purpose of detecting genetic abnormalities, metabolic disorders, chromosomal abnormalities, congenital malformations, or sex-linked disorders. The Act also aims to create public awareness against the practice of pre-conception sex selection and pre-natal determination of sex leading to female foeticide.
