




The National Capital Region Planning Board Rules, 1985, were made by the Central Government in exercise of the powers conferred by section 36 of the National Capital Region Planning Board Act, 1985 (2 of 1985). These rules provide the procedure for the constitution and functioning of the National Capital Region Planning Board, which is responsible for the planning and development of the National Capital Region.

The rules specify the composition of the Board, which includes the Union Minister of Works and Housing as the Chairman, the Chief Ministers of the participating States (Haryana, Rajasthan, and Uttar Pradesh), the Administrator of the Union Territory of Delhi, and other members nominated by the Government. The rules also provide for the terms and conditions of service of the members of the Board, including the disqualification and resignation of members.

The rules also provide for the constitution of the Planning Committee, which is responsible for preparing the draft regional plan for the National Capital Region. The Planning Committee consists of the Member-Secretary to the Board, the Joint Secretary to the Government of India in the Ministry of Works and Housing dealing with the work relating to the National Capital Region Planning Board, the Secretary to the Government of each participating State and the Union Territory in charge of the work relating to the National Capital Region Planning Board, the Vice Chairman, Delhi Development Authority, the Chief Planner, Town and Country Planning Organization, New Delhi, and the Director, Town and Country Planning or the Chief Town Planner, as the case may be, of each participating State.

The rules provide for the conduct of meetings of the Board and the Planning Committee, including the notice of meetings, the inclusion of items for discussion, the quorum, the presiding officer, voting, and the minutes of proceedings. The rules also provide for the conditions of service of the Member-Secretary and the fees and allowances to non-official members.

The rules also provide for the preparation and submission of the budget estimates of the Board, the opening of an account and operation of the fund of the Board, and the accounts and audit of the Board. The rules also provide for the grant-in-aid and loans to the participating State Governments or the Union territory administration or the State Governments having a counter-magnet area and the local authorities, urban development authorities, housing boards and such other authorities of the State Government or the Union territory or other State Government concerned as the case may be, for implementing the sub-regional plans and project plans or for developing the counter-magnet area.

In summary, the National Capital Region Planning Board Rules, 1985, provide the procedure for the constitution and functioning of the National Capital Region Planning Board, which is responsible for the planning and development of the National Capital Region. The rules specify the composition of the Board, the terms and conditions of service of the members, the constitution of the Planning Committee, the conduct of meetings, the preparation and submission of the budget estimates, the accounts and audit, and the grant-in-aid and loans to the participating State Governments or the Union territory administration or the State Governments having a counter-magnet area and the local authorities, urban development authorities, housing boards and such other authorities of the State Government or the Union territory or other State Government concerned as the case may be, for implementing the sub-regional plans and project plans or for developing the counter-magnet area.
