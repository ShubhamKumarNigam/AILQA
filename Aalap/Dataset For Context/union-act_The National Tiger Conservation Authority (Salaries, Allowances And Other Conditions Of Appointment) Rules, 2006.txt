The National Tiger Conservation Authority (Salaries, Allowances And Other Conditions Of Appointment) Rules, 2006, outline the regulations for the salaries, allowances, and other conditions of appointment for the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, members, and Member-Secretary of the National Tiger Conservation Authority. These rules were made in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (gii) of sub-section (1) of section 63 read with sub-section (4) of section 38-M of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972 (53 of 1972). The rules define various terms used in the Act and specify the travelling and other allowances for the Chairperson, Vice-Chairperson, and members. They also outline the appointment, terms, and other conditions of service for the Member-Secretary. The Member-Secretary must be an Inspector-General of Forests or an officer of equivalent rank in the Indian Forest Service with at least ten years of experience in a tiger reserve or wildlife management. The Member-Secretary is entitled to a salary in the pay scale of rupees 18400-500-22400/- and allowances, leave, provident fund, pension, official accommodation, and medical facilities as are admissible to officers of the Central Government belonging to Group `A' of the corresponding scale of pay.
