The Act includes provisions related to the composition, powers, and functions of Gram Panchayats, Panchayat Samitis, and Zilla Parishads.

The Act specifies the rules for the election and removal of members, Adhyakshas (presidents), and Up-Adhyakshas (vice-presidents) of Zilla Parishads and Pradhans and Up-Pradhans of Gram Panchayats. It also lays down the procedure for the conduct of meetings and decision-making processes of PRIs.

The Act includes provisions related to the finances of PRIs, including the maintenance of accounts, preparation of budgets, and the collection of taxes. It also specifies the limits of taxes that can be levied by a Zilla Parishad.

The Act lays down the rules for the appointment, terms, and conditions of service of the Secretary of a Gram Panchayat and the Chief Executive Officer, Chief Accounts Officer, Chief Planning Officer, and other staff members of a Zilla Parishad.

The Act includes provisions related to the superintendence and control of the affairs of Zilla Parishads by the Chief Executive Officer. It also specifies the powers, functions, and duties of Adhyakshas and Up-Adhyakshas of Zilla Parishads.

The Act includes provisions related to the appointment of members of the Finance Commission.

The Act includes provisions related to the bar to interference by courts in electoral matters and the taking of oath by members, Adhyakshas or Up-Adhyakshas of the Zilla Parishad and every Member, Pradhan or Up-Pradhan of a Gram Panchayat.

The Act repeals the Manipur Panchayati Raj Act, 1975 and includes provisions for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of this Act.
