Summary:
The National Capital Territory of Delhi Laws (Special Provisions) Act, 2011 is an Act to make special provisions for the National Capital Territory of Delhi for a further period up to the 31st day of December, 2011. The Act aims to address the phenomenal increase in population in Delhi, which has resulted in tremendous pressure on land and infrastructure, leading to encroachment and unauthorized developments. The Act specifically provides for strategies for housing for urban poor and dealing with the informal sector. The Act also includes provisions for regulation of urban street vendors and regularization of unauthorized colonies. The Act provides for a scheme for relocation and rehabilitation of slum dwellers and jhuggi-jhompri clusters, and the Delhi Urban Shelter Improvement Board Act, 2010 has been enacted for its implementation. The Act also includes provisions for regularization of schools, dispensaries, religious institutions, and cultural institutions. The Act ceases to have effect on the 31st day of December, 2011, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such cesser. The Act defines various terms such as building bye-laws, Delhi, encroachment, local authority, Master Plan, notification, punitive action, relevant law, and unauthorized development. The Act prohibits punitive action against encroachment or unauthorized development during the period of operation of this Act. The Act also provides for power of Central Government to give directions and validation of acts done or omitted to be done during 1st January, 2011 up to the date of commencement of this Act.
