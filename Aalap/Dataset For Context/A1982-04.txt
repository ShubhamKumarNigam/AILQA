Summary:
The Sugar Development Fund Act, 1982, was enacted to provide financing for the development of the sugar industry in India. The Act establishes a Sugar Development Fund, which is funded by the proceeds of the duty of excise levied and collected under the Sugar Cess Act, 1982, reduced by the cost of collection, and any other moneys received by the Central Government for the purposes of this Act.

The Fund is to be applied by the Central Government for making loans and grants for the rehabilitation, modernization, and development of sugar factories and sugarcane farming, research projects aimed at developing the sugar industry, building and maintaining buffer stocks of sugar, promoting sugar exports, and providing financial assistance to sugar factories towards interest on loans.

Applications for loans or grants must be made to the Committee, which is constituted under section 6 of the Act. The Committee's composition and functioning are to be prescribed by the Central Government. The Central Government may also require an occupier of a sugar factory to furnish statistical and other information for the purposes of this Act.

The Central Government may make rules for carrying out the provisions of this Act, including the manner and form of making loans and grants, the manner and form of applications, the composition and functioning of the Committee, the form and period of furnishing statistical and other information, and any other matter required to be prescribed. Every rule made under this section shall be laid before each House of Parliament for a total period of thirty days, and if both Houses agree in making any modification in the rule or both Houses agree that the rule should not be made, the rule shall have effect only in such modified form or be of no effect, as the case may be.

In summary, the Sugar Development Fund Act, 1982, provides financing for the development of the sugar industry in India, with the Central Government making loans and grants for the rehabilitation, modernization, and development of sugar factories and sugarcane farming, research projects aimed at developing the sugar industry, building and maintaining buffer stocks of sugar, promoting sugar exports, and providing financial assistance to sugar factories towards interest on loans. The Act establishes a Committee to consider applications for loans and grants and provides for the making of rules for carrying out the provisions of the Act.
