The Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017, published by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, govern the conservation and management of wetlands in India. The rules define various terms related to wetlands, such as "wetland," "ecological character," "wise use of wetlands," and "zone of influence." The rules apply to wetlands or wetland complexes that are categorized as 'wetlands of international importance' under the Ramsar Convention or notified by the Central Government, State Government, or Union Territory Administration. However, the rules do not apply to wetlands falling in areas covered under certain other laws and regulations.

The rules prohibit certain activities within wetlands, including conversion for non-wetland uses, setting up of industries, solid waste dumping, discharge of untreated wastes and effluents, and poaching. The rules also establish Wetlands Authorities at the State and Union Territory levels to conserve and manage wetlands. These authorities have the power to prepare lists of wetlands, define strategies for their conservation and wise use, review integrated management plans, and enforce regulations. The rules also establish a National Wetlands Committee to advise the Central Government on policies and action programs for the conservation and wise use of wetlands.

Under the rules, the State Government or Union Territory Administration is required to prepare a Brief Document for each wetland identified for notification, which includes information on the wetland's boundary, ecological character, pre-existing rights and privileges, and lists of permitted and regulated activities. The Authority makes recommendations to the State Government or Union Territory Administration for notifying the wetlands, and the wetlands are notified in the Official Gazette after considering any objections from concerned and affected persons. The rules also establish a dedicated web portal for information relating to wetlands and require the Central Government, State Government, and Union Territory Administration to upload all relevant information and documents pertaining to wetlands in their jurisdiction.
