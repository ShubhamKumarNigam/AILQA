Summary:

THE MIZORAM UNIVERSITY ACT, 2000 is an Act to establish and incorporate a teaching and affiliating University in the State of Mizoram and to provide for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act defines the University as an institution for higher education and research in various fields. The Act provides for the establishment, powers and functions of the University, including the power to grant degrees, diplomas and certificates, provide instruction and research facilities, and establish and maintain colleges and institutions. The Act also provides for the composition and powers of the University's authorities, such as the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, and the Finance Committee. The Act also includes provisions for the appointment and removal of officers, such as the Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Registrar, and Finance Officer. The Act also includes provisions for the admission of colleges and institutions to the privileges of the University, the maintenance of discipline among students, and the establishment of a Students' Council. The Act also includes provisions for the making of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations by the University. The Act also includes provisions for the transfer of properties of the North-Eastern Hill University to the University, and the jurisdiction of the University. The Act also includes provisions for the protection of action taken in good faith, the mode of proof of University records, and the removal of difficulties. The Act also includes an amendment to the North-Eastern Hill University Act, 1973. The Act also includes a Schedule, which contains the Statutes of the University.
