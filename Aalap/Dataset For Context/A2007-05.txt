Summary:
The Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Admission) Act, 2006, also known as Act No. 5 of 2007, was enacted on 3rd January, 2007. The Act aims to provide reservation in admission to students belonging to the Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), and Other Backward Classes (OBC) in certain Central Educational Institutions established, maintained, or aided by the Central Government.

The Act defines key terms such as "academic session," "annual permitted strength," "appropriate authority," "Central Educational Institution," "faculty," "Minority Educational Institution," "Other Backward Classes," "Scheduled Castes," "Scheduled Tribes," "specified north-eastern region," and "teaching or instruction in any branch of study."

The Act specifies the reservation of seats in admission and its extent in a Central Educational Institution. Fifteen percent of seats are reserved for SC, seven and a half percent for ST, and twenty-seven percent for OBC. However, the Act does not apply to certain institutions, including institutions of excellence, research institutions, institutions of national and strategic importance, Minority Educational Institutions, and courses or programs at high levels of specialization.

Every Central Educational Institution is required to increase the number of seats in a branch of study or faculty over and above its annual permitted strength. The Act also mandates that reservation of seats in admissions to the academic sessions commencing on and from the calendar year 2008 should be implemented. Every notification made under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament while it is in session for a total period of thirty days.

The Schedule to this Act lists the institutions of excellence, research institutions, institutions of national and strategic importance that are exempted from the provisions of this Act. These institutions include Homi Bhabha National Institute, Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, North-Eastern Indira Gandhi Regional Institute of Health and Medical Science, National Brain Research Centre, Jawaharlal Nehru Centre for Advanced Scientific Research, Physical Research Laboratory, Space Physics Laboratory, and Indian Institute of Remote Sensing.
