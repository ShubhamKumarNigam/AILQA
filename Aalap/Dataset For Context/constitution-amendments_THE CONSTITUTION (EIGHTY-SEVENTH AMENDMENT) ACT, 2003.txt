Summary:

The Constitution (Eighty-Seventh Amendment) Act, 2003, changes the year referenced in several articles of the Indian Constitution. Specifically, article 81, clause (3), proviso, clause (ii), references the year "1991" and changes it to "2001". Similarly, article 82, third proviso, clause (ii), and article 170, clauses (2) and (3), Explanation, third proviso, all reference the year "1991" and change it to "2001". Lastly, article 330, Explanation, proviso, references the year "1991" and changes it to "2001". This amendment is significant because it affects the way electoral constituencies and reserved seats for Scheduled Castes (SCs) and Scheduled Tribes (STs) are determined in India. The amendment allows for the readjustment of electoral constituencies based on the 2001 population census, without altering the number of seats reserved for States in the legislative bodies.
