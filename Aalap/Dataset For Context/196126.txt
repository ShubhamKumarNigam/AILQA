Summary:
THE SALAR JUNG MUSEUM ACT, 1961 is an Act declaring the Salar Jung Museum together with the Salar Jung Library at Hyderabad to be an institution of national importance and providing for its administration and certain other connected matters. The Act establishes a Board known as the Salar Jung Museum Board, which is a body corporate with perpetual succession and a common seal. The Board is responsible for managing the museum efficiently, planning and promoting its development, and performing other functions assigned to it by the Central Government. The Act also provides for the vesting of certain properties in the Board, grants by the Central Government to the Board, the maintenance of a Fund by the Board, and the preparation of a budget by the Board for the next financial year. The accounts of the Board are to be audited annually by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India, and the Board is required to furnish returns, statements, and particulars to the Central Government. The Act also includes provisions for the delegation of powers and duties, the protection of action taken under the Act, and the making of rules and regulations. The Schedule to the Act lists the properties that vest in the Board, including antiques, curios, cabinets, works of art, statues, paintings, furniture, books, qatas and manuscripts in all languages, and land.
