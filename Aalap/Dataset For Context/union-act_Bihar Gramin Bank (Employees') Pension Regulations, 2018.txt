2. The commuted value of pension shall be calculated in accordance with the following formula:


Commuted value = pension x (number of years' purchase)


3. The maximum limit of pension admissible to be commuted is one third of the pension including additional pension, if any.


4. In the case of an employee who retired before the effective date but died after his retirement before the notified date, the commuted value of pension shall be calculated with reference to the age of the employee as on his next birthday.


5. Where the commuted value of pension results in fraction of a rupee, such fraction shall be ignored for the purpose of commutation.


6. The date of coming into force of these regulations shall be the 1st day of April, 2018.


7. These regulations may be called the "Bihar Gramin Bank (Employees') Pension Regulations, 2018".
