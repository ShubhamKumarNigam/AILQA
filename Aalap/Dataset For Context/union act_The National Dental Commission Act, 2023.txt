The National Dental Commission Act, 2023 is an Act that establishes the National Dental Commission, a statutory body responsible for regulating dental education and dental professionals in India. The Act includes provisions related to the composition, appointment, and removal of members of the Commission, as well as the powers and functions of the Commission.

The Act defines key terms related to dental education, including dental institutions, dental auxiliaries, dental hygienist, dental mechanic, dental operating room assistant, dentist, dentistry, and licence. The Act also establishes the National Register, a database of all licensed dentists and dental auxiliaries in India, and sets forth the criteria for inclusion in the Register.

The Act includes provisions related to the recognition of dental qualifications granted by Universities or dental institutions in India, as well as those granted by dental institutions outside India. It also establishes a process for the withdrawal of recognition of dental qualifications granted by dental institutions in India.

The Act includes provisions related to the establishment and composition of State Dental Councils, as well as their powers and functions. It also sets forth the process for the enrolment of dentists and dental auxiliaries in the State Register.

The Act includes provisions related to the rights and obligations of licensed dentists and dental auxiliaries, as well as the penalties for practicing dentistry without a licence. It also includes provisions related to the grant of temporary registration to foreign citizens.

Overall, the National Dental Commission Act, 2023 aims to ensure the high quality of dental education and dental professionals in India, and to protect the public by ensuring that only qualified and licensed individuals are allowed to practice dentistry.
