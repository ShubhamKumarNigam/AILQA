Amendment of article 239AA.-

In article 239AA of the Constitution, after clause (6), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:-
"(7)Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (1), the President may, subject to the provisions of clause (2), by public notification, direct that the provisions of articles 239A and 239B shall apply to the Union territory of Delhi, in which case the said provisions shall apply to that Union territory subject to such exceptions and modifications as may be specified in the notification."


Amendment of article 54.-

In article 54 of the Constitution, in clause (1), after the words "Chief Justice of India", the words "and two other judges of the Supreme Court" shall be inserted.


Amendment of the First Schedule.-

In the First Schedule to the Constitution, in the Table of articles, after entry 239AA, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"239AB. Articles 239A and 239B to apply to Union territory of Delhi with exceptions and modifications.

The Constitution (Seventieth Amendment) Act, 1992, gives effect to the Constitution (Seventy-fourth Amendment) Bill, 1991, which was enacted as the Constitution (Sixty-ninth Amendment) Act, 1991. This Amendment Act includes a proposal to add a new sub-clause (b) to article 239AA(7), which was originally dropped due to the requirement of ratification by at least one-half of the State Legislatures. This sub-clause is now being inserted with retrospective effect from December 21, 1991, and ratification by States is also being proposed for this clause, along with ratification for the other clause of the Bill that amends article 54.

The Act has two sections, with section 3 being deemed to have come into force on December 21, 1991, and section 2 coming into force on a date notified by the Central Government. The Act amends article 239AA of the Constitution to include a new clause (7), which states that the President may, subject to certain provisions, direct that the provisions of articles 239A and 239B shall apply to the Union territory of Delhi with specified exceptions and modifications.

The Act also amends article 54 of the Constitution to include the words "and two other judges of the Supreme Court" after the words "Chief Justice of India" in clause (1). Additionally, the First Schedule to the Constitution is amended to include a new entry, 239AB, which states that articles 239A and 239B shall apply to the Union territory of Delhi with exceptions and modifications.
