Summary:
The High Court Judges (Salaries and Conditions of Service) Act, 1954, regulates the salaries and certain conditions of service of the Judges of High Courts in India. The Act is divided into four chapters. Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," defines various terms used in the Act. Chapter II, titled "Leave," outlines the kinds of leave admissible to a Judge, the leave account showing the amount of leave due, leave encashment, the aggregate amount of leave which may be granted, commutation of leave on half allowances into leave on full allowances, grant of leave not due, special disability leave, extraordinary leave, leave allowances, and combining leave with vacation. Chapter III, titled "Salaries and Pensions," specifies the salaries of the Chief Justice and other Judges of High Courts, pension payable to Judges, benefit of added years of service, special provision of pension in respect of Judges who are members of service, power of President to add to the service for pension, extraordinary pensions, family pensions and gratuities, and additional quantum of pension or family pension. Chapter IV, titled "Miscellaneous," includes provisions for travelling allowances to a Judge, facility of rent-free houses, conveyance facilities, sumptuary allowance, exemption from liability to pay income-tax on certain perquisites received by a Judge, facilities for medical treatment and other conditions of service, vacation of High Courts, special provisions in respect of Judges transferred from the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir, and medical facilities for retired Judges. The Act also includes schedules and forms for pension calculations and other related matters.
