Summary:
The Jawaharlal Institute of Post-Graduate Medical Education and Research, Puducherry Act, 2008, declares the Institute as an institution of national importance and provides for its incorporation. The Act consists of 32 sections and a schedule. The sections cover various aspects such as short title and commencement, declaration of the Institute as an institution of national importance, definitions, incorporation of the Institute, composition of the Institute, term of office of and vacancies among members, President of the Institute and his powers and functions, allowances of the President and members, meeting of the Institute, Governing Body and other committees of the Institute, staff of the Institute, objects of the Institute, functions of the Institute, vesting of property, payment to the Institute, Fund of the Institute, budget of the Institute, accounts and audit, annual report, pension and provident funds, authentication of orders and instruments of the Institute, acts and proceedings not to be invalidated by vacancies, etc., grant of medical degrees, diplomas, etc., by the Institute, recognition of medical qualifications granted by the Institute, control by the Central Government, resolution of differences, returns and information, transfer of service of existing employees, power to make rules, power to make regulations, laying of rules and regulations before Parliament, and power to remove difficulties. The schedule to the Act specifies the authorities of the Institute.
