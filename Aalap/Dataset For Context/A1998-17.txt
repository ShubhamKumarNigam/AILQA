Summary:

THE LOTTERIES (REGULATION) ACT, 1998 is an Act that regulates lotteries and related matters in India. The Act, which came into force on October 2, 1997, defines lottery as a scheme for distribution of prizes by lot or chance to those participating in the chances of a prize by purchasing tickets.

The Act prohibits the organization, conduct, or promotion of lotteries, except under certain conditions. These conditions include the prohibition of prizes being offered on pre-announced numbers or on the basis of a single digit, the printing of lottery tickets bearing the imprint and logo of the State, the sale of tickets by the State government or through distributors or selling agents, the crediting of the proceeds of the sale of lottery tickets into the public account of the State, the conduct of draws by the State government, and the distribution of unclaimed prize money to the government after a certain period.

The Act also prohibits the sale of tickets of a lottery organized, conducted, or promoted by another State within the State. The Central Government may prohibit a lottery organized, conducted, or promoted in contravention of the provisions of this Act or where tickets of such lottery are sold in contravention of the provisions of section 5.

The Act prescribes penalties for offenses, including rigorous imprisonment for a term that may extend to two years or a fine or both for organizing, conducting, or promoting a lottery in contravention of the provisions of this Act. The offenses under this Act are cognizable and non-bailable.

The Act also provides for offenses by companies and the liability of directors, managers, secretaries, or other officers of the company. The Central Government may give directions to the State Government as to carrying into execution in the State of any of the provisions of this Act or of any rule or order made thereunder.

The Act empowers the Central Government to make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act, and the State Government may make rules to carry out the provisions of this Act. Every rule made by the State Government shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is made, before each House of the State Legislature where it consists of two Houses, or where such Legislature consists of one House, before that House.

The Lotteries (Regulation) Ordinance, 1998, is hereby repealed, and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.
