Summary:
The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of the Birth Centenary of Late Shri Karpoori Thakur) Rules, 2024, have been enacted under the authority of the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules specify the details of a commemorative coin of one hundred rupees denomination that will be issued to mark the birth centenary of Late Shri Karpoori Thakur. The coin will be circular in shape, with a diameter of 44 millimeters and a standard weight of 35 grams. It will be composed of a quaternary alloy of silver (50%), copper (40%), nickel (5%), and zinc (5%). The obverse side of the coin will feature the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar, with the legend "सत्यमेव जायते" inscribed below, flanked by the word "INDIA" in English. The reverse side of the coin will bear the portrait of Shri Karpoori Thakur, with the inscription "Birth Centenary Of Late Shri Karpoori Thakur" in English and the year "2024" in international numerals. The remedy allowed in making the coin is 1/500 th plus or minus for Silver, that is to say the Silver content may vary from 498 to 502 per 1000, and 1/100 th plus or minus, that is to say weight could be 34.65 grams to 35.35 grams.
