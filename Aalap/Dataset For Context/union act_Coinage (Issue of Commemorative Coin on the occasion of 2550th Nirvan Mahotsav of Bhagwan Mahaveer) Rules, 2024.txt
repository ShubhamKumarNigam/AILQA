Summary:

The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin on the occasion of 2550th Nirvan Mahotsav of Bhagwan Mahaveer) Rules, 2024, have been made under the authority of the Coinage Act, 2011. These rules specify the details of a commemorative coin to be issued on the occasion of the 2550th Nirvan Mahotsav of Bhagwan Mahaveer.

Rule 2 states that the coin will be of one hundred rupees denomination.

Rule 3 outlines the dimensions and composition of the coin. It will be circular in shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters and will have 200 serrations. The coin will be made of a quaternary alloy consisting of 50% silver, 40% copper, 5% nickel, and 5% zinc.

Rule 4 specifies the design of the coin. The obverse side of the coin will bear the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the center, with the legend "सत्यमेव जयते" inscribed below. The word "भारत" will be written in Devnagari script on the left periphery, and the word "INDIA" will be written in English on the right periphery. The Rupee symbol "⟨₹⟩" and the denominational value "100" will be depicted below the Lion Capitol. The reverse side of the coin will feature a prominent and detailed image of the Nalanda Pawapuri Jal Mandir at the center. Below the image of the Jal Mandir, the inscription "भगवान महावीर 2550वां निर्वाण कल्याणक" will be written in Devnagari script, and the inscription "BHAGWAN MAHAVEER 2550TH NIRVAN KALYANAK" will be depicted in English. The year "2023" will be inscribed at the bottom periphery, flanked with two dots.

Rule 5 specifies the standard weight and remedy allowed in making the coin. The standard weight of the coin will be 35 grams, with a remedy allowed in making the coin. For silver, the remedy allowed is 1/500 th plus or minus, which means that the Silver content may vary from 498 to 502 per 1000. For weight, the remedy allowed is 1/100 th plus or minus, which means that the weight could vary from 34.65 grams to 35.35 grams.

These rules will come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.
