The Maulana Azad National Urdu University Act, 1996 establishes and incorporates the Maulana Azad National Urdu University at the national level to promote and develop the Urdu language and to impart vocational and technical education in Urdu medium through conventional teaching and distance education system. The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including 'Academic Council', 'academic staff', 'Board of Studies', 'Chancellor', 'Court', 'Department', 'distance education system', 'employee', 'Executive Council', 'Finance Committee', 'Hall', 'Institution', 'recognised Institution', 'Regulations', 'School', 'Statutes', 'Ordinances', 'Teachers of the University', and 'University'. The Act also lays down the powers and functions of the University, including providing instructions and research in relevant branches of learning, granting diplomas or certificates, organising extra-mural studies, training and extension services, instituting professorships, readerships, lectureships and other teaching or academic positions, creating administrative, ministerial and other posts, co-operating or collaborating with other universities or authorities or institutions of higher learning, establishing centres and specialised laboratories, instituting and awarding fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals and prizes, establishing and maintaining institutions and halls, making provision for research and advisory services, organising and conducting refresher courses, workshops, seminars and other programmes for teachers, evaluators and other academic staff, making special arrangements for the residence, discipline and teaching of women students, appointing, on contract or otherwise, visiting professors, emeritus professors, consultants, scholars and such other persons who may contribute to the advancement of the objects of the University, conferring autonomous status on a Department, determining standards of admission, demanding and receiving payment of fees and other charges, supervising the residences of the students of the University and making arrangements for promoting their health and general welfare, laying down conditions of service of all categories of employees including their code of conduct, regulating and enforcing discipline among the students and the employees and taking such disciplinary measures in this regard as may be deemed necessary, making arrangements for promoting the health and general welfare of the employees, receiving benefactions, donations and gifts and to acquire, hold, manage and dispose of any property, movable or immovable, including trust and endowment properties for the purposes of the University, borrowing, with the approval of the Central Government, on the security of the property of the University, money for the purposes of the University, organising suitable distance education programmes, establishing maintain or recognising Study Centres for the delivery of the distance education system in the manner laid down by the Ordinances, providing for the preparation of instructional materials including films, cassettes, tapes, video cassettes and other software in collaboration with various Open Universities and other Institutions dealing with the distance education system, devising and implementing suitable schemes for providing a base for promotion and dissemination of Urdu and to bring about the essential continuity in education with special reference to the delivery system in distance education consistent with the objectives of the University, and doing all such other acts and things as may be necessary, incidental or conducive to the attainment of all or any of its objects. The Act also lays down the jurisdiction of the University, which extends to the whole of India, and provides that the University shall be open to persons of either sex and of whatever caste, creed, race or class, and it shall not be lawful for the University to adopt or impose on any person any test whatsoever of religious belief or profession in order to entitle him to be appointed as a teacher of the University or to hold any other office therein or to be admitted as a student in the University or to graduate thereat or to enjoy or exercise any privilege thereof. The Act also provides for the appointment of the Visitor, who shall be the President of India, and lays down the powers and functions of the Visitor, including causing an inspection to be made by such person or persons as he may direct of the University, its buildings, laboratories and equipment and of any Institution maintained by the University and also of the examinations, teaching and other work conducted or done by the University and to cause an inquiry to be made in like manner in respect of any matter connected with the administration or finances of the University or Institutions, giving notice of his intention to cause an inspection or inquiry to be made to the University and the University shall have the right to make such representations to the Visitor as it may consider necessary, considering the representations, if any, made by the University, the Visitor may cause to be made such inspection or inquiry as is referred to in sub-section (3), addressing the Vice-Chancellor with reference to the result of such inspection or inquiry together with such views and advice with regard to the action to be taken thereon as the Visitor may be pleased to offer, and on receipt of address made by the Visitor, the Vice-Chancellor shall communicate to the Executive Council, the views of the Visitor with such advice as the Visitor may offer upon the action to be taken thereon, the Executive Council shall communicate, through the Vice-Chancellor, to the Visitor such action, if any, as it proposes to take or has been taken upon the result of such inspection or inquiry, and if the Executive Council does not, within a reasonable time, take action to the satisfaction of the Visitor, the Visitor may, after considering any explanation furnished or representation made by the Executive Council, issue such directions as he may think fit and the Executive Council shall comply with such directions, and the Visitor may direct the University to make provisions in the Statutes in respect of any matter specified by him and if the Executive Council is unable to implement such direction within sixty days of its receipt, the Visitor may, after considering the reasons, if any, communicated by the Executive Council for its inability to comply with such direction, make or amend the Statutes suitably. The Act also provides for the appointment of officers of the University, including the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellor, the Deans of Schools, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, and the Librarian, and lays down their powers and functions. The Act also provides for the authorities of the University, including the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Board of Studies, the Finance Committee, and such other authorities as may be declared by the Statutes to be the authorities of the University, and lays down their powers and functions. The Act also provides for the making of Statutes, Ordinances and Regulations by the University, and lays down the procedure for their making and publication. The Act also provides for the annual report of the University, the annual accounts and balance-sheet of the University, the conditions of service of employees, the disputes as to constitution of University authorities and bodies, the constitution of Committees, the filling of casual vacancies, the proceedings of University authorities or bodies not invalidated by vacancies, the protection of action taken in good faith, the mode of proof of University records, the removal of difficulties, and the transitional provisions. The Act also provides for the Statutes, the Ordinances and the Regulations to be published in the Official Gazette and to be laid before Parliament. The Schedule to the Act contains the Statutes of the University.
