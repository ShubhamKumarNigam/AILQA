Summary:

THE COURT-FEES (DELHI AMENDMENT) ACT, 1967, with the Act ID ACT NO. 28 OF 1967, was enacted on the 16th of December, 1967. This Act aims to amend the Court-fees Act, 1870, as in force in the Union territory of Delhi.

The Act comprises five sections. Section 1 specifies the short title and extent of the Act, stating that it may be called the Court-fees (Delhi Amendment) Act, 1967, and extends to the whole of the Union territory of Delhi.

Section 2 amends Section 4 of the Court-fees Act, 1870, as in force in the Union territory of Delhi. The amendments include changing the marginal heading to the first paragraph, replacing the words “in High Courts in their extraordinary jurisdiction” with “in the High Court of Delhi in its ordinary or extraordinary jurisdiction”; altering the first paragraph to replace “any of the said High Courts in any case coming before such Court in the exercise of its extraordinary original civil jurisdiction” with “the High Court of Delhi in any case coming before that Court in the exercise of its ordinary or extraordinary original civil jurisdiction”; substituting “other than” with “including” in the third paragraph; and adding new paragraphs in the exercise of jurisdiction to issue writs, etc., and in the exercise of its jurisdiction in any other matter after the fifth paragraph.

Section 3 amends Schedule II to the principal Act, adding a new sub-clause (ii-A) in clause (d) of article 1, specifying fees of fifty rupees for suits or other proceedings instituted before the High Court of Delhi under article 226 of the Constitution of India other than petitions for habeas corpus and petitions arising out of criminal proceedings.

Section 4 addresses the levy of fees in certain suits, etc., instituted before the commencement of this Act. It states that fees shall be levied in suits or other proceedings instituted on or after the 31st day of October, 1966, and pending immediately before the 7th October, 1967, in the High Court of Delhi by virtue, and in the exercise, of its ordinary original civil jurisdiction as if the principal Act, as amended by this Act, had been in force on the respective dates on which such suits or proceedings were instituted. Additionally, any fees levied in respect of suits or other proceedings instituted before the High Court of Delhi by virtue, and in the exercise, of its ordinary original civil jurisdiction, on or after the 31st day of October, 1966, and disposed of before the 7th October, 1967, shall be deemed to have been levied in accordance with law.

Finally, Section 5 deals with the repeal and saving. The Court-fees (Delhi Amendment) Ordinance, 1967 (7 of 1967), is hereby repealed. However, anything done or any action taken under the principal Act as amended by the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provision of the principal Act as amended by this Act as if this Act had commenced on the 7th October, 1967.
