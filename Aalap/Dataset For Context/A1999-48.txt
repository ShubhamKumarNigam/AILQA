Summary:

The Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999 is an Act to provide for the registration and better protection of geographical indications relating to goods. The Act defines geographical indications as an indication which identifies such goods as agricultural goods, natural goods or manufactured goods as originating, or manufactured in the territory of a country, or a region or locality in that territory, where a given quality, reputation or other characteristic of such goods is essentially attributable to its geographical origin and in case where such goods are manufactured goods one of the activities of either the production or of processing or preparation of the goods concerned takes place in such territory, region or locality, as the case may be. The Act establishes a Geographical Indications Registry and provides for the registration and maintenance of a Register of Geographical Indications. The Act also provides for the registration of authorized users of geographical indications. The Act includes provisions for the duration, renewal, removal and restoration of registration of geographical indications. The Act also includes provisions for the effect of registration, prohibition of registration of geographical indication as trade mark, protection to certain trade marks, and special provisions relating to trade mark and prior users. The Act includes provisions for rectification and correction of the register, appeals to the Appellate Board, appearance of Registrar in legal proceedings, costs of Registrar in proceedings before Appellate Board, and offences, penalties and procedure. The Act also includes provisions for protection of action taken in good faith, certain persons to be public servants, stay of proceedings where the validity of registration of the geographical indication is questioned etc., application for rectification of register to be made to Appellate Board in certain cases, implied warranty on sale of indicated goods, powers of Registrar, exercise of discretionary power by Registrar, evidence before Registrar, death of party to a proceeding, extension of time, abandonment, suit for infringement, etc., to be instituted before district court, relief in suit for infringement or for passing off, authorized user to be impleaded in certain proceedings, evidence of entries in register, etc., and things done by the Registrar, Registrar and other officers not compellable to produce register, etc., power to require goods to show indication of origin, certificate of validity, groundless threats of legal proceedings, address for service, trade usages, etc., to be taken into consideration, agents, indexes, documents open to public inspection, reports of Registrar to be placed before Parliament, fees and surcharge, savings in respect of certain matters in Chapter VIII, declaration as to title of geographical indication not registrable under the Registration Act, 1908, Government to be bound, special provisions relating to applications for registration from citizens of convention countries, provision as to reciprocity, powers of Central Government to remove difficulties, and power to make rules.
