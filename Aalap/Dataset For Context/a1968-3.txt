Summary:

The Jammu and Kashmir Representation of the People (Supplementary) Act, 1968, is a law that supplements the Jammu and Kashmir Representation of the People Act, 1957. This Act came into force on February 9, 1968. The Act applies the provisions of sections 116A, 116B, and 116C of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, to orders made by the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir, subject to certain modifications. These modifications include construing references to the High Court as including references to the High Court of Jammu and Kashmir, references to the State Legislature or to the Speaker or Chairman thereof as including references to the Legislature of the State of Jammu and Kashmir and to the Speaker or Chairman thereof, and references to the provisions of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, as references to the corresponding provisions of the Jammu and Kashmir Representation of the People Act, 1957. The Act also repeals the Jammu and Kashmir Representation of the People (Supplementary) Ordinance, 1968, and deems anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance to have been done or taken under this Act 1957. This Act is now applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh.
