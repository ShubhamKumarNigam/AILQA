The Constitution (Tenth Amendment) Act, 1961, was enacted to incorporate Dadra and Nagar Haveli as the seventh Union territory of India. The Act came into force on August 11, 1961. The Act amended the First Schedule to the Constitution to include Dadra and Nagar Haveli as a Union territory. Before the amendment, it was a separate territory that was not part of India. The Act also amended clause (1) of article 240 of the Constitution to enable the President to make regulations for the peace, progress, and good government of the territory. This means that the President has the power to create laws and regulations specific to the Union territory of Dadra and Nagar Haveli to ensure its smooth functioning and development.
