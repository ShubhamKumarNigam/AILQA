Summary:
The Police Act of 1949 is a law that establishes a general police-district that can include two or more Union territories in India. The Act creates a single police force for the general police-district, which is under the superintendence and administration of the Central Government. The powers, duties, and privileges of the police force in the general police-district are the same as those of a police force in a single state. The Act also specifies that the provisions of the Police Act of 1861 apply to the police force in the general police-district, except as otherwise expressly provided. The Act does not affect the provisions contained in the Delhi Special Police Establishment Act of 1946. The Act has been extended to Goa, Daman and Diu, Pondicherry, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, and Lakshadweep with modifications.
