Summary:

The Yes Bank Limited Reconstruction Scheme, 2020 is a legal document that outlines the terms and conditions for the reconstruction of Yes Bank Limited. The Scheme was enacted on March 13, 2020, under the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. The document defines several key terms, including "Act," "investor," "investor bank," "reconstructed bank," and "Reserve Bank."

Under the Scheme, the authorized capital of the reconstructed bank is altered to Rs.62,00,00,00,000 (Rupees Six thousand two hundred crore only) and the number of equity shares to 30,00,00,00,000 (Three thousand crore only) of rupees two only each. The investor bank and other investors can invest in the reconstructed bank, and the reconstructed bank shall allot equity shares of the reconstructed bank, at a price of rupees ten only with face value of rupees two only and premium of rupees eight only.

The Scheme also includes provisions related to the alteration of articles of association of the reconstructed bank, constitution of the Board of Directors, rights and liabilities of the reconstructed bank, continuation of services of employees, no change in offices or branch network of the reconstructed bank, furnishing statements and information, manner of service of notice, cessation of moratorium, and interpretation of the provisions of the Scheme.

The Scheme also includes provisions related to the lock-in period of three years for equity shares held by existing shareholders and shares allotted to investors. Additionally, there are provisions related to the voting rights of investors and the appointment of directors.

Overall, the Yes Bank Limited Reconstruction Scheme, 2020 provides a framework for the reconstruction of Yes Bank Limited and outlines the rights, liabilities, and obligations of the bank, its creditors, and its shareholders.
