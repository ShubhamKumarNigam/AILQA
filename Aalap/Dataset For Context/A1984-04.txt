Summary:

THE INDUSTRIES (DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATION) AMENDMENT ACT, 1984, is an amendment to the Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951. The Act came into force on the 12th day of January, 1984. The Act repeals certain sections and amends others. The repealed sections include the insertion of new section 11B, amendment of section 24, and amendment of section 29B.

The Act validates every notification made or purporting to have been made by the Central Government under the principal Act, on or after the 19th day of February, 1970, reserving any article or class of articles for production by any ancillary, or small scale, industrial undertaking or any class of ancillary, or small scale, industrial undertakings. The validation is deemed to have been in force at all material times, and such notification shall continue to have effect until it is altered or superseded by any fresh notification under the principal Act as amended by this Act.

The Act provides that no act or omission on the part of any person shall be punishable as an offence which would not have been so punishable if this section had not come into force. The Act also repeals and saves certain provisions relating to the repeal of the Act.

In summary, the THE INDUSTRIES (DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATION) AMENDMENT ACT, 1984, amends and validates certain provisions of the Industries (Development and Regulation) Act, 1951, and saves certain provisions relating to the repeal of the Act.
