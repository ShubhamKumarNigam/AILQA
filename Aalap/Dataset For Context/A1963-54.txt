Summary:
THE CENTRAL BOARDS OF REVENUE ACT, 1963, is an Act that provides for the constitution of separate Boards of Revenue for Direct Taxes and for Indirect Taxes and Customs. The Act was enacted on December 30, 1963, and came into force on a date appointed by the Central Government. The Act defines various terms, including "Board," which refers to the Central Board of Direct Taxes or the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, and "direct tax," which means any duty or tax chargeable under various acts, including the Estate Duty Act, 1953, the Wealth-tax Act, 1957, the Expenditure-tax Act, 1957, the Gift-tax Act, 1958, the Income-tax Act, 1961, and the Super Profits Tax Act, 1963. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for the purpose of regulating the transaction of business by each Board. The Act amends certain enactments, including the Estate Duty Act, 1953, the Wealth-tax Act, 1957, the Expenditure-tax Act, 1957, the Gift-tax Act, 1958, the Income-tax Act, 1961, and the Super Profits Tax Act, 1963, to substitute the words "Central Board of Direct Taxes constituted under the Central Boards of Revenue Act, 1963" for the words "Central Board of Revenue constituted under the Central Board of Revenue Act, 1924" or "Central Board of Revenue." The Act also transfers certain proceedings pending before the Central Board of Revenue to the Central Board of Direct Taxes or the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, depending on the nature of the proceeding. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make orders for removing any difficulty in giving effect to the provisions of this Act. The Central Board of Revenue Act, 1924, is repealed by the Act, and any appointment, assessment, order, rule, exemption, approval, recognition, notice, notification, direction, instruction, duty, penalty, fine, confiscation, form, thing, or action made or taken by the Central Board of Revenue under any law shall be deemed to have been made, granted, issued, levied, imposed, adjudged, prescribed, done, or taken by the Central Board of Direct Taxes or the Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs, as the case may be, and shall continue to be in force unless and until it is revised, withdrawn, or superseded by the concerned Board.
