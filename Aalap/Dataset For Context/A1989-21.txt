Summary:

The Representation of the People (Amendment) Act, 1989, is an Act that aims to further amend the Representation of the People Act, 1950. The Act has six sections, with the first section stating the short title of the Act as the Representation of the People (Amendment) Act, 1989.

Section 2 of the Act, which amended section 9 of the principal Act, has been omitted by the Repealing and Amending Act, 2001. Similarly, Section 3, which amended section 14, Section 4, which amended section 19, and Section 5, which amended the Fourth Schedule, have also been omitted by the Repealing and Amending Act, 2001.

The last section of the Act, Section 6, deals with the validation of all things done and all steps taken before the commencement of this section in relation to the preparation or revision of electoral rolls under Part III of the principal Act in the year 1989. These things and steps shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the principal Act as amended by sections 3 and 4 of this Act, as if the principal Act as so amended was in force at the time such things were done or such steps were taken.

In summary, the Representation of the People (Amendment) Act, 1989, aimed to further amend the Representation of the People Act, 1950, but most of the amendments have been omitted by the Repealing and Amending Act, 2001. The Act's last section validates all things done and all steps taken before its commencement in relation to the preparation or revision of electoral rolls under Part III of the principal Act in the year 1989.
