


The Constitution (Fiftieth Amendment) Act, 1984 aims to amend Article 33 of the Constitution of India. This amendment aims to expand the scope of Article 33 to include members of the forces responsible for protecting state property, individuals employed in intelligence or counter-intelligence bureaus or organizations established by the state, and individuals employed in or connected to telecommunication systems set up for the purposes of any force, bureau, or organization. The rationale behind this amendment is to ensure the proper discharge of duties and maintain discipline among these individuals, which is deemed crucial for national interest. The Act received presidential assent on December 20, 1984.
