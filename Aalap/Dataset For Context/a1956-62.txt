Summary:

The Jammu and Kashmir (Extension of Laws) Act, 1956, extends certain laws to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. The Acts and Ordinances mentioned in the Schedule and all rules, orders, and regulations made thereunder are extended to the State of Jammu and Kashmir, with certain amendments as specified in the Schedule. Any reference in any Act or Ordinance mentioned in the Schedule to a law that is not in force in the State of Jammu and Kashmir shall be construed as a reference to the corresponding law, if any, in force in that State. Any reference to an authority competent at the date of the passing of a law shall, where a corresponding new authority has been constituted by or under any law now extended to that State, have effect as if it were a reference to the new authority. If immediately before the commencement of this Act, there is in force in the State of Jammu and Kashmir any law corresponding to any Act or Ordinance now extended to that State, that law shall, save as otherwise expressly provided in this Act, stand repealed on such commencement. The repeal shall not affect any right, privilege, obligation, or liability acquired, accrued, or incurred under any law so repealed, or any penalty, forfeiture, or punishment incurred in respect of any offence committed against any law so repealed. Anything done or any action taken under any law repealed by that section shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of the Act or Ordinance now extended to that State, and shall continue to be in force accordingly unless and until superseded by anything done or any action taken under the said Act or Ordinance. The Schedule to the Act lists the Acts and Ordinances that are extended to the State of Jammu and Kashmir, along with the amendments made to them.
