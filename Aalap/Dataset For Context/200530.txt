Summary:
THE CREDIT INFORMATION COMPANIES (REGULATION) ACT, 2005

This Act aims to provide for the regulation of credit information companies and facilitate efficient distribution of credit. It includes provisions for registration, management, and audit of credit information companies. It also defines the functions and duties of credit information companies, credit institutions, and specified users in relation to credit information. The Act includes provisions for accuracy and security of credit information, privacy principles, and alteration of credit information files and credit reports. It also specifies offences and penalties for non-compliance with the Act. The Act includes provisions for disclosure of information before any court or tribunal or authority, obligations as to fidelity and secrecy, protection of action taken in good faith, bar of jurisdiction, power of Reserve Bank to exempt in certain cases, application of other laws not barred, amendment of certain enactments, removal of difficulties, power to make rules, and power of Reserve Bank to make regulations. The Act also includes a schedule with amendments to certain enactments.
