
THE CONSTITUTION (FIFTY-SEVENTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1987 

 Amendment of article 332.

In article 332 of the Constitution, after clause (5), the following clause shall be inserted and shall be deemed always to have been inserted, namely:-

"(6) Notwithstanding anything contained in clause (1) or clause (5), the number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of any State under clause (1) shall, for a period of twenty-five years from the commencement of the Constitution (Fifty-first Amendment) Act, 1987, be determined in the manner specified in clause (2) of article 332 and the seats so reserved shall be allotted by rotation to different constituencies in the State."



Summary:

The Constitution (Fifty-Seventh Amendment) Act, 1987 was enacted to amend the Constitution of India. This amendment aims to provide for special arrangements regarding the reservation of seats for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of certain North-Eastern States. The amendment states that if all the seats in the Legislative Assembly of these States are held by members of the Scheduled Tribes, all the seats except one will be reserved for them. In any other case, such a number of seats will be reserved for Scheduled Tribes as bears to the total number of seats a proportion not less than the number of members belonging to the Scheduled Tribes in the existing Assembly bears to the total number of seats in the existing Assembly. The amendment also specifies that the number of seats reserved for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly of any State shall, for a period of twenty-five years from the commencement of the Constitution (Fifty-first Amendment) Act, 1987, be determined in the manner specified in clause (2) of article 332 and the seats so reserved shall be allotted by rotation to different constituencies in the State. This amendment came into force on the date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
