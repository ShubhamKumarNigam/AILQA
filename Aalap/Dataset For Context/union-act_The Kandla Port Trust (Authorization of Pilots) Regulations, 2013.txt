The Kandla Port Trust (Authorization of Pilots) Regulations, 2013, is a set of rules and guidelines established by the Kandla Port Trust to regulate the authorization of pilots at the Kandla Port in India. These regulations came into force on the date of their publication in the Gazette of India.

The regulations outline the eligibility criteria, qualifications, and experience required for pilots to be authorized to operate at the Kandla Port. According to Regulation 4, a person can be authorized as a pilot if they are an Indian citizen, have a valid pilot's license, and have at least five years of experience as a pilot in a major port or five years of experience as a master of a ship of not less than 1000 gross tonnage.

Regulation 5 states that the Kandla Port Trust can authorize a pilot for a specific period, not exceeding five years, subject to their satisfactory performance. The regulations also provide for the renewal of the authorization of a pilot for a further period of five years, subject to the pilot's satisfactory performance and compliance with the regulations.

Regulation 6 outlines the powers and duties of authorized pilots, including the responsibility to ensure the safe navigation of vessels in the port limits, to assist in the berthing and unberthing of vessels, and to provide navigational advice to masters of vessels.

Regulation 7 sets out the procedure for the suspension or cancellation of the authorization of a pilot. The Kandla Port Trust may suspend or cancel the authorization of a pilot if they are found to be incompetent, negligent, or guilty of misconduct. The regulations also provide for the right of appeal by the pilot against the suspension or cancellation of their authorization.

Regulation 8 provides for the appointment of a medical board to examine the fitness of pilots, and Regulation 9 sets out the requirements for the medical examination of pilots.

Regulation 10 provides for the fees to be paid by pilots for their authorization, and Regulation 11 sets out the procedure for the payment of fees.

In summary, the Kandla Port Trust (Authorization of Pilots) Regulations, 2013, establish the eligibility criteria, qualifications, and experience required for pilots to be authorized to operate at the Kandla Port in India. The regulations also outline the powers and duties of authorized pilots, the procedure for the suspension or cancellation of their authorization, and the requirements for their medical examination.
