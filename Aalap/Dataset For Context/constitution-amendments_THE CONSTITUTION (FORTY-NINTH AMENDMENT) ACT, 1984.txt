
Amendment of article 334.-

 In article 334 of the Constitution, for the words "fifty years", the words "sixty years" shall be substituted.



The Constitution (Forty-ninth Amendment) Act, 1984, amends the Constitution of India by extending the period of reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies from fifty years to sixty years. This amendment was made to ensure continued representation for these marginalized communities in the democratic process. The Act also specifies that it will come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
