Summary:
The Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Teachers’ Cadre) Act, 2019, provides for the reservation of posts in appointments by direct recruitment of persons belonging to the Scheduled Castes, the Scheduled Tribes, the socially and educationally backward classes and the economically weaker sections to teachers’ cadre in certain Central Educational Institutions. The Act came into force on the 7th day of March, 2019.
The Act defines Central Educational Institution as a University established or incorporated by or under a Central Act, an institution of national importance established by an Act of Parliament, an institution, declared as an institution deemed to be University under section 3 of the University Grants Commission Act, 1956, and maintained by or receiving aid from the Central Government, whether directly or indirectly, and affiliated to an institution referred to in sub-clause (i) or sub-clause (ii), or a constituent unit of an institution referred to in sub-clause (iii), and an educational institution established by the Central Government under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.
The Act provides for reservation of posts in direct recruitment out of the sanctioned strength in teachers' cadre in a Central Educational Institution to the extent and in the manner as may be specified by the Central Government by notification in the Official Gazette. The reservation provisions do not apply to institutions of excellence, research institutions, institutions of national and strategic importance specified in the Schedule to this Act and a Minority Educational Institution.
The Act also provides that every notification made by the Central Government under this Act shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is made, before each House of Parliament while it is in session, for a total period of thirty days which may be comprised in one session or in two or more successive sessions.
The Central Educational Institutions (Reservation in Teachers' Cadre) Ordinance, 2019 is repealed by this Act and anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under this Act.
