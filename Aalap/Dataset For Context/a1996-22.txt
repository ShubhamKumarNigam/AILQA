Summary:
The Depositories Act, 1996, is an Act to provide for regulation of depositories in securities and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act includes chapters on preliminary, certificate of commencement of business, rights and obligations of depositories, participants, issuers and beneficial owners, enquiry and inspection, and miscellaneous provisions. The Act defines terms such as "beneficial owner," "Board," "bye-laws," "Company Law Board," "depository," "issuer," "participant," "prescribed," "record," "registered owner," "regulations," "security," and "service." The Act specifies the rights and obligations of depositories, participants, issuers, and beneficial owners, and includes provisions for the surrender of certificates of security, registration of transfer of securities with depositories, options to receive security certificates or hold securities with depositories, and the rights and obligations of depositories and beneficial owners. The Act also includes provisions for the maintenance of a register of beneficial owners, pledge or hypothecation of securities held in a depository, and the furnishing of information and records by depositories and issuers. The Act includes provisions for the option to opt out in respect of any security, and the application of the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 1891 to depositories. The Act also includes provisions for the indemnification of loss in certain cases, and the rights and obligations of depositories, participants, and issuers. The Act includes chapters on enquiry and inspection, and miscellaneous provisions, including provisions for the power of the Board to call for information and make enquiries, the power of the Board to give directions in certain cases, the penalty for failure to furnish information, return, etc., the penalty for failure to enter into an agreement, the penalty for failure to redress investors' grievances, the penalty for delay in dematerialisation or issue of certificate of securities, the penalty for failure to reconcile records, and the penalty for failure to comply with directions issued by the Board under section 19. The Act also includes provisions for the adjudication of penalties, the settlement of administrative and civil proceedings, and the recovery of amounts. The Act includes transitional provisions and provisions for appeals, and includes provisions for the power of the Central Government to make rules and the power of the Board to make regulations. The Act also includes provisions for the power of depositories to make bye-laws, and includes provisions for the removal of difficulties. The Act includes a schedule, which has been repealed. The Act also includes forms and footnotes.
