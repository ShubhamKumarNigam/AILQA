Summary:

The National Sports University Act, 2018 establishes and incorporates the National Sports University in the State of Manipur, India. The University aims to promote sports education in the areas of sports sciences, sports technology, sports management, and sports coaching, and to function as the national training center for select sports disciplines. The Act defines various terms and provides for the establishment, objects, powers, functions, and authorities of the University. The University shall have a Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Deans of Schools, Registrar, Finance Officer, Controller of Examinations, and Librarian. The Executive Council, Academic and Activity Council, Board of Sports Studies, and Finance Committee are the authorities of the University. The Act also provides for the appointment, powers, functions, and duties of these officers and authorities, as well as the appointment and powers of teachers and other employees. The Act also lays down the procedure for the removal of difficulties and the repeal and savings of the National Sports University Ordinance, 2018. The Schedule to the Act contains the Statutes of the University, which provide for the appointment, powers, functions, and duties of the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Deans of Schools, Registrar, Finance Officer, Controller of Examinations, and Librarian, as well as the constitution, powers, and functions of the Court, Executive Council, Academic and Activity Council, and Finance Committee.
