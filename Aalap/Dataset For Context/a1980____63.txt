Summary:

The Code of Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act, 1980, is an Act that amends the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973. The Act came into force on 23rd September, 1980, and its short title is the Code of Criminal Procedure (Amendment) Act, 1980.

The Act has repealed several sections of the principal Act, including sections 108, 109, 110, 196, 436, 437, 446, and 478. It has also inserted a new section 446A and substituted a new section for section 478. These amendments were made to update and streamline the criminal procedure in India.

Additionally, the Act includes a saving provision for pending proceedings under sections 108, 109, and 110. This means that any proceedings under these sections that were pending before a Judicial Magistrate of the first class immediately before the commencement of this Act shall be dealt with as if the Act had not been enacted.

The Act was repealed and saved by the Repealing and Amending Act (19 of 1988) with effect from 31st March, 1988.
