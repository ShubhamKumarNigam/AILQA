The National Tiger Conservation Authority (Annual Report and Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 2007 

 Repeal and savings.

- The National Tiger Conservation Authority (Annual Report and Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 2006 are hereby repealed.

Summary:

The National Tiger Conservation Authority (Annual Report and Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 2007 provide guidelines for the preparation and submission of the annual report and annual statement of accounts by the National Tiger Conservation Authority (Tiger Conservation Authority). The rules define various terms used in the document and specify the contents of the annual report and statement of accounts.

The annual report, prepared by the Member-Secretary or an authorized officer, must contain details such as the introduction, constitution of the Tiger Conservation Authority, meetings and important decisions taken, monitoring and evaluation of tiger reserves, committees constituted, financial and accounts, annual plan, and compliance issues. The report must be submitted to the Central Government by the end of August following the financial year to which it relates.

The Tiger Conservation Authority is required to maintain its accounts and prepare an annual statement of accounts in Form-B. The statement of accounts must contain details such as the balance sheet, receipts and payments account, significant accounting policies, contingent liabilities, and notes on accounts. The statement of accounts must be submitted to the Central Government by the end of August following the financial year to which it relates.

The rules also provide two forms, Form-A and Form-B, which must be used for the preparation of the annual report and annual statement of accounts, respectively. The rules repeal the National Tiger Conservation Authority (Annual Report and Annual Statement of Accounts) Rules, 2006.
