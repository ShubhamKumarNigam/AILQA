Summary:
THE OFFSHORE AREAS MINERAL (DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATION) ACT, 2002 is an Act of the Indian Parliament that provides for the development and regulation of mineral resources in the territorial waters, continental shelf, exclusive economic zone, and other maritime zones of India. The Act applies to all minerals, except mineral oils and hydrocarbons related thereto, and includes provisions for the grant of operating rights, such as reconnaissance permits, exploration licenses, and production leases. The Act also includes provisions for the protection of the environment, safety of persons and property, and prevention of pollution. The Act also empowers the Central Government to issue directions for the conservation and systematic development of offshore minerals, prevention of pollution, protection of marine environment, prevention of coastal erosion or prevention of danger to life or property including the marine life. The Act also provides for the power of entry, inspection, search, and seizure, offences and penalties, civil liability and adjudication, and miscellaneous provisions. The Act also includes Schedules that specify the rates of royalty and fixed rent for various minerals and the rates of fixed rent in rupees per standard block per annum.
