Summary:

The Jammu and Kashmir Official Languages Act, 2020 (Act No. 23 of 2020) was enacted on the 26th of September, 2020. The Act aims to provide for the languages to be used for official purposes in the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir.

The Act defines the "Administrator" as the Lieutenant Governor of the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir appointed by the President under Article 239 of the Constitution. The "Union territory" refers to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir.

According to Section 3 of the Act, with effect from a date appointed by the Administrator, the Kashmiri, Dogri, Urdu, Hindi, and English languages shall be the official languages to be used for all or any of the official purposes of the Union territory. The English language may continue to be used for administrative and legislative purposes in the Union territory for which it was being used before the commencement of this Act. The business in the Legislative Assembly of the Union territory shall be transacted in the official language or languages of the Union territory.

Furthermore, Section 4 of the Act empowers the Administrator to take necessary steps for the promotion and development of regional languages of the Union territory. The institutional mechanisms, such as the Academy of Art, Culture and Languages, shall make special efforts for the promotion and development of Gojri, Pahari, and Punjabi languages.

The Act came into force on the 29th of September, 2020, as per the notification No. S.O. 3394(E), dated 29th September, 2020.
