The Railway Property (Unlawful Possession) Act, 1966 is a law in India that aims to consolidate and amend the law relating to unlawful possession of railway property. The Act defines railway property as any goods, money or valuable security or animal, belonging to, or in the charge or possession of, a railway administration. It includes any offences related to the unlawful possession of railway property, including theft, dishonest misappropriation, and abetment or conspiracy to commit such offences. The Act also provides for the powers of arrest, search, and seizure, as well as the trial and punishment of offenders. The Act also empowers officers of the Force to summon any person whose attendance they consider necessary to give evidence or to produce a document, and imposes penalties for non-compliance. The Act also includes provisions for the forfeiture of property used in the commission of offences, and overrides any other laws that may be inconsistent with its provisions. The Act also applies to offences committed in the State of Jammu and Kashmir, with certain modifications to account for the fact that certain laws are not in force in that State. The Act repeals the Railway Stores (Unlawful Possession) Act, 1955, but provides for the continuation of proceedings for offences committed under that Act.
