Summary:

The Capital of Punjab (Development and Regulation) (Chandigarh Amendment) Act, 1973, is an amendment to the Capital of Punjab (Development and Regulation) Act, 1952, as in force in the Union territory of Chandigarh. The Act received the assent of the President on the 9th of April, 1973, and came into force on the 1st of November, 1966, except for Section 7, which came into force immediately.

The Act repeals certain sections of the Punjab Act 37 of 1952 and substitutes new sections for sections 8, 9, 10, and 19 of the principal Act. The new sections are not provided in the text of the amendment Act.

Section 7 of the amendment Act validates any action taken under the principal Act, as amended by this Act, and bars any suit or legal proceedings challenging such actions. The actions validated include the resumption of any site or building, forfeiture of any money, recovery of any arrears, imposition of any penalty, or any other action taken under the principal Act.

Therefore, the Capital of Punjab (Development and Regulation) (Chandigarh Amendment) Act, 1973, is an amendment Act that validates certain actions taken under the principal Act and bars any legal challenges to such actions. The amendment Act also repeals certain sections of the principal Act and substitutes new sections, but the text of the new sections is not provided in the amendment Act.
