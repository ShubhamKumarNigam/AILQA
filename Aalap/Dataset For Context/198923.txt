Summary:
The Assam University Act, 1989 establishes and incorporates a teaching and affiliating university in the State of Assam. The Act defines the objects of the University, which include disseminating and advancing knowledge, promoting inter-disciplinary studies and research, educating and training manpower for the development of Assam, and improving the social and economic conditions and welfare of the people of the state. The Act also outlines the powers of the University, including the ability to grant degrees, recognize institutions and teachers, create administrative and other posts, cooperate with other universities and institutions, and establish campuses, centers, and laboratories. The Act also specifies the jurisdiction of the University, which extends to the Cachar, Karimganj, North Cachar Hills, Karbi Anglong, and Hailakandi districts of Assam. The University is open to all classes, castes, and creeds, and the Act prohibits the University from adopting or imposing any test of religious belief or profession as a condition for appointment, admission, or graduation. The Visitor, who is the President of India, has the power to cause inspections and inquiries to be made and to annul any proceedings of the University that are not in conformity with the Act, the Statutes, or the Ordinances. The Chief Rector of the University is the Governor of Assam. The Act also establishes various authorities of the University, including the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Planning Board, the Boards of Schools, the Finance Committee, and other authorities as may be declared by the Statutes. The Act provides for the appointment of the Chancellor, the Vice-Chancellor, the Pro-Vice-Chancellors, the Deans of Schools, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, and other officers of the University. The Act also sets out the powers and duties of these officers. The Act provides for the making of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations, which are subject to the approval of the Visitor. The Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations may provide for the constitution, powers, and functions of the authorities and other bodies of the University, the appointment, powers, and duties of the officers of the University, the appointment of teachers, academic staff, and other employees of the University, the conditions of service of employees, the management of colleges and institutions admitted to the privileges of the University, and other matters. The Act also sets out the procedure for appealing decisions of the University and for resolving disputes. The Act also provides for the transfer of certain colleges and institutions to the University and for the transition to the new University structure. The Act came into force on the date specified by the Central Government.
