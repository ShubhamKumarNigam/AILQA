Summary:

THE SCHEDULED AREAS (ASSIMILATION OF LAWS) ACT, 1951 (ACT NO. 37 OF 1951) is a law that aims to assimilate certain laws in force in the scheduled areas to the laws in force in the Darrang and Lakhimpur districts of the State of Assam. The Act defines the terms "appointed day," "law," and "scheduled areas."

Section 1 of the Act states the short title and commencement, where the Act may be called the Scheduled Areas (Assimilation of Laws) Act, 1951, and it shall come into force on the date appointed by the Central Government.

Section 2 defines the terms used in the Act, including "appointed day," "law," and "scheduled areas."

Section 3 outlines the assimilation of laws, where all laws in force in the scheduled areas immediately before the appointed day shall cease to be in force, except for things done or omitted to be done before that day. Further, this section directs that all laws that extend to or are in force in the Darrang district of the State of Assam shall extend to or come into force in the areas specified in paragraph 1 of the Schedule, and all laws that extend to or are in force in the Lakhimpur district of the State of Assam shall extend to or come into force in the areas specified in paragraphs 2 and 3 of the Schedule.

Section 4 outlines the transitional provisions, where the Central Government or the State of Assam may direct that during a specified period, not exceeding twelve months from the appointed day, any law that was in force in the scheduled areas shall be deemed to have continued to be in force.

Section 5 outlines the savings, where all suits, cases, and other legal proceedings between parties belonging to the Scheduled Tribes specified in Item 2 of Part I—Assam, of the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, or such other tribe or tribes, as may be specified, shall be tried and continue to be tried under the Assam Frontier (Administration of Justice) Regulation, 1945 (Regulation 1 of 1945) as if this Act had not been passed.

Section 6 outlines the provisions for the removal of difficulties, where the Central Government may make such provisions as it considers necessary for the removal of any difficulty arising in relation to the transition from one law or group of laws to another law or group of laws.

The Schedule specifies the areas included in the Balipara Frontier Tract, the Abor Hills and Mishmi Hills districts (Sadiya Frontier Tract), and the Tirap Frontier Tract, which are the scheduled areas.

The Act aims to assimilate certain laws in force in the scheduled areas to the laws in force in the Darrang and Lakhimpur districts of the State of Assam. The Act provides provisions for the assimilation, transitional provisions, savings, and removal of difficulties, and specifies the scheduled areas.
