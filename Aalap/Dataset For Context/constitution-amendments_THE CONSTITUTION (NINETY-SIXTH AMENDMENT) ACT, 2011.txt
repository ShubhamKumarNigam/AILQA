
 Amendment of the English Schedule.-

In the English Schedule to the Constitution, in the list of languages under the heading “List of Languages specified in the Constitution in Part XVII”, for the language ‘Oriya’, the language ‘Odia’ shall be substituted.



 Explanation.-

For the removal of doubts, it is hereby declared that the reference in the English Schedule to the Constitution to ‘Oriya’ language shall be construed as a reference to the ‘Odia’ language.



 Summary:

The Constitution (Ninety-sixth Amendment) Act, 2011 changes the name of the language mentioned in the English Schedule of the Constitution of India from ‘Oriya’ to ‘Odia’. This amendment aims to accurately represent the language’s name in the Constitution. The Act includes a short title, an amendment to the English Schedule, and a declaration for clarification. The language’s name in the English Schedule will be changed from ‘Oriya’ to ‘Odia’, and any reference to the ‘Oriya’ language in the Constitution will be understood as a reference to the ‘Odia’ language.
