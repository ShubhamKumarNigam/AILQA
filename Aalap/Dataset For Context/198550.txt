Summary:
The Indira Gandhi National Open University Act, 1985 (Act No. 50 of 1985) establishes and incorporates the Indira Gandhi National Open University (IGNOU) to introduce and promote the open university and distance education systems in India. The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including "distance education system," "employee," "Finance Committee," "Governing Body," "Planning Board," "Regional Centre," "Statutes," "Regulations," "student," "Study Centre," "teachers," "University," and "Vice-Chancellor and Pro-Vice-Chancellor."

The Act empowers the University to provide instruction in various branches of knowledge, technology, vocations, and professions, plan and prescribe courses of study, hold examinations and confer degrees, diplomas, certificates, or other academic distinctions, institute professorships, readerships, lecturerships, and other academic positions, cooperate with other universities and institutions, institute and award fellowships, scholarships, prizes, and such other awards, establish and maintain Regional Centres and Study Centres, provide for the preparation of instructional materials, organize and conduct refresher courses, workshops, seminars, and other programs, recognize examinations of or periods of study at other universities or institutions, make provisions for research and development in educational technology and related matters, create administrative, ministerial, and other necessary posts, receive benefactions, donations, and gifts, borrow money, enter into contracts, demand and receive fees and other charges, provide, control, and maintain discipline, recognize any institution of higher learning or studies for specific purposes, and admit to its privileges any College in or outside India.

The Act also provides for the appointment of various officers of the University, including the Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellors, Directors, Registrars, the Finance Officer, and other officers. The powers and duties of these officers are prescribed by the Statutes. The University has various authorities, including the Board of Management, the Academic Council, the Planning Board, the Board of Recognition, the Schools of Studies, the Finance Committee, and other authorities. The composition, powers, and functions of these authorities are prescribed by the Statutes.

The Act provides for the appointment of teachers and other employees of the University, their emoluments and other conditions of service, the constitution of a pension or provident fund, the principles governing the seniority of service, the procedure for the settlement of disputes between the employees or students and the University, the conferment of autonomous status on Colleges and Study Centres, the co-ordination and determination of standards in the open university and distance education systems, the conditions required to be fulfilled for admission of the Colleges to the privileges of the University, and the conditions for the grant of fellowships, awards, and the like.

The Act provides for the making of Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations by the University. The Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations made under the Act shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall be laid before Parliament.

The First Schedule to the Act sets out the objects of the University, which include strengthening and diversifying the degree, certificate, and diploma courses related to the needs of employment, providing access to higher education for large segments of the population, promoting acquisition of knowledge and training in the various arts, crafts, and skills of the country, providing education and training in various fields, providing or arranging training of teachers required for such activities or institutions, providing suitable post-graduate courses of study and promoting research, providing counselling and guidance to students, and contributing to the improvement of the educational system in India.

The Second Schedule to the Act sets out the Statutes of the University, which include provisions regarding the Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellors, Directors, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, the Finance Committee, the Board of Management, the Academic Council, the Planning Board, the Board of Recognition, the Schools of Studies, the appointment of teachers and other academic staff, the recognition of teachers, committees, terms and conditions of service and code of conduct of the teachers and other academic staff, terms and conditions of service and code of conduct of other employees of the University, removal of employees of the University, maintenance of discipline amongst students of the University, and forms.
