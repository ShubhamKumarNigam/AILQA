Summary:

The Kalakshetra Foundation Rules, 1998, made under Section 31 of the Kalakshetra Foundation Act, 1993, came into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. These rules define certain terms, outline the powers and functions of the Governing Board, set the travel and daily allowance for certain members of the Governing Board, and specify the functions of the Finance Committee.

The Governing Board has the power to form committees, formulate policies and programmes, review ongoing programmes, approve budgets and annual reports, maintain accounts, create posts, enter into agreements, acquire property, invest funds, borrow money, and sanction expenditures. However, certain powers, such as creating posts, acquiring property, borrowing money, and sanctioning expenditures, require the concurrence of the Finance Committee.

Certain members of the Governing Board, nominated under clauses (b) and (c) of Section 11, are entitled to traveling allowance and daily allowance for attending meetings at the rates applicable to non-official members of High Powered Committees and Commissions appointed by the Central Government.

The Finance Committee has additional functions, including considering construction works, scrutinizing new proposals, examining proposals for new staff, scrutinizing re-appropriation statements, and advising the Governing Board on any other financial questions affecting the affairs of the Foundation.
