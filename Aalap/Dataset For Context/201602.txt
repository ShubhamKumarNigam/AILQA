This can be done by the State Government on the recommendation of a Committee or Board, keeping the best interest of the child in mind. The total period of stay of the child in a Children's Home or a special home shall not be increased by such transfer.

In addition, the Act allows for the release of a child from an institution. The Committee or the Board may consider the release of a child on certain conditions, such as allowing the child to live with parents or guardians, or under the supervision of an authorized person. If a child fails to fulfill the conditions of their release, the Board or Committee may cause the child to be taken back to the concerned home.

The Act also includes provisions for leave of absence for children placed in an institution. The Committee or the Board may grant leave of absence to a child for special occasions, such as examinations or marriages, under supervision, for a period not exceeding seven days.

Reports related to the child and considered by the Committee or the Board are considered confidential. However, the Committee or the Board may communicate the substance of the report to another Committee or Board, or to the child or their parent or guardian, and give them an opportunity to produce evidence. The victim is not denied access to their case record, orders, and relevant papers.

The Act also includes provisions for the protection of action taken in good faith, and for appeals and revisions. An appeal may be made to the Children's Court against an order made by the Committee or the Board within 30 days. The Court of Sessions or the District Magistrate may entertain the appeal after the expiry of the 30-day period if it is satisfied that the appellant was prevented by sufficient cause from filing the appeal in time.
