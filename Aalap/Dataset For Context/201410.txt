Summary:
The Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University Act, 2014 is an Act to establish and incorporate a University in the Bundelkhand region for the development of agriculture and allied sciences. The University is declared to be an institution of national importance. The Act consists of 45 sections, divided into 10 chapters, and a schedule. The first chapter deals with the short title, commencement, and declaration of the University as an institution of national importance. The second chapter defines various terms used in the Act. The third chapter deals with the University, its objects, jurisdiction, and powers. The fourth chapter deals with the officers of the University, including the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Deans, Directors, Registrar, Comptroller, and University Librarian. The fifth chapter deals with the authorities of the University, including the Board of Management, Academic Council, Research Council, Extension Education Council, Finance Committee, Faculties, and Board of Studies. The sixth chapter deals with the Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations of the University. The seventh chapter deals with the annual report and accounts of the University. The eighth chapter deals with the conditions of service of employees, disputes as to the constitution of University authorities, constitution of committees, filling of casual vacancies, and proceedings of the University authorities not to be invalidated by vacancy. The ninth chapter deals with the protection of action taken in good faith, mode of proof of University records, and removal of difficulties. The tenth chapter deals with transitional provisions and statutes, ordinances, and regulations to be published in the Official Gazette and to be laid before Parliament. The schedule to the Act contains the Statutes of the University. The Act aims to promote agricultural education, research, and extension education in the Bundelkhand region, and to declare the University as an institution of national importance.
