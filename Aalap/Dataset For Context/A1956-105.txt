Summary:

THE WOMEN’S AND CHILDREN’S INSTITUTIONS (LICENSING) ACT, 1956, is an Act that aims to provide for the licensing of institutions that care for women and children in India. The Act applies to the whole country, except for the state of Jammu and Kashmir. It defines several key terms, including "child" as someone under 18 years old, "institution" as a place that receives, cares for, protects, and provides welfare to women or children, "licensing authority" as the government or an officer or authority designated by the government, and "prescribed" as anything specified by rules made by the government.

Under the Act, no person can establish or maintain an institution without a license. To obtain a license, a person must apply to the licensing authority in a prescribed form and provide the necessary details. The licensing authority may grant or refuse the license after making any necessary inquiries. If a license is refused, the reasons for the refusal must be communicated to the applicant. A license, unless revoked earlier, remains in force for a specified period and can be renewed for a further period.

A license granted under this Act is not transferable. The licensing authority may revoke a license if the holder breaches any of the conditions of the license or any provisions of the Act or any rules made under it. The licensing authority may also revoke a license if it is not satisfied with the condition, management, or superintendence of the institution. Before revoking a license, the holder must be given an opportunity to show cause why the license should not be revoked.

Any person who contravenes any provisions of this Act or any rules made under it or any of the conditions of a license is punishable with imprisonment for a term that may extend to three months, or with a fine that may extend to 250 rupees, or with both.

The Act does not apply to hostels or boarding houses attached to or controlled or recognized by educational institutions or any protective home established under the Suppression of Immoral Traffic in Women and Girls Act, 1956.

The government may make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act. These rules may provide for the form of application for a license, the form of the license, the management of institutions, the reception, care, protection, and welfare of women and children in institutions, the inspection of institutions, the maintenance of registers and accounts and submission of returns and audit of such accounts, the discharge from institutions of women and children and their transfer from one institution to another, the manner of filing appeals under this Act and the time within which such appeals shall be filed, the manner of service of orders and notices under this Act, and any other matter that may be prescribed.

On the date of commencement of this Act in any State, any State Act corresponding to this Act in force in that State immediately before such commencement shall stand repealed. However, anything done or any action taken under the provisions of such State Act shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the provisions of this Act as if the said provisions were in force when such thing was done or such action was taken and shall continue in force accordingly until superseded by anything done or any action taken under this Act.
