Summary:
THE TELECOM REGULATORY AUTHORITY OF INDIA ACT, 1997 (ACT NO. 24 OF 1997)
This act establishes the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) and the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT) to regulate the telecommunication services, adjudicate disputes, dispose of appeals and to protect the interests of service providers and consumers of the telecom sector, to promote and ensure orderly growth of the telecom sector.
Chapter I: Preliminary

* This act may be called the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India Act, 1997.
* It extends to the whole of India.
* It shall be deemed to have come into force on the 25th day of January, 1997.
