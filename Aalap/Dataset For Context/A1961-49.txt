Summary:
The Assam Municipal (Manipur Amendment) Act, 1961 is an Act that further amends the Assam Municipal Act, 1956 as in force in the Union territory of Manipur. This Act is divided into seven sections and includes amendments to sections 14, 15, 16, and 301 of the principal Act.

Section 1 of the Act states that it may be called the Assam Municipal (Manipur Amendment) Act, 1961 and will come into force on a date appointed by the Chief Commissioner, Manipur.

Section 2 of the Act substitutes a new section 14 in the principal Act, which specifies the persons entitled to vote at elections of Commissioners and the manner in which the electoral roll for a ward is deemed to be the electoral roll for that ward.

Section 3 of the Act substitutes a new clause (i) in section 15 of the principal Act, which specifies the disqualifications for contesting an election.

Section 4 of the Act inserts a new section 15A in the principal Act, which specifies the right to vote for every person whose name is entered in the electoral roll for a ward.

Section 5 of the Act amends section 16 of the principal Act, which specifies the manner of making nominations and withdrawals of candidates.

Section 6 of the Act inserts a new section 26A in the principal Act, which specifies the transitional provisions for Commissioners of, and general election to, Imphal Municipal Board.

Section 7 of the Act amends section 301 of the principal Act, which specifies the powers and duties of the Commissioners.

Overall, this Act aims to further amend the Assam Municipal Act, 1956 as in force in the Union territory of Manipur, specifically focusing on electors and electoral rolls, disqualifications for contesting an election, and the powers and duties of the Commissioners.
