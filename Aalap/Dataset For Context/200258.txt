Summary:
The Unit Trust of India (Transfer of Undertaking and Repeal) Act, 2002 is an Act to provide for the transfer and vesting of the undertaking (excluding the specified undertaking) of the Unit Trust of India to the specified company to be formed and registered under the Companies Act, 1956, and the transfer and vesting of the specified undertaking of the Unit Trust of India in the Administrator and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto and also to repeal the Unit Trust of India Act, 1963.

The Act has 25 sections divided into 5 chapters. Chapter I is the preliminary chapter, which includes the short title and commencement of the Act, and the definitions of various terms used in the Act. Chapter II deals with the transfer and vesting of the undertaking of the Trust in the specified company and the transfer and vesting of the specified undertaking of the Trust in the Administrator. Chapter III deals with the appointment of an Administrator to manage the specified undertaking of the Trust. Chapter IV deals with the powers and functions of the Administrator. Chapter V contains miscellaneous provisions.

The Act also includes two schedules. Schedule I lists the schemes and assets and investments of the specified undertaking of the Trust, and Schedule II lists the schemes and plans of the undertaking of the Trust.

The Act repeals the Unit Trust of India Act, 1963 and provides for the transfer of the undertaking of the Trust to the specified company, the transfer of the specified undertaking of the Trust to the Administrator, and the appointment of an Administrator to manage the specified undertaking of the Trust. The Act also provides for the powers and functions of the Administrator and includes miscellaneous provisions. The Act includes two schedules, which list the schemes and assets and investments of the specified undertaking of the Trust and the schemes and plans of the undertaking of the Trust.
