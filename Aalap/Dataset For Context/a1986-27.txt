Summary:

The Coal Mines Labour Welfare Fund (Repeal) Act, 1986, is a law that repeals the Coal Mines Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1947, and dissolves the Coal Mines Labour Housing Board. The Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Central Government.

The Act defines certain terms, including "the Act" which refers to the Coal Mines Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1947, "appointed day" which means the date on which this Act comes into force, and "Housing Board" which means the Coal Mines Labour Housing Board constituted under section 6 of the Act.

On the appointed day, the Coal Mines Labour Welfare Fund Act, 1947, stands repealed, and the Coal Mines Labour Housing Board stands dissolved. The rights and privileges of the Housing Board become the rights and privileges of the Central Government. All properties, movable and immovable, including cash balances, reserve funds, investments, and moneys lying to the credit of the Housing Board, and all rights and interests in or arising out of such properties, as well as all books of account, registers, and records and all other documents of whatever nature relating thereto, shall vest in the Central Government.

All borrowings, liabilities, and obligations of the Housing Board of whatever kind and subsisting immediately before the appointed day shall be deemed on and from such day to be the borrowings, liabilities, and obligations, as the case may be, of the Central Government. All contracts entered into, and all matters and things engaged to be done by, with, or for, the Housing Board, and subsisting immediately before the appointed day, shall be deemed, on and from such day, to have been entered into or engaged to be done by, with, or for, the Central Government.

The Central Government may direct that the right, title, and interest of the Housing Board in relation to any property shall vest in a Government company instead of continuing to vest in it. The liability or obligation of the Housing Board, in relation to such property, shall become the liability or obligation, as the case may be, of the Government company.

If, on the appointed day, any suit, appeal, or other proceeding of whatever nature in relation to the Housing Board is pending by or against such Board, the same shall not abate, be discontinued, or be in any way prejudicially affected by reason of the dissolution of the Housing Board. The suit, appeal, or other proceeding may be continued, prosecuted, and enforced by or against the Central Government or the Government company, as the case may be.

On the appointed day, all moneys and cash balances lying in the credit of the housing account and the general welfare account of the Coal Mines Labour Housing and General Welfare Fund constituted under section 4 of the Act, shall become part of, and be credited to, the Consolidated Fund of India.

Notwithstanding the repeal of the Act, the proceeds of duty levied before the appointed day under section 3 of the Act shall be paid or, as the case may be, collected and paid, into the Reserve Bank of India for being credited to the Consolidated Fund of India.
