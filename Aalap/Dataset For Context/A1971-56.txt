Summary:
THE COMPTROLLER AND AUDITOR-GENERAL’S (DUTIES, POWERS AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE) ACT, 1971

This Act lays down the conditions of service of the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India and defines his duties and powers. It consists of four chapters:

Chapter I: Preliminary

* This chapter defines the short title and the definitions used in the Act.

Chapter II: Salary and other conditions of service of the Comptroller and Auditor-General

* This chapter defines the salary, term of office, leave, and pension of the Comptroller and Auditor-General.

Chapter III: Duties and powers of the Comptroller and Auditor-General

* This chapter defines the duties and powers of the Comptroller and Auditor-General in relation to the compilation, preparation, and submission of accounts of the Union, States, and Union territories having Legislative Assemblies.
* It also defines the Comptroller and Auditor-General’s duties and powers in relation to the audit of receipts and expenditure of bodies or authorities substantially financed from Union or State revenues.

Chapter IV: Miscellaneous

* This chapter defines the delegation of power of the Comptroller and Auditor-General, the power to make rules, the power to make regulations, the power to dispense with detailed audit, repeal, and removal of doubts.

The Act also includes a schedule that lays down the conditions of service of the Comptroller and Auditor-General.
