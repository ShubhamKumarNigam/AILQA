Summary:

THE LAND ACQUISITION (MINES) ACT, 1885, is a law that applies to the whole of India, except for certain territories. The Act is designed to provide for cases in which mines or minerals are located under land that is being acquired under the Land Acquisition Act of 1870. The Act includes provisions for saving the mineral rights of the government, declaring that mines are not needed, giving notice before working mines, preventing or restricting working, determining compensation, working mines in a proper manner, mining communications, paying compensation for injury to mines, paying compensation for injury from airways or other works, entering and inspecting the working of mines, penalties for refusal to allow inspection, requiring means to be adopted for safety of land acquired, and defining local authority and Company. The Act is to be read in conjunction with the Land Acquisition Act of 1870.
