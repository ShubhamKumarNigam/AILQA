The Act aims to ensure the sale of plots, apartments, or buildings in an efficient and transparent manner, protect the interests of consumers in the real estate sector, and establish an adjudicating mechanism for speedy dispute redressal. The Act applies to the whole of India except the state of Jammu and Kashmir and came into force on a date appointed by the Central Government.
