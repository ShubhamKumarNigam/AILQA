The Act is divided into five chapters. Chapter I, Preliminary, defines various terms used in the Act. Chapter II, Bureau of Indian Standards, establishes the Bureau and its Governing Council, Executive Committee, and Advisory Committees. Chapter III, Indian Standards, Certification and Licence, establishes the Indian Standards, prohibits the publication, reproduction, or recording of Indian Standards without authorization, and grants licenses or certificates of conformity for goods, articles, processes, systems, or services that conform to Indian Standards. The Act aims to promote and monitor the quality of goods, articles, processes, systems, and services in India, protect the interests of consumers and various other stakeholders, and prevent unfair trade practices. The Act establishes the Bureau of Indian Standards as the national standards body responsible for the development and promotion of Indian Standards, conformity assessment and quality assurance of goods, articles, processes, systems, and services. The Act provides for the establishment of a Governing Council, Executive Committee, and Advisory Committees to oversee the activities of the Bureau. The Act also establishes the Indian Standards, which are the standards established and published by the Bureau, and grants licenses or certificates of conformity for goods, articles, processes, systems, or services that conform to Indian Standards.
