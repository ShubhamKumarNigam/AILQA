Summary:

THE DELHI LAWS (SPECIAL PROVISIONS) ACT, 2006 is an Act that aims to provide temporary relief to the people of Delhi against punitive action for a period of one year. This Act was enacted due to the phenomenal increase in population, which put tremendous pressure on land and infrastructure, leading to developments that are not in consonance with the Master Plan of Delhi 2001 and building bye-laws. The Act provides a definition of various terms such as building bye-laws, Delhi, encroachment, local authority, Master Plan, notification, punitive action, relevant law, and unauthorized development.

The Act states that the Central Government shall take all possible measures to finalize norms, policy guidelines, and feasible strategies to deal with the problem of unauthorized development in a sustainable and planned manner. It also states that status quo as on the 1st day of January, 2006 shall be maintained in respect of the categories of unauthorized development mentioned in sub-section (1), and all notices issued by any local authority for initiating action against the categories of unauthorized development referred to in sub-section(1) shall be deemed to have been suspended.

However, the Act does not provide relief in respect of certain categories of unauthorized development, such as any construction unauthorized started or continued on or after the 1st day of January, 2006, commencement of any commercial activity in residential areas in violation of the provisions of the Master Plan of Delhi 2001 on or after the 1st day of January, 2006, encroachment on public land except in those cases which are covered under clause (c) of sub-section (1) of section 3, and removal of slums and Jhuggi-Jhompri dwellers and hawkers and street vendors, in accordance with the relevant policies approved by the Central Government for clearance of land required for specific public projects.

The Central Government may also issue directions to the local authorities for giving effect to the provisions of this Act, and it shall be the duty of the local authorities to comply with such directions. The Act shall cease to have effect on the expiry of one year from the date of its commencement, except as respects things done or omitted to be done before such cesser.
