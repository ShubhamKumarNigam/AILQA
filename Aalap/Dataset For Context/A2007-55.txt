Summary:
The Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007 (Act No. 55 of 2007) establishes the Armed Forces Tribunal (AFT) to exercise jurisdiction, powers, and authority over service matters and appeals against any order, decision, finding, or sentence passed by a court martial. The AFT is composed of a Chairperson and Judicial and Administrative Members, who are appointed by the President. The Act provides for the qualifications, appointment, term of office, salaries, allowances, resignation, removal, and terms and conditions of service of the Chairperson and Members. The AFT has the same powers as a Civil Court for the purpose of adjudicating service matters and appeals. The Act also includes provisions for the distribution of business among the Benches, procedure, limitations, and execution of orders of the Tribunal. An appeal to the Supreme Court lies against the final decision or order of the Tribunal, subject to certain conditions and limitations. The Act also includes provisions for the transfer of pending cases, condonation of delay, exclusion of jurisdiction of civil courts, protection of action taken in good faith, and power of the Central Government to make rules.
