Summary:
The Export Report (Form) Regulations, 1976, outlines the rules for creating and submitting an Export Report for goods being transported out of India. The regulations specify that the report should be submitted in duplicate, cover all the goods in a vehicle, and be printed on durable white paper. The form itself is divided into two parts: Part A (Cargo) and Part B (Stores). Part A includes information about the owner or operator, the date and time of departure, the vehicle registration number, and details about the cargo, such as the waybill number, number and description of packages, identifying marks and numbers, quantity and weight, destination of goods, name of the consignor or exporter, description of goods, shipment particulars, and any remarks. Part B includes information about non-duty paid consumable or non-consumable articles, such as their description, unit, weight, quantity, and any remarks. These regulations were first introduced in 1976 and have been put in force by the Central Board of Excise and Customs.
