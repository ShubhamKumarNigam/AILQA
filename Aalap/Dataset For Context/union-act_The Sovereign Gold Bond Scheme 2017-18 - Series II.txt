The Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2017-18 - Series II is a government-backed investment scheme that allows individuals, trusts, charitable institutions, universities, and Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs) to invest in sovereign gold bonds. These bonds are denominated in grams of gold and are issued by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) on behalf of the Government of India.

The minimum investment limit is one gram of gold, and the maximum limit is 500 grams per person per fiscal year. The issue price of the bonds is calculated based on the simple average of the closing price of gold of 999 purity published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Limited for the week preceding the subscription period, with a discount of 50 rupees per gram.

Applications for the bonds can be made to any authorized receiving office, which includes nationalized banks, scheduled private banks, scheduled foreign banks, designated post offices, Stock Holding Corporation of India Ltd., and authorized stock exchanges. The application should be made in Form A or a form similar to it, and the receiving office will issue an acknowledgment receipt in Form B if all requirements are fulfilled.

The bonds will be issued on the 28th day of July 2017 in the form of a Stock Certificate as specified in Form C. The bonds will be eligible to be converted into Demat form.

The bonds will earn a fixed rate of interest at 2.50% per annum on the nominal value, payable in half-yearly rests. The interest shall be payable in Indian Rupees and the redemption price shall be based on simple average of closing price of gold of 999 purity of previous week published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Limited.

The bonds will be repayable on the expiration of eight years from the date of issue, and premature redemption may be permitted after five years. The receiving office shall inform the investor one month in advance about the date of maturity.

The investment in the bonds will be eligible for Statutory Liquidity Ratio purpose and can be used as collateral security for any loan. The capital gains tax arising on redemption of SGB to an individual is exempted and indexation benefits will be provided to long-term capital gains arising to any person on transfer of bond.

Nomination and transfer of bonds can be made in accordance with the provisions of the Government Securities Act, 2006, and the Government Securities Regulations, 2007. The bonds shall be eligible for trading from such date as may be notified by the RBI. The commission for distribution shall be paid at the rate of rupee one per hundred of the total subscription received by the receiving offices and receiving offices shall share at least 50% of the commission so received with the agents or sub-agents for the business procured through them.

The Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme 2017-18 - Series II is a safe and convenient way to invest in gold, with the added benefits of earning interest and avoiding the costs associated with physical gold. It is a good option for those who want to invest in gold as a long-term investment or as part of their diversified investment portfolio.
