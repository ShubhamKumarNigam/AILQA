Summary:

THE PROHIBITION OF ELECTRONIC CIGARETTES (PRODUCTION, MANUFACTURE, IMPORT, EXPORT, TRANSPORT, SALE, DISTRIBUTION, STORAGE AND ADVERTISEMENT) ACT, 2019, prohibits the production, manufacture, import, export, transport, sale, distribution, storage and advertisement of electronic cigarettes in India. The Act defines electronic cigarettes as devices that heat a substance, with or without nicotine and flavors, to create an aerosol for inhalation. It includes all forms of Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems, Heat Not Burn Products, e-Hookah, and similar devices. The Act came into force on September 18, 2019, and deems it expedient in the public interest that the Union should take control of the electronic cigarettes industry. The Act defines various terms, including advertisement, authorized officer, distribution, electronic cigarette, export, import, manufacture, notification, person, place, production, and sale. The Act prohibits the production, manufacture, import, export, transport, sale, distribution, and advertisement of electronic cigarettes. It also prohibits the storage of electronic cigarettes, except for existing stocks, which must be disposed of in accordance with the law. The Act empowers authorized officers to enter, search, and seize without a warrant any place where electronic cigarettes are produced, supplied, distributed, stored, or transported, or where any advertisement of electronic cigarettes is being made. The Act provides for punishment for contravention of the provisions of the Act, with imprisonment, fine, or both. The Act also provides for the trial of offenses, disposal of seized stock, offenses by companies, cognizance of offenses, offenses to be cognizable, Act to have overriding effect, application of other laws not barred, protection of action taken in good faith, power to remove difficulties, repeal and savings.
