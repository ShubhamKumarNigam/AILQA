Summary:

THE SHILLONG (RIFILE RANGE AND UMLONG) CANTONMENTS ASSIMILATION OF LAWS ACT, 1954, is an Act to assimilate certain laws in force in the scheduled areas to the laws in force in the Khasi and Jaintia Hills District. The Act is divided into four sections, including the Short title and commencement, Definitions, Assimilation of laws, and Removal of difficulties.

Under Section 1, the Act can be called the Shillong (Rifle Range and Umlong) Cantonments Assimilation of Laws Act, 1954, and will come into force on the date appointed by the Central Government.

Section 2 provides the definitions of terms used in the Act, including appointed day, law, and scheduled areas. The appointed day refers to the date appointed under sub-section (2) of section 1 for the coming into force of this Act. Law means so much of any Act, Ordinance, Regulation, rule, order, or bye-law as relates to any of the matters enumerated in Lists I and III in the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution. Scheduled areas mean the areas specified in the Schedule.

Section 3 deals with the assimilation of laws. According to sub-section (1), all laws which immediately before the appointed day extend to, or are in force in the scheduled areas shall on that day cease to be in force in the scheduled areas except as respects things done or omitted to be done before that day. For the removal of doubts, it is herby declared that section 6 of the General Clauses Act, 1897 (10 of 1897) shall apply in relation to such successor as it applies in relation to the repeal of an enactment by a Central Act. Sub-section (2) states that all laws which immediately before the appointed day extend to, or in force in, the Khasi and Jaintia Hills District shall as from that day extend to and be in force in the scheduled areas.

Under Section 4, if any difficulty arises in relation to the transition under section 3 from one law or group of laws to another law or group of laws, the Central Government may, by order notified in the Official Gazette, make such provisions as it considers necessary for the removal of the difficulty.

The Schedule specifies the Shillong (Rifle Range) Cantonment and the Shillong (Umlong) Cantonment, including their boundaries and the bearings and distances from magnetic north. The annexure, appendix, forms, and footnotes are not provided in the document.
