The Small Industries Development Bank of India (Staff) Regulations, 2001, published on December 26, 2001, provide guidelines for the appointment, conditions of service, and other terms and conditions of employment for the staff of the Small Industries Development Bank of India (SIDBI). These regulations apply to all employees of SIDBI, including its branches and offices.

The regulations cover various aspects of employment, such as eligibility criteria for appointment, qualifications and experience required for different positions, probation period, confirmation of service, leave rules, and termination of service. They also outline the powers and responsibilities of the appointing authorities, the Board of Directors, and the Management Committee of SIDBI.

The regulations provide for the appointment of officers and other staff in various grades and scales of pay, based on their qualifications and experience. The Bank may also recruit employees on a contract basis, subject to certain conditions and for a specified period.

The regulations specify the probation period for different categories of employees, which may range from six months to two years. During the probation period, the employee's performance is evaluated, and if found satisfactory, the employee is confirmed in service.

The regulations provide for various types of leave, such as earned leave, casual leave, medical leave, and maternity leave. The entitlement to leave and the duration of leave vary depending on the length of service and the category of employee.

The regulations also provide for the termination of service of an employee, which may be either voluntary or involuntary. Voluntary retirement is permitted after completion of a specified period of service, while involuntary retirement may be initiated on grounds of misconduct or underperformance.

Overall, the Small Industries Development Bank of India (Staff) Regulations, 2001, provide a comprehensive framework for the appointment, conditions of service, and other terms and conditions of employment for the staff of SIDBI, ensuring a fair and transparent system of employment and promoting a conducive work environment.
