THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994 

 Insertion of new article.-

After article 31C of the Constitution, the following article shall be inserted, namely:-

"31D. Protection of laws providing for the reservation of appointments or posts in favour of any backward class of citizens which, in the opinion of the State, is not adequately represented in the services under the State.

(1) Notwithstanding anything contained in article 14, 15, 16, 19 or 30, no law providing for the reservation of appointments or posts in favour of any backward class of citizens which, in the opinion of the State, is not adequately represented in the services under the State shall be deemed to be void on the ground that it is discriminatory or that it violates the right to equality of opportunity in matters of public employment on the ground of religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth, residence or any of them.

(2) In making any provision for reservation under clause (1), the State shall have regard to the following factors, namely:-

(a) the total number of posts in the cadre and the level of cadres, composition of the cadre, the number of existing reservations and the desirability of maintaining a balance between the reservations;

(b) the steps taken by the State to provide adequate representation in the services under the State to the persons belonging to the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes and to the other backward classes of citizens;

(c) the claims of the members of the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes and the other backward classes of citizens in respect of the services under the State;

(d) the availability of persons belonging to the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes and the other backward classes of citizens qualified to fill the posts and the desirability of filling up such posts by persons belonging to such classes;

(e) the desirability of avoiding all forms of discrimination and of providing adequate opportunities for suitable employment to all persons belonging to the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes and the other backward classes of citizens;

(f) the desirability of providing for the advancement of the persons belonging to the Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled Tribes and the other backward classes of citizens in the services under the State.".


THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994 

 Amendment of article 338.-

In article 338 of the Constitution, for the words "National Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes", the words "National Commission for Scheduled Castes" shall be substituted.


THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994 

 Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

In the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, after entry 252 and before the Explanation, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"252A. The Tamil Nadu Backward Classes, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Seats in Educational Institutions and of appointments or posts in the Services under the State) Act, 1993 (Tamil Nadu Act 45 of 1994).".


THE CONSTITUTION (SEVENTY-SIXTH AMENDMENT) ACT, 1994 

 Repeal and savings.-

(1) The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (Members' Salaries and Allowances) (Amendment) Ordinance, 1994 is hereby repealed.

(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly (Members' Salaries and Allowances) Act, 1955 (Tamil Nadu Act 18 of 1955).


Summary:

The Constitution (Seventy-sixth Amendment) Act, 1994, was enacted to provide protection to laws that provide for the reservation of appointments or posts in favor of any backward class of citizens that, in the opinion of the State, is not adequately represented in the services under the State. This amendment inserted a new article, 31D, in the Constitution, which provides that such laws shall not be deemed void on the ground that they are discriminatory or violate the right to equality of opportunity in matters of public employment on the ground of religion, race, caste, sex, descent, place of birth, residence, or any of them.

The amendment also provided for the factors that the State shall consider while making any provision for reservation, such as the total number of posts in the cadre, the composition of the cadre, the number of existing reservations, the claims of the members of the Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and other backward classes, the availability of qualified persons belonging to these classes, and the desirability of avoiding all forms of discrimination and providing adequate opportunities for suitable employment to all persons belonging to these classes.

Further, the amendment also amended article 338 of the Constitution, replacing the words "National Commission for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes" with "National Commission for Scheduled Castes."

Finally, the amendment included the Tamil Nadu Backward Classes, Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Reservation of Seats in Educational Institutions and of appointments or posts in the Services under the State) Act, 1993 (Tamil Nadu Act 45 of 1994) in the Ninth Schedule to the Constitution, to provide it protection under article 31B of the Constitution in regard to judicial review.
