Summary:
The Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Orders (Amendment) Act, 1976, is an Act that amends the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, and the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, to include or exclude certain castes and tribes from the lists of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The Act also provides for the re-adjustment of representation of parliamentary and assembly constituencies in so far as such re-adjustment is necessitated by such inclusion or exclusion. The Act defines various terms such as "census authority", "Commission", "Delimitation Act", "last census", "Scheduled Castes Order", and "Scheduled Tribes Orders". The Act consists of five sections, with Section 3 and Section 4 being repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988. Section 5 of the Act deals with the determination of population of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes, while Section 6 provides for the re-adjustment of constituencies by the Election Commission. Section 7 outlines the procedure and powers of the Commission, and Section 8 deals with the publication of amendments and their dates of operation. The Act also includes two schedules, which have been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988. The Act validates all acts done and steps taken before its commencement, in conformity with its provisions. The Act came into force on the 18th of September, 1976.
