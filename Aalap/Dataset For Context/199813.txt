The National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research Act, 1998, declares the National Institute of Pharmaceutical Education and Research (NIPER) to be an institution of national importance. This Act establishes NIPER as a body corporate and provides for its incorporation and related matters. The Act sets out the powers and functions of the Institute, the composition and powers of the Board of Governors, and the establishment and functions of the Senate. The Act also includes provisions for grants by the Central Government, the maintenance of accounts and audit, and the establishment of pension and provident funds for employees. Additionally, the Act sets out the procedures for making Statutes and Ordinances, and includes miscellaneous provisions such as the power to remove difficulties and transitional provisions. The Act was amended in 2002 and 2007 to make various changes, including the addition of new sections and the modification of existing sections.
