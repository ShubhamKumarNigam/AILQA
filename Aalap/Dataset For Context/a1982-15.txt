Summary:

THE CUSTOMS TARIFF (AMENDMENT) ACT, 1982, with the Act ID ACT NO. 15 OF 1982, was enacted on the 11th of May, 1982. This Act aims to amend the Customs Tariff Act of 1975.

The Act comprises three sections. Section 1 is the short title, stating that this Act may be referred to as the Customs Tariff (Amendment) Act, 1982.

Section 2, which has been repealed by the Repealing and Amending Act, 1988, had previously amended the First Schedule of the principal Act. However, this amendment no longer applies from the 31st of March, 1988.

Section 3 focuses on the amendment with respect to certain stainless steel articles, which is deemed to have retrospective effect from the 1st of January, 1981. During the period from that day until the introduction of the Customs Tariff (Amendment) Bill, 1982 in the House of the People, circles, angles, shapes, and sections of stainless steel were exempt from any duty of customs that exceeded 220 per cent. ad valorem. The auxiliary duty of customs chargeable on such circles, angles, shapes, and sections was the same as that chargeable on sheets falling under sub-heading No. (2) of Heading No. 73.15.
