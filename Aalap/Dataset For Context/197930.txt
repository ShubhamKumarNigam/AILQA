Summary:

THE INTER-STATE MIGRANT WORKMEN (REGULATION OF EMPLOYMENT AND CONDITIONS OF SERVICE) ACT, 1979

Act ID: ACT NO. 30 OF 1979

This Act aims to regulate the employment of inter-State migrant workmen and provide for their conditions of service and related matters. It applies to every establishment in which five or more inter-State migrant workmen are employed or were employed on any day of the preceding twelve months, and to every contractor who employs or employed five or more inter-State migrant workmen on any day of the preceding twelve months. The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including "appropriate Government", "contractor", "controlled industry", "establishment", "inter-State migrant workman", "prescribed", "principal employer", "recruitment", and "wages".

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary provisions, including the short title, extent, commencement, and application of the Act.

Chapter II focuses on the registration of establishments employing inter-State migrant workmen. Every principal employer of an establishment to which this Act applies is required to register the establishment with the registering officer within a specified period. The registering officer may grant or refuse the registration and issue a certificate of registration to the principal employer. The registration may be revoked in certain cases.

Chapter III deals with the licensing of contractors who recruit inter-State migrant workmen. A contractor cannot recruit any person in a State for the purpose of employing him in any establishment situated in another State without a license. The licensing officer may grant or refuse the license and impose conditions on the license. The license may be revoked in certain cases.

Chapter IV imposes duties on contractors, including the duty to furnish particulars of inter-State migrant workmen, issue a pass book to every inter-State migrant workman, and furnish a return in respect of every inter-State migrant workman who ceases to be employed.

Chapter V provides for wages, welfare, and other facilities to be provided to inter-State migrant workmen. The wage rates, holidays, hours of work, and other conditions of service of an inter-State migrant workman shall be the same as those applicable to any other workman in the establishment. Every contractor shall ensure regular payment of wages to such workmen, equal pay for equal work, suitable conditions of work, suitable residential accommodation, medical facilities, protective clothing, and report fatal accidents or serious bodily injury to any such workman.

Chapter VI deals with inspecting staff appointed under the Act. The appropriate Government may appoint inspectors for the purposes of the Act and define their local limits. The inspector may enter any premises or place, examine any person, require any information, seize or take copies of any register or record, and exercise such other powers as may be prescribed.

Chapter VII contains miscellaneous provisions, including the powers to make rules, repeal and saving, and the power to remove difficulties.

The Schedule to the Act lists the enactments under which an inter-State migrant workman shall, on and from the date of his recruitment, be deemed to be employed and actually worked in the establishment or, as the case may be, the first establishment in connection with the work of which he is employed.

The Appendix to the Act contains forms for registration, license, and other purposes.
