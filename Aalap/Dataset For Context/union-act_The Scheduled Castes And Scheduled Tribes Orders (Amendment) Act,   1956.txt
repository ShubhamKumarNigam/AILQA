Summary:

The Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Orders (Amendment) Act, 1956, is an Act that amends the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, and the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) (Part C States) Order, 1951, in relation to Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. The Act includes definitions for terms such as "article," "census authority," "last census," and "prescribed."

The Act amends the Scheduled Castes Orders in two ways. Firstly, it amends the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) Order, 1950, as specified in Schedule I. Secondly, it amends the Constitution (Scheduled Castes) (Part C States) Order, 1951, as specified in Schedule II.

Similarly, the Act amends the Scheduled Tribes Orders in two ways. Firstly, it amends the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, as specified in Schedule III. Secondly, it amends the Schedule to the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) (Part C States) Order, 1951, as specified in Schedule IV.

The Act also includes provisions for determining the population of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. If the list of Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes in relation to any State is varied by this Act, the population of the Scheduled Castes or Scheduled Tribes in that State shall be ascertained or estimated by the census authority in such manner as may be prescribed and shall be notified by that authority in the Gazette of India.

Additionally, the Act imposes duties on the Delimitation Commission constituted under section 43 of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956, to redetermine the number of seats to be reserved for the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes of a State in the House of the People and in the Legislative Assembly of that State, having regard to the relevant provisions of the Constitution and of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956.

Finally, the Act empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act. Every rule made under this Act shall be laid before each House of Parliament, while it is in session, for a total period of thirty days, and if both Houses agree in making any modification in the rule or both Houses agree that the rule should not be made, the rule shall have effect only in such modified form or be of no effect, as the case may be.
