Summary:
The Andhra Scientific Company Limited (Acquisition and Transfer of Undertakings) Act, 1982 (Act No. 71 of 1982) is a law enacted on 13th November, 1982 to acquire and transfer the undertakings of the Andhra Scientific Company Limited. The objective of this Act is to ensure the proper management of the Company's undertakings, which are vital to the needs of the country, and to secure the continuity of production of scientific instruments. The Act specifies the provisions for the transfer of the Company's undertakings to the Central Government, the management of the Company's undertakings, the payment of amounts, the appointment of a Commissioner of Payments, and miscellaneous provisions. The Act includes a Schedule that outlines the order of priorities for the discharge of the Company's liabilities. The Act also provides for the protection of actions taken in good faith, the delegation of powers, and the power to make rules.
