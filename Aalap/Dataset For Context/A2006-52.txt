Summary:
The Uttaranchal (Alteration of Name) Act, 2006, was enacted on December 21, 2006, with the purpose of changing the name of the State of Uttaranchal to Uttarakhand. The Act specifies that the name change will take effect on a date appointed by the Central Government. The Act defines certain terms, including "appointed day," "appropriate Government," and "law."

The Act's main provision is Section 3, which states that the State of Uttaranchal will be known as the State of Uttarakhand from the appointed day. Section 4 amends the First Schedule of the Constitution to reflect the new name of the State. Similarly, Section 5 amends the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution.

Section 6 of the Act grants the appropriate Government the power to adapt any law made before the appointed day, whether by way of repeal or amendment, as may be necessary or expedient. The Government has one year from the appointed day to make such adaptations. Section 7 allows any court, tribunal, or authority to construe the law in such a manner as may be necessary or proper in regard to the matter before it.

Finally, Section 8 states that where any legal proceedings are pending to which the State of Uttaranchal is a party, the State of Uttarakhand shall be deemed to be substituted for the State of Uttaranchal in those proceedings. The appointed day for the commencement of this Act was January 1, 2007.
