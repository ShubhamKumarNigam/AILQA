Summary:

THE PROTECTION OF CHILDREN FROM SEXUAL OFFENCES ACT, 2012 (ACT NO. 32 OF 2012) was enacted on 19th June, 2012, to protect children from sexual offences, sexual harassment, and pornography, and to establish Special Courts for trial of such offences. The Act aims to ensure the best interests and well-being of the child, protecting their privacy and confidentiality during judicial proceedings. The Act also requires State parties to undertake measures to prevent the inducement, coercion, or exploitation of children in unlawful sexual activities, prostitution, or pornographic performances and materials. The Act defines various terms, including "child," "child pornography," "domestic relationship," "penetrative sexual assault," "sexual assault," "sexual harassment," and "Special Court." The Act also specifies the punishment for various offences, including penetrative sexual assault, aggravated penetrative sexual assault, sexual assault, aggravated sexual assault, and sexual harassment. The Act also includes provisions for reporting cases, the obligation of media, studio, and photographic facilities to report cases, and punishment for failure to report or record a case. The Act also includes provisions for the medical examination of a child and designation of Special Courts. The Special Courts have jurisdiction to try offences under the Act, and the Act also includes provisions for the procedure and powers of Special Courts and recording of evidence of the child. The Act also includes miscellaneous provisions, such as guidelines for a child to take assistance of experts, the right of a child to take assistance of a legal practitioner, provisions of sections 3 to 13 not to apply in certain cases, alternative punishment, and the Act not to be in derogation of any other law. The Act also includes provisions for public awareness about the Act and monitoring of its implementation. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out its purposes. The Schedule to the Act lists the Armed Forces and Security Forces constituted under various laws.
