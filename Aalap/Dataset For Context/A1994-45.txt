Summary:

THE PUNJAB MUNICIPAL CORPORATION LAW (EXTENSION TO CHANDIGARH) ACT, 1994

Act ID: ACT NO. 45 OF 1994

Enactment Date: [22nd July, 1994.]

This Act extends the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 to the Union territory of Chandigarh. The Act includes references to the State, authorities, or City mentioned in column 1 of Part I of the Schedule as references to the State, authorities, or City mentioned in column 2 of that Part. The Act also amends the Punjab Municipal Corporation Act, 1976 as specified in Part II of the Schedule. The Punjab Municipal Act, 1911 insofar as it applies to the Union territory of Chandigarh or any part thereof is repealed on the coming into force of this Act. However, the repeal shall not affect the previous operation of the Punjab Municipal Act, 1911, any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under the said Act, or any investigation, legal proceeding or remedy in respect of any such right, privilege, obligation or liability. The Act also includes a transitory provision for the appointment of a Special Officer to exercise the powers and discharge the functions of the Municipal Corporation of Chandigarh until the day on which the first meeting of the Corporation is held after the commencement of this Act. The Act also repeals the Punjab Municipal Corporation Law (Extension to Chandigarh) Ordinance, 1994 and saves anything done or any action taken under the Ordinance.
