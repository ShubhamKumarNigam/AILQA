Summary:

THE INDIAN INSTITUTES OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY ACT, 2014

Act ID: ACT NO. 30 OF 2014

Enactment Date: [8th December, 2014.]

The Indian Institutes of Information Technology Act, 2014, declares certain institutions of information technology as institutions of national importance, with the objective of developing new knowledge in information technology and providing manpower of global standards for the information technology industry. The Act establishes these institutions as not-for-profit legal entities, with the power to create infrastructure, institute and award fellowships, scholarships, and other academic distinctions or titles, and to co-operate with other educational or research institutions in India and abroad.

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary matters, including the short title and commencement of the Act, the declaration of certain institutions as institutions of national importance, and definitions of key terms used in the Act.

Chapter II of the Act outlines the authorities of the Institutes, including the Board of Governors, Senate, Finance Committee, Building and Works Committee, and Research Council. The Board of Governors is the principal executive body of the Institute, responsible for the superintendence, direction, and control of the affairs of the Institute. The Senate is the principal academic body of the Institute, responsible for enacting, amending, modifying, or rescinding Ordinances governing academic matters and the affairs and well-being of students in the Institute.

Chapter III of the Act deals with the finance and accounts of the Institutes, including the powers and duties of the Finance Committee, the Building and Works Committee, and the Research Council. The Finance Committee is responsible for examining the accounts, scrutinizing proposals for expenditure and financial estimates, and submitting them to the Board of Governors for approval. The Building and Works Committee is responsible for construction, maintenance, and repair of the Institute's buildings and other capital works. The Research Council is responsible for promoting and conducting research and innovation in information technology and allied fields.

Chapter IV of the Act deals with miscellaneous matters, including the power of the Central Government to issue directions to the Institutes, the application of the Right to Information Act, 2005 to the Institutes, and transitional provisions.

The Schedule to the Act lists the existing Institutes that are declared as institutions of national importance under the Act. These Institutes are the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Allahabad, the Atal Bihari Vajpayee Indian Institute of Information Technology and Management, Gwalior, the Indian Institute of Information Technology and Design and Manufacturing, Jabalpur, the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Design and Manufacturing, Kancheepuram, and the Indian Institute of Information Technology, Kurnool.
