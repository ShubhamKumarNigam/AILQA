Summary:
The National Rural Employment Guarantee (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Act, 2007, also known as Act No. 23 of 2007, came into effect on the 11th of May, 2007. This Act aims to extend the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (42 of 2005) and all related rules, orders, and schemes to the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

Section 1 of the Act specifies the short title and commencement of the Act. It states that this Act may be called the National Rural Employment Guarantee (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Act, 2007, and it shall come into force on the date appointed by the Central Government.

Section 2 of the Act deals with the extension and amendment of the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005. It states that the principal Act and all rules, orders, and schemes made thereunder by the Central Government are extended to and shall be in force in the State of Jammu and Kashmir. Additionally, with effect from the date of commencement of this Act, in the principal Act, the words "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir" shall be omitted from sub-section (2) of section 1.

Section 3 of the Act deals with the construction of references to a law not in force in Jammu and Kashmir. It states that any reference in any Act mentioned in the principal Act to a law that is not in force in the State of Jammu and Kashmir shall, in relation to that State, be construed as a reference to the corresponding law, if any, in force in that State.

The document also includes a footnote that mentions that this Act is made applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh vide notification No. S.O. 3912(E), dated 30th October, 2019.
