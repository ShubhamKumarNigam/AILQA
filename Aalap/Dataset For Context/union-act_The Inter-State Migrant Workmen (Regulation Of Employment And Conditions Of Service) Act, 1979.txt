The Inter-State Migrant Workmen (Regulation Of Employment And Conditions Of Service) Act, 1979, is a law that regulates the employment and conditions of service of inter-state migrant workmen. The Act applies to every establishment in which five or more inter-state migrant workmen are employed or were employed on any day of the preceding twelve months. It also applies to every contractor who employs or employed five or more inter-state migrant workmen on any day of the preceding twelve months. The Act defines "inter-state migrant workman" as any person who is recruited by or through a contractor in one State under an agreement or other arrangement for employment in an establishment in another State, whether with or without the knowledge of the principal employer in relation to such establishment.

Under this Act, the appropriate Government may appoint registering officers to register certain establishments. Every principal employer of an establishment to which this Act applies shall make an application to the registering officer, in such form and manner and on payment of such fees as may be prescribed, for the registration of the establishment. Within one month after the receipt of an application for registration, the registering officer shall register the establishment and issue a certificate of registration if the application is complete in all respects.

The Act also prohibits the employment of inter-state migrant workmen in any establishment without a certificate of registration. However, this provision does not apply to any establishment in respect of which an application for registration is pending before a registering officer.

The Act also regulates the licensing of contractors who recruit inter-state migrant workmen. Every contractor to whom this Act applies shall obtain a license before recruiting any person in a State for the purpose of employing him in any establishment situated in another State. The license may contain conditions including the terms and conditions of the agreement or other arrangement under which the workmen will be recruited, the remuneration payable, hours of work, fixation of wages and other essential amenities in respect of the inter-state migrant workmen.

The Act also imposes duties on contractors, including the duty to furnish particulars and a pass book to every inter-state migrant workman, the duty to ensure regular payment of wages, equal pay for equal work, suitable conditions of work, suitable residential accommodation, medical facilities, protective clothing, and the duty to report fatal accidents or serious bodily injury to any such workman.

The Act also provides for the responsibility of the principal employer for the payment of wages to inter-state migrant workmen and the recovery of any unpaid wages or allowances from the contractor.

The Act also empowers the appropriate Government to appoint inspectors to inspect establishments and contractors and to seize or take copies of any register, record of wages, or notices or portions thereof as may be considered relevant in respect of an offence under this Act.

The Act also provides for the transfer of proceedings in respect of industrial disputes to corresponding authorities in the State wherein the recruitment of such workman was made, on the application of an inter-State migrant workman.

The Act also requires every principal employer and every contractor to maintain registers and records giving particulars of the inter-state migrant workmen employed, the nature of work performed by such workmen, the rates of wages paid to the workmen and such other particulars in such form as may be prescribed.

The Act also penalizes the obstruction of inspectors or authorized persons in the discharge of their duties, the refusal to afford reasonable facility for making any inspection, examination, inquiry or investigation, the wilful refusal to produce any register or other document kept in pursuance of this Act, and the contravention of provisions regarding employment of inter-state migrant workmen or contravention of any condition of a licence granted under this Act. The Act also provides for the punishment of offences by companies and the cognizance of offences. The Act also provides for the limitation of prosecutions and the effect of laws and agreements inconsistent with the Act. The Act also empowers the appropriate Government to exempt in special cases and provides for the protection of action taken under the Act. The Act also gives power to the Central Government to give directions to the Government of any State as to the carrying into execution in the State of the provisions contained in this Act. The Act also provides for the power to remove difficulties and the power to make rules.
