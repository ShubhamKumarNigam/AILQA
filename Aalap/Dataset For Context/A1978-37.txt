Summary:

The Press Council Act, 1978, establishes the Press Council of India to preserve the freedom of the press and maintain and improve the standards of newspapers and news agencies in India. The Council consists of a Chairman and 28 other members, nominated from various categories such as working journalists, newspaper owners, news agency representatives, and experts in education, science, law, and literature and culture.

The Council has the power to censure newspapers, news agencies, editors, or journalists if they offend against the standards of journalistic ethics or public taste or commit any professional misconduct. The Council can also levy fees on registered newspapers and news agencies and has the power to summon witnesses, enforce attendance, and require the discovery and inspection of documents.

The Council prepares an annual report, giving a summary of its activities during the previous year and an account of the standards of newspapers and news agencies and factors affecting them. The report is forwarded to the Central Government, which causes it to be laid before both Houses of Parliament. The Council may also prepare interim reports at any time during the year, giving a summary of such of its activities as it considers to be of public importance.

Every member of the Council and every officer or other employee appointed by the Council is deemed to be a public servant within the meaning of section 21 of the Indian Penal Code (45 of 1860). No suit or other legal proceeding shall lie against the Council or any member thereof or any person acting under the direction of the Council in respect of anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done under this Act.

The Central Government may make rules to carry out the purposes of this Act, and the Council may make regulations not inconsistent with this Act and the rules made thereunder. The regulations made by the Council shall be made only with the prior approval of the Central Government.

This Act applies to the whole of India, including the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh.
