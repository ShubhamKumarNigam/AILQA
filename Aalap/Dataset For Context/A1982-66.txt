Summary:
The Suppression of Unlawful Acts against Safety of Civil Aviation Act, 1982 is an Act to give effect to the Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts against the Safety of Civil Aviation and for matters connected therewith. It extends to the whole of India and applies to any offence under section 3 committed outside India by any person. The Act defines terms such as "aircraft", "aircraft registered in India", "airport", "Convention country", "military aircraft", and "Montreal Convention". It outlines offences and penalties for committing violence on board an aircraft in flight, destroying or damaging an aircraft in service, placing a device or substance on an aircraft in service, and communicating false information that endangers the safety of an aircraft in flight. The Act also includes provisions for jurisdiction, investigation, and trial of offences. Additionally, the Act includes provisions for extradition, bail, protection of action taken in good faith, and previous sanction necessary for prosecution. The Act was enacted on 6th November, 1982.
