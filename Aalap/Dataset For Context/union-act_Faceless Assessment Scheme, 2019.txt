Summary:

The Faceless Assessment Scheme, 2019 is a set of rules that govern the assessment of income tax in India. It aims to facilitate the conduct of e-assessment proceedings in a centralized manner, and to optimize the use of resources through an automated allocation system. The Scheme applies to the assessment of income tax for certain territorial areas, persons, classes of persons, incomes, or class of incomes, as specified by the Board.

Under the Scheme, the National e-assessment Centre is responsible for making assessments in accordance with the provisions of the Income-tax Act, 1961. The Centre may set up Regional e-assessment Centres, assessment units, verification units, technical units, and review units to perform various functions related to the assessment process. These units are responsible for making assessments, verifying information, providing technical assistance, and reviewing draft assessment orders, among other tasks.

The Scheme provides for the exchange of communications between the National e-assessment Centre and the assessee, or any other person, exclusively by electronic mode. This includes the service of notices, orders, or any other electronic communication, and the filing of responses by the assessee. The time and place of dispatch and receipt of electronic records are determined in accordance with the provisions of the Information Technology Act, 2000.

The Scheme also provides for personal hearings in certain circumstances, which shall be conducted exclusively through video conferencing. The Board is required to establish suitable facilities for video conferencing to ensure that the assessee, or his authorized representative, or any other person is not denied the benefit of the Scheme merely on the consideration that such assessee or his authorized representative, or any other person does not have access to video conferencing at his end.

The Principal Chief Commissioner or the Principal Director General, in charge of the National e-assessment Centre, is responsible for laying down the standards, procedures, and processes for effective functioning of the National e-assessment Centre, Regional e-assessment Centres, and the units set up under the Scheme. This includes the format, mode, procedure, and processes in respect of service of notice, receipt of information or documents, provision of e-proceeding facility, accessing, verification and authentication of information and response, general administration, and grievance redressal mechanism in the respective Centres and units.
