80.

Section 586 (Application of this Part to unregisteredcompanies)


In section 586, for the word "company", wherever itoccurs, substitute the words "limited liability partnership".



 
 
 


 
 
 


The Companies Act, 1956 has provisions that can be applied to Limited Liability Partnerships (LLPs) with certain modifications. These modifications include substituting the term "company" with "limited liability partnership", "articles" with "limited liability partnership agreement", "director" with "designated partner", "promoter" or "member" or "contributory" with "partner", "Court" with "Tribunal" (except in certain sections), and "this Act" with "Limited Liability Partnership Act". There are also specific modifications to certain sections of the Act as outlined in the Table provided. Additionally, there are explanations and clarifications for the application of the provisions of the Companies Act, 1956 to LLPs.
