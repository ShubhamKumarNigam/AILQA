Summary:
The National Commission for Indian System of Medicine (General) Regulations, 2023, are a set of rules made under the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine Act, 2020. These regulations provide for the functioning of the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine, including the appointment of officers and employees, the conduct of meetings, the procedure for decision-making, and the maintenance of records. The regulations also provide for the establishment of Autonomous Boards and the appointment of experts, professionals, officers, and other employees to these Boards. The regulations seek to ensure transparency, accountability, and efficiency in the functioning of the National Commission for Indian System of Medicine and its Autonomous Boards. The regulations also provide for the interpretation of any of these regulations to settle any dispute, whose decision shall be final and binding with respect to consultant or outsource employee or contract employee or any other person directly affected by these regulations.
