Summary:

The Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (Conduct Of Business) Regulations, 1999, is a document that outlines the rules and regulations for the conduct of business by the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (CERC) in India.

The regulations start by providing the short title, commencement, and interpretation of the document. The regulations are called "The Central Electricity Regulatory Commission (Conduct of Business) Regulations, 1999," and they will come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette. These regulations extend to the whole of India except the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

The regulations then provide definitions of various terms used in the document. For example, "Act" refers to the Electricity Regulatory Commissions Act, 1998 (14 of 1998), "Commission" means the Central Commission constituted under the Act, and "petition" means and includes all petitions, applications, complaints, appeals, replies, rejoinders, supplemental pleadings, other papers and documents.

The regulations also specify the Commission's office, office hours, and sittings. The place of the offices of the Commission may be specified by the Commission from time to time. The headquarters and other offices of the Commission are open daily except on Saturdays, Sundays, and Central Government holidays. The Commission may hold sittings for hearing matters at the headquarters or any other place on days and times specified by the Commission.

The regulations also provide that the proceedings of the Commission shall be conducted in English or Hindi, unless otherwise permitted by an order of the Commission. No petition, documents, or other material contained in any language other than English or Hindi if permitted by the Commission may be accepted by the Commission, unless the same is accompanied by a translation thereof in English or Hindi.
