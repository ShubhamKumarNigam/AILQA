Summary:
THE SEXUAL HARASSMENT OF WOMEN AT WORKPLACE (PREVENTION, PROHIBITION AND REDRESSAL) ACT, 2013 is an Act to provide protection against sexual harassment of women at workplace and for the prevention and redressal of complaints of sexual harassment. The Act defines "aggrieved woman" as a woman who alleges to have been subjected to any act of sexual harassment by the respondent. The Act also defines "appropriate Government" as the Central Government or the State Government, depending on the nature of the workplace. The Act requires every employer to constitute an Internal Complaints Committee to receive and address complaints of sexual harassment. The Act also provides for the constitution of Local Complaints Committees by the District Officer to receive complaints of sexual harassment from establishments where the Internal Committee has not been constituted. The Act sets out the procedures for inquiry into complaints of sexual harassment and the actions that can be taken against the respondent if the complaint is found to be true. The Act also provides for penalties for false or malicious complaints and false evidence. The Act prohibits the publication or making known the contents of the complaint and inquiry proceedings. The Act also provides for the duties of the employer, including providing a safe working environment, displaying information about the penal consequences of sexual harassment, organizing workshops and awareness programs, providing necessary facilities to the Internal Committee, and assisting in securing the attendance of respondents and witnesses before the Internal Committee. The Act also provides for the duties and powers of the District Officer, including monitoring the timely submission of reports by the Local Committee and taking measures to engage non-governmental organizations for creating awareness on sexual harassment and the rights of women. The Act also includes provisions for the submission of annual reports by the Internal Committee or the Local Committee, the inclusion of information in the employer's annual report, the monitoring of implementation and maintenance of data by the appropriate Government, and the power of the appropriate Government to make rules. The Act also includes provisions for the cognizance of offences by courts, the penalty for non-compliance with the provisions of the Act, and the power of the appropriate Government to remove difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act.
