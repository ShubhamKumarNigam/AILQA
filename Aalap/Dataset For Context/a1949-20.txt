Summary:

The West Godavari District (Assimilation of Laws on Federal Subjects) Act, 1949, is a law that aims to assimilate certain laws in force in different parts of the West Godavari District of the Province of Madras. The Act comes into force on a date appointed by the Central Government. The Act defines various terms such as "appointed day," "Eluru Taluk," "law," and "Scheduled area."

The Act provides for the assimilation of laws that are in force in the Eluru Taluk but not in the Scheduled area, to extend to the Scheduled area. Similarly, laws that are in force in the Scheduled area but not in the Eluru Taluk, will cease to be in force in the Scheduled area, except for things done or omitted before the appointed day.

The Act also provides for the removal of difficulties that may arise in relation to the transition from the laws mentioned in sub-section (2) of section 3 to the laws mentioned in sub-section (1) thereof. The Central Government may make such provisions as it considers necessary for the removal of such difficulty.

The Schedule to the Act lists the villages that are included in the Scheduled area. These villages are divided into three categories, namely, villages in Polavaram Firka, villages in Zangareddigudem Firka, and villages in Jeelugumilli Firka.

The Act was enacted on the 14th of April, 1949. The appointed day for the coming into force of this Act is the 1st of July, 1949.
