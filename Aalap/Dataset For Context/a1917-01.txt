The Inland Vessels Act, 1917, is an Act of the Indian Parliament that regulates the survey and operation of mechanically propelled vessels on inland waterways in India. The Act was enacted on 7th February 1917, and extends to the whole of India except the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including "inland vessel" or "inland mechanically propelled vessel," which means a mechanically propelled vessel that ordinarily plies on inland water but does not include fishing vessels and ships registered under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958. "Inland water" is defined as any canal, river, lake, or other navigable water within a State, any area of tidal water deemed to be the inland water by the Central Government, or waters declared by the Central Government to be smooth and partially smooth waters under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958. "Mechanically propelled vessel" means every description of vessel propelled wholly or in part by electricity, steam, or other mechanical power.

The Act provides that an inland mechanically propelled vessel shall not proceed on any voyage or be used for any service unless she has a certificate of survey in force in the zone intended for operation and applicable to such voyage or service in such zone. The Act defines "zone" as any inland water area specified by the State Government for the purpose of this Act based on maximum significant wave height criteria.

The Act empowers the State Government to declare places of survey and appoint surveyors for the purposes of the Act. Every surveyor is deemed to be a public servant within the meaning of the Indian Penal Code. The surveyor may inspect the mechanically propelled vessel and every part thereof, including the hull, boilers, engines, and other machinery, and all equipment and articles on board. The owner, master, and officers of the mechanically propelled vessel shall afford to the surveyor all reasonable facilities for a survey and provide all necessary information.

Before a survey is commenced, the owner or master of the mechanically propelled vessel to be surveyed shall pay a fee calculated on the tonnage of the vessel according to the rates mentioned in Schedule I or any other prescribed rates. The surveyor shall give a declaration to the owner or master of the vessel if satisfied that the vessel is fit for the voyage or service intended. The declaration shall contain particulars regarding the hull, boilers, engines, and other machinery, equipments, the time for which the vessel will be sufficient, any limit beyond which the vessel is not fit to ply, the number of passengers that the vessel is fit to carry, the nature and quantum of cargo that the vessel is fit to carry, and any other prescribed particulars.

The owner or master of a mechanically propelled vessel to whom a declaration is given shall send the declaration to the officer appointed by the State Government within fourteen days after the date of the receipt thereof. Failure to send a declaration within the specified time shall result in the forfeiture of a sum not exceeding five rupees for every day during which the sending of the declaration is delayed.

The State Government shall grant a certificate of survey if satisfied that all provisions of the Act have been complied with in respect of the declaration sent under section 8. The certificate shall be in the prescribed form and contain a statement to the effect that all provisions of the Act with respect to the survey of the mechanically propelled vessel and the declaration of survey have been complied with. The certificate shall set forth the particulars concerning the mechanically propelled vessel mentioned in the declaration of survey and any other prescribed particulars. The State Government may delegate all or any of the functions assigned to it under this section to any person.

The Act prescribes penalties for various offenses, including failure to comply with the provisions of the Act, making false declarations, and obstructing a surveyor in the performance of his duties. The Act also provides for the seizure and forfeiture of mechanically propelled vessels that are not in compliance with the provisions of the Act.
