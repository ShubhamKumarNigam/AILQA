Summary:

The Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (MGNREGA) is a social welfare legislation enacted by the Parliament of India to provide at least one hundred days of guaranteed wage employment in every financial year to every household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work in rural areas. The Act aims to enhance the livelihood security of the rural population and promote rural development.

The Act defines various terms used in the legislation, including "adult", "applicant", "Block", "Central Council", "District Programme Coordinator", "household", "implementing agency", "minimum wage", "National Fund", "notification", "preferred work", "prescribed", "Programme Officer", "project", "rural area", "Scheme", "State Council", "unskilled manual work", and "wage rate".

The Act is divided into six chapters. Chapter I, titled "Preliminary", deals with the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. Chapter II, titled "Guarantee of Employment in Rural Areas", provides for the guarantee of employment to rural households and the payment of wages. Chapter III, titled "Employment Guarantee Schemes and Unemployment Allowance", deals with the Schemes made by the State Government for providing employment and the payment of unemployment allowance. Chapter IV, titled "Implementing and Monitoring Authorities", establishes the Central Employment Guarantee Council and the State Employment Guarantee Council to discharge the functions and perform the duties assigned to them. Chapter V, titled "Establishment of National and State Employment Guarantee Funds and Audit", provides for the establishment of the National Employment Guarantee Fund and the State Employment Guarantee Fund to meet the cost of the Schemes. Chapter VI, titled "Miscellaneous", deals with various miscellaneous provisions, including penalty for non-compliance, power to delegate, power of the Central Government to give directions, and protection of action taken in good faith.

The Act also contains two schedules. Schedule I lays down the minimum features of a rural employment guarantee scheme, and Schedule II lays down the conditions for guaranteed rural employment under a scheme and the minimum entitlements of laborers.

The Act has been amended twice, first by the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee (Amendment) Act, 2009, and then by the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee (Amendment) Act, 2013. The amendments have made various changes to the Act, including the change of name from the National Rural Employment Guarantee Act to the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, the extension of the Act to the Union territories of Jammu and Kashmir and Ladakh, and the increase in the wage rate from sixty rupees per day to one hundred rupees per day.

Overall, the MGNREGA is a landmark legislation that aims to provide employment opportunities to the rural population, promote rural development, and enhance the livelihood security of rural households.
