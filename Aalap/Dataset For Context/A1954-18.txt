Summary:

The Lushai Hills District (Change of Name) Act, 1954, is an Act that aims to change the name of the Lushai Hills District to Mizo District. This Act received the President's assent on April 29, 1954, and came into force on September 1, 1954.

The Act consists of four sections, and there are no schedules, annexures, appendices, or forms attached to it.

Section 1 of the Act deals with the short title and commencement of the Act. It states that the Act may be called the Lushai Hills District (Change of Name) Act, 1954, and it shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may appoint by notification in the Official Gazette.

Section 2 of the Act states that the Lushai Hills District shall, as from the commencement of this Act, be known as the Mizo District.

Section 3 of the Act deals with the amendment of the Sixth Schedule to the Constitution. It states that in the Sixth Schedule to the Constitution, in paragraph 20, after sub-paragraph (2), the following sub-paragraph shall be inserted, namely: “(2A) The Mizo District shall comprise the area which at the commencement of this Constitution was known as the Lushai Hills District.” It also states that in sub-paragraph (3), after the words “United Khasi Jaintia Hills District,” the words “and the Mizo District” shall be inserted, and in Part A of the table, for the words “The Lushai Hills District,” the words “The Mizo District” shall be substituted.

Section 4 of the Act deals with the reference to the Lushai Hills District. It states that any reference to the Lushai Hills District in any law, instrument, or other document shall, unless the context otherwise requires, be construed as a reference to the Mizo District.

In conclusion, the Lushai Hills District (Change of Name) Act, 1954, is a simple Act that changes the name of the Lushai Hills District to Mizo District. It also amends the Sixth Schedule to the Constitution to reflect this change.
