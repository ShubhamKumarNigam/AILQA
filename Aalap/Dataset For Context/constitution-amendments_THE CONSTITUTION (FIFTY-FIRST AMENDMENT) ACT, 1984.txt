Amendment of article 334.-

In article 334 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "sixty years", the words "seventy years" shall be substituted.


Explanation.-

In this Act, "appointed day" means the date on which the Constitution (Forty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1978, comes into force.



This document is the Constitution (Fifty-first Amendment) Act, 1984. It was enacted by Parliament in the thirty-fifth year of the Republic of India. The Act has two main provisions. Firstly, it amends article 334 of the Constitution. This article deals with the reservation of seats for Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes in the Lok Sabha and the State Legislative Assemblies. The amendment changes the duration of this reservation from sixty years to seventy years. Secondly, the Act defines the term "appointed day" as the date on which the Constitution (Forty-fourth Amendment) Act, 1978, comes into force. This definition is relevant for the interpretation of other provisions in the Act. The Act will come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
