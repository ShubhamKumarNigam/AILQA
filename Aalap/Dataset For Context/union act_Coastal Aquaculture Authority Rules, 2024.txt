The Coastal Aquaculture Authority Rules, 2024, are a set of regulations made under the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act, 2005. These rules govern the registration and regulation of coastal aquaculture units in India.

The rules define various terms related to coastal aquaculture, such as "farm," "hatchery," "live feed," "nursery," "Specific Pathogen Free," and "Guidelines." The Guidelines mentioned in these rules are issued by the Coastal Aquaculture Authority to ensure that coastal aquaculture does not cause any detriment to the coastal environment and to protect the livelihood of various sections of the people living in the coastal areas.

The rules also specify the terms and conditions of service of the Chairperson and members of the Coastal Aquaculture Authority. The Chairperson is entitled to a salary and allowances similar to a Secretary to the Government of India, while the members are entitled to sitting fees and travel allowances.

The rules specify the functions of the Coastal Aquaculture Authority, which include ensuring that agricultural lands, mangroves, wetlands, forest lands, land for village common purposes and the land meant for public purposes and national parks and sanctuaries are not converted for construction of coastal aquaculture farms. The Authority is also required to deal with any issues pertaining to coastal aquaculture, survey the entire coastal area of the country, advise the Central and State Governments on formulating suitable strategies for achieving eco-friendly coastal aquaculture development, and develop a nationwide aquaculture mapping and zonation.

The rules also provide for the registration and fees for coastal aquaculture units. Every application for registration of a coastal aquaculture unit or activity must be made in Form-I or Form-II, which can be obtained from the office of the Member Convener of Sub-Divisional Level Committee or District Level Committee, or downloaded from the website of the Authority. The application must be accompanied by a fee and the documents specified in Schedule-II.

The rules also provide for the cancellation of registration of a coastal aquaculture unit if the owner has obtained the certificate of registration by furnishing false information or in contravention of any of the provisions of these rules or of the conditions mentioned in the certificate of registration.

In addition to the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Rules, 2024, there are other legal terms and concepts related to Indian laws that are relevant to coastal aquaculture. These include the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act, 2005, the Environment Protection Act, 1986, and various guidelines and regulations issued by the Coastal Aquaculture Authority and other authorities.

In summary, the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Rules, 2024, are an important set of regulations that govern the registration and regulation of coastal aquaculture units in India. These rules aim to ensure that coastal aquaculture is carried out in an eco-friendly manner and does not cause any detriment to the coastal environment or harm the livelihood of coastal communities. The rules define various terms related to coastal aquaculture, specify the functions of the Coastal Aquaculture Authority, and provide for the registration and fees for coastal aquaculture units. Other Indian laws, such as the Coastal Aquaculture Authority Act, 2005, and the Environment Protection Act, 1986, also play a crucial role in regulating coastal aquaculture in India.
