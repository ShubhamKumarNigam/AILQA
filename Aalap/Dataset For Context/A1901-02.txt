Summary:

THE INDIAN TOLLS (ARMY AND AIR FORCE) ACT, 1901 (ACT NO. 2 OF 1901) is a law that amends the law related to the exemption from tolls of persons and property belonging to the Army or Air Force. The Act is divided into three sections and one schedule.

Section 1 of the Act deals with the short title, extent, and commencement of the Act. The Act can be called the Indian Tolls (Army and Air Force) Act, 1901, and it extends to the whole of India. The Act came into force on the first day of April, 1901.

Section 2 of the Act defines various terms used in the Act. The terms defined include authorized followers, carriage, ferry, the regular forces, horse, Irregular Corps, Indian Reserve Forces, landing-place, public authority, and tolls.

Section 3 of the Act deals with exemptions from tolls. The following persons and property are exempted from payment of any tolls:

* All officers, soldiers, and airmen of the Regular Forces or any Irregular Corps
* All members of the Territorial Army or of the National Cadet Corps, when on duty or when proceeding to or returning from duty
* All officers, soldiers, and airmen of the Indian Reserve Forces when proceeding from their place of residence on being called out for service, training, or muster or when proceeding back to their place of residence after such service, training or muster
* All authorized followers of the Regular Forces or any Irregular Corps or the Territorial Army or the National Cadet Corps
* All members of the families of officers, soldiers, airmen, or authorized followers of the Regular Forces or any Irregular Corps when accompanying anybody of troops, or any officer, soldier, airman, or authorized follower thereof on duty or on the march
* All prisoners under military or air force escort
* The carriages, horses, and baggage, and the persons (if any) employed in driving the carriages or in carrying the baggage, of any persons exempted under any of the foregoing clauses, when such carriages, horses, baggage, or persons accompanying the persons so exempted under the circumstances mentioned in those clauses respectively
* All carriages and horses belonging to Government or employed in the Indian military or air-force service and all persons in charge of or accompanying the same, when conveying any such persons as hereinbefore in this section mentioned or when conveying baggage or stores, or when returning, unladen from conveying such persons, baggage or stores
* All carriages and horses, when moving under the orders of military or air-force authority for the purpose of being employed in the Indian military or air-force service
* All animals accompanying any body of troops which are intended to be slaughtered for food or kept for any purpose connected with the provisioning of such troops
* All persons in charge of any carriage, horse, or animal exempted under any of the foregoing clauses when accompanying the same under the circumstances mentioned in those clauses respectively

The persons or property exempted under clauses (d), (e) (g) and (j) shall be deemed to accompany the Forces, troops, persons, or property concerned, when the move of the former is the direct result of, or is connected with the move of the latter, irrespective of the interval of space and time between the two moves.

Section 4 of the Act deals with tolls on vessels transporting troops and baggage, etc., of troops embarked or disembarked. No tolls shall be leviable by any local authority in respect of any vessel employed by the Central Government solely for the transport of troops, or the horses, baggage, or other effects of any troops embarking or disembarking at any port, or carriages belonging to Government or employed in the Indian military or air-force service embarking or disembarking at any port. In respect of all such vessels or troops, their families, their horses, baggage, and their effects, or any such carriages as aforesaid, the local authority concerned shall perform and supply all such reasonable services and accommodation as may, from time to time, be required by the Central Government, and shall receive payment for all such services and accommodation on such terms and for such periods as may, from time to time, be determined by the Central Government in consultation with such local authority.

Section 5 of the Act deals with penalty. Any person who demands and receives any toll in contravention of the provisions of section 3 or section 4 shall be punishable with fine which may extend to fifty rupees.

Section 6 of the Act deals with compensation. If any owner or lessee, or any Company, railway administration, or local authority claims compensation for any loss alleged to have been incurred owing to the operation of this Act, the claim shall be submitted to the Central Government. On receiving any such claim, the Central Government shall pass such order thereon as justice requires, and shall give all necessary directions for the purpose of ascertaining the fact of the case and of assessing the compensation, if any, to be paid.

Section 7 of the Act deals with rules. The Central Government may make rules to carry out the purposes and objects of this Act. The power to make rules under this section is subject to the condition of the rules being made after previous publication. All rules made under this section shall be published in the Official Gazette and, on such publication, shall have effect as if enacted by this Act. Every rule made by the Central Government under this section shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is made, before each House of Parliament, while it is in session, for a total period of thirty days which may be comprised in one session or in two or more successive sessions, and if, before the expiry of the session immediately following the session or the successive sessions aforesaid, both Houses agree in making any modification in the rule or both Houses agree that the rule should not be made, the rule shall thereafter have effect only in such modified form or be of no effect, as the case may be; so, however, that any such modification or annulment shall be without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done under that rule.

The Schedule of the Act has been repealed.

In conclusion, THE INDIAN TOLLS (ARMY AND AIR FORCE) ACT, 1901 (ACT NO. 2 OF 1901) provides exemptions from tolls for persons and property belonging to the Army or Air Force. The Act defines various terms used in the Act and provides penalties for demanding and receiving tolls in contravention of the provisions of the Act. The Act also provides for compensation for any loss alleged to have been incurred due to the operation of the Act. The Act has been extended to various parts of India and has undergone several amendments since its enactment.
