Summary:

THE FOREIGN TRADE (DEVELOPMENT AND REGULATION) ACT, 1992
Act ID: ACT NO. 22 OF 1992
Enactment Date: [7th August, 1992.]

This Act aims to provide for the development and regulation of foreign trade by facilitating imports and increasing exports. It is divided into six chapters, each focusing on different aspects of foreign trade regulation.

Chapter I: Preliminary
This chapter defines key terms used throughout the Act, including "Adjudicating Authority," "Appellate Authority," "conveyance," "Director General," "import," "export," "Importer-exporter Code Number," "licence," "Order," and "prescribed."

Chapter II: Power of Central Government to Make Orders and Announce Foreign Trade Policy
This chapter outlines the power of the Central Government to make orders and announce the foreign trade policy. The Central Government may prohibit, restrict, or regulate the import or export of goods or services or technology. The Central Government may also formulate and announce the foreign trade policy, which may be amended from time to time.

Chapter III: Importer-Exporter Code Number and Licence
This chapter outlines the requirements for obtaining an Importer-exporter Code Number and licence. No person shall make any import or export except under an Importer-exporter Code Number granted by the Director General or the officer authorized by the Director General. The Director General may also suspend or cancel an Importer-exporter Code Number or licence under certain conditions.

Chapter IIIA: Quantitative Restrictions
This chapter outlines the power of the Central Government to impose quantitative restrictions on the import of goods. The Central Government may impose such restrictions if it is satisfied that any goods are imported into India in such increased quantities and under such conditions as to cause or threaten to cause serious injury to domestic industry.

Chapter IV: Search, Seizure, Penalty, and Confiscation
This chapter outlines the power of the Central Government to authorize any person for the purposes of exercising powers with respect to entering premises, searching, inspecting, and seizing goods, documents, things, and conveyances connected with the import and export of goods. The chapter also outlines the penalties and confiscation for contravention of the provisions of this Act, rules, orders, and the foreign trade policy.

Chapter IVA: Controls on Export of Specified Goods, Services and Technology
This chapter outlines the controls on export of specified goods, services, and technology. The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005 shall apply to exports, transfers, re-transfers, brought in transit, trans-shipment of, and brokering in specified goods, technology, or services.

Chapter V: Appeal and Review
This chapter outlines the appeal and review process for decisions or orders made by the Adjudicating Authority. An appeal may be preferred to the Central Government or the Director General, and the Appellate Authority may make such orders as it thinks fit, confirming, modifying, or reversing the decision or order appealed against.

Chapter VI: Miscellaneous
This chapter includes provisions for protection of action taken in good faith, application of other laws not barred, power to make rules, and repeal and savings.

The Act also includes schedules, annexure, appendix, and forms.
