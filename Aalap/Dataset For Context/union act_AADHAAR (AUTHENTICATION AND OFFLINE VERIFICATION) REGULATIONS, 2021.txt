AADHAAR (AUTHENTICATION AND OFFLINE VERIFICATION) REGULATIONS, 2021

Summary:

The Aadhaar (Authentication and Offline Verification) Regulations, 2021, provide the framework for the use of Aadhaar authentication and offline verification services in India. These regulations supersede the Aadhaar (Authentication) Regulations, 2016, and cover various aspects such as types of authentication facilities, modes of authentication, offline verification services, and the roles and responsibilities of requesting entities and authentication service agencies. The regulations also specify the eligibility criteria for appointment as requesting entities and authentication service agencies, as well as the procedures for their appointment.

Key definitions under these regulations include:

* Act: The Aadhaar (Targeted Delivery of Financial and Other Subsidies, Benefits and Services) Act 2016
* Aadhaar number: A 12-digit identification number issued to an individual under the Act
* Aadhaar number holder: An individual who has been issued an Aadhaar number under the Act
* Authentication: The process by which the Aadhaar number along with demographic information or biometric information of an individual is submitted to the Central Identities Data Repository for its verification and such Repository verifies the correctness, or the lack thereof, on the basis of information available with it
* Authentication facility: The facility provided by the Authority for authenticating the Aadhaar number along with demographic information or biometric information of an Aadhaar number holder through the process of authentication, by providing a Yes/No response or e-KYC data, as applicable
* Authentication record: The record of the time of authentication and identity of the requesting entity and the response provided by the Authority thereto
* Authentication Service Agency (ASA): A licensed entity providing necessary infrastructure for ensuring secure network connectivity and related services for enabling a requesting entity to perform authentication using the authentication facility provided by the Authority
* Authentication User Agency (AUA): A requesting entity that uses the Yes/No authentication facility provided by the Authority
* Authority: The Unique Identification Authority of India established under the Act
* Central Identities Data Repository (CIDR): A centralised database in one or more locations containing Aadhaar numbers issued to Aadhaar number holders along with the corresponding demographic information and biometric information of such individuals and other information related thereto
* Child: A person who has not completed eighteen years of age
* e-KYC authentication facility: A type of authentication facility in which the biometric information and/or OTP and Aadhaar number securely submitted with the consent of the Aadhaar number holder through a requesting entity, is matched against the data available in the CIDR, and the Authority returns a digitally signed response containing e-KYC data along with other technical details related to the authentication transaction
* e-KYC data: Full or limited demographic information and/or photograph of an Aadhaar number holder. The e-KYC data may contain full or masked Aadhaar number
* e-KYC User Agency (KUA): A requesting entity which, in addition to being an AUA, uses e-KYC authentication facility provided by the Authority
* License Key: The key generated by a requesting entity as per the process laid down by the Authority
* Offline Verification: The process of verifying the identity of the Aadhaar number holder without authentication, through such offline modes as may be specified by regulations
* Offline Verification Seeking Entity (OVSE): Any entity desirous of undertaking offline verification of an Aadhaar number holder
* Offline Aadhaar Data: The data relating to offline Aadhaar verification, having characteristics as specified by the Authority from time to time including the requirement of masking Aadhaar numbers before storing
* PID Block: The Personal Identity Data element which includes necessary demographic and/or biometric and/or OTP collected from the Aadhaar number holder during authentication
* Registered Devices: Biometric devices that are registered with the Authority
* Requesting entity: An agency or person that submits the Aadhaar number, and demographic information or biometric information, of an individual to the Central Identities Data Repository for authentication
* Sub-AUA: A requesting entity that uses the Yes/No authentication facility provided by the Authority through an existing AUA
* Sub-KUA: A requesting entity that uses e-KYC authentication facility provided by the Authority through an existing KUA
* UID Token: A 72-character alphanumeric string generated by the Authority mapped to the Aadhaar number and specific to a requesting entity
* Virtual Identifier (VID): An interchangeable 16-digit random number mapped with the Aadhaar number of the Aadhaar number holder

Under these regulations, there are two types of authentication facilities provided by the Authority:

1. Yes/No authentication facility: This can be carried out using any of the modes specified in regulation 4(2), including demographic authentication, one-time pin based authentication, biometric-based authentication, and multi-factor authentication.
2. e-KYC authentication facility: This can be carried out only using OTP and/or biometric authentication modes as specified in regulation 4(2).

There are also various modes of authentication, including:

1. Demographic authentication: The Aadhaar number and demographic information of the Aadhaar number holder obtained from the Aadhaar number holder is matched with the demographic information of the Aadhaar number holder in the CIDR.
2. One-time pin based authentication: A One Time Pin (OTP), with limited time validity, is sent to the mobile number and/or e-mail address of the Aadhaar number holder registered with the Authority, or generated by other appropriate means. The Aadhaar number holder shall provide this OTP along with his Aadhaar number during authentication and the same shall be matched with the OTP generated by the Authority.
3. Biometric-based authentication: The Aadhaar number and biometric information submitted by an Aadhaar number holder are matched with the biometric information of the said Aadhaar number holder stored in the CIDR. This may be fingerprints-based or iris-based authentication or other biometric modalities based on biometric information stored in the CIDR.
4. Multi-factor authentication: A combination of two or more of the above modes may be used for authentication.

A requesting entity may choose suitable mode(s) of authentication for a particular service or business function as per its requirement.

The regulations also provide for the use of offline verification services, which may be carried out by the entity as per the specifications given by the Authority from time to time. The Authority shall provide various means to download QR Code, e-Aadhaar or Aadhaar Paperless Offline e-KYC through website, mobile application or other means.

Requesting entities and authentication service agencies are required to meet certain technical and security criteria as specified by the Authority from time to time. Entities seeking to become requesting entities to use the authentication facility provided by the Authority shall apply for appointment as requesting entities in accordance with the procedure as may be specified by the Authority. The Authority may require the applicant to furnish further information or clarifications, and shall verify the information through physical verification of documents, infrastructure, and technological support. After verification, the Authority may approve the application and enter into an agreement with the entity incorporating the terms and conditions for use of the Authority’s authentication facility.

Requesting entities shall establish and maintain necessary authentication related operations, including own systems, processes, infrastructure, technology, security, etc. They shall employ only those devices, equipment, or software, which are duly registered with or approved or certified by the Authority for this purpose. Requesting entities shall also ensure that their operations and systems are audited by an information systems auditor certified by a recognised body on an annual basis.

Requesting entities shall obtain the consent of the Aadhaar number holder for the authentication, and shall maintain logs or records of the consent obtained. They shall also inform the Aadhaar number holder of the nature of information that will be shared by the Authority upon authentication, the uses to which the information received during authentication may be put, and that no service to the resident will be denied for refusing to, or being unable to, undergo authentication.

The regulations also provide for the appointment of authentication service agencies, which shall provide necessary infrastructure for ensuring secure network connectivity and related services for enabling a requesting entity to perform authentication using the authentication facility provided by the Authority. Authentication service agencies shall comply with the technical and security criteria as specified by the Authority, and shall enter into an agreement with the Authority incorporating the terms and conditions for provision of services.

The regulations also provide for the use of virtual identifiers (VIDs), which may be used in lieu of Aadhaar number for online authentication or e-KYC. Aadhaar number holders may generate or retrieve their VIDs through the UIDAI website, SMS, mobile application, eAadhaar download and any other means as provided by Authority from time to time.

These regulations also provide for the protection of information, including the requirement for requesting entities and authentication service agencies to ensure the secure transmission and storage of PID Blocks, and the prohibition on the storage of core biometric information. They also provide for the maintenance of authentication logs by requesting entities and authentication service agencies, which shall be retained for a period of two years.

The regulations also provide for the appointment of requesting entities and authentication service agencies, and specify the eligibility criteria for appointment. Requesting entities and authentication service agencies shall apply for appointment in accordance with the procedure as may be specified by the Authority, and shall furnish the information and clarification required by the Authority. The Authority may verify the information through physical verification of documents, infrastructure, and technological support, and may approve the application and enter into an agreement with the entity incorporating the terms and conditions for use of the Authority’s authentication facility.

The regulations also provide for the revocation of consent by the Aadhaar number holder, and the deletion of e-KYC data by the KUA in a verifiable manner. They also provide for the sharing of e-KYC data with Sub-KUAs, subject to the specific consent of the Aadhaar number holder and the guidelines issued by the Authority.
