The Constitution (One Hundred and Five Amendment) Act, 2021, makes several amendments to the Constitution of India. Firstly, it amends Article 338B by adding a proviso to clause (9), which clarifies that it does not apply for the purposes of clause (3) of Article 342A. Secondly, it amends Article 342A, which deals with the identification of socially and educationally backward classes (SEBCs). The amendment substitutes the phrase "the socially and educationally backward classes which shall for the purposes of this Constitution" with "the socially and educationally backward classes in the Central List which shall for the purposes of the Central Government." It also adds an explanation to define the "Central List" as the list of SEBCs prepared and maintained by and for the Central Government. Finally, it amends Article 366, which defines various terms used in the Constitution, by substituting the definition of "socially and educationally backward classes" with a new one. The new definition refers to the classes so deemed under Article 342A for the purposes of the Central Government or the State or Union territory, as the case may be.

Overall, the amendments made by the Constitution (One Hundred and Five Amendment) Act, 2021, are aimed at clarifying and streamlining the identification and recognition of SEBCs in India, both at the Central and State levels. The amendments also seek to ensure that the identification and recognition of SEBCs are consistent and transparent, and that the process is not open to arbitrary or subjective decision-making. By doing so, the amendments aim to promote social justice and equity, and to ensure that all citizens of India have equal opportunities to access education and employment.
