Summary:

The Auroville Foundation (Framework for Selection of Working Committee) Regulations, 2024, is a legal document made under the authority of the Auroville Foundation Act, 1988. It outlines the process for selecting members to the Working Committee, which is responsible for managing the affairs of the Auroville Foundation.

The regulations establish the Selection Process Committee, which is responsible for reviewing and proposing amendments to the selection process. The Committee is composed of five experts in the field of administration, appointed by the Governing Board after a call for nominations from the Residents Assembly. The Committee is authorized to regulate its own procedures and is provided secretarial assistance by the Auroville Foundation Secretariat.

The regulations also outline the membership criteria for serving on the Working Committee. Candidates must be residents who have been entered in the Register of Residents for a minimum of five consecutive years, comply with all laws, rules, and regulations, be available full-time, and serve for one term.

The Selection Process Committee is responsible for ensuring that the selection process is fair, transparent, non-discriminatory, inclusive, and adheres to the ideals of Auroville. The Committee must also ensure that the proposed selection process fosters a climate in Auroville that strictly adheres to the principles and guidance of Auroville's organization given by the Mother. The Committee must consider the proposed selection process's representation of age, gender, and different nationalities and ensure an inbuilt mechanism of regular training for the members of the Working Committee.

The regulations also outline the process for finalizing the selection process document. The reviewed selection process prepared by the Selection Process Committee is uploaded on the Auroville Foundation website for feedback from the Residents Assembly. The residents are allowed 14 days to submit their feedback, which is considered by the Selection Process Committee and uploaded on the Auroville Foundation website along with its response. The Selection Process Committee then finalizes the selection process method and submits it to the Residents Assembly and the Governing Board for approval. The approved document is uploaded on the Auroville Foundation website and becomes effective immediately.

The regulations are supported by quotes from Sri Aurobindo and the Mother, which emphasize the importance of unity, harmony, and spirituality in the organization and management of Auroville. The quotes highlight the need for a divine anarchy, where individuals organize themselves spontaneously without fixed rules and laws, guided by their psychic being and the Divine. The regulations also emphasize the need for a spiritualized society that can bring about a reign of individual harmony and communal happiness, a new kind of theocracy where the kingdom of God is established upon earth.
