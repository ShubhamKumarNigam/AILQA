Summary:
THE FARIDABAD DEVELOPMENT CORPORATION ACT, 1956 was enacted on 28th December, 1956, to provide for the establishment and regulation of a trading Corporation for the purpose of carrying on and promoting trade and industry in the town of Faridabad, assisting in the rehabilitation of displaced persons settled therein and for matters connected therewith.

Chapter I of the Act deals with preliminary matters, including the short title of the Act and definitions of various terms used throughout the Act.

Chapter II establishes the Faridabad Development Corporation, which is to consist of a Chairman and not less than four and not more than eight other members appointed by the Central Government. The term of office and conditions of service of the Chairman and other members are to be prescribed.

Chapter III outlines the powers and functions of the Corporation, which include the power to acquire and hold property, carry on or promote any trade, business or industry, give financial or other assistance to persons to enable them to carry on trade, business or industry in Faridabad, construct residential or other buildings and sell or let such buildings, advance loans, supply electrical energy, take measures for the rehabilitation of displaced persons settled in Faridabad, and take steps for improving the economic and social conditions of the inhabitants of Faridabad.

Chapter IV contains miscellaneous provisions, including the power of the Central Government to give directions to the Corporation, the submission of returns and reports by the Corporation, the recovery of moneys due to the Corporation, the delegation of powers by the Corporation, the status of members and officers of the Corporation as public servants, the removal of disqualification for membership of Parliament, the bar of legal proceedings, the validation of certain transactions, the power to remove difficulties, and the power to make rules.

The Schedule to the Act defines the area of Faridabad.

The Appendix to the Act contains forms for various purposes.

The Act has been amended several times, including by the Punjab Reorganisation and Delhi High Court (Adaptation of Laws on Union Subjects) Order, 1968, and by Act 4 of 2005.
