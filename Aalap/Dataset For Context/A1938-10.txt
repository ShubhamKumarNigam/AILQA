Summary:

The Cutchi Memons Act, 1938, is a law that governs the succession and inheritance of Cutchi Memons according to the Muhammadan Law. The Act received the assent of the Governor-General on the 8th of April, 1938, and came into force on the 1st of November, 1938.

The Act has two sections and a savings provision. Section 1 is about the short title and commencement of the Act. It states that the Act may be called the Cutchi Memons Act, 1938, and that it shall come into force on the 1st day of November, 1938.

Section 2 is about Cutchi Memons being governed in matters of succession and inheritance by the Muhammadan Law. It clarifies that all Cutchi Memons shall be subject to the Muhammadan Law in matters of succession and inheritance, except as provided in section 3.

Section 3 is a savings provision that states that nothing in this Act shall affect any right or liability acquired or incurred before its commencement or any legal proceeding or remedy in respect of any such right or liability. Any such legal proceeding or remedy may be continued or enforced as if this Act had not been passed.

The Act has been applied to the Sonthal Parganas district and the Chota Nagpur division subject to certain modifications, and it has also been applied to all the partially-excluded areas of the Province of Orissa.
