The Act also includes provisions for the fixation or revision of rates of wages for working journalists, which are made by a Wage Board consisting of representatives of employers and working journalists, as well as independent persons.

The Act also includes provisions for non-journalist newspaper employees, including the fixation or revision of rates of wages for non-journalist newspaper employees, which are made by a Wage Board consisting of representatives of employers and non-journalist newspaper employees, as well as independent persons. The Act also includes provisions for the application of certain acts to newspaper employees, including the Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946, and the Employees’ Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952.

The Act includes provisions for the recovery of money due from an employer to a newspaper employee, the maintenance of registers, records, and muster-rolls, and the appointment of inspectors to enforce the provisions of the Act. The Act also includes provisions for penalties for contravention of the provisions of the Act, indemnity for certain actions taken in good faith, and the power to make rules to carry out the purposes of the Act.
