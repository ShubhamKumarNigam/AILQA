Insertion of new articles 371A and 371B.-

(1)After article 371 of the Constitution, the following articles shall be inserted, namely:

"371A. Special provisions with respect to the State of Sikkim.

(1)Notwithstanding anything in this Constitution,

(a)the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim shall consist of not less than thirty members;

(b)as from the date of commencement of the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973, the total number of members in the Council of Ministers in the State of Sikkim shall not exceed one-sixth of the total number of members in the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim;

(c)the Governor of Sikkim shall have special responsibility with respect to law and order in the State of Sikkim and shall be consulted by the Government of India on such matters relating thereto as are relevant to the affairs of the State of Sikkim;

(d)notwithstanding anything in the foregoing provisions of this Constitution, the Governor of Sikkim shall have the power to appoint the Chief Minister of Sikkim and such other Ministers of the Government of Sikkim as he may from time to time think fit and the Chief Minister so appointed shall, in the first instance, continue in office during the pleasure of the Governor and thereafter during the pleasure of the President;

(e)the High Court of Sikkim shall be deemed to be a High Court for the purposes of article 217 and shall be called the Sikkim High Court;

(f)in making any law relating to any of the matters specified in the State List in regard to the State of Sikkim, the Governor of Sikkim shall have the power to direct that any provision of any such law shall not apply to the State of Sikkim or any part thereof, in so far as such provision relates to any matter specified in the Concurrent List, but no such direction shall be issued except after the consent of the President has been obtained thereto;

(g)no Act of Parliament in respect of any of the matters specified in the State List shall apply to the State of Sikkim unless the Legislative Assembly of the State of Sikkim by a resolution so decides;

(h)the President may, by public notification, declare that this clause shall cease to be operative on such date as may be specified in the notification.

(2)Nothing in this article shall apply to the State of Sikkim for a period of six years from the date of commencement of the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973.

371B. Special provisions with respect to the State of Nagaland.

(1)Notwithstanding anything in this Constitution,-

(a)no Act of Parliament in respect of religious or social practices of the Nagas, Naga customary law and procedure, administration of justice and ownership and transfer of land and its resources shall apply to the State of Nagaland unless the Legislative Assembly of the State of Nagaland by a resolution so decides;

(b)the Governor of Nagaland shall have special responsibility with respect to law and order in the State of Nagaland and shall be consulted by the Government of India on such matters relating thereto as are relevant to the affairs of the State of Nagaland;

(c)no decision affecting the interests of the Nagas shall be made by the Government of India without consulting the Government of Nagaland.

(2)Nothing in this article shall apply to the State of Nagaland for a period of ten years from the date of commencement of the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973.


Amendment of article 371.-

(1)Clause (1) of article 371 of the Constitution shall be re-numbered as clause (1A) thereof and before clause (1A) as so re-numbered, the following clause shall be inserted, namely:

"(1)Notwithstanding anything in this Constitution, the President may, by order, provide for the constitution of an Administrative Tribunal for the State of Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura and Mizoram with jurisdiction to deal with certain disputes and grievances relating to public services in the State of Assam, Meghalaya, Manipur, Tripura and Mizoram, as may be specified in the order and the provisions of clause (2) and clause (3) shall apply in relation to that Administrative Tribunal as they apply in relation to the Administrative Tribunal referred to in clause (1) of article 323A."

(2)In clause (2) of article 371 of the Constitution, in the opening portion, for the words "the Administrative Tribunal", the words "each of the Administrative Tribunals" shall be substituted.

(3)In clause (3) of article 371 of the Constitution, for the words "the Administrative Tribunal", the words "each of the Administrative Tribunals" shall be substituted.


Amendment of article 372.-

In article 372 of the Constitution, in clause (1),-

(a)the words "and the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973" shall be inserted at the end;

(b)for the words "the commencement of the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971", the words "the commencement of the Constitution (Twenty-fifth Amendment) Act, 1971, or, as the case may be, the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973" shall be substituted.


Repeal of clause (1) of article 371.-

Clause (1) of article 371 of the Constitution shall be repealed.



Summary:

The Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973, aims to provide constitutional authority for implementing the Six-Point Formula, specifically in relation to equitable opportunities for people from different areas in educational institutions and public employment, and the establishment of an Administrative Tribunal to handle certain disputes and grievances. The Act also empowers Parliament to establish a Central University in the State and includes provisions for validating past appointments. Additionally, the Act repeals clause (1) of article 371, which pertains to the Regional Committee, due to the discontinuance of the committee under the Six-Point Formula. The Act also inserts new articles 371A and 371B, which provide special provisions for the States of Sikkim and Nagaland, respectively. These provisions include the power of the Governor to appoint the Chief Minister and other Ministers, the establishment of a High Court for Sikkim, and the protection of Naga religious and social practices, customary law, and land ownership. These provisions are not applicable to the States of Sikkim and Nagaland for a specified period after the commencement of the Act. The Act also amends article 371 and 372 to include the Constitution (Thirty-second Amendment) Act, 1973 and make necessary changes in the provisions related to Administrative Tribunal.
