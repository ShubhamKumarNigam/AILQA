Summary:
The Institute of Teaching and Research in Ayurveda Act, 2020 establishes an Institute of Teaching and Research in Ayurveda and declares it as an Institution of national importance for the promotion of quality and excellence in education, research and training in Ayurveda and allied disciplines. The Act consists of 31 sections divided into 4 chapters. Chapter I, Preliminary, includes sections 1-3, which deal with the short title and commencement, declaration of the institute as an institution of national importance, and definitions. Chapter II, The Institute, includes sections 4-13, which deal with the establishment and incorporation of Anteceding Institutions as Institute of Teaching and Research in Ayurveda, effect of incorporation, composition of the Institute, terms of office of and vacancies among members, President of the Institute, meetings of the Institute, Governing Body and other committees of the Institute, staff of the Institute, objects of the Institute, and functions of the Institute. Chapter III, Financial Provisions, includes sections 14-17, which deal with payment to the Institute, Fund of the Institute, budget of the Institute, accounts and audit, and annual report. Chapter IV, Miscellaneous, includes sections 18-31, which deal with pension and provident funds, authentication of orders and instruments of the Institute, acts and proceedings not to be invalidated by vacancies, etc., grant of degrees, diplomas, etc., by the Institute, recognition of medical qualifications granted by the Institute, control by the Central Government, resolution of differences, returns and information, power to make rules, power to make regulations, rules and regulations to be laid before Parliament, power to remove difficulties, and transitional provisions. The Schedule of the Act deals with the transitional provisions, specifically the continuation of the Board of Governors or any other governing system of the Anteceding Institutions and the committees constituted in relation to the Anteceding Institutions before the commencement of this Act.
