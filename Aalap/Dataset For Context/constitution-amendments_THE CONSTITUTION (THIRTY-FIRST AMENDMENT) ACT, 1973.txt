
Amendment of article 81.-

In article 81 of the Constitution,---

 (a) in clause (1), in sub-clause (a), for the words "five hundred", the words "five hundred and twenty-five" shall be substituted;

(b) in clause (2), for the words "the population", the words "the population as ascertained at the last preceding census of which the relevant figures have been published" shall be substituted;

(c) after clause (2), the following clause shall be inserted, namely:---

"(3) Provided that the provisions of sub-clause (a) of clause (2) shall not be applicable to any State so long as the population of the State does not exceed six millions.";

Amendment of article 330.-

In article 330 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "Nagaland", the words "Nagaland and Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh and Mizoram" shall be substituted.

Amendment of article 332.-

In article 332 of the Constitution, in clause (1), for the words "Nagaland", the words "Nagaland and Meghalaya" shall be substituted.



Explanation.-

In this Act, the expression "the relevant figures" means the figures ascertained by the Director of Census Operations in the State concerned in the last preceding census of which the relevant figures have been published.

This summary explains the content of the Constitution (Thirty-first Amendment) Act, 1973. The Act amends article 81 of the Constitution to increase the upper limit for representation of the States in the House of the People from 500 to 525 and decrease the limit for the Union territories from 25 to 20. Additionally, the Act amends article 81 to provide that the provisions of sub-clause (a) of clause (2) shall not be applicable to any State so long as the population of the State does not exceed six millions. The Act also amends article 330 to include Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, and Mizoram in the list of predominantly tribal units where the provisions of this article do not apply. Similarly, the Act amends article 332 to include Meghalaya in the list of predominantly tribal units where there is no reservation of seats for Scheduled Tribes in the Legislative Assembly. The Act provides an explanation of the term "the relevant figures" as the figures ascertained by the Director of Census Operations in the State concerned in the last preceding census of which the relevant figures have been published.
