The Goa, Daman and Diu Mining Concessions (Abolition and Declaration as Mining Leases) Act, 1987 (Act No. 16 of 1987) is an Act that aims to abolish mining concessions in operation in the Union territory of Goa, Daman and Diu and declare them as mining leases under the Mines and Minerals (Regulation and Development) Act, 1957. The Act was enacted on 23rd May, 1987, and applies to mining concessions granted under the former Portuguese Mining Laws in perpetuity.

The Act has three chapters. The first chapter, titled "Preliminary," defines key terms used in the Act. The second chapter, titled "Abolition of Mining Concessions and Declaration as Mining Leases under the Mines and Minerals Act," specifies the mining concessions that will be deemed to be mining leases under the Mines and Minerals Act, 1957, and sets out the conditions for such leases. The third chapter, titled "Payment of Amounts," specifies the amounts payable to concession holders for the abolition of mining concessions and declaring them as mining leases.

Under the Act, every mining concession specified in the First Schedule is deemed to have been abolished on and from the appointed day and is deemed to be a mining lease granted under the Mines and Minerals Act. Similarly, every mining concession specified in the Second Schedule is deemed to have been abolished and is deemed to be a mining lease granted under the Mines and Minerals Act, with effect from the day next after the date of grant of the said concession. The Act also empowers the Central Government to correct any error, omission, or misdescription in relation to the particulars of any mining concession or the name and residence of any concession holder specified in the First or the Second Schedule.

The concession holder is deemed to have become the holder of the mining lease under the Mines and Minerals Act in relation to the mine to which the mining concession relates, subject to the condition that the period of such lease shall extend up to a period of six months from the date of assent. On the expiry of the period of any mining lease, it may be renewed on such terms and conditions, and up to the maximum period for which, such lease can be renewed under the provisions of that Act and the rules made thereunder.

Every concession holder specified in the second column of the First and the Second Schedules shall be given by the Central Government, in cash, and in the manner specified in Chapter IV, for the abolition of the mining concessions held by him, and declaring it as a mining lease, under section 4, an amount equal to the amount specified against him in the corresponding entry in the eighth column of the First Schedule and the ninth column of the Second Schedule, as the case may be. The Central Government shall also pay simple interest at the rate of four per cent. per annum on the amount specified against such concession holder in the corresponding entry in the eighth column of the First Schedule and the ninth column of the Second Schedule, as the case may be, for the period commencing on the date of assent and ending on the date of payment of such amount to the Commissioner.

The Central Government shall appoint a Commissioner of Payments for the purpose of disbursing the amounts payable to the concession holders under this Act. The Commissioner may authorise one or more of the persons appointed to assist him to exercise all or any of the powers exercisable by him under this Act. Any person authorised by the Commissioner to exercise any of the powers exercisable by the Commissioner may exercise those powers in the same manner and with the same effect as if they have been conferred directly by this Act.

Every concession holder, having a claim for the payment of the amount referred to in section 6, shall prefer such claim before the Commissioner within six months from the specified date. The Commissioner shall maintain records in respect of each of the concession holders in relation to which payments have been made to him under this Act.
