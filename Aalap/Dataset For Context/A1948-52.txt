Summary:

The Bombay Public Security Measures (Delhi Amendment) Act, 1948, is an Act to amend the Bombay Public Security Measures Act, 1947, as extended to the Province of Delhi. The Act comprises three sections.

The first section of the Act, titled 'Short title and extent,' specifies the name of the Act as the Bombay Public Security Measure (Delhi Amendment) Act, 1948, and states that it extends to the Province of Delhi.

The second section of the Act, titled 'Amendment of section 13 of Bombay Act 6 of 1947 as extended to Delhi,' inserts a new sub-section (2A) to section 13 of the Bombay Public Security Measures Act, 1947, as extended to the Province of Delhi. This new sub-section enables a Special Judge trying an offence under this Act to tender a pardon to any person supposed to have been directly or indirectly concerned in, or privy to, the offence, on condition of his making a full and true disclosure of the whole circumstances within his knowledge relative to the offence and to every other person concerned, whether as principal or abettor, in the commission thereof. The pardon so tendered is deemed to have been tendered under section 338 of the Code.

The third section of the Act, titled 'Repeal of Ordinance 14 of 1948,' repeals the Bombay Public Security Measures Act (Delhi Amendment) Ordinance, 1948 (14 of 1948).

This Act aims to provide more comprehensive and effective measures to maintain public security in the Province of Delhi by amending the Bombay Public Security Measures Act, 1947, and repealing the Bombay Public Security Measures Act (Delhi Amendment) Ordinance, 1948.
