Summary:
The Maritime Zones of India (Regulation of Fishing by Foreign Vessels) Act, 1981, regulates fishing by foreign vessels in certain maritime zones of India. The Act defines key terms such as "exclusive economic zone of India," "fish," "fishing," "foreign vessel," "Indian vessel," "licence," "maritime zones of India," "master," "owner," "permit," "processing," "specified ports," "territorial waters of India," and "vessel." The Act prohibits foreign vessels from fishing in the maritime zones of India without a license or permit and sets out the conditions for granting licenses and permits. The Act also provides for the cancellation or suspension of licenses or permits and sets out the powers of search and seizure for authorized officers. The Act defines offences and penalties for contravention of the Act, and includes provisions for the confiscation of vessels, equipment, and fish. The Act also includes provisions for offences committed by companies and the cognizance and trial of offences. The Act includes provisions for the protection of action taken in good faith and sets out the power to make rules.
