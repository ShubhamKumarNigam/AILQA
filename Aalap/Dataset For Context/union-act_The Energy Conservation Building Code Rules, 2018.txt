

(i)The Energy Conservation Building Code Rules, 2018, also known as the ECBC Rules, are a set of regulations that govern the energy efficiency of commercial buildings in India. These rules apply to buildings with a connected load of 100 kilowatts (kW) or more, or a contract demand of 120 kilovolt-amperes (kVA) or more. The ECBC Rules cover various components of a building, including the building envelope, comfort systems and controls, lighting and controls, and electrical and renewable energy systems. The rules are designed to ensure that buildings are designed and constructed in a way that optimizes energy efficiency and reduces energy consumption.

The ECBC Rules provide two methods for ensuring compliance with energy performance requirements: the Prescriptive Method and the Whole Building Performance Method. The Prescriptive Method requires that buildings comply with mandatory and prescriptive requirements for envelope components, comfort systems and controls, lighting and controls, and electrical and renewable energy systems. The Whole Building Performance Method, on the other hand, requires that buildings comply with all mandatory measures and the requirements specified in the whole building performance method of the Code, and that the energy performance index of the proposed design under this method is the same or less than the energy performance index of the standard baseline design of the building.

To ensure compliance with the ECBC Rules, building owners must submit construction documents to the authority having jurisdiction, along with compliance documents certified by Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building). The Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building) are responsible for reviewing the construction documents and certifying that they meet the requirements of the ECBC Rules. The authority having jurisdiction may require submission of documents in electronic form or hard copy of the documents.

The ECBC Rules also establish a compliance mechanism that requires building owners to ensure that the energy performance of the building is monitored and verified by the State Energy Conservation Building Code Implementation Committee for two consecutive years after the building has been fully operational. If the energy performance index ratio of the building is more than one, the authority having jurisdiction may issue a provisional occupancy certificate subject to the condition that the owner shall undertake an energy audit of the building to identify additional energy conservation measures to achieve the energy performance index ratio of the building approved in the sanctioned plan or permit within a period of three years. If the owner fails to achieve the energy performance index ratio as specified in clause (iii) within a period of three years from the date of occupancy of the building, the authority having jurisdiction shall place the matter before the State Energy Conservation Building Code Technical Grievances Redressal Committee, which shall hear the owner and the Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building) and make recommendations in the matter accordingly.

The ECBC Rules also establish committees to promote energy efficiency standards and procedures for erection of Code compliant buildings. The State Energy Conservation Building Code Implementation Committee is responsible for promoting energy efficiency standards, creating awareness about the ECBC Rules, and ensuring compliance with the Rules. The National Energy Conservation Building Code Implementation Committee is responsible for evaluating the recommendations of the State Energy Conservation Building Code Implementation Committee and finalizing recommendations regarding formulation of national energy consumption norms and standards climate zone wise, classification-wise of Code compliant buildings.

The ECBC Rules also establish responsibilities and duties for building owners, Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building), and State designated agencies. Building owners are responsible for engaging Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building) in development of building design, installation of energy conservation measures and equipment, and ensuring that the building complies with the ECBC Rules. Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building) are responsible for verifying and certifying the design of the building, energy conservation measures, construction documents, and compliance forms. State designated agencies are responsible for coordinating, regulating, and enforcing provisions of the ECBC Rules in the State, ensuring that commercial buildings or establishments are constructed in compliance with the Rules, and monitoring the performance of Empanelled Energy Auditors (Building) to improve the quality, consistency, and rate of compliance with the Rules.
