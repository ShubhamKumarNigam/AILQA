Summary:
The Destructive Insects and Pests (Amendment and Validation) Act, 1992, is an amendment to the Destructive Insects and Pests Act of 1914. The Act became effective on March 31, 1992, and validates the notification No.S.O.867(E), dated October 27, 1989, and any fees levied or collected for making an application for a permit to import, or for making inspection, fumigation, disinfection, disinfestation, or supervision of, any article or class of articles or any insect or class of insects under the principal Act or the said notification.

Section 3 of the Act validates the notification and fees and deems them to have always been validly issued or levied in accordance with the provisions of the principal Act. The section also states that no suit or other proceeding shall be maintained or continued for the refund of any fees so collected, and no court or other authority shall enforce any decree or order directing the refund of any fees so collected. It also allows for the recovery of any fees levied but not collected and deems anything done or any action taken under the principal Act to have been validly done in accordance with the law.

Section 4 of the Act repeals the Destructive Insects and Pests (Amendment and Validation) Ordinance, 1992, but states that anything done or any action taken under the principal Act, as amended by the said Ordinance, shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the principal Act, as amended by this Act.

In summary, the Destructive Insects and Pests (Amendment and Validation) Act, 1992, validates the notification and fees related to the Destructive Insects and Pests Act of 1914 and deems them to have always been validly issued or levied. The Act also repeals the Destructive Insects and Pests (Amendment and Validation) Ordinance, 1992, but states that anything done or any action taken under the principal Act, as amended by the said Ordinance, shall be deemed to have been done or taken under the principal Act, as amended by this Act.
