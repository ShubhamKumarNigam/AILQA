* Suspension of Sentences: Where a sentence is suspended, the offender shall be released from custody. The period during which the sentence is under suspension shall be reckoned as part of the term of such sentence. The authority or officer specified in section 130 may order that the offender be committed to undergo the unexpired portion of the sentence or that the sentence be remitted.
* Reconsideration of Case after Suspension: The case may be reconsidered by the authority or officer specified in section 130, or by any officer not below the rank of a Deputy Inspector-General duly authorized by the authority or officer specified in section 130. If the conduct of the offender since his conviction has been such as to justify a remission of the sentence, the matter shall be referred to the authority or officer specified in section 130.
* Fresh Sentence after Suspension: If an offender is sentenced for any other offence while a sentence on him is suspended, the sentences shall run concurrently if both sentences are suspended. If the further sentence is for a period of three months or more and is not suspended, the offender shall be committed to prison or force custody for the unexpired portion of the previous sentence, but both sentences shall run concurrently. If the further sentence is for a period of less than three months and is not suspended, the offender shall be committed on that sentence only, and the previous sentence shall continue to be suspended.
* Powers of Suspension: The powers conferred by sections 130 and 134 shall be in addition to, and not in derogation of, the power of mitigation, remission and commutation.

If such other sentence is remitted under section 134, the punishment of dismissal shall also be remitted.

* Protection for Acts of Members of the Force: Members of the Force are protected from liability in respect of acts done in pursuance of a warrant or order of a competent authority. Any legal proceeding against a member of the Force must be commenced within three months after the act complained of was committed and notice in writing of such proceeding and of the cause thereof must be given to the defendant or his superior officer at least one month before the commencement of such proceeding.
