Summary:
The Rajiv Gandhi University Act, 2006 establishes and incorporates a teaching and affiliating university in the State of Arunachal Pradesh. The Act defines the University and its various components, including the Chancellor, Vice-Chancellor, Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Executive Council, Academic Council, College Development Council, Board of Studies, Finance Committee, and other authorities. The Act also lays down the powers and functions of these authorities and officers, as well as the procedures for their appointment and removal. Additionally, the Act outlines the process for making Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations, and provides for the establishment and maintenance of colleges, institutions, and halls. The Act also includes provisions for the admission of colleges and institutions to the privileges of the University, the withdrawal of such privileges, and the maintenance of discipline among students. The Act further provides for the appointment of teachers, academic staff, and other employees, and sets out their terms and conditions of service. The Act also includes provisions for the conferment of honorary degrees, the withdrawal of degrees and academic distinctions, and the maintenance of accounts and the submission of annual reports.
