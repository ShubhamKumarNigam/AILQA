Summary:

The Uttaranchal (Alteration Of Name) Act, 2006 is an Act passed by the Indian Parliament that changes the name of the State of Uttaranchal to Uttarakhand. The Act defines certain terms, including "appointed day," "appropriate Government," and "law." The appointed day is the date appointed by the Central Government for the Act to come into force. The appropriate Government refers to the Central Government for laws related to matters enumerated in List I of the Seventh Schedule to the Constitution and the State Government for any other law. Law includes any enactment, ordinance, regulation, order, bye-law, rule, scheme, notification, or other instrument having the force of law in the whole or any part of the State of Uttaranchal.

The Act provides that as of the appointed day, the State of Uttaranchal shall be known as the State of Uttarakhand. The Act also amends the First Schedule to the Constitution, under the heading "I. THE STATES," in entry 27, under the column "Name," by substituting the word "Uttaranchal" with "Uttarakhand." Furthermore, the Act amends the Fourth Schedule to the Constitution, under the heading "TABLE," in entry 18, in the second column, by substituting the word "Uttaranchal" with "Uttarakhand."

The Act grants the appropriate Government the power to adapt any law made before the appointed day, whether by way of repeal or amendment, as may be necessary or expedient. The appropriate Government has one year from the appointed day to make such adaptations and modifications. However, nothing in the section prevents a competent Legislature or other competent authority from repealing or amending any law adapted or modified by the appropriate Government under the said sub-section.

The Act also provides that any court, tribunal, or authority required or empowered to enforce a law may construe the law in such a manner, without affecting the substance, as may be necessary or proper in regard to the matter before the court, tribunal, or authority. Additionally, where immediately before the appointed day any legal proceedings are pending to which the State of Uttaranchal is a party, the State of Uttarakhand shall be deemed to be substituted for the State of Uttaranchal in those proceedings.
