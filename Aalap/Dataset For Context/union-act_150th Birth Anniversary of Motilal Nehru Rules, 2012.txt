Summary:

The 150th Birth Anniversary of Motilal Nehru Rules, 2012, published by the Ministry of Finance, Department of Economic Affairs, on June 20, 2012, outline the specifications for the minting of commemorative coins in denominations of One Hundred Fifty Rupees and Five Rupees. These rules are made under the authority of the Coinage Act, 2011.

The One Hundred Fifty Rupees coin will be circular, with a diameter of 44 millimeters, and will have 200 serrations. It will be composed of a Quaternary Alloy made up of Silver (50%), Copper (40%), Nickel (5%), and Zinc (5%). The obverse side of the coin will feature an image of Motilal Nehru, his year of birth and death, the legend "Motilal Nehru" in Devnagari, the name of the country, the year of issue, and an image of the Nehru Planetarium in New Delhi. The reverse side of the coin will display the legend "150th Birth Anniversary of Motilal Nehru," the legend "Nehru Planetarium" in Devnagari, and an image of the Nehru Planetarium in New Delhi.

The Five Rupees coin will be circular, with a diameter of 23 millimeters, and will have 10 serrations. It will be composed of Nickel Brass, made up of Copper (75%), Zinc (20%), and Nickel (5%). The obverse side of the coin will feature an image of Motilal Nehru, his year of birth and death, the legend "Motilal Nehru" in Devnagari, the name of the country, and the year of issue. The reverse side of the coin will display the legend "150th Birth Anniversary of Motilal Nehru," the legend "Nehru Memorial Museum & Library" in Devnagari, and an image of the Nehru Memorial Museum & Library in New Delhi.

These coins will be legal tender in payment of any amount according to their face value. The term "Quaternary Alloy" refers to an alloy made up of four metals.
