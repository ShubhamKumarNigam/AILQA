Summary:

THE INTEGRATED GOODS AND SERVICES TAX (EXTENSION TO JAMMU AND KASHMIR) ACT, 2017, with the Act ID of ACT NO. 27 OF 2017, was enacted on 23rd August, 2017. The Act aims to extend the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 to the State of Jammu and Kashmir.

Section 1 of this Act states the short title and commencement of the Act. The Act is named "The Integrated Goods and Services Tax (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Act, 2017," and it is deemed to have come into force on the 8th day of July, 2017.

Section 2 of the Act provides for the extension and amendment of the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017. This section extends the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017, and all rules, notifications, schemes, and orders made under it by the Central Government to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. Additionally, with the commencement of this Act, the words "except the State of Jammu and Kashmir" are omitted from section 1, sub-section (2) of the principal Act.

Section 3 of the Act deals with the repeal and saving. This section repeals the Integrated Goods and Services Tax (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Ordinance (Ord. 4 of 2017). However, anything done or any action taken under the said Ordinance is deemed to have been done or taken under the corresponding provisions of this Act.

According to the footnote on page 2, this Act is made applicable to the Union territory of Jammu and Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh vide notification No. S.O. 3912(E), dated 30th October, 2019.

Overall, this Act extends the Integrated Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 to the State of Jammu and Kashmir, making it applicable to the region and its residents.
