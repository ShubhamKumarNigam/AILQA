Summary:
The Khuda Bakhsh Oriental Public Library Act, 1969 declares the Khuda Bakhsh Oriental Public Library at Patna, Bihar, as an institution of national importance. The Act establishes a Board to administer the library and provides for its composition, powers, and duties. The Board consists of the Governor of Bihar, the Accountant General, Bihar, a member of the family of the late Maulvi Khuda Bakhsh Khan Bahadur of Muradpur, eight persons nominated by the Central and State Governments with knowledge and experience in library administration, and the Director of the library as the Member-Secretary. The Act defines the powers and duties of the Board, including the management and development of the library, the acquisition of manuscripts, books, and other materials, and the appointment of staff. The Act also provides for the financing of the library through grants from the Central and State Governments, fees and charges, and other sources, and establishes a Fund for the management of the library's finances. The Act includes provisions for the audit and reporting of the library's accounts, the submission of returns and reports to the Central Government, and the delegation of powers and duties by the Board. The Act also includes provisions for the protection of action taken under the Act and the status of the library's officers and employees as public servants. The Act includes provisions for the making of rules and regulations by the Central Government and the Board, respectively, to give effect to the provisions of the Act. The Act also includes a Schedule that sets out the procedure for the appointment of the Chairman and Members of the Board.
