Summary:
The Constitution (Sixty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1991, was enacted to amend the Constitution of India in the Forty-second Year of the Republic of India. The Act seeks to amend Clause (4) of Article 356 of the Constitution to extend the Proclamation up to a total period of five years in relation to the State of Punjab due to continuous terrorist violence. This amendment aims to facilitate fair, free, and peaceful elections to the Legislative Assembly of Punjab. The Act may be called the Constitution (Sixty-eighth Amendment) Act, 1991.
