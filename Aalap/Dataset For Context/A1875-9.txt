Summary:

The Majority Act, 1875, is an Act that amends the law regarding the age of majority. The Act is applicable to India except for the State of Jammu and Kashmir. It specifies that every person domiciled in India will attain the age of majority upon completing the age of eighteen years, and this age is calculated from the day of birth. The Act also includes illustrations to clarify the calculation of majority for individuals born on different dates. The Act does not affect the capacity of persons to act in matters of marriage, dower, divorce, and adoption or the religion or religious rites and usages of any class of citizens of India. The Act also does not affect the capacity of any person who has already attained majority under the law applicable to them before this Act comes into force. The Act has been extended to Dadra and Nagar Haveli, Lakshadweep, and Pondicherry with certain modifications.
