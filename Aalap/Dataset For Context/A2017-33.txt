The Indian Institutes of Management Act, 2017 (Act No. 33 of 2017) was enacted on 31st December, 2017. The Act declares certain Institutes of management to be institutions of national importance, with the aim of empowering these institutions to attain global excellence in management, management research, and allied areas.

The Act includes a preliminary chapter that defines certain terms used throughout the document. The subsequent chapters outline the authorities and functions of the Institutes, the powers and duties of the Board of Governors, the role of the members of the Society, and the establishment and functions of the Coordination Forum.

Chapter I, titled "Preliminary," includes Section 3, which defines various terms used in the Act. These definitions include "Academic Council," "Board," "Chairperson," "Coordination Forum," "corresponding Institute," "Director," "existing Institute," "Institute," "notification," "Ordinances," "prescribed," "regulations," "Schedule," and "society."

Chapter II, titled "The Institutes," includes Sections 4-9. Section 4 states that every existing Institute shall become a body corporate by the same name as mentioned in column (5) of the Schedule, and shall have perpetual succession and a common seal. Section 5 states that all properties, rights, debts, and liabilities of every existing Institute shall vest in the corresponding Institute.

Section 6 outlines the objects of the Institutes, which include educating and supporting leaders in various sectors, conducting research, and supporting and developing programs promoting social and gender equity. Section 7 outlines the powers and functions of the Institutes, which include administering and managing the Institutes, providing courses of study, conducting examinations, granting degrees, and acquiring and holding property.

Section 8 states that the Institutes shall be open to all persons, regardless of sex, race, creed, caste, or class, and shall not impose any conditions or obligations opposed to the spirit and object of the section. Section 9 states that the Institutes shall be not-for-profit legal entities, and shall strive to raise funds for self-sufficiency and sustainability.

Chapter III, titled "The Authorities of Institutes," includes Sections 10-14. Section 10 states that the Board of Governors shall be the principal executive body of each Institute, and outlines the composition of the Board. Section 11 outlines the powers and functions of the Board, which include taking decisions on questions of policy, examining and approving the annual budget estimates, and establishing departments and faculties.

Section 12 states that the term of office of the Chairperson or any other member of the Board shall be four years, and that the Board may nominate a person to fill a temporary vacancy for a period of up to three months. Section 13 states that the Chairperson may resign from his or her office by notice in writing to the Board.

Section 14 states that the Academic Council shall be the principal academic body of each Institute, and outlines the composition of the Council. Section 15 outlines the powers and functions of the Academic Council, which include specifying the academic content of programs and courses of study, and specifying the academic calendar and guidelines for conducting examinations.

Chapter IV, titled "Accounts and Audit," includes Sections 21-28. Section 21 states that the Central Government may provide grants to the Institutes for the purpose of enabling them to discharge their functions efficiently. Section 22 states that each Institute shall maintain a Fund, to which shall be credited all moneys received by the Institute.

Section 23 states that the accounts of each Institute shall be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India, and that the Comptroller and Auditor-General or any person appointed by him shall have the same rights and privileges as the Comptroller and Auditor-General has in connection with the audit of government accounts.

Section 24 states that each Institute shall keep proper updated books of account, and that the accounts shall be maintained in accordance with the accounting standards specified by the Central Government. Section 25 states that each Institute shall appoint an auditor, including an internal auditor, to scrutinize the balance sheet and statement of income and expenditure of the Institute.

Section 26 states that the Director of each Institute shall prepare an annual report, which shall include information on the state of affairs of the Institute, the productivity of research projects, and the performance indicators and internal standards set by the Institute.

Chapter V, titled "Coordination Forum," includes Sections 29-30. Section 29 states that a Coordination Forum shall be established for all the Institutes, and outlines the composition of the Forum. Section 30 states that the Coordination Forum shall facilitate the sharing of experiences, ideas, and concerns among the Institutes, and shall perform certain functions, including recommending the institution of scholarships and deliberating on matters of common interest.

Chapter VI, titled "Miscellaneous," includes Sections 31-39. Section 31 states that the validity of acts of the Institutes or the Board or the Academic Council or any other body set up under the Act shall not be affected by any vacancy or defect in the constitution of such body. Section 32 states that each Institute shall furnish to the Central Government such returns or other information as the Central Government may require.

Section 33 states that the Institutes shall be public authorities under the Right to Information Act, 2005. Section 34 states that the Central Government may make rules for carrying out the provisions of the Act, and Section 35 states that the Board may make regulations not inconsistent with the Act and the rules made thereunder.

Section 36 states that Ordinances shall be made by the Academic Council, and outlines the matters that may be provided for by the Ordinances. Section 37 states that every rule made by the Central Government and the first regulation made by the Board shall be laid before each House of Parliament.

Section 38 states that the Central Government may make orders to remove difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of the Act, and Section 39 states that certain transitional provisions shall apply to the existing Institutes.

The Schedule to the Act lists the names of the existing Institutes and the corresponding Institutes that shall be established under the Act. The Appendix to the Act includes various forms that may be used for various purposes under the Act.
