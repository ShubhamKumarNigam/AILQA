Summary:

The Dr. Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University Act, 2016 (Act No. 32 of 2016) establishes and incorporates the Dr. Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University for the development of agriculture and for the furtherance of learning and research in agriculture and allied sciences. The Act declares the institution as one of national importance. The Act includes 45 sections and 1 schedule that outline the university's powers, jurisdiction, authorities, officers, and employees. The university shall have the power to make provisions for instructions, research, dissemination of findings, examinations, degrees, diplomas, certificates, fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals, and prizes. The university shall also have the power to borrow money, receive benefactions, donations, and gifts, and acquire, hold, manage, and dispose of property. The university shall be open to all classes, castes, creeds, races, or classes, and it shall not be lawful for the university to adopt or impose any test whatsoever of religious belief or profession for entitlement to be appointed as a teacher, officer, or student. The Visitor of the university shall be the President of India. The Board shall be the principal executive body of the university, and the Academic Council shall be the principal academic body. The Research Council and the Extension Education Council shall exercise general supervision over the research and extension education policies and programmes of the university. The Finance Committee shall manage the finances and investments of the university. The Board of Studies shall recommend courses and curricula for various degrees. The university shall have a Chancellor, a Vice-Chancellor, Deans, Directors, a Registrar, a Comptroller, a University Librarian, and other officers as prescribed by the Statutes. The university shall have authorities such as the Board, the Academic Council, the Research Council, the Extension Education Council, the Finance Committee, the Faculties, and the Board of Studies. The university shall also have the power to make Statutes, Ordinances, and Regulations. The Act includes provisions for the appointment, conditions of service, and removal of employees, as well as provisions for the appointment of visiting professors, emeritus professors, consultants, and officers on special duty. The Act also includes provisions for the appointment of Selection Committees for making recommendations for appointments to various posts in the university.
