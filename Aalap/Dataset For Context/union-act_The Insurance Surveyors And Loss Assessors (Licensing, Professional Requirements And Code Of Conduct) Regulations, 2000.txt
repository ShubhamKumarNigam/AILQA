Summary:

The Insurance Surveyors and Loss Assessors (Licensing, Professional Requirements And Code Of Conduct) Regulations, 2000 outline the rules and regulations for licensing, professional requirements, and code of conduct for insurance surveyors and loss assessors in India. These regulations apply to all licensed insurance surveyors and loss assessors. The document begins by defining important terms used in the regulations. It then outlines the application process for obtaining a license, including the qualifications and requirements necessary for eligibility. The regulations also specify the fee structure for grant of license, and the procedure for renewal of license. The document also covers the procedure for refusal or cancellation of license, suspension of license, and issuance of duplicate license. The regulations establish a Committee called "Surveyors and Loss Assessors Committee" to assist the Authority on matters and affairs relating to Insurance Surveyors and Loss Assessors. The Committee is responsible for recommending the syllabus for examination and practical training requirements, improving and developing the status and standard of the profession, coordinating with educational institutions, and dealing with complaints of insured/insurer in respect of survey work done by surveyors and loss assessors. The regulations also set out the duties and responsibilities of a surveyor and loss assessor, including the need to maintain confidentiality and neutrality, conducting inspections and re-inspections, estimating the quantum and description of the subject under loss, advising on loss minimization and control measures, assessing liability under the contract of insurance, and submitting reports to the insurer. The regulations also specify that a surveyor or loss assessor shall submit his report to the insurer as expeditiously as possible, but not later than 30 days of his appointment.
