The Coinage (Issue of Commemorative Coin to commemorate the occasion of 150th Birth Anniversary of Srila Bhakti Siddhanta Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad) Rules, 2024, have been made under the Coinage Act of 2011. These rules establish guidelines for the creation and issuance of a commemorative coin in honor of the 150th birth anniversary of Srila Bhakti Siddhanta Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad. The coin will be denominated in 150 rupees and will be minted in a quaternary alloy of silver (50%), copper (40%), nickel (5%), and zinc (5%). The coin will have a circular shape with a diameter of 44 millimeters and will feature 200 serrations on its edge.

The obverse side of the coin will display the Lion Capitol of Ashoka Pillar in the center, with the legend "सत्यमेव जयते" (Satyameva Jayate) inscribed below. The word "भारत" (Bharat) will be written in Devnagri script on the left periphery, and the word "INDIA" will be written in English on the right periphery. The Rupee symbol "⟨₹⟩" and the denomination "150" will be depicted below the Lion Capitol.

The reverse side of the coin will feature an image of Srila Bhakti Siddhanta Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad in the center. The inscription "श्रील भक्ति सिद्धांत की 150वीं जयंती सरस्वती गोस्वामी प्रभुपाद" (Shri Bhakti Siddhanta Ki 150vim Jayanti Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad) will be written in Devnagari script on the upper periphery, and the inscription "150th Birth Anniversary of Srila Bhakti Siddhanta Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad" will be written in English on the lower periphery. The year "1874" and "2024" will be written in international numerals on the left and right sides of the coin, respectively.

The standard weight of the coin is 35 grams, with a remedy allowed in the composition of silver of 1/500th plus or minus, and a remedy allowed in weight of 1/100th plus or minus. The coin is to be issued by the Central Government on the occasion of the 150th birth anniversary of Srila Bhakti Siddhanta Saraswati Goswami Prabhupad.
