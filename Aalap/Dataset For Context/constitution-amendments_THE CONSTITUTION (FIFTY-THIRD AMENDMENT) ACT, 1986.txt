Summary:

The Constitution (Fifty-Third Amendment) Act, 1986, provides that certain Acts of Parliament shall not apply to the new State of Mizoram unless decided by the Mizoram Legislature. These Acts relate to religious or social practices of Mizos, Mizo customary law and procedure, administration of civil and criminal justice involving decisions according to Mizo Customary law, and ownership and transfer of land. However, this provision does not apply to Central Acts already in force in the Union territory of Mizoram before the Act comes into force.

The Legislative Assembly of the proposed new State of Mizoram will consist of not less than forty members. This amendment is specific to the proposed new State of Mizoram, and a separate Bill for its establishment has also been introduced. The Act will come into force on a date appointed by the Central Government through a notification in the Official Gazette.
