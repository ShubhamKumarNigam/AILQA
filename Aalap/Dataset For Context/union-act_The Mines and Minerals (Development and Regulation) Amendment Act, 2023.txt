The Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2023, amends the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act of the principal Act. The Amendment Act modifies various sections and adds new sections to the principal Act. Some of the key changes are:

1. In section 4, the words "or of a exploration licence" are added after "prospecting licence".
2. In section 4A, the marginal heading is substituted with a new one.
3. In section 5, the marginal heading is substituted with "Restrictions on the grant of mineral concession".
4. In section 6, the marginal heading is substituted with "Maximum area for which mineral concession may be granted". A new clause (ab) is added in sub-section (1), allowing for the grant of one or more exploration licenses covering a total area of more than five thousand square kilometers, with each license not exceeding one thousand square kilometers. The words "reconnaissance permit, mining lease or prospecting licence" are replaced with "mineral concession" in clause (c).
5. Chapter III's heading is substituted with "PROCEDURE FOR OBTAINING MINERAL CONCESSION IN RESPECT OF LAND IN WHICH THE MINERALS VEST IN THE GOVERNMENT".
6. In section 10, the marginal heading is substituted with "Application for mineral concession". In sub-section (4), the words, figures, and letters "sections 10B, 11, 11A or the rules made under section 11B" are replaced with "sections 10B, 10BA, 11, 11A, 11B or 11D".
7. New section 10BA is inserted, which outlines the grant of exploration licenses for minerals specified in the Seventh Schedule through auction. The section provides the conditions and procedures for granting exploration licenses, including the auction process and the holder's entitlement to a share of the applicable amount quoted in the auction of mining leases.
8. New section 11D is inserted, which allows the Central Government to conduct auctions for granting mining leases or composite licenses in respect of minerals specified in Part D of the First Schedule. The royalty, dead rent, applicable amount quoted in the auction, and any other statutory payment in relation to the mining lease or composite license auctioned by the Central Government shall accrue to the State Government or concerned authorities.
9. In section 12, the marginal heading is substituted with "Registers of mineral concession". Two new clauses, (g) and (h), are added to sub-section (1), which pertain to a register of applications for exploration licenses and a register of exploration licenses.

The Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Amendment Act, 2023, introduces significant changes to the principal Act, including the addition of new sections and the modification of existing ones. These amendments aim to streamline the process of obtaining mineral concessions, introduce auction-based systems for granting exploration and mining licenses, and clarify the procedures for registering mineral concessions.
