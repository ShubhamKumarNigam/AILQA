Summary:

The Prasar Bharati (Broadcasting Corporation of India) Act, 1990 establishes a Broadcasting Corporation for India called Prasar Bharati. The Act outlines the composition, functions, and powers of the Corporation, as well as provisions for its management and governance.

The Corporation is responsible for organizing and conducting public broadcasting services to inform, educate, and entertain the public, and to ensure a balanced development of broadcasting on radio and television. The Corporation is guided by certain objectives, including upholding the unity and integrity of the country, safeguarding the citizen's right to be informed, promoting social justice and combating exploitation, and serving the rural and weaker sections of the society.

The Corporation has the power to determine and levy fees and other service charges for or in respect of advertisements and such programmes as may be specified by regulations. The Central Government has the power to determine the maximum limit of broadcast time in respect of the advertisement.

The Act also establishes a Parliamentary Committee to oversee that the Corporation discharges its functions in accordance with the provisions of this Act and, in particular, the objectives set out in section 12. A Broadcasting Council is also established to receive and consider complaints referred to in section 15 and to advise the Corporation in the discharge of its functions.

The Corporation is granted certain assets, liabilities, and obligations from the Central Government, and is entitled to receive grants, equity, grant-in-aid, or loans from the Central Government. The Corporation is required to maintain proper accounts and prepare an annual statement of accounts, which are to be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India.

The Act also includes provisions for the delegation of powers, the preparation of annual reports, and the making of rules and regulations. The rules and regulations made under this Act are to be laid before each House of Parliament. The Act also includes a provision for the removal of difficulties in giving effect to the provisions of this Act.
