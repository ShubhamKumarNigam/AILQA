Summary:
The Weapons of Mass Destruction and their Delivery Systems (Prohibition of Unlawful Activities) Act, 2005 is an Indian law that prohibits unlawful activities related to weapons of mass destruction and their delivery systems. The Act aims to safeguard India's national security as a Nuclear Weapon State and prevent non-State actors and terrorists from acquiring weapons of mass destruction and their delivery systems. The Act applies to citizens of India outside India, companies or bodies corporate registered or incorporated in India, ships, aircraft, or other means of transport registered in India, foreigners while in India, and persons in the service of the Government of India, within and beyond India. The Act prohibits the unlawful manufacture, acquisition, possession, development, or transport of nuclear weapons, biological or chemical weapons, and their means of delivery. It also prohibits the transfer of weapons of mass destruction and their means of delivery to non-State actors or terrorists. The Act regulates the export, transfer, re-transfer, transit, and transshipment of items related to relevant activity and imposes penalties for violations of its provisions. The Act also provides for the appointment of Advisory Committees, Licensing Authority, and Appellate Authority and delegates powers to authorities for implementing its provisions. The Act applies in addition to any other relevant Act for the time being in force in relation to any matter covered under this Act. The Act extends to the whole of India, including its Exclusive Economic Zone, and applies to offences committed beyond India. The Act defines various terms used in the Act, including biological weapons, chemical weapons, fissile material, radioactive material, item, non-State actor, nuclear weapon or other nuclear explosive device, public domain, relevant activity, re-transfer, technology, terrorist, trans-shipment, unlawful, and weapons of mass destruction.
