Summary:

THE INDUSTRIAL DISPUTES (BANKING AND INSURANCE COMPANIES) ACT, 1949, is an Act to provide for the adjudication of industrial disputes concerning certain banking and insurance companies. The Act extends to the whole of India, except the State of Jammu and Kashmir. The definitions of "award," "banking company," "industrial dispute," and "insurance company" are the same as those assigned to them in section 2 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.

Section 4 of the Act prohibits State Governments or any officer or authority subordinate to such Government from referring an industrial dispute concerning any banking or insurance company, or any matter relating to such dispute, to any tribunal or other authority for adjudication, inquiry, or settlement.

Section 5 provides that where any industrial dispute concerning any banking or insurance company or any matter relating to such dispute has been referred by a State Government or any officer or authority subordinate to such Government to any tribunal or other authority for adjudication or settlement before April 30, 1949, then on that date, such reference shall be deemed to have been withdrawn and all such proceedings shall have abated. The Central Government shall refer under section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, every industrial dispute to which the provisions of sub-section (1) apply to an Industrial Tribunal constituted under the said Act for adjudication.

Section 6 empowers the Central Government to refer under section 10 of the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947, the dispute or any of the matters in dispute to an Industrial Tribunal constituted under the said Act for adjudication if any award or decision has been made in respect of any industrial dispute concerning any banking or insurance company by any tribunal or other authority constituted or appointed by a State Government, or any officer or authority subordinate to such Government.

The Industrial Disputes (Banking and Insurance Companies) Second Ordinance, 1949, has been repealed by this Act. However, anything done or any action taken in the exercise of any power conferred by or under the said Ordinance shall be deemed to have been done or taken in the exercise of the powers conferred by or under this Act.
