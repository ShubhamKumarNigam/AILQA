Summary:

The National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences, Bangalore Regulations, 2013 outlines the rules and regulations for the governance of the National Institute of Mental Health and Neuro-Sciences (NIMHANS) in Bangalore, India. These regulations cover various aspects of the Institute's functioning, including its composition, powers, and duties of various authorities such as the President, Governing Body, Director, and standing and ad hoc committees. The regulations also cover the conduct of business, quorum, and voting procedures for meetings, as well as the transaction of business by circulation of papers.

The regulations also provide for the appointment, powers, and duties of the Director, who is the Chief Executive Officer of the Institute. The Director is responsible for the administration of the Institute and is authorized to delegate any of his powers to any officer of the Institute.

The regulations also cover the admission of students to courses of study, the award of degrees, diplomas, certificates, and fellowships, and the conduct of examinations. The Institute is authorized to charge fees for various services and courses, as specified in the schedules attached to the regulations.

The regulations also provide for the terms and conditions of service of employees, including their qualifications, method of recruitment, scale of posts, and period of probation. The regulations also cover the grant of leave, seniority, and superannuation of employees.

The regulations also provide for the conduct, discipline, and penalties of employees, as well as other conditions of service. The regulations also cover the pay of re-employed persons, building and land belonging to the Institute, medical facilities for employees, proceedings by or against the Institute, and the exercise of powers by various authorities.

These regulations are intended to ensure the smooth functioning of the Institute and to provide a framework for its governance, administration, and academic activities. They are binding on all members, employees, and officers of the Institute and are subject to amendment by the Governing Body with the prior approval of the Central Government.
