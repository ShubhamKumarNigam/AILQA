Summary:
The Immigrants (Expulsion from Assam) Act, 1950 is a law in India that provides for the expulsion of certain immigrants from Assam. The Act extends to the whole of India and empowers the Central Government to expel any person or class of persons who have come into Assam from outside India and whose stay in Assam is deemed detrimental to the interests of the general public of India or any section thereof or of any Scheduled Tribe in Assam. The Central Government may direct such person or class of persons to remove themselves from India or Assam within a specified time and by a specified route. The Act also grants the Central Government the power to delegate its powers and duties under section 2 to any officer subordinate to the Central Government or to the Government of Assam, Meghalaya, or Nagaland, or any officer subordinate to that Government. Any authority empowered by or in pursuance of the provisions of this Act to exercise any power may take or cause to be taken such steps and use or cause to be used such force as may be reasonably necessary for the effective exercise of such power. Any person who contravenes or attempts to contravene or abets the contravention of any order made under section 2, fails to comply with any direction given by any such order, or harbours any person who has contravened any order made under section 2 or has failed to comply with any direction given by any such order, shall be punishable with imprisonment which may extend to three years and shall also be liable to fine. No suit, prosecution or other legal proceeding shall lie against any person for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done under this Act. The Repealing and Amending Act, 1957 (36 of 1957), s. 2 and Schedule repealed and saved certain provisions of this Act.
