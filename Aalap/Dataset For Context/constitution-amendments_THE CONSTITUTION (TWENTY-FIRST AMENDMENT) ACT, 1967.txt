Summary:

The Constitution (Twenty-first Amendment) Act, 1967, is a law that adds the Sindhi language to the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution of India. This amendment renumbers entries 12 to 14 as entries 13 to 15 and inserts entry 12 as "Sindhi." This act recognizes Sindhi as an official language of the Republic of India, making it the fifteenth language to be included in the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution. The Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution lists official languages of the country, and the languages listed are eligible for representation in the Commission for Official Languages. The Twenty-first Amendment Act, 1967, is significant as it recognizes Sindhi as an official language, expanding the cultural and linguistic diversity of India.
