Summary:
The Competition Commission of India (Lesser Penalty) Regulations, 2024, are regulations made under the Competition Act, 2002, which provide for the grant of lesser penalty to enterprises or individuals who make a full, true, and vital disclosure of a cartel, enabling the Commission to form a prima facie opinion of its existence, or establish the contravention of the provisions of section 3 of the Act. The regulations set out the conditions for granting lesser penalty or lesser penalty plus, including the requirement to cease participation in the cartel, provide vital disclosure, cooperate genuinely and continuously, not conceal or destroy evidence, and not give false evidence. The regulations also provide for the grant of priority status to applicants, the procedure for granting lesser penalty or lesser penalty plus, and the confidentiality of information provided by the applicant. The regulations repeal the Competition Commission of India (Lesser Penalty) Regulations, 2009, and provide for the continuation of any proceedings or decisions taken under the repealed regulations. The regulations also provide for the removal of difficulties and savings. The regulations define various terms used in the regulations, including cartel, applicant, party, priority status, and vital disclosure. The regulations also provide for the contents of the application for lesser penalty and lesser penalty plus.
