Summary:
The East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction Act (Extension to Chandigarh) Act, 1974, extends the East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction Act, 1949, to the Union territory of Chandigarh. The Act defines "the Act" as the East Punjab Urban Rent Restriction Act, 1949, as it was in force in certain areas in the pre-reorganisation State of Punjab immediately before the 1st day of November, 1966. The Act came into force in Chandigarh on the 4th day of November, 1972.

The Act includes several modifications to the original Act, including the substitution of "Central Government" for "State Government" throughout the Act, and changes to the definitions of "notification" and "urban area". The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for the purpose of carrying out all or any of the provisions of this Act.

It is important to note that anything done or any action taken under the Act shall be deemed to be valid and effective as if the provisions of this Act had been in force at all material times when such thing was done or such action was taken. Additionally, the Act does not render any person guilty of any offence for any contravention of the provisions of the Act which occurred before the commencement of this Act.
