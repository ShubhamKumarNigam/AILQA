Summary:
The Delhi School Education Act, 1973, is an Act to provide for better organization and development of school education in the Union territory of Delhi. The Act has 29 sections divided into 8 chapters. The first chapter, Preliminary, defines important terms used in the Act. Chapter II, Establishment, Recognition, Management of, and Aid to, Schools, deals with the power of the Administrator to regulate education in schools, recognition of schools, scheme of management, and aid to recognized schools. Chapter III, School Property, regulates the management of school property and the transfer, mortgage, or lease of such property. Chapter IV, Terms and Conditions of Service of Employees of Recognized Private Schools, sets out the rules for the terms and conditions of service of employees of recognized private schools. Chapter V, Provisions Applicable to Un-aided Minority Schools, provides for the power to prescribe minimum qualifications for recruitment and a Code of Conduct for employees of unaided minority schools. Chapter VI, Admission to Schools and Fees, deals with admission to recognized schools and fees and other charges. Chapter VII, Taking Over the Management of Schools, provides for the taking over of the management of schools by the Administrator in certain circumstances. Chapter VIII, Miscellaneous, contains miscellaneous provisions, including the power to make rules, jurisdiction of civil courts barred, protection of action taken in good faith, liability of manager to punishment, power to remove difficulties, and repeal and savings. The Act applies to the whole of the Union territory of Delhi and came into force on such date as the Administrator may, by notification, appoint.
