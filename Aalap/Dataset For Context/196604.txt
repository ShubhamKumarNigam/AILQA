Summary:

This document is the Seamen’s Provident Fund Act, 1966, which establishes a provident fund for seamen. The Act defines various terms related to the Fund, such as "agreement with the crew," "Board," "continuous discharge certificate," "contribution," "employer," "Fund," "Government," "master," "member," "Merchant Shipping Act," "Scheme," "seaman," "service," and "wages." The Act also lays out the powers and duties of the Board, the method of appointment and conditions of service of the Seamen’s Provident Fund Commissioner and other officers, and the mode of recovery of moneys due from employers. The Act also provides for the protection of the Fund against attachment and the priority of payment of contributions over other debts. The Act applies to every seaman and the employer of such seaman, unless otherwise expressly provided.
