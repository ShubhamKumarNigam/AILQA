Summary:
The Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission for UT of Jammu & Kashmir and UT of Ladakh (Appointment of Consultants) Regulations, 2023, outlines the rules and procedures for appointing consultants for specialized tasks to assist the Commission in the performance of its functions. These regulations are made under the Electricity Act, 2003, and apply to both institutional and individual consultants.

The regulations define various terms, including "Act," "Commission," "Committee," "Consultant," and "Secretary," and specify the scope of work for consultants. Consultants may be appointed for specific tasks or to assist the Commission in the discharge of its functions, and the terms and conditions of engagement are to be agreed upon between the consultant and the Commission.

The regulations also specify the period of appointment for consultants, which may not exceed four years in total, and categorize consultants into four categories: Institutional consultants, Individual consultants, Professional experts, and Staff consultants. Institutional consultants are firms, companies, or associations of persons, while individual consultants are individuals with specialized knowledge, experience, or skills. Professional experts are engaged for advice on specialized and urgent issues, and staff consultants are appointed for assisting the Commission in discharging its functions.

The regulations outline the process for appointing institutional consultants, which includes preparing terms of reference, inviting proposals, evaluating proposals, and negotiating with the selected consultant. The evaluation of proposals is based on both technical competence and quoted cost, and the Commission may decide to evaluate technical and financial proposals separately.

Individual consultants may be selected based on their qualifications and experience for the assignment, and the regulations specify the minimum qualification and age criteria for individual consultants. Individual consultants are generally appointed on a consolidated fee basis, and the fee and other charges payable to individual consultants are specified in the regulations.

The regulations also provide for the appointment of professional experts and staff consultants, and specify the process for their appointment. Single source selection may be resorted to in appropriate cases, and consultants shall not be hired for any assignment that would be in conflict with their prior or current obligations.

The consultant selected for appointment is required to execute an agreement with the Commission, and the Commission has the power to amend any provision of these regulations as deemed necessary. If any difficulty arises in giving effect to any of the provisions of these regulations, the Commission may, by general or special order, do anything necessary to remove the difficulties.
