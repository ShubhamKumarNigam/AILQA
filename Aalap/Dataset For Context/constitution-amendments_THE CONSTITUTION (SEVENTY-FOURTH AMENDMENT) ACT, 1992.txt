
Summary:

The Constitution (Seventy-Fourth Amendment) Act, 1992, aims to incorporate provisions related to Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) in the Constitution. The purpose is to address inadequacies in the relationship between State Governments and ULBs regarding functions, taxation powers, and revenue sharing. It also seeks to ensure regular conduct of elections, timely elections in case of supersession, and provide adequate representation for weaker sections like Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and women.

The Act adds a new part to the Constitution, defining three types of Municipalities: Nagar Panchayats for areas in transition from rural to urban, Municipal Councils for smaller urban areas, and Municipal Corporations for larger urban areas. Composition of Municipalities will be determined by State Legislatures, with provisions for direct election, representation of Chairpersons of Committees, and representation of persons with special knowledge or experience in Municipal Administration.

Reservation of seats is mandatory for Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and women, with one-third of total seats reserved for women. The Act also ensures a fixed tenure of five years for Municipalities, with re-election within six months of the end of tenure. Devolution of powers and responsibilities to Municipalities for economic development and social justice is also provided.

The Act allows for the levy of taxes and duties by Municipalities, assigning of such taxes and duties to Municipalities by State Governments, and grants-in-aid by the State to the Municipalities as per State law. A Finance Commission will review the finances of Municipalities and recommend principles for determining taxes, sharing of taxes, and grants-in-aid.

The Act also includes provisions for the audit of accounts of Municipal Corporations by the Comptroller and Auditor-General of India, making of law by a State Legislature with respect to elections, application of the provisions to Union territories, and exemption of Scheduled areas and tribal areas from the application of the provisions. Disqualifications for membership of a Municipality and bar of jurisdiction of Courts in matters relating to elections to the Municipalities are also included.
