Summary:

THE LIFE INSURANCE CORPORATION (MODIFICATION OF SETTLEMENTS) ACT, 1976

This Act, enacted on 29th May, 1976, is titled "THE LIFE INSURANCE CORPORATION (MODIFICATION OF SETTLEMENTS) ACT, 1976." The purpose of this Act is to modify the settlements reached between the Life Insurance Corporation of India (Corporation) and their workmen.

The Act defines the following terms:

* Corporation: the Life Insurance Corporation of India established under section 3 of the Life Insurance Corporation Act, 1956 (31 of 1956).
* Salary: basic pay, special pay, dearness allowance, and additional dearness allowance.
* Settlements: the settlement reached between the Corporation and their workmen on the 24th day of January, 1974, and the settlement reached between the Corporation and their workmen on the 6th day of February, 1974, in respect of which there was no approval as provided for in sub-clause (2) of clause 12 thereof.

The Act contains a single section, Section 3, which states that the provisions of each of the settlements, insofar as they relate to the payment of an annual cash bonus to every Class III and Class IV employee of the Corporation at the rate of fifteen per cent. of his annual salary, shall not have any force or effect and shall not be deemed to have had any force or effect on and from the 1st day of April, 1975. This means that the payment of annual cash bonuses to Class III and Class IV employees of the Corporation at the rate of fifteen per cent. of their annual salary as per the settlements reached between the Corporation and their workmen on the 24th day of January, 1974, and the 6th day of February, 1974, is no longer valid from the 1st day of April, 1975.
