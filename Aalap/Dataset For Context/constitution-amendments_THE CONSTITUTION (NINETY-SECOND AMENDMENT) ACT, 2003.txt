The Constitution (Ninety-second Amendment) Act, 2003, is an amendment that added four languages to the Eighth Schedule of the Indian Constitution. These languages are Bodo, Dogri, Maithili, and Santali. This addition brought the total number of officially recognized languages to 22. The amendment was an attempt to provide national recognition to these languages, safeguarding the interests of their speakers and preserving the linguistic diversity of the country.
