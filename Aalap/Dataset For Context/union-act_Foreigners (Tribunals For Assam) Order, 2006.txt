The Foreigners (Tribunals For Assam) Order, 2006, is an order made under the Foreigners Act, 1946, by the Central Government. It applies to the State of Assam. The Order provides for the constitution of Tribunals, which will determine whether a person is or is not a foreigner within the meaning of the Foreigners Act, 1946. The Tribunal shall consist of one or more persons having judicial experience. The Tribunal shall have the power to regulate its own procedure and shall have the powers of a civil court while trying a suit. The Order also specifies the procedure for disposal of questions and the powers of the Tribunal, including the power to summon and enforce the attendance of any person, require the discovery and production of any document, and issue commissions for the examination of any witness.
