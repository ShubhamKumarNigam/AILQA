Summary:

THE NATIONAL COMMISSION FOR BACKWARD CLASSES (REPEAL) ACT, 2018, with the Act ID ACT NO. 24 OF 2018, was enacted on the 14th of August, 2018. The Act aims to repeal the National Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993 (27 of 1993). Upon the commencement of this Act, the National Commission for Backward Classes, established under section 3(1) of the repealed Act, will stand dissolved.

Section 2(1) of this Act repeals the National Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993. However, the repeal will not impact any actions taken, rights, obligations, or penalties incurred under the repealed Act. Any proceedings or remedies regarding such rights, obligations, or penalties may continue, and any penalties or punishments may be imposed as if the repealed Act were still in force (Section 2(2)). The general principles of the General Clauses Act of 1897 regarding the effects of repeal still apply (Section 2(3)).

This summary clarifies that the National Commission for Backward Classes (Repeal) Act, 2018, repeals the National Commission for Backward Classes Act, 1993, and explains the consequences of this repeal.
