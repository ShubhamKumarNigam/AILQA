Summary:

The Joint Electricity Regulatory Commission for Manipur and Mizoram (Preparation of Annual Report) Rules, 2010 outlines the regulations for the preparation and submission of an annual report by the Joint Commission. The report must be prepared every year, summarizing the activities of the Joint Commission during the previous financial year. The report should include several key pieces of information, such as goals and objectives, annual targets and their performance, important additions or changes in regulations, functioning of the State Advisory Committee, trends of important parameters, cases challenged in courts, and dispute resolution. The copies of the annual report should be submitted to the Central Government and the Participating State Governments by the end of October each year.

These rules are made under the Electricity Act, 2003 and define terms such as "Act," "Joint Commission," "Participating State," and "Schedule" to clarify their meaning in the context of these rules. The report should be prepared in the form specified in the Schedule, and words and expressions used in these rules but not defined shall have the meanings assigned to them in the Electricity Act, 2003.
