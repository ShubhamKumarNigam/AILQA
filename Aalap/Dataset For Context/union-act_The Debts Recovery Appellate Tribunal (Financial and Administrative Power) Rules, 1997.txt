


The Debts Recovery Appellate Tribunal (Financial and Administrative Power) Rules, 1997, is a set of rules made by the Central Government under the Recovery of Debts Due to Banks and Financial Institutions Act, 1993. These rules came into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

The rules define certain terms used in them, including "Act," "Appellate Tribunal," "Chairperson," and other expressions that are not defined but have meanings assigned to them in the Act.

The rules confer financial and administrative powers on the Chairperson of the Appellate Tribunal, who is authorized to exercise the same powers as a Head of Department under various rules and regulations, including the General Financial Rules, 1963, the Delegation of Financial Powers Rules, 1978, the Fundamental Rules, the Supplementary Rules, the Central Civil Services (Leave) Rules, 1972, the Central Civil Services (Joining Time) Rules, 1979, the Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 1972, the Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964, the Central Civil Services (Classification, Control and Appeal) Rules, 1965, and the General Provident Fund (Central Services) Rules, 1960, as amended from time to time. However, the exercise of powers by the Chairperson is subject to instructions issued by the Central Government from time to time.
