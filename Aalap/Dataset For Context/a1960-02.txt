Summary:

THE DISPLACED PERSONS (COMPENSATION AND REHABILITATION) AMENDMENT ACT, 1960

Act No. 2 of 1960

This Act is an amendment to the Displaced Persons (Compensation and Rehabilitation) Act, 1954, and received the President's assent on February 27, 1960. The amendment repeals various sections of the original Act and inserts a new section 20B. The Act also validates certain actions taken before its commencement.

Section 1 of the Act specifies the short title as the Displaced Persons (Compensation and Rehabilitation) Amendment Act, 1960.

Section 2 repeals section 2 of the original Act, which dealt with the appointment of Deputy Chief Settlement Commissioners.

Section 3 repeals section 3 of the original Act, which dealt with the powers and duties of Deputy Chief Settlement Commissioners.

Section 4 repeals section 19 of the original Act, which dealt with appeals.

Section 5 repeals section 20 of the original Act, which dealt with revisions.

Section 6 inserts a new section 20B in the original Act, which dealt with the power of the Central Government to make rules.

Section 7 repeals section 26 of the original Act, which dealt with the power to make rules.

Section 8 repeals section 31 of the original Act, which dealt with the power to make rules.

Section 9 repeals section 34 of the original Act, which dealt with the power to make rules.

Section 10 repeals section 40 of the original Act, which dealt with the power to make rules.

Section 11 validates certain actions taken before the commencement of this Act. Specifically, any fees levied under the original Act before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been validly levied. Additionally, any orders, actions, or appointments made by any person exercising the powers of a Deputy Chief Settlement Commissioner before the commencement of this Act shall be deemed to have been validly made.
