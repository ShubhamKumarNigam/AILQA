Summary:

THE GENERAL INSURANCE BUSINESS (NATIONALISATION) ACT, 1972, is an Act that aims to provide for the acquisition and transfer of shares of Indian insurance companies and undertakings of other existing insurers to serve better the needs of the economy by securing the development of general insurance business in the best interests of the community. The Act also aims to ensure that the operation of the economic system does not result in the concentration of wealth to the common detriment.

The Act is divided into several chapters, and this summary will focus on the key provisions in each chapter.

Chapter I: Preliminary

The Act begins with a short title and a declaration as to the policy of the State. The Act then proceeds to define key terms used throughout the Act. These definitions include terms such as "acquiring company," "appointed day," "Companies Act," "Corporation," "existing insurer," "foreign insurer," "general insurance business," "Government company," "Indian insurance company," "Insurance Act," "Life Insurance Corporation," "notification," "prescribed," "Schedule," and "words and expressions used in this Act."

Chapter II: Transfer to Public Ownership of General Insurance Business

This chapter deals with the transfer of shares of Indian insurance companies and undertakings of other existing insurers to the Central Government. Section 4 of the Act states that all the shares in the capital of every Indian insurance company shall, by virtue of this Act, stand transferred to and vested in the Central Government free of all trusts, liabilities, and encumbrances affecting them. The Central Government shall then transfer not less than ten shares of every such company to specified persons to enable the Indian insurance company to function as a Government company.

Section 5 of the Act deals with the transfer of undertakings of other existing insurers. The undertaking of every existing insurer who is not an Indian insurance company shall stand transferred to and vested in the Central Government, and the Central Government shall immediately thereafter provide for the transfer of that undertaking to an Indian insurance company.

Section 6 of the Act deals with the effect of the transfer of undertakings. The undertaking of every such existing insurer shall be deemed to include all assets, rights, powers, authorities, and privileges and all property, movable and immovable, cash balances, reserve funds, investments, and all other rights and interests in or arising out of such property as were immediately before the appointed day in the ownership, possession, power, or control of such existing insurer in relation to the undertaking.

Chapter III: General Insurance Corporation of India

This chapter deals with the formation of the General Insurance Corporation of India. Section 9 of the Act states that the Central Government shall form a Government company in accordance with the provisions of the Companies Act to be known as the General Insurance Corporation of India for the purpose of superintending, controlling, and carrying on the business of general insurance. The authorized capital of the Corporation shall be rupees two hundred and fifty crores, divided into two hundred and fifty lakhs fully paid-up shares of one hundred rupees each.

Chapter IV: Amounts to be Paid for Acquisitions

This chapter deals with the amounts to be paid for the transfer and vesting of shares or undertakings. Section 11 of the Act states that for the transfer of the shares of each Indian insurance company to, and vesting in, the Central Government, there shall be paid by the Central Government to the Corporation, for distribution to the shareholders of each such company, the amount specified against such company in the corresponding entry under column (3) of Part A of the Schedule.

Chapter V: Scheme for Reorganisation of General Insurance Business

This chapter deals with the framing of schemes for the merger of companies and the transfer of their undertakings. Section 16 of the Act states that the Central Government may, by notification, frame one or more schemes providing for the merger in one Indian insurance company of any other Indian insurance company or the formation of a new company by the amalgamation of two or more Indian insurance companies.

Chapter VI: Functions of Corporation and Acquiring Companies and Their Management

This chapter deals with the functions of the Corporation and the acquiring companies and their management. Section 18 of the Act states that the functions of the Corporation shall include the carrying on of any part of the general insurance business, aiding, assisting, and advising the acquiring companies in the matter of setting up of standards of conduct and sound practice in general insurance business, and advising the acquiring companies in the matter of controlling their expenses.

Chapter VII: Miscellaneous

This chapter deals with miscellaneous provisions. Section 24 of the Act states that the Corporation and the acquiring companies shall have the exclusive privilege of carrying on general insurance business in India.
