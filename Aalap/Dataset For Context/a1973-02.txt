Summary:
The Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Oaths and Fees) (Extension to Jammu and Kashmir) Act, 1973, is a law that extends the Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Oaths and Fees) Act, 1948 to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. This Act came into force on 13th March, 1973. The Act has three sections. Section 1 states the short title and commencement of the Act. Section 2 extends the Diplomatic and Consular Officers (Oaths and Fees) Act, 1948 to the State of Jammu and Kashmir and applies the provisions of the principal Act and the rules made thereunder to the State of Jammu and Kashmir. However, clause (d) of section 2 of the principal Act is omitted. Section 3, which was originally an amendment of section 8, has been repealed. The Act applies to the Union territory of Jammuand Kashmir and the Union territory of Ladakh vide Notification No. S.O. 3912 (E), dated 30th October, 2019.
