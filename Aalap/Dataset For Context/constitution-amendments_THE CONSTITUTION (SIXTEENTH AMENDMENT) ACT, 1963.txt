
Amendment of the Seventh Schedule.-

In the Seventh Schedule, under the heading "Union List," after entry 92, the following entry shall be inserted, namely:-

"92A. Elections to Parliament and to the Legislature of every State and to the offices of President and Vice-President of India.

Explanation.-

In this entry, the expression "elections" includes the preparation of electoral rolls and the conduct of all elections, whether to the offices of President and Vice-President of India or to Parliament or to the Legislature of any State."



Amendment of the Ninth Schedule.-

In the Ninth Schedule,-

(a) entries 61 and 62 shall be omitted;

(b) for entry 63, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"63. The Uttar Pradesh Zamindari and Lagaan Act, 1950 (U.P. Act No. 1 of 1951);";

(c) entries 64 to 70 shall be renumbered as entries 62 to 68 respectively;

(d) for entry 71, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"71. The Bombay Tenancy and Agricultural Lands Act, 1948 (Bom. Act No. XLI of 1948);";

(e) entries 72 to 75 shall be renumbered as entries 69 to 72 respectively;

(f) for entry 76, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"76. The Madhya Pradesh Agricultural Lands (Control on Transfers) Act, 1950 (Madhya Pradesh Act No. 14 of 1951);";

(g) entries 77 to 80 shall be renumbered as entries 73 to 76 respectively;

(h) for entry 81, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"81. The Bihar Land Reforms Act, 1950 (Bihar Act No. 2 of 1951);";

(i) entries 82 to 84 shall be renumbered as entries 77 to 79 respectively;

(j) for entry 85, the following entry shall be substituted, namely:-

"85. The West Bengal Land Reforms Act, 1955 (West Bengal Act No. LVII of 1955);".



The Constitution (Sixteenth Amendment) Act, 1963, was enacted to amend the Constitution of India. This Act has two main amendments. The first amendment adds a new entry, 92A, under the Union List in the Seventh Schedule, which deals with the "Elections to Parliament and to the Legislature of every State and to the offices of President and Vice-President of India." The second amendment is the modification of the Ninth Schedule of the Constitution. This amendment removes entries 61, 62, and replaces entry 63 with the Uttar Pradesh Zamindari and Lagaan Act, 1950. The amendment also renumbers entries 64 to 70 as entries 62 to 68, entry 71 as entry 69, entries 72 to 75 as entries 69 to 72, entry 76 as entry 73, entries 77 to 80 as entries 74 to 77, entry 81 as entry 78, entries 82 to 84 as entries 79 to 81, and entry 85 as entry 82. These amendments are aimed at streamlining the election process and modifying the land reforms acts in various states.
