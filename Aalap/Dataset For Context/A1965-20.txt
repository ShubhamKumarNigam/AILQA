Summary:

THE WAREHOUSING CORPORATIONS (SUPPLEMENTARY) ACT, 1965, is an act that supplements the provisions of the Warehousing Corporations Act, 1962. The act applies to the states specified in the schedule, which currently includes Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Gujarat, Haryana, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Meghalaya, Karnataka, Orissa, Punjab, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal. The act will come into force on a date appointed by the central government. The Warehousing Corporations Act, 1962, will have effect as if the words and figures “being a commodity with respect to which Parliament has power to make laws by virtue of entry 33 in List III in the Seventh Schedule to Constitution” had been omitted in clause (e) of section 2 of that Act. The act was enacted on 22nd September, 1965.
