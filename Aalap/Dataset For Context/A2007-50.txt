Summary:
The Tyre Corporation of India Limited (Disinvestment of Ownership) Act, 2007, Act No. 50 of 2007, was enacted on 12th December, 2007. This Act aims to provide for the disinvestment of the Government's equity in the Tyre Corporation of India Limited, which was formed by the nationalization of Inchek Tyres Limited and National Rubber Manufacturers Limited in 1984. The purpose of this disinvestment is to enable the private sector to invest in the company and ensure the continuation of the manufacture, production, and distribution of tyres, tubes, and other rubber goods, which are essential to the country's economy.

The Act has the following key provisions:

1. Short title: This Act may be called the Tyre Corporation of India Limited (Disinvestment of Ownership) Act, 2007.
2. Disinvestment in the company: The Central Government, on the recommendations of the Board for Reconstruction of Public Sector Enterprises, may pass an order providing for the transfer, exchange, or relinquishment of shares in the company to any person on such terms and conditions as may be agreed upon.
3. Payment of consideration for disinvestment in the company: The consideration for the transfer of shares of the company to the transferee shall be agreed upon between the transferor, the company, and the transferee. The payment shall be made in accordance with a valuation method as specified by the Central Government.
4. Manner of disinvestment: The Central Government may specify that disinvestment of shares shall be effected by one or more of the following methods: (a) making a public offer, preferential allotment, or private placement; (b) directing the company to make further issue of equity capital to the members of the public or preferential allotment or private placement.
5. Provision in respect of officers and other employees of the company: Every officer or other employee of the company, except the Chairman and Directors, serving in its employment immediately before the disinvestment of the company under this Act, shall continue in office or service after such disinvestment on the same terms and conditions as would have been admissible to him if there had been no such disinvestment and shall continue to do so until the expiry of the period of three years from the date of disinvestment.
6. Act to have overriding effect: The provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in the Inchek Tyres Limited and National Rubber Manufacturers Limited (Nationalisation) Act, 1984 (17 of 1984) or any other law for the time being in force.

The objective of this Act is to enable the private sector to invest in the Tyre Corporation of India Limited and ensure the continuation of the manufacture, production, and distribution of tyres, tubes, and other rubber goods, which are essential to the country's economy. The Act provides for the disinvestment of the Government's equity in the company and specifies the terms and conditions for the transfer of shares, payment of consideration, and the manner of disinvestment. The Act also provides for the continuation of the officers and other employees of the company on the same terms and conditions as before the disinvestment. The provisions of this Act shall have an overriding effect on any inconsistent provisions contained in any other law for the time being in force.
