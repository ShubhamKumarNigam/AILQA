Summary:
The Mumbai Port Trust (Transport, Handling and Storage of Dangerous Goods) Regulations, 2007, are a set of rules that govern the handling, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods within the Mumbai Port Trust area. These regulations are applicable to all docks, wharves, quays, bunders, jetties, railways, buildings, and other works constructed or acquired by or vested in the Board of Trustees of the Port of Bombay. The regulations cover a wide range of dangerous goods, including explosives, gases, flammable liquids, flammable solids, oxidizing substances, organic peroxides, toxic and infectious substances, radioactive material, and corrosive substances.

The regulations set out specific rules for the classification, handling, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods. They also establish the roles and responsibilities of various parties involved in the handling, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods, including the owner, agent, master, and barge owner/operator. The regulations also provide for the appointment of a Competent Authority and Inspector of Dangerous Goods, who are responsible for ensuring compliance with the regulations.

The regulations also provide for penalties for non-compliance with the regulations, including fines and imprisonment. They also provide for the seizure and disposal of dangerous goods that are found to be in contravention of the regulations.

Overall, the Mumbai Port Trust (Transport, Handling and Storage of Dangerous Goods) Regulations, 2007, provide a comprehensive framework for the safe handling, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods within the Mumbai Port Trust area. They aim to ensure the safety of people, property, and the environment, while also facilitating the efficient and effective handling, storage, and transportation of dangerous goods.
