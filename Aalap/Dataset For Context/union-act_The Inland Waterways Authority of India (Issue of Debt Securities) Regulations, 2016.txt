Summary:

The Inland Waterways Authority of India (Issue of Debt Securities) Regulations, 2016 governs the issuance of debt securities by the Inland Waterways Authority of India (IWAI). These regulations define the terms used in the document, such as "Act," "Authority," "Committee," "Debt Securities," "funds," and "prospectus." The Authority is authorized to borrow money by issuing debt securities, subject to the approval of the Central Government. The funds collected through the issuance of debt securities can be used for various purposes, including the development of fairways, manufacturing of barges, terminal construction and development, waterway transportation, infrastructural facilities for waterways, and consultancy and research services.

The regulations also provide for the establishment of an Investment Committee, which is responsible for formulating and overseeing the investment policies and management of funds collected through the issuance of debt securities. The Committee is composed of the Vice-Chairman of the Authority, one member of the Authority, one Company Secretary, and two external advisers with experience in financial matters, commerce, financial regulation, law, economics, and public administration. The Committee is required to submit a report to the Authority within thirty days of the request by the Chairman of the Authority.

The regulations also outline the procedure for issuing debt securities, which involves passing a resolution by the Authority with a simple majority. The Authority must consider the report submitted by the Committee and provide reasons for going against its recommendations in writing. The Authority also has the power to re-issue and consolidate its debt securities.

The funds collected through the issuance of debt securities are required to be used exclusively for the purpose stated in the prospectus and for no other purpose, except with the express permission of the Central Government. After the purpose of the issue has been achieved, the surplus funds, if any, shall be used as per the recommendations of the Committee and for any of the purposes of the Authority as provided in the Act.

The Committee and every member shall be accountable for such monies and funds actually been invested after the same has been accounted in the Authority and a member shall not be responsible or accountable for any neglect, default, acts or omissions or commissions of any banker or attorney or other person with whom any securities have been deposited or kept.

Finally, the regulations state that public issue of all the debt securities shall be in accordance with the relevant provisions of the regulations made by the Securities and Exchange Board of India or any other law for the time being in force.
