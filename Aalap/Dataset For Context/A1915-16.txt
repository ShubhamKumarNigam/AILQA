Summary:
The Banaras Hindu University Act, 1915, establishes and incorporates a teaching and residential Hindu University at Banaras. The Act outlines the powers and duties of the University, including the ability to provide instruction in various branches of learning, grant degrees and other academic distinctions, and hold examinations. The University is open to all classes, castes, and creeds, and religious instruction may be given to those who consent to receive it. The Act also establishes various authorities of the University, including the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, and the Finance Committee, and sets out their powers and duties. The Visitor, who is the President of India, has the power to cause inspections and inquiries to be made and to address the Vice-Chancellor with reference to the result of such inspection and inquiry. The Chancellor, who is elected by the Court, is the Head of the University and presides at convocations and meetings of the Court. The Vice-Chancellor is the principal executive and academic officer of the University and exercises general supervision and control over the affairs of the University. The Act also establishes the powers and duties of other officers of the University, including the Rector, the Registrar, the Finance Officer, the Deans of Faculties, the Dean of Students, the Librarian, and the Chief Proctor. The Act provides for the maintenance and admission to privileges of colleges and institutions, and the recognition of degrees and other academic distinctions granted by the University. The University may also found and maintain other colleges and institutions for the purpose of carrying out instruction and research. The Act also provides for the establishment of a pension or provident fund for the benefit of officers, teachers, and other employees of the University.
