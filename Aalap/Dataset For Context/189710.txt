Summary:

Title: THE GENERAL CLAUSES ACT, 1897
Act ID: ACT NO. 10 OF 1897
Enactment Date: [11th March, 1897.]

This Act, known as the General Clauses Act, 1897, aims to consolidate and extend the General Clauses Acts of 1868 and 1887. It includes definitions of various terms used in Central Acts and Regulations made after its commencement, unless there is anything repugnant in the subject or context. These definitions cover legal terms such as 'abet', 'act', 'affidavit', 'barrister', 'British India', 'British possession', 'Central Act', 'Central Government', 'Chapter', 'Chief Controlling Revenue Authority', 'Collector', 'Colony', 'commencement', 'Commissioner', 'Constitution', 'consular officer', 'District Judge', 'document', 'enactment', 'father', 'financial year', 'immovable property', 'imprisonment', 'indictable offence', 'local authority', 'Magistrate', 'master', 'merged territories', 'month', 'movable property', 'oath', 'offence', 'official Gazette', 'part', 'particulars', 'Political Agent', 'Presidency-town', 'Province', 'Provincial Act', 'Provincial Government', 'public nuisance', 'registered', 'Regulation', 'rule', 'schedule', 'Scheduled District', 'section', 'ship', 'sign', 'son', 'State', 'State Act', 'State Government', 'sub-section', 'swear', 'will', and 'year'.

The Act also outlines general rules of construction, such as the coming into operation of enactments, the effect of repeal, the revival of repealed enactments, the construction of references to repealed enactments, the commencement and termination of time, the computation of time, the measurement of distances, duty to be taken pro rata in enactments, gender and number, official chiefs and subordinates, provisions as to orders, rules, etc., made under enactments, recovery of fines, provision as to offences punishable under two or more enactments, meaning of service by post, citation of enactments, saving for previous enactments, rules and bye-laws, application of Act to Ordinances, and application of Act to Acts made by the Governor-General.

The schedule attached to the Act, which has been repealed, originally listed the enactments repealed by the General Clauses Act.
