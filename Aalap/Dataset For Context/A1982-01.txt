Summary:

The African Development Fund Act, 1982, is an Act passed by the Indian Parliament to implement the African Development Fund Agreement and related matters. The Act has the following key provisions:

1. Short title, extent, and commencement: This Act may be called the African Development Fund Act, 1982, and extends to the whole of India. It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint.
2. Definitions: The Act defines the "Agreement" as the African Development Fund Agreement and "Fund" as the African Development Fund established under the Agreement.
3. Payments to Fund: The Central Government shall pay out of the Consolidated Fund of India all such sums as may be required for the purpose of paying the subscription payable by the Central Government to the Fund under Articles 6, 7, 8, and 9 of the Agreement, any sums payable by the Central Government to the Fund under Article 13 of the Agreement, and any charges payable by the Central Government to the Fund under Article 16 of the Agreement.
4. Reserve Bank to be depository for Fund: The Reserve Bank of India shall be the depository of the Indian currency holdings of the Fund.
5. Conferment of status and certain immunities, exemptions, and privileges on Fund and conferment of certain immunities, exemptions, and privileges on its officers and employees: Notwithstanding anything to the contrary contained in any other law, the provisions of the Agreement set out in the Schedule shall have the force of law in India. The Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, amend the Schedule in conformity with any amendments, duly made and adopted, of the provisions of the Agreement set out therein.
6. Power to make rules: The Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, make rules for carrying out the purposes of this Act.
7. Notifications issued under section 5 and rules made under section 6 to be laid before Parliament: Every notification issued under sub-section (2) of section 5 and every rule made under section 6 shall be laid, as soon as may be after it is issued or made, before each House of Parliament, while it is in session, for a total period of thirty days which may be comprised in one session or in two or more successive sessions, and if, before the expiry of the session immediately following the session or the successive sessions aforesaid, both Houses agree in making any modification in the notification or, as the case may be, in the rule or both Houses agree that the notification or rule should not be issued or made, the notification or rule shall thereafter have effect only in such modified form or be of no effect, as the case may be; so, however, that any such modification or annulment shall be without prejudice to the validity of anything previously done under that notification or rule.

The Schedule to the Act contains the provisions of the Agreement which shall have the force of law, specifically Chapter VIII, which deals with the status, immunities, exemptions, and privileges of the Fund. The provisions include immunity from legal process, immunity of assets, immunity of archives, freedom of assets from restriction, privilege for communications, immunities and privileges of officials and personnel, and exemption from taxation. The Fund may waive these immunities, exemptions, and privileges in certain cases where it deems necessary.

In summary, the African Development Fund Act, 1982, provides for the implementation of the African Development Fund Agreement and related matters in India, including the payment of subscriptions and charges to the Fund, the appointment of the Reserve Bank of India as the depository for the Fund's Indian currency holdings, and the conferment of status, immunities, exemptions, and privileges on the Fund and its officers and employees. The Act also empowers the Central Government to make rules for carrying out its provisions and requires that notifications issued under the Act and rules made under it be laid before Parliament.
