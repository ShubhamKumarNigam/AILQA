Summary:

THE INCHEK TYRES LIMITED AND NATIONAL RUBBER MANUFACTURERS LIMITED (NATIONALISATION) ACT, 1984

Title: This Act is titled the Inchek Tyres Limited and National Rubber Manufacturers Limited (Nationalisation) Act, 1984.

ID: The ID of this Act is ACT NO. 17 OF 1984.

Enactment Date: This Act was enacted on [31st March, 1984].

Act Definition: The purpose of this Act is to acquire and transfer the undertakings of the Inchek Tyres Limited and the National Rubber Manufacturers Limited, with a view to securing the proper management of such undertakings so as to subserve the interests of the general public by ensuring the continued manufacture, production and distribution of tyres, tubes and other rubber goods which are essential to the needs of the economy of the country and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto.

Chapters: The Act is divided into several chapters:

* Chapter I: Preliminary
* Chapter II: Acquisition and Transfer of the Undertakings of the Two Companies
* Chapter III: Payment of Amounts
* Chapter IV: Management, Etc., of the Undertakings of the Two Companies
* Chapter V: Provisions Relating to Employees of the Two Companies
* Chapter VI: Commissioner of Payments
* Chapter VII: Miscellaneous

Chapter II, Sections 3 and 4: These sections deal with the transfer and vesting of the undertakings of the two companies in the Central Government and the rights and liabilities of the companies and the Central Government.

Chapter III, Sections 8 and 9: These sections deal with the payment of amounts to the two companies for the transfer and vesting of their undertakings.

Chapter IV, Section 10: This section deals with the management, etc., of the undertakings of the two companies.

Chapter V, Section 13: This section deals with the continuance of employees of the two companies.

Chapter VI, Sections 15 and 16: These sections deal with the appointment of the Commissioner of Payments and the payment of amounts to the Commissioner.

Chapter VII, Section 25: This section states that the provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force or in any instrument having effect by virtue of any law, other than this Act, or in any decree or order of any court, tribunal or other authority.

First Schedule: The First Schedule lists the amount payable to each of the two companies for the transfer and vesting of their undertakings.

Second Schedule: The Second Schedule lists the order of priorities for the discharge of liabilities of the two companies.

The Inchek Tyres Limited and National Rubber Manufacturers Limited (Nationalisation) Act, 1984 is an Act of the Indian Parliament that provides for the acquisition and transfer of the undertakings of the Inchek Tyres Limited and the National Rubber Manufacturers Limited. The Act was enacted on [31st March, 1984] and is divided into several chapters that deal with various aspects of the acquisition and transfer of the undertakings of the two companies. The Act states that the undertakings of the two companies shall, by virtue of the Act, stand transferred to, and shall vest in, the Central Government. The Act also provides for the payment of amounts to the two companies for the transfer and vesting of their undertakings and lists the order of priorities for the discharge of liabilities of the two companies. The Act also deals with the management, etc., of the undertakings of the two companies and the continuance of employees of the two companies. The Act also provides for the appointment of the Commissioner of Payments and the payment of amounts to the Commissioner. The Act states that the provisions of this Act shall have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any other law for the time being in force or in any instrument having effect by virtue of any law, other than this Act, or in any decree or order of any court, tribunal or other authority.
