Summary:
The Panjab District Boards Act, 1883, is an Act to make better provision for local self-government in the Districts of the Panjab. The Act provides for the constitution of district boards and local boards in the territories administered by the Lieutenant-Governor of the Panjab, and defines and regulates the powers to be exercised by those boards. The Act consists of five chapters, with various sections under each chapter. Chapter I contains preliminary provisions, including the short title, repeal of the Panjab Local Rates Act, 1878, and definitions of terms used in the Act. Chapter II deals with the local rate on land, including the power to recover a share of the rate from occupancy-tenants. Chapter III focuses on district and local boards, including their constitution, duties, and powers. Chapter IV contains supplemental provisions as to taxation, such as the recovery of rates, assessment and collection of taxes, and appeals. Chapter V, which has been repealed, contained amendments to the Northern India Ferries Act, 1878. The Act includes various schedules and forms, as well as footnotes providing clarification and context. The Act aims to improve local governance and administration in the Panjab region, and provides a framework for the establishment and operation of district and local boards, as well as provisions for taxation and revenue collection.
