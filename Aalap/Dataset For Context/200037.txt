Summary:
The Semiconductor Integrated Circuits Layout-Design Act, 2000 is an Act to provide for the protection of semiconductor integrated circuits layout-designs and for matters connected therewith or incidental thereto. The Act establishes a Register of Layout-Designs and defines the terms used in the Act. The Act also lays down the conditions for registration and the duration of registration. It also outlines the rights conferred by registration and the infringement of layout-design. The Act establishes an Appellate Board to hear appeals and make decisions regarding the registration of layout-designs. The Act also sets out the powers and duties of the Registrar and the staff of the Appellate Board. The Act provides for the cancellation of registration and the appeal process. The Act also includes provisions for the protection of security of India and the punishment for offences under the Act. The Act also includes miscellaneous provisions such as the protection of action taken in good faith, the exemption of certain persons employed in the ordinary course of business, and the power of the Registrar.
