Summary:
The Nagaland University Act, 1989 establishes and incorporates a teaching and affiliating university in the State of Nagaland. The Act defines the objects of the university, which include disseminating and advancing knowledge, promoting innovations in teaching-learning processes, inter-disciplinary studies and research, educating and training manpower for the development of the State of Nagaland, and promoting the intellectual, academic and cultural development of the people of the State. The Act transfers all properties of the North-Eastern Hill University in the State of Nagaland to the University and vests them in the University. The University has the power to provide instruction in various branches of learning, grant degrees or other academic distinctions, organize extra-mural studies, training and extension services, institute Principalships, Professorships, Readerships, Lecturerships and other teaching or academic positions, and recognize institutions of higher learning for various purposes. The University also has the power to create administrative, ministerial and other posts, cooperate or collaborate with other universities or authorities or institutions of higher learning, establish campuses, special centers, specialised laboratories or other units for research and instruction, institute and award fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals and prizes, establish and maintain colleges, institutions and halls, make provisions for research and advisory services, and organize and conduct refresher courses, workshops, seminars and other programs for teachers, evaluators and other academic staff. The University has the jurisdiction to extend to the whole of the State of Nagaland, and is open to persons of either sex and of whatever caste, creed, race or class, subject to the provisions of the Act. The Visitor is the President of India, who may cause an inspection or inquiry to be made by such person or persons as he may direct, of the University, its buildings, laboratories and equipment, and of any College or Institution maintained by the University or admitted to its privileges. The University shall have a Chancellor, a Vice-Chancellor, a Pro-Vice-Chancellor, Deans of Schools, a Registrar, a Finance Officer, and such other officers as may be declared by the Statutes to be officers of the University. The authorities of the University include the Court, the Executive Council, the Academic Council, the Planning Board, the Boards of Schools, the Finance Committee, and such other authorities as may be declared by the Statutes to be the authorities of the University. The Statutes, Ordinances and Regulations made under the Act provide for the constitution, powers and functions of the authorities and other bodies of the University, the appointment and continuance in office of the members of the said authorities and bodies, the appointment, powers and duties of the officers of the University and their emoluments, the appointment of teachers, academic staff and other employees of the University and their emoluments, the conditions of service of employees including their code of conduct, the procedure for arbitration in cases of dispute between employees or students and the University, the procedure for appeal to the Executive Council by any employee or student against the action of any officer or authority of the University, the conferment of autonomous status on a College or an Institution or a Department, the establishment and abolition of Schools, Departments, Centres, Halls, Colleges and Institutions, the conferment of honorary degrees, the withdrawal of degrees, diplomas, certificates and other academic distinctions, the conditions under which Colleges and Institutions may be admitted to the privileges of the University and the withdrawal of such privileges, the institution of fellowships, scholarships, studentships, medals and prizes, the delegation of powers vested in the authorities or officers of the University, the management of Colleges and Institutions established by the University, the supervision and management of Colleges and Institutions admitted to the privileges of the University, and the setting up of a machinery for redressal of grievances of employees.
