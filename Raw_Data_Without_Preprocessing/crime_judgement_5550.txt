                       VERSUS


      MANGESH VASANTRAO DESHPANDE & ANR                RESPONDENTS




                                  O R D E R

                  Leave granted.

                  Having heard learned counsel for the parties to  the  lis,
      we are of the opinion that the High Court, in exercise of  its  powers
      under Section 482 of the Criminal Procedure Code, ought  not  to  have
      interfered with the orders passed by the learned District  &  Sessions
      Judge,  Akola  in  Case   No.Misc.Cr.Appln.No.803   of   2011,   dated
      13.12.2011. Accordingly, we allow this appeal and set aside the orders
      passed  by  the  learned  single  Judge  in  Case   No.Crl.Application
      (APPLN)No.6 of 2012 dated 29.02.2012 and restore the orders passed  by
      the District & Sessions Judge, Akola.
                  Ordered accordingly.
