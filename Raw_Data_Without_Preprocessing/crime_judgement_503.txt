                                  O R D E R

1.          Leave granted.

2.          We have heard learned counsel for the parties to the lis.

3.          Vide order dated 16th January, 2012, while issuing notice,  this
Court has ordered that :
            “,,,,,Meanwhile, petitioners shall not be arrested....”

4.          In the facts and circumstances  of  the  case  and  having  gone
through the records of the case, we are of the opinion that the order  dated
16.01.2012 be made absolute and is made absolute with  a  further  direction
that they shall join investigation as and when called for  and  shall  abide
by  the  provisions  of  Section  438(2)  of  Cr.P.C.  Appeal  disposed   of
accordingly.
