Supreme Court of India Lal Sahab Singh vs The State Of Uttar Pradesh on 24 August, 2017 1 IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CRIMINAL APPEAL NO(S) 1496 OF 2017 [Arising out of Special Leave Petition (Criminal) No.4018 of 2017] LAL SAHAB SINGH ...APPELLANT(S) VERSUS THE STATE OF UTTAR PRADESH ...RESPONDENT(S) ORDER 1. We have heard the learned counsel for the petitioner as well as the learned counsel for the State of Uttar Pradesh. 2. Leave granted. 3. The accused appellant is in custody since 13th November, 2014. Though the learned counsel for the State submits that charges have been framed the said fact is disputed by the learned counsel for the accused appellant. In any event, the trial has not commenced. Taking into account the long period of custody suffered and the facts stated above we are inclined to release the appellant on bail.
