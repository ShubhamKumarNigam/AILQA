Appellant(s) Versus STATE OF KERALA & ANR. ... Respondent(s) ORDER Leave granted. Having heard learned counsel for the respective parties, and the submissions made on behalf of the appellant, that the fine amount of Rs.25,000/- has already been deposited, we while confirming the conviction, set aside the sentence regarding imprisonment. The judgment and order of the High Court is modified accordingly. The appeal is disposed of with the aforesaid modification of the order impugned. ...................J.
