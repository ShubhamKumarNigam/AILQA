Supreme Court of India Preetam @ Preetam Prasad & Anr vs State Of M.P on 3 December, 2009Bench: Harjit Singh Bedi, J.M. Panchal IN THE SUPREME COURT OF INDIA CRIMINAL APPELLATE JURISDICTION CRIMINAL APPEAL NO. 362 OF 2003 PREETAM @ PREETAM PRASAD & ANR. ..... APPELLANTS VERSUS STATE OF M.P. ..... RESPONDENT O R D E R We find that the appellants have already got away lightly as they were prosecuted under Section 302 of the IPC but they have been convicted only under Section 326 and the sentence has been reduced by the High court to that already undergone by them. Moreover, we are of the opinion that the concurrent findings of fact call for no interference. The appeal is dismissed. ..................J [HARJIT SINGH BEDI] ..................J [J.M. PANCHAL] NEW DELHI DECEMBER 03, 2009.
